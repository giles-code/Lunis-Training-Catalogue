## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining personal liability, the protections available to directors and a practical plan for the director's first year on the board.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the routes to personal regulatory, civil and criminal liability
- Evaluate protections including indemnities, insurance and independent advice
- Produce a personal induction and development plan

## Syllabus

- Enforcement against individuals: case studies and lessons
- Indemnities, directors' and officers' insurance and independent advice
- Record-keeping, dissent and when to resign
- Workshop: the director's first-year induction plan
