## Aim and Purpose

How to assess whether a firm handles complaints fairly and promptly, analyses root causes, and uses complaints data to improve products, services and customer outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the fairness and timeliness of a firm's complaints process
- Assess the quality of root-cause analysis and management information
- Use complaints data as an indicator of wider conduct risk

## Syllabus

- Complaints policies, timelines, redress and escalation to dispute resolution schemes
- Complaints classification, recording and management information
- Root-cause analysis and feedback into products and processes
- Complaints as a proxy for culture and conduct risk
