## Aim and Purpose

How firms assess whether funds deliver value for the costs charged, and how to disclose costs, risks and objectives in a way that is fair, clear and not misleading.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess whether a fund delivers value relative to its costs
- Ensure cost and charges disclosure is complete and accurate
- Evaluate the clarity and accuracy of fund documents and marketing

## Syllabus

- Fee structures, undue costs and value assessments
- Cost and charges disclosure, including transaction costs
- Prospectus, key information documents and periodic reporting
- Marketing communications and sustainability-related claims
