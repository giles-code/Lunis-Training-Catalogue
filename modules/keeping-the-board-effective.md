## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board effectiveness action plan covering evaluation, succession, skills, induction and continuing development.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the design of board effectiveness reviews and the follow-through on findings
- Use a skills matrix to identify gaps and inform succession and development
- Produce a board effectiveness action plan

## Syllabus

- Board evaluation: internal and external reviews, and tracking actions to completion
- Succession planning for the chair, independent directors and key executives
- Skills matrices, induction and tailored development
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month effectiveness action plan
