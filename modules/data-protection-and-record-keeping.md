## Aim and Purpose

How to assess firms' record-keeping arrangements and their handling of customer data, so that records are complete, retrievable and protected, and evidence the firm's compliance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain record-keeping obligations and their importance to supervision
- Assess data protection governance and the handling of customer data
- Evaluate whether records are complete, retrievable and secure

## Syllabus

- Record-keeping requirements: transactions, communications and decisions
- Data protection principles and governance in regulated firms
- Data breaches, incident response and notification
- Testing record retrieval during inspections
