## Aim and Purpose

How authorities monitor the build-up of system-wide vulnerabilities and choose among macroprudential tools, matching each tool to the risk it is designed to address.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a systemic risk dashboard drawing on firm and market data
- Assess the main macroprudential tools and their trade-offs
- Match tools to specific systemic risk drivers and decide when to deploy them

## Syllabus

- Systemic risk dashboards and system-wide stress testing
- Countercyclical and systemic capital buffers
- Sectoral capital requirements and borrower-based measures
- Case study: an emerging systemic risk and the policy response
