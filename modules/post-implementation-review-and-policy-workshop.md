## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining how the authority evaluates whether rules have achieved their aims, and applying the full policy cycle to a live scenario.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a post-implementation review with measurable success criteria
- Evaluate evidence on whether a rule has met its objectives
- Produce a policy proposal applying the full policy cycle

## Syllabus

- Setting success measures at the design stage
- Post-implementation review methods and data
- Amending, simplifying or withdrawing rules that do not work
- Workshop: from problem statement to draft rule and review plan
