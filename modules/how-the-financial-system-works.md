## Aim and Purpose

The core functions of the financial system, the main institutions and markets that perform them, and how money, credit and risk flow through the economy.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the economic functions of banks, insurers, markets and payment systems
- Describe how institutions and markets connect households, firms and government
- Identify the links between the financial sector, public finances and growth

## Syllabus

- Intermediation, maturity transformation, risk transfer and payments
- Banks, insurers, pension schemes, funds and non-bank lenders
- Money, bond, equity and derivatives markets
- The financial sector's contribution to growth, tax and employment
