## Aim and Purpose

How to recognise a complaint, handle it fairly and promptly, and use complaints as insight into customer harm and its root causes.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise expressions of dissatisfaction that amount to a complaint
- Handle complaints fairly, promptly and with empathy
- Explain how root cause analysis turns complaints into improvements

## Syllabus

- What counts as a complaint, however and wherever it is expressed
- Handling and resolving complaints: timeliness, fairness and redress
- Referral to the external dispute resolution body
- Root cause analysis and learning from complaints
