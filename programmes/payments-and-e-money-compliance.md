## Aim and Purpose

To give payment and e-money firms a practical understanding of the rules and supervisory expectations that apply to their business, from authorisation and safeguarding to fraud and wind-down. Participants leave able to strengthen their controls, evidence compliance and see their payments business the way a supervisor does.

## What It Covers

Authorisation and the regulatory perimeter, safeguarding, governance and capital, operational and security risk, fraud and authorised push payment scams, AML/CFT and sanctions, agents and distributors, open banking, wind-down planning, and stablecoins and new payment rails.

## Intended Audience

- Compliance officers, MLROs and risk managers at payment institutions and e-money issuers
- Finance, operations, safeguarding and fraud teams responsible for day-to-day controls
- Directors, senior managers and founders of payment and fintech firms

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the regulatory framework and authorisation requirements for payment and e-money firms
- Design and evidence robust safeguarding arrangements
- Assess governance, capital, operational and security risk controls
- Implement fraud, AML/CFT and sanctions controls suited to payment business models
- Oversee agents, distributors and open banking arrangements effectively
- Prepare a credible wind-down plan and assess the impact of new payment rails

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Modules combine short briefings with case studies, group exercises and practical templates, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's business model.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of payments, e-money or compliance is helpful. No prior knowledge of specific payment services rules is required.

## Core Reading

- CPMI, Fast Payments: Enhancing the Speed and Availability of Retail Payments (2016)
- FATF, International Standards on Combating Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation: The FATF Recommendations (as updated)
- CPMI and World Bank, Payment Aspects of Financial Inclusion (2016)
