## Aim and Purpose

How to assess the mandate, independence, resourcing and reporting lines of a firm's compliance function, and whether its risk-based monitoring programme provides real assurance.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the independence, authority and resourcing of the compliance function
- Evaluate the design and coverage of a compliance monitoring programme
- Judge whether compliance reporting gives the board an accurate picture

## Syllabus

- Compliance mandate, reporting lines and the head of compliance role
- Compliance risk assessment and the risk-based monitoring plan
- Testing methodology, findings tracking and reporting to the board
- Red flags: under-resourcing, outsourced compliance and box-ticking
