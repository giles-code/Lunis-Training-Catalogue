## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to help participants think through the practical considerations in building a supervisory authority's own SupTech capability, including tool selection, in-house analytics skills, and the build-versus-buy decision.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior staff involved in the authority's own data and technology infrastructure.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what SupTech is and how it differs from RegTech
- Describe common categories of SupTech tools
- Evaluate build-versus-buy considerations for SupTech capability
- Assess the in-house analytics skills needed to sustain SupTech tools
- Identify practical steps for starting or scaling a SupTech capability

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Introduction to SupTech (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain SupTech and its common categories*
- SupTech versus RegTech: whose tools are they?
- Categories: data collection, analytics, reporting automation, market surveillance
- Global examples of SupTech deployment
- Realistic expectations of what SupTech can and cannot do
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: current use of technology within participants' own authorities

**Session 2: Build versus buy (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Evaluate build-versus-buy considerations*
- Factors favouring build: control, customisation, cost over time
- Factors favouring buy: speed, vendor expertise, lower upfront risk
- Hybrid approaches
- Procurement considerations specific to public authorities
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a build-versus-buy decision for a sample SupTech use case

**Session 3: Skills and getting started (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess required skills and identify practical next steps*
- In-house analytics skills needed to sustain SupTech tools
- Resourcing and retention challenges for specialist technical staff
- Starting small: pilot approaches
- Case study: an authority's SupTech journey
Activities:
- Case study: design a phased SupTech capability-building plan

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: SupTech categories, build-versus-buy, skills, starting points
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Introduction to SupTech (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Build versus buy (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Skills and getting started (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- SupTech capability is as much about people and process as about the tools themselves
- Build-versus-buy has no universal right answer — it depends on the authority's resourcing and objectives
- Starting small with a focused pilot is usually more successful than a large, all-at-once build
