## Aim and Purpose

Where regulatory obligations attach across the centralised to decentralised spectrum, how they vary by service, and the compliance consequences of operating from one licence across several jurisdictions.

## Learning Objectives

- Map the firm's activities to regulated crypto-asset services
- Identify business model red flags that attract supervisory concern
- Manage home and host obligations when serving clients across borders

## Syllabus

- The centralised to decentralised spectrum and perimeter questions
- Obligations by service: trading platforms, brokers, custodians, advisers, issuers
- Red flags: overlapping roles, opaque fees and fragmented group structures
- Cross-border services, home and host supervisors and substance
