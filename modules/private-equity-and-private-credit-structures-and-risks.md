## Aim and Purpose

How private equity and private credit funds raise, deploy and return capital, the financing techniques now common in private markets, and the risks these create for investors and the system.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain private equity and private credit fund structures and lifecycles
- Assess fund finance, continuation vehicles and other liquidity techniques
- Identify concentration and interconnectedness risks in private credit

## Syllabus

- Buyout, growth, direct lending and asset-based finance strategies
- Subscription lines, net asset value loans and payment-in-kind interest
- Continuation vehicles and manager-led secondaries
- Links between private credit, banks and insurers
