## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of systemic risk and contagion channels, and the macroprudential tools available to address them.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior staff involved in crisis preparedness, recovery, and resolution planning.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept of systemic risk and its distinguishing features
- Describe common contagion channels between firms and markets
- Identify systemic risk indicators relevant to a supervisory authority
- Assess the macroprudential tools available to address systemic risk
- Evaluate a scenario for systemic risk and propose a policy response

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Understanding systemic risk (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain systemic risk and its features*
- What distinguishes systemic risk from firm-specific risk
- Interconnectedness, size, and substitutability as systemic risk drivers
- Historical episodes of systemic risk crystallising
- The evolving perimeter of systemic risk beyond banks
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: systemic risk episodes participants have studied or observed

**Session 2: Contagion channels and indicators (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe contagion channels and identify indicators*
- Contagion channels: direct exposure, fire sales, confidence effects
- Systemic risk indicators used by supervisory authorities
- Data challenges in monitoring systemic risk
- Cross-border dimensions of contagion
Activities:
- Group exercise: map contagion channels for a sample interconnected market

**Session 3: Macroprudential tools and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess macroprudential tools and propose a policy response*
- Macroprudential tools: capital buffers, liquidity requirements, exposure limits
- Matching tools to specific systemic risk drivers
- Case study: a scenario with emerging systemic risk
Activities:
- Case study: assess a systemic risk scenario and propose a macroprudential policy response

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: systemic risk features, contagion channels, macroprudential tools
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Understanding systemic risk (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Contagion channels and indicators (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Macroprudential tools and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Systemic risk is fundamentally about interconnectedness, not just the size of any single firm
- Contagion can spread through confidence effects as fast as through direct financial exposure
- Macroprudential tools need to be matched deliberately to the specific systemic risk driver they address
