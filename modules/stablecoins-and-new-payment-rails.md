## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together with the developments reshaping payments: instant payments, stablecoins, tokenised deposits and agentic payments, closing with a compliance action plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how instant payments, stablecoins and tokenised deposits work
- Assess the regulatory and risk implications of new payment rails
- Produce a payments compliance action plan

## Syllabus

- Instant payments and settlement risk
- Stablecoins, e-money tokens and tokenised deposits under regional crypto-asset regimes
- Agentic payments and programmable money
- Workshop: seeing the payments business the way a supervisor does
