## Aim and Purpose

How to build terrorist and proliferation financing indicators into transaction monitoring, investigate alerts quickly and file urgent, usable reports with the national FIU.

## Learning Objectives

- Design monitoring scenarios that reflect terrorist and proliferation typologies
- Investigate alerts and reach a timely decision on suspicion
- File urgent reports and respond to authority requests without tipping off

## Syllabus

- Scenario design, thresholds and network analysis
- Investigating alerts: profiling, links and open-source information
- Urgent reporting, report quality and working with the national FIU
- Law enforcement requests, account monitoring and continuing relationships
