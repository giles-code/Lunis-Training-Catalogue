## Aim and Purpose

How customs and FIUs use risk indicators and data analytics to identify suspicious trade, and the particular vulnerabilities of free trade zones and transhipment hubs.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply trade-based laundering red-flag indicators to cases
- Use price, volume and route analysis to identify anomalies
- Assess the risks posed by free trade zones and transhipment

## Syllabus

- Risk indicators for traders, goods, routes and payments
- Unit-price analysis, mirror trade data and data matching
- Free trade zones: oversight gaps and risk indicators
- Risk targeting and selection for examination
