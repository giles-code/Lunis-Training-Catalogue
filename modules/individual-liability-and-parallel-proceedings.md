## Aim and Purpose

How investigations reach senior individuals, how the firm manages conflicts between corporate and personal interests, and how to handle criminal, civil and cross-border proceedings running in parallel.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how authorities pursue individuals and what reasonable steps they expect
- Manage conflicts, indemnities and separate representation for individuals
- Coordinate the response across criminal, civil and multiple-authority proceedings

## Syllabus

- Senior manager accountability, reasonable steps and prohibition orders
- Conflicts of interest, indemnities and employment actions during investigations
- Criminal referral, sequencing and the national FIU and other agencies
- Cross-border cooperation between authorities and follow-on civil claims
