## Aim and Purpose

A practical workshop in which participants plan and conduct interviews with the board of a case-study firm, test real board decisions and reach an evidenced supervisory conclusion.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan an interview strategy and question set for the chair, directors and executives
- Test whether the board, rather than a shareholder or management, owned key decisions
- Formulate a supervisory conclusion on board effectiveness and the response

## Syllabus

- Testing an optimistic business plan and an intra-group outsourcing decision
- A board's response to a breach of its own risk appetite limit
- Role-play interviews with feedback on questioning and evidence
- Workshop: findings, rating and proposed supervisory response
