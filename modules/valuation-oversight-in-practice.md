## Aim and Purpose

The difference between calculating a NAV and valuing the fund's assets, how the valuation operating model works, and how boards gain assurance on hard-to-value positions.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish NAV calculation from the valuation of underlying assets
- Evaluate the valuation operating model and the choice of calculation agent
- Challenge fair value judgements and price overrides

## Syllabus

- Valuation policy, pricing hierarchy and independent price verification
- Selecting and overseeing the calculation agent and pricing vendors
- Fair value accounting and level three assets
- Price challenges, overrides and valuation committee reporting
