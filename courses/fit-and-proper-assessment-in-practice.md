## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in assessing the fitness and propriety of controllers, directors, and senior managers, both at authorisation and on an ongoing basis, applying consistent, defensible criteria.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level authorisations/licensing caseworkers assessing new-entrant applications.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the fitness and propriety criteria: competence, capability and probity
- Apply structured techniques for assessing competence and capability
- Assess probity using available evidence sources
- Evaluate consistency in fit-and-proper decision-making
- Formulate a fit and proper assessment for a sample candidate

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Fitness and propriety criteria (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the fit-and-proper criteria*
- Competence, capability and probity as distinct assessment strands
- Sources of evidence: CVs, references, disclosures, public record checks
- Assessing fitness at authorisation versus on an ongoing basis
- Proportionality in fit-and-proper assessment
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: fit-and-proper challenges participants have faced

**Session 2: Assessing competence and probity (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply techniques for assessing competence and probity*
- Structured techniques for assessing competence and capability
- Assessing probity: honesty, integrity, and financial soundness
- Handling adverse information and how to weigh it
- Consistency across different candidates and roles
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample candidate's fitness and propriety file

**Session 3: Consistency and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate consistency and formulate an assessment*
- Ensuring consistent decisions across similar cases
- Documenting fit-and-proper decisions defensibly
- Case study: a borderline fit-and-proper case
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a fit and proper assessment and decision for a sample candidate

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: criteria, assessment techniques, consistency
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Fitness and propriety criteria (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing competence and probity (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Consistency and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Fit and proper assessment rests on three distinct strands — competence, capability, and probity — that should each be assessed explicitly
- Consistency across cases is essential to defensibility and fairness
- Fit and proper assessment does not end at authorisation; it continues throughout a person's tenure in a role
