## Aim and Purpose

What good board information looks like, why oversized packs harm decisions, how to get the information the board needs without micromanaging, and how directors handle technical topics such as technology and AI.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate board papers against the qualities of good information
- Reduce information asymmetry between management and non-executives
- Engage with technical presentations and know what questions to ask

## Syllabus

- Good information: accurate, timely, clear, comparable and focused on decisions
- Board pack size, information overload and cover papers that state the question
- Information asymmetry, independent assurance and the fear of micromanaging
- Technical topics, technology and AI: building enough knowledge to challenge
