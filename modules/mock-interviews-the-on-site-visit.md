## Aim and Purpose

Participants rotate through mock interviews with the case-study firms' management and board, played from confidential role cards, uncovering facts that the desk analysis could not reveal.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply interview planning and questioning techniques under realistic conditions
- Uncover and record new evidence on governance, controls and the business model
- Give and receive structured feedback on interviewing practice

## Syllabus

- Interviewing the chief executive, finance director, a key function head and the chair
- Roles: lead interviewer, second interviewer and note-taker, interviewee and observer
- Observer checklist: objective, questions, listening, evidence, control and record
- Debrief: revising ratings and drafting risk mitigation actions
