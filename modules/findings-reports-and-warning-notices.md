## Aim and Purpose

How to analyse the evidence against the alleged breaches, draft investigation reports and warning notices, and test the case before it goes to decision.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess whether the evidence establishes each element of the breach
- Draft clear, balanced investigation findings and warning notices
- Test the case for weaknesses and procedural risk

## Syllabus

- Analysing evidence against the standard of proof
- Structuring the investigation report and proposed findings
- Warning notices, representations and access to material
- Internal challenge, legal review and case conferences
