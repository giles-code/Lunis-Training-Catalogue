## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a practical, jargon-free grounding in crypto-assets, stablecoins and tokenisation, the business models built on them and the risks they bring. Participants leave able to challenge management's digital asset strategy, set clear risk appetite and evidence effective oversight to supervisors and auditors.

## What It Covers

Crypto-assets, stablecoins and tokenisation, strategic opportunity and business model risk, custody and safeguarding, financial crime, market integrity, technology and cyber risk, international regulatory approaches, risk appetite, board oversight questions and a board action plan.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs, non-executive and independent directors of firms offering, or planning to offer, digital asset products and services
- Executive directors and senior managers accountable for digital asset strategy, risk, compliance and operations
- Board committee members and company secretaries supporting oversight of new business lines

## Learning Objectives

- Explain in plain language how crypto-assets, stablecoins and tokenised assets work and where the firm is exposed to them
- Challenge management's digital asset strategy, business case and business model assumptions
- Assess custody, safeguarding, financial crime and market integrity risks using structured board-level questions
- Evaluate technology, cyber and third-party risk in digital asset operations
- Compare international regulatory approaches and what supervisors expect of boards
- Set a digital asset risk appetite and agree a board action plan

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two- to three-day programme or as a series of board briefing sessions. Each module combines a short briefing with case studies and a board question set, and modules can be selected or tailored to the board's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Board or senior management experience in financial services is helpful. No technical background in blockchain or digital assets is required.

## Core Reading

- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Crypto-Asset Activities and Markets (2023)
- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets (2023)
- Basel Committee, Prudential Treatment of Cryptoasset Exposures (2022)
