## Aim and Purpose

Designing, approving and reviewing products so that they meet the needs of a defined target market and are distributed to the right customers.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a product approval and review process with clear accountability
- Define target markets and distribution strategies
- Assess existing products against target market needs

## Syllabus

- Product approval committees and governance
- Target market definition and testing
- Manufacturer and distributor responsibilities
- Product reviews, withdrawal and closed-book customers
