## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to deepen participants' understanding of issuer disclosure as a core investor-protection mechanism, drawing on IOSCO's Principles for Issuers, and to build practical skills in assessing whether disclosure practices are full, accurate, and timely.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors assessing consumer protection outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of disclosure in protecting investors
- Describe IOSCO's Principles for Issuers and their disclosure requirements
- Assess whether disclosure is full, accurate and timely
- Evaluate the quality of financial and non-financial disclosures
- Identify red flags in issuer disclosure practices
- Formulate supervisory conclusions on disclosure adequacy

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Disclosure as investor protection (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain why disclosure matters as an investor-protection tool*
- IOSCO's investor-protection objective and the role of disclosure
- The Principles for Issuers: full, accurate and timely disclosure
- Financial versus non-financial disclosure
- Consequences of disclosure failures for investors and markets
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: notable disclosure failures and their investor impact

**Session 2: Assessing disclosure quality (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess whether disclosure meets the full/accurate/timely standard*
- Frameworks for assessing disclosure completeness and accuracy
- Timeliness expectations and common failure points
- Assessing narrative versus quantitative disclosure
- The role of auditors and gatekeepers
Activities:
- Group exercise: review a sample issuer disclosure against the full/accurate/timely standard

**Session 3: Red flags and supervisory application (13:30–15:00)**
*Objective: Identify red flags and form supervisory conclusions*
- Common red flags in issuer disclosure
- Linking disclosure weaknesses to wider governance or business model risk
- Supervisory tools for challenging inadequate disclosure
- Case study: a disclosure red flag scenario
Activities:
- Case study: analyse an issuer's disclosure and produce a supervisory assessment

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (15:00–15:15)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: disclosure as protection, quality assessment, red flags
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Disclosure as investor protection (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing disclosure quality (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Red flags and supervisory application (13:30–15:00)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (15:00–15:15)

**Key Takeaways**
- Disclosure is one of the most direct mechanisms by which regulation protects investors
- Full, accurate and timely disclosure is a single standard with three distinct failure modes
- Disclosure red flags are often the earliest visible sign of deeper governance problems
