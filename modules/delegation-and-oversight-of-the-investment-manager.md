## Aim and Purpose

How the board delegates portfolio management while retaining responsibility, and the oversight framework needed to hold the investment manager to account without making investment decisions itself.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the due diligence and contractual terms underpinning delegation
- Evaluate performance, risk and mandate compliance reporting from the investment manager
- Challenge the manager while respecting the line between oversight and management

## Syllabus

- Delegation versus abdication: what the board cannot hand over
- Initial and ongoing due diligence, mandates and investment restrictions
- Performance, risk and breach reporting to the board
- Fear of micromanaging: monitoring without directing investment decisions
