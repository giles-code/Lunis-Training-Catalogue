## Aim and Purpose

The obligations and risks of issuing or distributing stablecoins, focusing on reserve quality, redemption rights, disclosure and the heightened expectations that apply to systemically significant tokens.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish stablecoin designs and their regulatory treatment
- Evaluate reserve quality, liquidity and redemption resilience under stress
- Design reserve, redemption and disclosure controls for an issuer

## Syllabus

- Single-currency, multi-asset and algorithmic designs and their failure modes
- Reserve composition, custody, attestation and concentration risk
- Redemption rights, run dynamics and stress scenarios
- FSB recommendations on global stablecoin arrangements and significance
