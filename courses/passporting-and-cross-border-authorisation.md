## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how passporting and cross-border authorisation regimes operate in practice, and the cooperation expected between home and host supervisors.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level authorisations/licensing caseworkers assessing new-entrant applications.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept and legal basis of passporting
- Describe the notification and authorisation process for passporting firms
- Assess the respective roles of home and host supervisors under passporting
- Evaluate the risks of passporting for host-country consumer protection
- Identify practical cooperation steps between home and host authorities

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Passporting fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain passporting and its legal basis*
- What passporting is and why it exists
- Legal basis for passporting regimes
- Freedom of establishment versus freedom to provide services
- Which activities are typically passportable
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: passporting experiences from participants' own jurisdictions

**Session 2: Process and home/host roles (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe the passporting process and home/host roles*
- Notification and authorisation process for passporting firms
- Respective responsibilities of home and host supervisors
- Practical challenges: language, timelines, information gaps
- When host intervention powers apply
Activities:
- Group exercise: map the passporting process for a sample cross-border firm

**Session 3: Risks and cooperation (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate consumer protection risks and identify cooperation steps*
- Consumer protection risks in host markets under passporting
- Practical cooperation steps between home and host authorities
- Case study: a passporting firm causing host-market concern
Activities:
- Case study: propose a cooperation response to a passporting concern

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: passporting basis, process, roles, risks
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Passporting fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Process and home/host roles (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Risks and cooperation (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Passporting relies on trust between home and host supervisors that must be actively maintained
- Host supervisors retain some intervention powers even for passported firms, and should know when to use them
- Consumer protection risk in host markets deserves specific attention under passporting regimes
