## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in gathering and preserving evidence to a standard suitable for enforcement or court proceedings, including disclosure obligations and maintaining a defensible evidential record.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level enforcement investigators and case officers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the evidential standards required for enforcement action
- Describe disclosure obligations during an investigation
- Apply techniques for maintaining a defensible chain of custody
- Draft an evidentially sound witness statement
- Evaluate a sample evidence file for gaps or weaknesses

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Evidential standards (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain evidential standards and disclosure obligations*
- Evidential standards required for enforcement versus supervisory action
- Disclosure obligations to the subject of an investigation
- Types of evidence: documentary, testimonial, digital, expert
- Legal privilege and its limits
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: evidential challenges from participants' own investigations

**Session 2: Chain of custody and evidence preservation (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply chain-of-custody techniques*
- Maintaining a defensible chain of custody
- Preserving digital evidence without contamination
- Documenting the provenance of evidence
- Common evidence-handling errors that undermine cases
Activities:
- Exercise: identify chain-of-custody gaps in a sample evidence file

**Session 3: Witness statements and evidence review (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Draft a witness statement and evaluate an evidence file*
- Structuring an evidentially sound witness statement
- Avoiding leading or suggestive statement-taking
- Case study: a case undermined by evidential weaknesses
Activities:
- Case study: draft a witness statement and evaluate a sample evidence file for gaps

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: standards, disclosure, chain of custody, statements
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Evidential standards (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Chain of custody and evidence preservation (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Witness statements and evidence review (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Evidence gathered to a supervisory standard is often insufficient for enforcement action — the bar is higher
- A broken chain of custody can undermine an otherwise strong case
- Disclosure obligations must be actively managed throughout an investigation, not just at its conclusion
