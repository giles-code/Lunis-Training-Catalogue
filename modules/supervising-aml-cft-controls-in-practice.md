## Aim and Purpose

How to assess a firm's AML/CFT governance, business-wide risk assessment, due diligence and monitoring, and translate findings into consistent supervisory action.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess a firm's AML/CFT governance and business-wide risk assessment
- Evaluate customer due diligence, monitoring and suspicious activity reporting
- Apply consistent supervisory judgement and follow-up

## Syllabus

- Board oversight, the AML compliance officer and management information
- Customer due diligence, enhanced due diligence and beneficial ownership
- Desk-based reviews, onsite inspections and sample testing
- From findings to remediation plans and enforcement referral
