## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in the payments value chain, the forms of money in use today, and the infrastructure that clears and settles retail, wholesale and cross-border payments.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish central bank money, commercial bank money and e-money
- Explain clearing, settlement and the role of payment infrastructures
- Describe card, account-to-account, mobile money and wallet payments

## Syllabus

- Forms of money and the singleness of money
- Large-value, retail and fast payment systems
- Card schemes, mobile money, wallets and payment initiation
- The PFMI and the oversight of payment infrastructures
