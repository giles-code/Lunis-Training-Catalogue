## Aim and Purpose

Why central banks are exploring retail and wholesale digital currencies, the objectives they serve, and the risks to banks, privacy and the financial system that government must weigh.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish retail and wholesale CBDC and their objectives
- Assess impacts on bank funding, credit and financial stability
- Evaluate privacy, inclusion and public trust considerations

## Syllabus

- Motivations: monetary sovereignty, inclusion, efficiency and resilience
- Disintermediation, holding limits and remuneration choices
- Privacy, data and civil liberties
- Lessons from launched, piloted and paused CBDC projects
