## Aim and Purpose

Understanding the limits of ESG data and ratings, assessing providers and building data governance fit for risk management and disclosure.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the quality, coverage and comparability of ESG data
- Evaluate ESG ratings methodologies and their limitations
- Design data governance for sustainability data

## Syllabus

- ESG data sources, gaps and estimation
- Ratings divergence and methodology transparency
- Due diligence on ESG data and ratings providers
- IOSCO recommendations on ESG ratings and data providers
