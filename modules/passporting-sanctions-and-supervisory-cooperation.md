## Aim and Purpose

How MiCA passporting works for home and host authorities, the sanctions obligations that apply to crypto operations, and the cooperation mechanisms linking national competent authorities, ESMA and the EBA.

## Learning Objectives

- Manage home and host responsibilities for passporting CASPs
- Assess sanctions screening, including wallet screening and self-hosted wallet controls
- Operate information exchange and coordinated responses to cross-border concerns

## Syllabus

- Passport notifications, reverse solicitation limits and host authority powers
- Letter-box risk, substance and home–host information gaps
- Significant CASPs, ESMA convergence tools and peer reviews
- Sanctions evasion typologies and EU restrictive measures in crypto markets
