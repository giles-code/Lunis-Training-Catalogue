## Aim and Purpose

The technical grounding needed to engage credibly with AI governance: how models are built, trained and deployed, how generative AI differs from traditional approaches, and what each choice means for risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning models are built and deployed
- Distinguish statistical models, machine learning and generative AI, and their risk implications
- Assess the governance trade-offs between building AI in-house and buying from vendors

## Syllabus

- The AI system lifecycle: data, training, testing, deployment and retirement
- Statistics, machine learning and large language models compared
- Build versus buy: accountability for vendor and third-party AI
- Documenting the business case for an AI use case
