## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together: criminal referral and parallel proceedings, cooperation with domestic and foreign authorities, giving evidence before tribunals and courts, and feeding outcomes back into supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Decide when criminal referral is appropriate and manage parallel proceedings
- Use information-sharing gateways with prosecutors and domestic and foreign authorities
- Prepare to give evidence and defend a decision before a tribunal or court

## Syllabus

- Criminal referral, sequencing and the risk of prejudicing proceedings
- Cooperation with police, tax authorities, the national FIU and foreign regulators
- Giving evidence, appeals and publication of outcomes
- Workshop: taking a case from referral to outcome
