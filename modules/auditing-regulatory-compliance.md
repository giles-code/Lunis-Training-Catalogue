## Aim and Purpose

How to audit the firm's compliance framework and its adherence to regulatory requirements, including the compliance function itself, regulatory reporting and the management of regulatory change.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan audits of the compliance framework and compliance monitoring
- Test the accuracy and controls behind regulatory returns
- Evaluate how the firm identifies and implements regulatory change

## Syllabus

- Auditing the compliance function: mandate, resources and monitoring programme
- Regulatory reporting: data lineage, reconciliations and sign-off
- Regulatory change management and obligations registers
- Breach identification, recording and notification to the authority
