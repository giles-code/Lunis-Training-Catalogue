## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board action plan, examining the governance questions raised by AI, sustainability claims and tokenised fund structures.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess governance risks in the use of AI by managers and service providers
- Challenge sustainability claims and the risk of greenwashing
- Produce a board action plan for emerging risks

## Syllabus

- AI in portfolio management, risk and operations: board oversight questions
- ESG strategies, disclosures and greenwashing risk
- Tokenised fund units and distributed ledger infrastructure
- Workshop: the fund board's twelve-month oversight plan
