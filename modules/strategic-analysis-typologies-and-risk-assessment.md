## Aim and Purpose

How FIUs use aggregated data to identify trends, typologies and vulnerabilities, and how strategic products inform national risk assessments, policy-making and supervisory priorities.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish strategic from operational analysis and their audiences
- Design a strategic analysis project from question to finished product
- Contribute FIU findings to national and sectoral risk assessments

## Syllabus

- Purposes and audiences of strategic analysis
- Methods: trend analysis, aggregated data and environmental scanning
- Writing typologies and red-flag indicators
- Informing national risk assessment, policy and supervisory priorities
