## Aim and Purpose

How capital and liquidity behave as stress develops, how to project resources through recovery and wind-down, and how the plan links to the firm's internal capital and liquidity adequacy assessment.

## Learning Objectives

- Project capital and liquidity through a stress scenario and wind-down period
- Identify sources of liquidity strain, including margin calls, client outflows and trapped cash
- Link wind-down resource needs to the firm's internal adequacy assessment

## Syllabus

- Liquidity forecasting, buffers and contingency funding
- Capital depletion, losses and the timing of breaches
- Estimating wind-down costs and the resources to hold against them
- Integrating recovery and wind-down with internal capital and liquidity assessments
