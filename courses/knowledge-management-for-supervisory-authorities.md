## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in designing systems and practices that preserve institutional knowledge and supervisory precedent despite staff turnover.

## Intended Audience

Managers and learning & development leads responsible for building supervisory capability within the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the risks that staff turnover poses to institutional memory
- Describe knowledge management approaches suited to supervisory work
- Assess the adequacy of a case precedent system
- Evaluate handover practices for departing staff
- Formulate a knowledge management improvement plan

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Institutional memory and turnover risk (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain turnover risk to institutional memory*
- Why supervisory judgement depends heavily on tacit institutional knowledge
- The cost of losing precedent and context when staff leave
- Common knowledge management failure modes in regulatory bodies
- Balancing documentation burden against knowledge preservation needs
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: knowledge loss episodes participants have experienced

**Session 2: Knowledge management approaches (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe approaches and assess precedent systems*
- Approaches: case precedent databases, communities of practice, mentoring
- Designing an accessible, searchable case precedent system
- Balancing formal systems against informal knowledge networks
- Assessing whether a precedent system is actually used
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample case precedent system for usability and completeness

**Session 3: Handover practices and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate handover practices and formulate an improvement plan*
- Good practice in staff handover and offboarding
- Capturing tacit knowledge before it is lost
- Case study: a knowledge gap discovered after a departure
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a knowledge management improvement plan for a sample authority

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: turnover risk, knowledge management approaches, handover practice
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Institutional memory and turnover risk (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Knowledge management approaches (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Handover practices and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Supervisory judgement depends heavily on institutional memory that is easily lost through turnover
- A precedent system only has value if it is genuinely used, not just maintained
- Good handover practice should be a deliberate discipline, not left to individual goodwill
