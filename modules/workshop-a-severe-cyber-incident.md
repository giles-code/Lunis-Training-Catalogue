## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a board simulation of an unfolding cyber incident affecting an important business service, followed by a board action plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the programme's frameworks to a live crisis scenario
- Evaluate the board's decisions, information needs and communications under pressure
- Produce a board action plan for resilience and cyber oversight

## Syllabus

- Simulation: a ransomware attack on a critical service provider
- Decision points: escalation, tolerance breach, ransom and disclosure
- Debrief: what the board needed to know and when
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month resilience oversight plan
