## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a committee improvement plan, covering self-assessment, external review, skills, succession and learning from failures.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate committee effectiveness using structured self-assessment
- Use a skills matrix to plan membership, succession and development
- Produce a committee improvement plan

## Syllabus

- Committee self-assessment and external review
- Skills, induction and succession for committee chairs and members
- Lessons from control and audit failures
- Workshop: the committee's twelve-month improvement plan
