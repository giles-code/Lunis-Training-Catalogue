## Aim and Purpose

How fund managers delegate portfolio management, risk management and administration, how supervisors test whether the manager retains real control, and the operational resilience risks delegation creates.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the regulatory framework for delegation and outsourcing by fund managers
- Assess whether a manager retains effective oversight and sufficient substance
- Identify concentration, continuity and letter-box entity risks

## Syllabus

- Delegation models: portfolio management, risk management, administration and distribution
- Substance tests: people, expertise, decision-making and oversight evidence
- Third-party concentration, technology dependency and operational resilience
- Red flags of letter-box entities and white-label platforms
