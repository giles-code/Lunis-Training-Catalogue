## Aim and Purpose

Assessing and controlling money laundering, terrorist financing and sanctions evasion risk in crypto-asset activities, using blockchain analytics and implementing the FATF travel rule.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm's exposure to crypto-specific financial crime typologies
- Build CDD, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and MLRO arrangements
- Implement the travel rule, including for unhosted wallets and counterparty due diligence

## Syllabus

- Typologies: mixers, privacy coins, ransomware and sanctions evasion
- Blockchain analytics, wallet screening and alert management
- FATF Recommendation 16 and the travel rule in practice
- MLRO independence, suspicious transaction reporting and the national FIU
