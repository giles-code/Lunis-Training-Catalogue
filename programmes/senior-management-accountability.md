## Aim and Purpose

To give executives and senior managers a clear, practical understanding of individual accountability: what they are responsible for, what reasonable steps look like, and how authorities judge failure. Participants leave able to define their responsibilities, oversee what they delegate and evidence their decisions in a way that stands up to supervisory and enforcement scrutiny.

## What It Covers

The purpose of senior manager accountability regimes, statements of responsibilities, the duty of responsibility and reasonable steps, delegation, oversight and handovers, conduct rules and fitness and propriety, evidencing reasonable steps, lessons from enforcement cases, and personal accountability for AI and technology decisions.

## Intended Audience

- Executive directors, senior managers and heads of business lines holding senior manager functions
- Heads of compliance, risk, internal audit and other control functions with personal accountability
- Company secretaries, HR and compliance staff who administer the accountability regime

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and building blocks of senior manager accountability and certification regimes
- Define personal responsibilities clearly in statements of responsibilities
- Apply the duty of responsibility and the reasonable steps test to real scenarios
- Design delegation, oversight and handover arrangements that preserve accountability
- Evidence reasonable steps through proportionate, contemporaneous records
- Assess personal accountability for AI and technology and see the firm the way a supervisor does

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Modules combine short briefings with scenario exercises, generic enforcement cases and group discussion, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's accountability framework.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some management experience in a regulated firm is helpful. No legal background is required.

## Core Reading

- FSB, Strengthening Governance Frameworks to Mitigate Misconduct Risk: A Toolkit for Firms and Supervisors (2018)
- Group of Thirty, Banking Conduct and Culture: A Call for Sustained and Comprehensive Reform (2015)
- Basel Committee, Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015)
