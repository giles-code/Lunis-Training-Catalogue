## Aim and Purpose

The fraud and scam tactics aimed at customers, staff and the firm, from impersonation and investment scams to insider fraud, and how staff can intervene before money is lost.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise common authorised payment, investment and impersonation scams
- Identify signs of insider fraud and payment diversion
- Intervene appropriately when a customer may be the victim of a scam

## Syllabus

- External fraud: impersonation, investment, romance and invoice scams
- Internal fraud, collusion and abuse of system access
- Spotting a customer under pressure: conversations that protect them
- Deepfakes, synthetic identities and AI-enabled fraud
