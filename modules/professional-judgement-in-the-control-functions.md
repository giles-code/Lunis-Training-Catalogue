## Aim and Purpose

Why compliance and risk work depends on judgement rather than rule-checking alone, where uncertainty comes from, and what it means to be personally accountable for a call.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of judgement in risk-based compliance and risk work
- Identify the sources of uncertainty in everyday control function decisions
- Describe what a supervisor or reviewer will expect to see behind a judgement

## Syllabus

- From checklist compliance to judgement-led advice and oversight
- Ambiguous rules, incomplete facts and commercial pressure
- The cost of over-cautious and under-cautious calls
- Hindsight: how judgements are reviewed after the event
