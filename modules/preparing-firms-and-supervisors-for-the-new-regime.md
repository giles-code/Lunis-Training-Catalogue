## Aim and Purpose

Turning the package into a practical transition plan: gap analysis against the single rulebook, data and system changes, readiness for AMLA's methodology and possible selection, and the shift in supervisory expectations, ending with a planning workshop.

## Learning Objectives

- Conduct a structured gap analysis against the AML Regulation and emerging technical standards
- Prioritise remediation of data, systems, policies and governance
- Design a transition roadmap for the firm or authority

## Syllabus

- Gap analysis: policies, CDD data, beneficial ownership and reporting
- Preparing for AMLA data requests, methodology and possible direct supervision
- Supervisory transition: methodologies, staffing and coordination with AMLA
- Workshop: a twelve-month AML package transition roadmap
