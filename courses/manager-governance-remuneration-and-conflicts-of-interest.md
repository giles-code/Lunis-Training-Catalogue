## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in assessing an investment manager's own governance arrangements, remuneration structures, and conflicts-of-interest controls.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment management supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the governance arrangements expected of an investment manager
- Describe remuneration code requirements and their purpose
- Assess whether a remuneration structure creates inappropriate incentives
- Identify common conflicts of interest within an investment manager
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a manager's governance and conflicts framework

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Manager governance and remuneration codes (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain governance arrangements and remuneration codes*
- Governance expectations for investment managers
- Remuneration code requirements and their risk-alignment purpose
- Deferral, clawback and malus provisions
- International approaches to remuneration regulation
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: remuneration structures participants have reviewed

**Session 2: Assessing remuneration incentives (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess whether remuneration creates inappropriate incentives*
- Identifying incentive structures that encourage excessive risk-taking
- Assessing alignment between remuneration and client outcomes
- Case examples of remuneration-driven misconduct
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample remuneration structure for incentive risk

**Session 3: Conflicts of interest and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify conflicts and formulate an assessment*
- Common conflicts of interest: side-by-side management, personal account dealing, allocation
- Assessing the adequacy of conflicts management frameworks
- Case study: a manager's governance and conflicts framework under review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a manager's governance and conflicts framework

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: governance, remuneration, conflicts of interest
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Manager governance and remuneration codes (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing remuneration incentives (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Conflicts of interest and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Remuneration structures shape behaviour — assessing incentive alignment is as important as reviewing the numbers
- Conflicts of interest in asset management are pervasive and require active, ongoing management, not a one-off policy
- Manager-level governance quality is often a leading indicator of the quality of client outcomes
