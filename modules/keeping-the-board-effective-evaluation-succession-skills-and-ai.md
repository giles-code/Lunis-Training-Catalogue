## Aim and Purpose

The mechanisms a board uses to stay effective over time, what good practice looks like in each, and the emerging question of how AI is used in matters that come to the board.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the quality and follow-through of board evaluations
- Evaluate succession planning and the use of a skills matrix
- Identify how reliance on AI in board decision-making is governed and assured

## Syllabus

- Internal and external board evaluation and tracking actions to completion
- Succession for the chair, independent directors and key executives
- Skills matrices, capability gaps and appointments on relationships
- AI in the boardroom: validation, accountability and model limits
