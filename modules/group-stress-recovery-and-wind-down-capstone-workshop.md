## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a cross-border stress scenario, covering contagion, recovery and wind-down planning, ring-fencing pressures and communication with supervisors, followed by an action plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify how stress spreads between entities and jurisdictions in a group
- Assess the local entity's recovery and wind-down arrangements in a group context
- Produce an action plan for strengthening group and local governance

## Syllabus

- Contagion channels: funding, services, reputation and shared clients
- Recovery and wind-down planning for a group entity
- Simulation: a parent in difficulty and two supervisors seeking reassurance
- Workshop: the group and local governance action plan
