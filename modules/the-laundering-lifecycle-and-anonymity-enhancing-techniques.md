## Aim and Purpose

How illicit proceeds are placed, layered and integrated using crypto-assets, how mixers, privacy coins, bridges and chain-hopping obscure the trail, and what the firm can still see and control.

## Learning Objectives

- Map placement, layering and integration to the firm's own services
- Explain how mixers, privacy coins, bridges and decentralised protocols break transaction links
- Set policy on anonymity-enhanced transfers and exposure to high-risk services

## Syllabus

- Placement: cash-to-crypto, kiosks, peer-to-peer and over-the-counter trading
- Layering: rapid hops, chain-hopping, bridges, swaps and decentralised protocols
- Mixers, tumblers and privacy coins: what remains visible to the firm
- Integration and off-ramps: where the firm is the exit point
