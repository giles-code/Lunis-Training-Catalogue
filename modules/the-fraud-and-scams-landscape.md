## Aim and Purpose

How fraud has shifted from unauthorised to authorised and socially engineered losses, who the perpetrators are, and why fraud is now a conduct, financial crime and resilience risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish unauthorised fraud, authorised scams, insider and first-party fraud
- Explain the organised crime and technology drivers of current fraud trends
- Identify the firm's obligations and exposures arising from fraud losses

## Syllabus

- Fraud taxonomy: unauthorised, authorised, insider and first-party
- Organised crime, fraud-as-a-service and money mule networks
- Links between fraud, money laundering and consumer harm
- Measuring losses: what the numbers show and conceal
