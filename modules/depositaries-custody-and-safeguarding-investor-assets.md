## Aim and Purpose

Why the separation of fund assets from the manager is fundamental to investor protection, the safekeeping and oversight roles of depositaries and custodians, and what happens when safeguarding fails.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain IOSCO standards on the safekeeping and segregation of fund assets
- Assess depositary and custodian duties, independence and liability
- Evaluate safeguarding arrangements for non-traditional and digital assets

## Syllabus

- Segregation, safekeeping and asset verification across custody chains
- Depositary oversight of the manager and cash flow monitoring
- Independence, conflicts of interest and liability for loss of assets
- Case study: a safeguarding failure and its lessons for investors
