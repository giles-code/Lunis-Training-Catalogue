## Aim and Purpose

How tax authorities obtain information from abroad through exchange on request, automatic and spontaneous exchange, and how that information can, where permitted, support criminal investigations and FIU work.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain exchange on request, automatic exchange and spontaneous exchange
- Use financial account and country-by-country data in investigations
- Apply the conditions governing use of exchanged information for non-tax purposes

## Syllabus

- Exchange on request and the standard of foreseeable relevance
- The Common Reporting Standard and automatic exchange of financial account information
- Spontaneous exchange and country-by-country reporting
- Using tax information for non-tax purposes: conditions and gateways
