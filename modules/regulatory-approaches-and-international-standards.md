## Aim and Purpose

The policy choices countries have made, from prohibition to bespoke regimes, and the international recommendations from the FSB, IOSCO, BCBS and FATF that shape national frameworks.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare prohibition, existing-law, bespoke and hybrid regulatory approaches
- Explain the international standards and the peer review of their implementation
- Identify the gaps and arbitrage risks created by divergent national regimes

## Syllabus

- Same activity, same risk, same regulation: principle and practice
- FSB global framework and IOSCO policy recommendations for crypto-assets
- Prudential treatment of bank crypto exposures
- Regional crypto-asset regimes compared: licensing, conduct and market abuse
