## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by showing how sustainability applies across different roles, when to raise concerns, and practical scenarios on climate risk, client conversations and sustainability claims.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the sustainability responsibilities attached to their own role
- Escalate sustainability and greenwashing concerns through the right channels
- Apply the programme to realistic workplace scenarios

## Syllabus

- Sustainability in front-office, operations, technology, marketing and support roles
- The firm's own footprint: emissions, suppliers and people
- Speaking up on misleading claims and sustainability risks
- Workshop: what would you do? Sustainable finance scenarios
