## Aim and Purpose

How authorities design policy for new business models and technologies, from innovation hubs and regulatory sandboxes to technology-neutral rules and perimeter guidance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and design of innovation hubs and regulatory sandboxes
- Assess the risks of innovation facilitation, including perimeter and consumer risks
- Apply technology-neutral principles when drafting rules for new activities

## Syllabus

- Innovation hubs, sandboxes and testing environments compared
- Eligibility, safeguards, exit and lessons-learned reporting
- Technology neutrality and same activity, same risk, same regulation
- Perimeter guidance for new products and business models
