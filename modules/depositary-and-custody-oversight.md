## Aim and Purpose

The depositary's safekeeping, cash monitoring and oversight duties, the custody and prime broker models behind them, and how the board builds an effective relationship with the depositary.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how custody risk varies by asset type, location and use of assets
- Assess custody, sub-custody and prime broker models and their insolvency risks
- Use depositary reporting to strengthen board oversight

## Syllabus

- Safekeeping, record-keeping, cash flow monitoring and oversight duties
- Custody chains, prime brokers, rehypothecation and insolvency risk
- Cash, financial instruments and other assets: differing risk profiles
- Depositary reports, escalations and liability for loss
