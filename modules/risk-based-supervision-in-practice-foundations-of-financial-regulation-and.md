## Aim and Purpose

How the authority assesses the impact and probability of harm, allocates scarce resources and decides where supervisory attention should go.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain impact and probability as the basis of risk-based supervision
- Apply a simple risk assessment framework to a firm
- Prioritise supervisory work across a portfolio of firms

## Syllabus

- Impact, probability and the authority's risk appetite
- Risk assessment frameworks and supervisory ratings
- Proactive, reactive and thematic supervision
- Proportionality: matching intensity to risk
