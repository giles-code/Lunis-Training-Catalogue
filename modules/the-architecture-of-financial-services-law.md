## Aim and Purpose

How financial services legislation is typically structured, the objectives it must serve, and how international standards and existing national law shape the drafter's task.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the typical structure of a financial services statute
- Explain how statutory objectives guide regulators and courts
- Identify the international standards that legislation must reflect

## Syllabus

- Single statutes, sectoral statutes and consolidation
- Statutory objectives, principles and duties of the regulator
- Translating core principles and FATF standards into national law
- Interaction with company, insolvency, contract and criminal law
