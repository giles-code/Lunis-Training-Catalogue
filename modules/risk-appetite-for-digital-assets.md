## Aim and Purpose

How boards decide which digital asset activities the firm will and will not undertake, and set a risk appetite with measurable limits, triggers and escalation.

## Learning Objectives

- Set a digital asset risk appetite with clear in-scope and prohibited activities
- Define limits and key risk indicators for exposure, counterparties and products
- Link risk appetite to capital, liquidity and wind-down planning

## Syllabus

- Drafting a risk appetite statement for digital assets
- Limits on exposures, counterparties, assets and client types
- Stress scenarios: price collapse, stablecoin de-peg and platform failure
- Orderly exit and wind-down planning
