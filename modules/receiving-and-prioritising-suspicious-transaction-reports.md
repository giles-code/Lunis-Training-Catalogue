## Aim and Purpose

How reports and other disclosures arrive, how their quality is checked, and how a risk-based triage process directs scarce analytical resource to the cases that matter most.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the main report types and disclosures received by the FIU
- Assess report quality and recognise common reporting weaknesses
- Design a risk-based triage and prioritisation model

## Syllabus

- Suspicious transaction reports, threshold reports and cross-border declarations
- Reporting quality: defensive reporting, gaps and data standards
- Triage criteria, risk scoring and case selection
- Managing backlogs, urgent requests and time-critical cases
