## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into proportionate supervisory expectations for quantum readiness, the international work under way and an action plan for the authority's own systems.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare emerging supervisory and international approaches to quantum readiness
- Formulate proportionate supervisory expectations for different types of firm
- Design a quantum readiness plan for the authority and its regulated population

## Syllabus

- International work: BIS, G7 Cyber Expert Group and national cyber agencies
- Embedding quantum risk in cyber and operational resilience supervision
- The authority's own cryptographic exposure and migration
- Workshop: the authority's quantum readiness action plan
