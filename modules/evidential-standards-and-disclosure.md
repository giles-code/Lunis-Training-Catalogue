## Aim and Purpose

The higher evidential bar that enforcement action requires, the types of evidence available, and the disclosure and privilege obligations that run throughout an investigation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the evidential standards for enforcement compared with supervisory action
- Describe disclosure obligations to the subject of an investigation
- Identify the limits that legal privilege places on evidence

## Syllabus

- Standards of proof and why supervisory evidence is often insufficient
- Documentary, testimonial, digital and expert evidence
- Disclosure obligations: managed throughout, not at the end
- Legal privilege and its limits
