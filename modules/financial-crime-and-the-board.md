## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in the financial crime risks a firm faces, the international standards behind them and why supervisors hold the board accountable for the firm's defences.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain money laundering, terrorist and proliferation financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, bribery and tax evasion
- Identify the international standards that shape national requirements
- Define the board's responsibilities and distinguish them from management's

## Syllabus

- The financial crime landscape and how criminals exploit financial firms
- FATF Recommendations, mutual evaluations and the risk-based approach
- Board accountability and senior executive responsibility for financial crime
- Lessons from major enforcement cases
