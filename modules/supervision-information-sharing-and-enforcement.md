## Aim and Purpose

How competent authorities integrate DORA into risk-based supervision, cyber threat information-sharing arrangements, cooperation with cybersecurity authorities, and the remedial measures and penalties available, ending with a roadmap workshop.

## Learning Objectives

- Integrate DORA into supervisory planning, inspections and firm risk assessment
- Explain information-sharing arrangements and cooperation with NIS2 authorities and CSIRTs
- Apply remedial measures and administrative penalties proportionately

## Syllabus

- Supervisory planning, ESA peer reviews and convergence on DORA
- Cyber threat information-sharing arrangements among financial entities
- Cooperation with NIS2 authorities, CSIRTs and other competent authorities
- Workshop: a twelve-month DORA supervisory or remediation roadmap
