## Aim and Purpose

The purpose of capital and liquidity requirements, how they are measured and how supervisors judge whether a firm holds enough to absorb losses and meet obligations.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of capital and liquidity in protecting customers and markets
- Describe how capital requirements and liquidity measures are calculated in outline
- Assess whether a firm's capital and liquidity position supports its business plan

## Syllabus

- Loss absorption, quality of capital and own funds
- Risk-weighted assets, leverage and fixed overheads requirements in outline
- Liquidity risk, funding profiles, buffers and stress
- Reading regulatory returns and capital planning
