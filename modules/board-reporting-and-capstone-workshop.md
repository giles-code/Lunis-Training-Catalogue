## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into board reporting that gives directors a clear view of customer outcomes, followed by a workshop in which participants assess their own firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Design board reporting that evidences customer outcomes
- Explain how the board should challenge outcome reporting
- Produce a prioritised action plan for the firm's conduct risk management

## Syllabus

- The annual board outcomes report
- Board challenge and the use of outcome management information
- Seeing the firm's customers the way a supervisor does
- Workshop: the firm's twelve-month customer outcomes action plan
