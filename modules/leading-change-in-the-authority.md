## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a leadership plan for change, covering culture, capability, transformation programmes and the authority's response to new risks and expectations.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the authority's readiness and capacity for change
- Apply change leadership techniques to a supervisory transformation
- Produce a strategic action plan for the authority

## Syllabus

- Leading cultural and organisational change in a public authority
- Building supervisory capability, talent and succession
- Managing transformation: technology, structure and new mandates
- Workshop: the authority's three-year strategic action plan
