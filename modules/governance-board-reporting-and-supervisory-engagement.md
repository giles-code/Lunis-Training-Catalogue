## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a governance and reporting model for quantum readiness: accountability, board information, assurance and the evidence the firm should be able to show the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Allocate accountability for quantum readiness across the three lines of defence
- Specify the management information and assurance the board needs
- Prepare the evidence of quantum readiness that supervisors will expect

## Syllabus

- Ownership, steering groups and the role of internal audit
- Board reporting: inventory coverage, milestones, exceptions and dependencies
- International work: BIS, G7 Cyber Expert Group and national cyber agencies
- Workshop: the firm's quantum readiness plan and board paper
