## Aim and Purpose

Why regulators are given independence, the accountability mechanisms parliament controls, and how to conduct effective scrutiny hearings and inquiries.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the rationale for regulatory independence and its limits
- Use annual reports, hearings and inquiries to scrutinise regulators
- Distinguish legitimate scrutiny from interference in individual cases

## Syllabus

- Independence, mandates and appointments
- Annual reports, budgets and performance information
- Preparing for and conducting a scrutiny hearing
- A question set for regulators and ministers
