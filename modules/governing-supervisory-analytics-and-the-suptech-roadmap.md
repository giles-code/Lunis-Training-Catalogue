## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a governance and strategy view of supervisory analytics, including model risk, ethics, information security and a phased roadmap for the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the governance, model risk and ethical considerations for supervisory analytics
- Assess the authority's data and SupTech maturity against its supervisory priorities
- Design a phased SupTech and data roadmap

## Syllabus

- Model risk management for the authority's own tools
- Confidentiality, information security and cloud use for supervisory data
- Change management and embedding analytics in supervisory culture
- Workshop: the authority's phased SupTech roadmap
