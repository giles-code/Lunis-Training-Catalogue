## Aim and Purpose

How capital, liquidity and wind-down requirements apply to crypto-asset service providers and issuers, and how supervisors identify financially fragile firms before problems become critical.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply capital and liquidity requirements to different service provider and issuer types
- Evaluate liquidity mismatches, contingency funding and recovery planning
- Identify prudential red flags and select appropriate supervisory responses

## Syllabus

- Own funds, liquidity and wind-down planning for service providers
- Revenue volatility, market cycles and reliance on affiliate funding
- Red flags: thin capital, related-party exposures and untested projections
- Prudential returns analysis, stress testing and early warning indicators
