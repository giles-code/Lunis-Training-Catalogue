## Aim and Purpose

How to write clear, authoritative and defensible reports, internal papers and supervisory letters that set out findings, expectations and next steps for the right audience.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the principles of clear, authoritative regulatory writing
- Structure internal papers and supervisory letters for decision and action
- Evaluate drafts for clarity, tone and legal and reputational risk

## Syllabus

- Writing for the reader: decision-makers, boards and firms
- Structuring a supervisory letter: findings, expectations and deadlines
- Calibrating tone: firmness, fairness and avoiding ambiguity
- Drafting exercise: a supervisory letter following a review
