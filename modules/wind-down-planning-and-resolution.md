## Aim and Purpose

How payment and e-money firms prepare for an orderly wind-down that returns customer funds promptly, and what supervisors expect to see in a credible plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a credible wind-down plan and trigger framework
- Identify the resources and dependencies needed for an orderly exit
- Test the plan and integrate it with safeguarding and recovery arrangements

## Syllabus

- Wind-down triggers, indicators and governance
- Returning customer funds and insolvency considerations
- Critical staff, systems, partners and costs of exit
- Testing, review and board approval
