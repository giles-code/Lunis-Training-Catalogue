## Aim and Purpose

What remains with Member States under AMLD6: the powers and risk-based approach of national supervisors, strengthened FIU mandates and cooperation, and the interconnected registers of beneficial ownership and bank accounts.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the minimum powers, independence and risk-based approach required of national supervisors
- Describe the strengthened role of FIUs and their cooperation through AMLA
- Use beneficial ownership and bank account registers as supervisory and compliance tools

## Syllabus

- Risk-based supervision, sector risk assessments and administrative sanctions powers
- FIU powers, suspension of transactions and joint analyses
- Central beneficial ownership registers, verification and legitimate interest access
- Bank account registers, the single access point and real estate information
