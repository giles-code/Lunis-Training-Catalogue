## Aim and Purpose

How supervisory authorities can use AI and data analytics in their own work, drawing on SupTech and RegTech case studies, and the governance and resourcing constraints they face.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how RegTech and SupTech tools support compliance and supervision
- Analyse case studies of AI-enabled supervisory tools in practice
- Evaluate the opportunities and limits of AI adoption within the authority

## Syllabus

- RegTech used by firms for compliance monitoring and reporting
- SupTech applications, including natural language processing of returns
- Anomaly detection, network analysis and risk dashboards
- Data quality, governance, skills and resourcing for supervisory AI
