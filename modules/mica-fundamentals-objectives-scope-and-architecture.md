## Aim and Purpose

MiCA's objectives, perimeter and taxonomy, how Level 1 is built out through ESMA and EBA technical standards and guidelines, and where gaps and ambiguity create supervisory risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain MiCA's objectives and its place within the wider EU financial services rulebook
- Apply MiCA's taxonomy and perimeter, including the boundary with financial instruments
- Use the full Level 1, 2 and 3 rulebook in authorisation and thematic work

## Syllabus

- Consumer protection, market integrity and financial stability objectives
- ARTs, EMTs and other crypto-assets; the boundary with MiFID financial instruments
- Perimeter questions: NFTs issued in series, fractionalisation and DeFi
- Roles of the Commission, ESMA, the EBA and national competent authorities
