## Aim and Purpose

How firms and individuals move through the regulatory lifecycle, and how authorisation, supervision, enforcement and resolution teams hand over and work together.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe each stage of the lifecycle and the function responsible for it
- Explain how information flows between authorisation, supervision and enforcement
- Identify points where risks are most often missed or lost in handover

## Syllabus

- The gateway: authorisation and fit and proper standards
- Ongoing supervision and the firm's supervisory relationship
- Referral to enforcement and the range of outcomes
- Firm failure, orderly exit and resolution
