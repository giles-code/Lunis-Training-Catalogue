## Aim and Purpose

The legal framework for non-bank payment and e-money providers, the core protections it must deliver, and the policy choices in licensing, safeguarding and supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the licensing and prudential regime for payment and e-money institutions
- Assess safeguarding and insolvency protections for customer funds
- Allocate oversight responsibilities between the central bank and other authorities

## Syllabus

- Licensing categories, capital and governance requirements
- Safeguarding, segregation and failures of payment firms
- Conduct, transparency of charges and fraud reimbursement
- Central bank oversight versus conduct supervision
