## Aim and Purpose

Delegation is permitted but responsibility is retained. This module covers how the board decides what to delegate, how it oversees delegates and service providers, and what effective oversight looks like to a regulator.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain which decisions the board must retain and which it may delegate
- Design an oversight framework for delegates and service providers
- Evidence effective oversight through reporting, dialogue and escalation

## Syllabus

- Delegation permitted, responsibility retained: reserved matters and delegated authority
- The relationship between the board, the management company and delegates
- Reporting, open dialogue and the board's right to information from delegates
- Regulatory findings on delegate oversight and the reasonable steps expected
