## Aim and Purpose

How the board responds when the authority identifies serious failings, oversees remediation and look-back exercises, and avoids repeat findings.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how supervisors assess financial crime controls and escalate concerns
- Evaluate remediation plans, independent reviews and look-backs
- Challenge management on root causes, timelines and sustainable fixes

## Syllabus

- Supervisory inspections, findings letters and risk mitigation programmes
- Remediation governance: ownership, milestones and board reporting
- Look-backs, independent reviewers and validation of closure
- Repeat findings as a sign of a firm that is not learning
