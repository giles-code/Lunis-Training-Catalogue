## Aim and Purpose

How to assess where the firm, its staff and associated persons could facilitate tax evasion, and how to reflect tax crime in the business-wide financial crime risk assessment.

## Learning Objectives

- Map facilitation risk across clients, products, jurisdictions and distribution
- Assess incentives and opportunities for staff and associated persons
- Integrate tax crime into the business-wide risk assessment and risk appetite

## Syllabus

- Client, product, jurisdiction and channel risk factors
- Remuneration, sales incentives and introducer arrangements
- Workshops and interviews with front-line and product teams
- Recording residual risk and linking it to controls
