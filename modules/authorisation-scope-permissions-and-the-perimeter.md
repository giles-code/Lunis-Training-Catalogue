## Aim and Purpose

How investment services and financial instruments are defined, how the firm's permissions shape what it may do, and how to manage perimeter and scope-creep risks.

## Learning Objectives

- Map the firm's services and instruments to its permissions
- Identify perimeter risks from new products, channels and jurisdictions
- Maintain an accurate regulatory business plan and notifications

## Syllabus

- Investment services: execution, dealing, advice, portfolio management and custody
- Financial instruments, derivatives and perimeter judgements
- Scope creep, variations of permission and cross-border services
- Change in control, notifications and the regulatory business plan
