## Aim and Purpose

Extending the firm's model risk framework to machine learning and generative AI, with the interpretation techniques, documentation, validation and monitoring standards that withstand internal and external scrutiny.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply interpretation techniques such as feature importance and counterfactual explanations
- Extend model risk management to machine learning and generative AI
- Design documentation, independent validation and ongoing monitoring for AI models

## Syllabus

- Black-box models and practical interpretation techniques
- Explainability needs by use case, from credit decisions to trading
- Documentation and validation standards before and after deployment
- Drift monitoring, retraining and model retirement protocols
