## Aim and Purpose

How the FATF and its regional bodies work, what the 40 Recommendations require, and why a country's AML/CFT performance matters for its financial sector, reputation and economy.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the FATF, FATF-style regional bodies and the global network
- Summarise the structure and main requirements of the FATF Recommendations
- Explain the economic and reputational consequences of weak AML/CFT systems

## Syllabus

- The FATF, regional bodies and observer organisations
- The 40 Recommendations: policy, criminal justice, preventive and transparency measures
- Proliferation financing and targeted financial sanctions
- De-risking, correspondent banking and the cost of listing
