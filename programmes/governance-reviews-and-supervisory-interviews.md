## Aim and Purpose

To show licensees how a supervisory governance assessment works, from desk review of board packs to interviews with directors and key staff, so that they can strengthen and evidence their governance. Participants leave knowing what supervisors look for in the board, senior management, risk architecture and internal audit, and how to prepare for interviews without scripting or over-coaching.

## What It Covers

How supervisors assess governance, the governance pillars, board packs and board dynamics, decision-making and challenge, the role of interviews in supervision, and preparing for and taking part in supervisory interviews.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs, executive and non-executive directors likely to be interviewed by the supervisor
- Senior managers and control function holders in risk, compliance and internal audit
- Company secretaries and governance staff who prepare board packs and coordinate supervisory visits

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how supervisors assess governance and what evidence they use
- Assess the firm's governance against the four pillars of board, senior management, risk architecture and internal audit
- Evaluate board packs and minutes as a supervisor would read them
- Demonstrate effective board challenge and decision-making with evidence
- Prepare directors and staff for supervisory interviews openly and appropriately
- Respond to interview follow-up and governance findings constructively

## Structure and Format

Six modules of about one hour each, delivered as a one-day workshop in person, virtually or in a hybrid format. It combines short briefings with a board pack review case study and interview practice. It can be delivered on its own or with the companion workshops on business models and on supervisory findings and remediation.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of board or senior management work in a regulated firm is helpful. No prior experience of supervisory interviews is required.

## Core Reading

- G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance (2023)
- FSB, Guidance on Supervisory Interaction with Financial Institutions on Risk Culture (2014)
- Amy C. Edmondson, The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace (2018)
