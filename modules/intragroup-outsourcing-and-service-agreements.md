## Aim and Purpose

How local entities rely on group functions, technology and staff, and how to document, oversee and evidence those arrangements to the same standard as an external outsourcing.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify intragroup dependencies on people, systems, data and functions
- Assess intragroup service agreements, service levels and exit arrangements
- Oversee group-provided services so that responsibility is demonstrably retained locally

## Syllabus

- Why intragroup does not mean low risk: the supervisor's view
- Service agreements: scope, standards, data, audit rights and termination
- Oversight, management information and escalation for group services
- Operational resilience and continuity when the group provider fails
