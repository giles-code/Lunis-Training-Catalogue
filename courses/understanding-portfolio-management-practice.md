## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how portfolio managers construct and manage portfolios against mandates, and how supervisors assess mandate compliance and risk budgeting practice.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment management supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how portfolios are constructed against an investment mandate
- Describe risk budgeting techniques used in portfolio management
- Assess mandate compliance for a sample portfolio
- Evaluate risk budgeting practice for consistency with stated risk appetite
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of portfolio management practice

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Portfolio construction against mandate (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain mandate-driven portfolio construction*
- The investment mandate as the foundation of portfolio management
- Asset allocation and security selection within a mandate
- Common mandate restrictions and how they are monitored
- Risk budgeting as a portfolio management discipline
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: mandate compliance issues participants have encountered

**Session 2: Assessing mandate compliance (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess mandate compliance for a sample portfolio*
- Techniques for testing mandate compliance
- Common breaches: concentration limits, asset class restrictions, leverage limits
- Pre-trade versus post-trade compliance monitoring
- Escalation when breaches occur
Activities:
- Group exercise: test a sample portfolio for mandate compliance

**Session 3: Risk budgeting and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate risk budgeting and formulate an assessment*
- Assessing whether risk budgeting is consistent with stated risk appetite
- Case study: a portfolio drifting from its stated risk budget
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of portfolio management practice

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: mandate construction, compliance testing, risk budgeting
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Portfolio construction against mandate (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing mandate compliance (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Risk budgeting and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Mandate compliance monitoring is the primary control that keeps portfolio management within agreed risk boundaries
- Risk budgets can drift gradually, making periodic review as important as point-in-time compliance checks
- Understanding portfolio construction helps supervisors ask sharper questions about manager practice
