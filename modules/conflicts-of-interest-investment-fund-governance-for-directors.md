## Aim and Purpose

Where conflicts arise in fund groups and between the fund, its manager and service providers, and how the board identifies, manages and records them.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify conflicts arising from group structures, fees and related parties
- Evaluate the conflicts policy, register and disclosure to investors
- Challenge decisions where the manager's interests may differ from investors'

## Syllabus

- Sources of conflict: affiliated service providers, cross-trades and allocation
- Directors' own conflicts, multiple directorships and independence
- Conflicts policies, registers and board minutes
- Case studies of conflicts that harmed investors
