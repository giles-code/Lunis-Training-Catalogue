## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how traditional financial assets are tokenised and traded on distributed ledger infrastructure, and the supervisory considerations as tokenised and traditional market infrastructure converge.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in digital assets/crypto-asset regulation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the process of tokenising a traditional financial asset
- Describe how tokenised securities are traded and settled
- Assess the interoperability challenges between tokenised and traditional infrastructure
- Evaluate the investor protection implications of tokenisation
- Formulate a supervisory view on a sample tokenisation initiative

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The tokenisation process (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain how assets are tokenised*
- What tokenisation means: representing an asset on a distributed ledger
- Types of assets being tokenised: bonds, funds, real estate, equities
- Legal structures underpinning tokenised assets
- Drivers behind growing interest in tokenisation
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: tokenisation initiatives participants are aware of

**Session 2: Trading, settlement and interoperability (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe trading/settlement and assess interoperability*
- How tokenised securities are traded and settled on-chain
- Interoperability challenges between DLT and traditional market infrastructure
- The DLT Pilot Regime as a regulatory sandbox approach
- Settlement finality considerations for tokenised assets
Activities:
- Group exercise: map the interoperability challenges for a sample tokenised bond issuance

**Session 3: Investor protection and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate investor protection and formulate a supervisory view*
- Investor protection implications: custody, disclosure, redress
- Case study: a tokenisation initiative raising supervisory questions
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory view on a sample tokenisation initiative

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: tokenisation process, trading/settlement, investor protection
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The tokenisation process (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Trading, settlement and interoperability (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Investor protection and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Tokenisation raises genuine interoperability questions as DLT and traditional market infrastructure begin to converge
- Investor protection principles apply to tokenised assets just as they do to their traditional equivalents
- Regulatory sandbox approaches like the DLT Pilot Regime allow supervised experimentation with tokenisation
