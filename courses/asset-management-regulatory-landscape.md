## Aim and Purpose

Focus: AIFMD, UCITS and Cross-Border Regulation in Asset Management. Objective: exploring the complexities of asset management regulations.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment funds supervision.

## Syllabus

**Block 1 | Workshop 1** — Understanding investment funds and the value chain Describe an investment fund, explain how it operates and identify pros and cons for investors Discuss how the investment fund market has developed in Malta and across the EU Explain the investment fund value chain from product development and distribution through to management, operations and administration Present a schema for investment fund regulation across the value chain

**Block 2 | Workshops 1–2** — Understanding supervisory risks and EU investment fund regulation Explain the key supervisory risks in funds and asset management Identify key parts of the EU’s framework of investment fund regulation that support supervisors in mitigating these risks Discuss the purpose of the UCITS Directive and explain the high-level rules applying to its management, operation and distribution Analyse whether the UCITS Directive has been effective in reducing harm caused by recent investment fund scandals Discuss the AIFM Directive and other EU regulations applicable to other types of investment fund

**Block 3 | Workshops 3–4** — Understanding cross-border fund rules and sustainable investment disclosures Explain the rules applicable to fund leverage, liquidity management and fund valuation Discuss cross-border fund regulation in the EU, including cross-border distribution, passporting, investor disclosure and investment services Explain how the EU’s regulation for sustainable finance applies to investment funds, including fund categorisation Discuss whether sustainable finance disclosure requirements are effective in preventing greenwashing/bleaching

**Block 4 | Workshop 4** — Understanding costs, charges and other regulatory topics Describe the regulation of costs levied by investment funds, including the prevention of undue costs being charged to investors Explain other EU regulation that indirectly applies to/influences funds (MiFID, DORA, SSR, AML, EMIR, etc.) Identify regulatory topics that will influence investment fund and asset management regulation going forward

**Session | Date** — Learning Objectives & Content

**Workshop 1 | 2 July 2024** — Today’s focus: what funds are, how the market works, and risk fundamentals Level-setting: what are funds and what is asset management Overview of the Maltese and EU investment fund and asset management market Introduction of the fund regulation ‘schema’: product, distribution, management, operations and admin Break-out group mapping of supervisory risk areas to the regulation schema Overview of EU fund regulation: UCITSD V+VI, AIFMD I+II, MMFR, ELTIF I+II/EuVECA/EuSEF Overview of cross-border fund distribution regulation: CBDFR/D and PRIIPs I+II

**Workshop 2 | 3 July 2024** — Today’s focus: UCITS Directive in depth Recap of Workshop 1 Overview of the UCITS Directive — why it exists, what it is, what it covers Deeper dive into management and product-related rules and guidance in the UCITS Directive Break-out case study (the ‘Woodford’ case): are the UCITS rules effective?

**Workshop 3 | 5 September 2024** — Today’s focus: AIFM Directive, leverage, liquidity and valuation Recap of the first two sessions Overview of the AIFM Directive I+II — why it exists, what it is, what it covers Overview of how fund leverage and liquidity are regulated, and what can go wrong Overview of investment fund valuation regulation and red flags to look out for Deeper dive into other EU fund regulations: MMFR, ELTIF I+II/EuVECA/EuSEF

**Workshop 4 | 25 September 2024** — Today’s focus: sustainable finance, cross-border distribution, costs and the regulatory horizon Recap of Workshop 3 Overview of cross-border fund regulation, including CBDFD/R and passporting Overview of sustainable finance regulation applicable to investment funds, including the SFDR Overview of disclosure regulations including PRIIPs and ESG-related disclosures; break-out discussion on greenwashing/bleaching Overview of regulation on undue costs for investment funds Overview of other EU regulation indirectly applying to funds: MiFID, DORA, SSR, AML, EMIR Forward look: emerging regulatory topics, including the EU’s Retail Investment Strategy proposals
