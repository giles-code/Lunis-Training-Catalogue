## Aim and Purpose

How to conduct compelled and voluntary interviews to an enforcement standard and take witness statements that are accurate, fair and evidentially sound.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan and conduct interviews to an enforcement standard
- Draft an evidentially sound witness statement
- Avoid leading or suggestive questioning that weakens evidence

## Syllabus

- Compelled versus voluntary interviews and the rights of interviewees
- Interview planning, disclosure before interview and legal representation
- Structuring a witness statement and avoiding suggestion
- Case study: a case undermined by weak interview evidence
