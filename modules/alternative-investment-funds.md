## Aim and Purpose

The main alternative fund types, how their strategies, liquidity and investor base differ, and the specialist fund labels used for long-term investment.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe hedge funds, private equity and real estate funds
- Explain how loan origination funds work and their risks
- Outline specialist and labelled long-term investment funds

## Syllabus

- Hedge funds: strategies, leverage and liquidity terms
- Private equity and loan origination funds
- Real estate funds
- Specialist alternative funds and long-term, venture and social investment fund labels
