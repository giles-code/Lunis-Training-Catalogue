## Aim and Purpose

The practical choices involved in building SupTech capability, including build versus buy, public procurement, skills, resourcing and starting with focused pilots.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate build-versus-buy and hybrid approaches for SupTech tools
- Assess the skills, roles and resourcing needed to sustain SupTech
- Identify practical steps for starting or scaling a SupTech programme

## Syllabus

- Build, buy and hybrid models: control, speed, cost and lock-in
- Procurement considerations specific to public authorities
- Data scientists, data engineers and supervisory translators
- Pilots, proofs of concept and scaling what works
