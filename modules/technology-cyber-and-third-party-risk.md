## Aim and Purpose

How digital asset operations fail and are attacked, how dependency on protocols and providers concentrates risk, and whether the firm can withstand and recover from a serious incident.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the main technology and cyber threats to digital asset operations
- Assess dependency on blockchains, protocols and critical third parties
- Evaluate incident response, resilience testing and escalation to the board

## Syllabus

- Hacks, key compromise, smart contract bugs and bridge exploits
- Protocol, network and oracle dependency risk
- Outsourcing and critical third-party oversight
- Incident response and recovery: what the board should require
