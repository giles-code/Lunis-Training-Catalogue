## Aim and Purpose

How committees build a joined-up view of where assurance comes from across the three lines of defence and external providers, and where it is missing or duplicated.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the three lines model and its limitations
- Use an assurance map to identify gaps and overlaps
- Challenge reliance on first-line self-assessment

## Syllabus

- The three lines model in practice
- Building and using an assurance map
- Combined assurance across risk, compliance, internal and external audit
- Recognising comfort without evidence
