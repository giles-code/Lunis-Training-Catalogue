## Aim and Purpose

Managing operational risk through risk and control self-assessment, loss event data, key risk indicators and scenario analysis that produce reliable insight.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the sources of operational risk and how they crystallise
- Design and run a risk and control self-assessment that challenges the business
- Apply loss data, key risk indicators and scenario analysis

## Syllabus

- Operational risk events, causes and impacts
- Running risk and control self-assessments with credible challenge
- Loss event data, near misses and key risk indicators
- Control testing and the link to internal audit
