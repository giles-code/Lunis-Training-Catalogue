## Aim and Purpose

How a new fund is designed, authorised and launched, how risk is managed through its life, and how a fund is terminated and wound up.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the considerations for establishing and authorising a new fund
- Describe how risk is managed across the fund lifecycle
- Explain how a fund is terminated, merged or wound up

## Syllabus

- Product design, domicile choice and the authorisation process
- Launch, seeding and bringing a fund to market
- Risk management through the lifecycle: investment, liquidity, operational and counterparty
- Workshop: closing, merging and winding up a fund
