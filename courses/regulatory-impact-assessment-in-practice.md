## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build practical skills in assessing the impact of proposed rules — costs and benefits to industry and consumers, proportionality, and identifying unintended consequences before rules are finalised.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level policy officers drafting or contributing to rules, guidance, and consultation papers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and principles of regulatory impact assessment
- Apply cost-benefit analysis techniques to a proposed rule
- Assess the proportionality of a proposed regulatory intervention
- Identify potential unintended consequences before finalisation
- Formulate a completed impact assessment for a proposed rule

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Principles of impact assessment (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the purpose and principles of impact assessment*
- Why regulators assess impact before making rules
- Core principles: proportionality, evidence-based analysis, transparency
- Common impact assessment frameworks
- Data sources for impact assessment
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: impact assessment practice in participants' own authorities

**Session 2: Cost-benefit analysis and proportionality (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply cost-benefit analysis and assess proportionality*
- Techniques for quantifying costs to industry and consumers
- Qualitative benefits that resist easy quantification
- Assessing proportionality: is the intervention justified by the risk?
- Distributional effects across firm sizes
Activities:
- Group exercise: conduct a cost-benefit analysis for a sample proposed rule

**Session 3: Unintended consequences and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify unintended consequences and complete an impact assessment*
- Common sources of unintended consequences
- Stress-testing a rule against edge cases and market reactions
- Case study: a rule with significant unintended consequences
Activities:
- Case study: complete an impact assessment for a proposed rule, including unintended consequences analysis

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: principles, cost-benefit, proportionality, unintended consequences
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Principles of impact assessment (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Cost-benefit analysis and proportionality (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Unintended consequences and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Impact assessment forces rule-makers to justify intervention with evidence, not just intuition
- Proportionality assessment should explicitly weigh the risk being addressed against the burden imposed
- Stress-testing a rule against edge cases catches unintended consequences before they become real problems
