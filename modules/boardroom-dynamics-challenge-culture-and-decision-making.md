## Aim and Purpose

How boardroom culture, personalities and bias shape decisions, how to challenge well, and practical counter-measures against group think, dominant voices and poor decision-making.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the risk factors for poor board decision-making
- Challenge constructively and handle difficult board members
- Apply counter-measures to group think and judgemental bias

## Syllabus

- Risk factors for poor decisions: dominant voices, time pressure and isolation
- Challenge well: preparation, curiosity and a bracing rather than collegial culture
- Difficult personalities, non-attendance and managing difficult conversations
- Heuristics, framing and group think, and practical counter-measures
