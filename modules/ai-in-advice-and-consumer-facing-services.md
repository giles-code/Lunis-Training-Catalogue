## Aim and Purpose

The growing use of AI in robo-advice, chatbots, personalisation and pricing, and the suitability, disclosure and fair-treatment questions supervisors should put to firms.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI is used in digital advice and customer engagement
- Assess the suitability and disclosure implications of AI-driven recommendations
- Evaluate consumer protection risks from personalisation and automated advice

## Syllabus

- Robo-advice and automated portfolio recommendation engines
- Chatbots and virtual assistants: accuracy, complaints and vulnerable customers
- Personalisation and dynamic pricing: fair-treatment implications
- The guidance versus regulated advice boundary in AI-driven journeys
