## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how firms design and test operational resilience scenarios, and how supervisors assess the adequacy of impact tolerances and testing programmes.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept of impact tolerance in operational resilience
- Describe severe-but-plausible scenario design principles
- Assess the adequacy of a firm's impact tolerance setting
- Evaluate a firm's operational resilience testing programme
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of operational resilience testing

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Impact tolerance and scenario design (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain impact tolerance and scenario design*
- What impact tolerance means and how it differs from risk appetite
- Designing severe-but-plausible scenarios
- Important business services as the focus of resilience testing
- Regulatory frameworks for operational resilience
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: operational resilience frameworks participants have reviewed

**Session 2: Assessing impact tolerance setting (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess the adequacy of impact tolerance setting*
- Criteria for assessing whether impact tolerances are appropriately set
- Common weaknesses: tolerances set too loosely, poorly justified
- Linking impact tolerance to important business services
- Board-level engagement in setting tolerances
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's impact tolerance statements

**Session 3: Testing programmes and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate a testing programme and formulate an assessment*
- What a credible operational resilience testing programme includes
- Assessing whether tests are genuinely severe-but-plausible
- Case study: a firm's operational resilience testing programme under review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's testing programme

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: impact tolerance, scenario design, testing programme assessment
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Impact tolerance and scenario design (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing impact tolerance setting (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Testing programmes and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Impact tolerance should be set with genuine board engagement, not delegated entirely to operational teams
- Severe-but-plausible scenarios must actually be severe enough to test real resilience, not just convenient
- A credible testing programme evolves over time as new vulnerabilities and dependencies are identified
