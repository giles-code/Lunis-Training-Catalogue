## Aim and Purpose

How banks and insurers are regulated for safety and soundness, what capital and liquidity rules aim to achieve, and the policy choices government faces in implementing them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of capital, liquidity and solvency requirements
- Assess proportionality choices for smaller and domestic institutions
- Identify the fiscal and growth implications of prudential policy

## Syllabus

- Capital, leverage and liquidity standards for banks
- Solvency and policyholder protection for insurers
- Proportionality, local adaptation and timing of implementation
- Credit supply, lending to the economy and the cost of prudential rules
