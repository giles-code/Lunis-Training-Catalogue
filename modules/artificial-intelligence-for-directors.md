## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in how machine learning and generative AI are built and deployed, how they differ from rules-based tools, and why they change the firm's risk profile.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI systems learn, decide and fail
- Distinguish machine learning, generative AI and traditional models, and their risks
- Identify where AI is already embedded in the firm, including through vendors

## Syllabus

- How AI works: data, training, testing and deployment
- Machine learning versus generative AI and large language models
- Build versus buy: accountability for third-party AI
- The vocabulary directors need: drift, bias, hallucination, explainability
