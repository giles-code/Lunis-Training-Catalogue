## Aim and Purpose

Why supervisors interview, how supervisory interviews differ from enforcement interviews and recruitment interviews, and how mindset shapes what the interviewer hears.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of interviews in risk assessment, inspections and governance reviews
- Distinguish supervisory from enforcement interviews and their evidential standards
- Recognise how assumptions and mindset affect an interview

## Syllabus

- Interviews in the supervisory cycle: inspections, reviews and fitness and propriety
- Supervisory versus enforcement interviews: purpose, powers and safeguards
- Introducing a PEACE-style structure for supervisory interviews
- The right supervisory perspective: curious, sceptical and fair
