## Aim and Purpose

How to look beneath a crypto firm's governance documents to judge whether its management body, risk, compliance and audit functions are genuinely effective, and how to intervene proportionately.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the composition and functioning of the management body and its oversight functions
- Evaluate the independence, resourcing and influence of risk, compliance and internal audit
- Apply proportionate supervisory interventions to governance and control weaknesses

## Syllabus

- Board composition, fitness and propriety and founder-dominated governance
- Three lines of defence and risk appetite in a crypto business
- Red flags: governance capture, nominal independence and marginalised control functions
- Intervention tools: management requirements and independent expert reviews
