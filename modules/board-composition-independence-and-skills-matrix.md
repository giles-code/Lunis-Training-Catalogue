## Aim and Purpose

How to compose a fund board that matches the fund's risks, test independence in substance as well as form, and build a skills matrix that drives appointments and development.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish executive, non-executive and independent directors and test independence
- Build a skills matrix linked to the fund's strategy and risk profile
- Assess diversity, location, time commitment and fitness and probity

## Syllabus

- Board profile: executive, non-executive and independent roles
- Independence tests, tenure, affiliations and multiple directorships
- Building a skills matrix from the fund's risk profile
- Tool: a completed skills matrix and gap analysis
