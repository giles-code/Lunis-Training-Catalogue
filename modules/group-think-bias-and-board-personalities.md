## Aim and Purpose

The cognitive and social traps that undermine board decisions, the board personalities that distort discussion, and practical counter-measures the chair and directors can use.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the warning signs of group think and common decision biases
- Identify difficult board behaviours and how the chair should respond
- Apply practical techniques to counter bias in board decisions

## Syllabus

- Group think, framing, availability and motivated blindness
- Decision traps: time pressure, short horizons and isolation
- Board personalities: the dominator, the silent director, the absentee and the box-ticker
- Counter-measures: devil's advocate, anonymous polling and options before advocacy
