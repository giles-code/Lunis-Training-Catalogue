## Aim and Purpose

How agents initiate payments and transactions across traditional and new payment rails, and the authorisation, authentication and dispute issues this raises.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how agents initiate payments across card, account and tokenised rails
- Assess strong authentication and authorisation for agent-initiated payments
- Design dispute, refund and error-handling processes for agent transactions

## Syllabus

- Agent-initiated payments, tokens and programmable money
- Authentication, consent and transaction limits
- Unauthorised, mistaken and disputed agent transactions
- Settlement, reconciliation and stablecoin rails
