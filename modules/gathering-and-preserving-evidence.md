## Aim and Purpose

Practical techniques for obtaining evidence using formal powers, preserving it without contamination and maintaining a chain of custody that will survive challenge.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply formal information-gathering powers appropriately
- Maintain a defensible chain of custody and record of provenance
- Identify evidence-handling errors that undermine cases

## Syllabus

- Information requirements, document production and on-site visits
- Preserving digital evidence: devices, communications and data
- Chain of custody and documenting provenance
- Exercise: finding the gaps in a sample evidence file
