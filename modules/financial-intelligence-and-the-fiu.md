## Aim and Purpose

The role of the national FIU in receiving, analysing and disseminating financial intelligence, and how its effectiveness is measured and demonstrated to assessors.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the FIU's core functions, independence and resources
- Assess the quality and use of suspicious transaction reports and FIU disseminations
- Demonstrate the use of financial intelligence by law enforcement

## Syllabus

- FIU models, autonomy and access to information
- Reporting quality, feedback and public-private partnerships
- Operational and strategic analysis products
- Statistics and case studies that evidence effectiveness
