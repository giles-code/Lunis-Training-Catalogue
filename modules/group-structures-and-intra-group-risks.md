## Aim and Purpose

How financial groups are organised, why firms sit within wider groups and how intra-group transactions, shared services and parent support affect the regulated entity.

## Learning Objectives

- Map a group structure and identify the regulated entity's position within it
- Assess intra-group dependencies, transactions and contagion risk
- Frame supervisory questions on group governance and ownership

## Syllabus

- Holding companies, branches, subsidiaries and ownership chains
- Intra-group funding, guarantees, outsourcing and shared services
- Opaque and complex structures: a supervisory red flag
- Group-wide supervision and cooperation with other authorities
