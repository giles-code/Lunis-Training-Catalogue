## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together around error and breach management, investor compensation and the periodic review of service providers, closing with a board oversight plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply materiality thresholds and correction policies to NAV errors and breaches
- Oversee root cause analysis, investor compensation and regulatory notification
- Design a risk-based programme of service provider reviews

## Syllabus

- NAV errors, investment breaches and materiality thresholds
- Root cause analysis, compensation and notification to the authority
- Due diligence visits, scorecards and annual service reviews
- Workshop: the board's operational oversight plan
