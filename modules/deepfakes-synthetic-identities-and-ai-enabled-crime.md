## Aim and Purpose

How criminals use generative AI to create synthetic identities, forge documents, impersonate customers and executives, and scale social engineering, and how the firm builds resilience.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise generative-AI-enabled typologies and red flags
- Assess the resilience of onboarding and authentication to deepfakes
- Design layered controls and staff awareness against AI-enabled attacks

## Syllabus

- Synthetic identities and AI-generated documents
- Voice and video deepfakes in onboarding and executive impersonation
- AI-generated phishing, scam content and automated social engineering
- Liveness detection, call-back controls and layered defences
