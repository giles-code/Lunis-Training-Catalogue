## Aim and Purpose

How to plan and conduct compelled interviews with firm staff and subjects, balancing effective questioning with the rights of the interviewee.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan a compelled interview strategy and question outline
- Conduct interviews that are fair, structured and evidentially sound
- Handle legal representatives, privilege objections and evasive answers

## Syllabus

- Compelled and voluntary interviews: rights, cautions and use of answers
- Interview planning, document bundles and questioning structure
- Managing lawyers, objections and difficult interviewees
- Role-play: interviewing a senior manager under compulsion
