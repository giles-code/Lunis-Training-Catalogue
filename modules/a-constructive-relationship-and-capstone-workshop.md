## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together around building trust with the authority, followed by a workshop in which participants assess a case-study firm as a supervisor would and plan its engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Design an engagement strategy built on openness and no surprises
- Apply the supervisor's method to assess a firm end to end
- Produce an action plan to strengthen the firm's supervisory relationship

## Syllabus

- Openness, notifications and the cost of surprises
- Relationship mapping, regulatory liaison and board reporting on supervision
- Common misunderstandings between firms and supervisors
- Workshop: assessing a case-study firm and planning its supervisory engagement
