## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in applying a sanctioning framework consistently, weighing aggravating and mitigating factors and maintaining consistency with precedent while addressing the specific facts of a case.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level enforcement investigators and case officers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of sanctions within the enforcement toolkit
- Apply a structured sanctioning framework to a case
- Weigh aggravating and mitigating factors systematically
- Assess consistency with precedent when determining a sanction
- Formulate a proportionate sanction for a sample case

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The purpose of sanctions (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the purpose of sanctions*
- Sanctions as deterrence, punishment, and market signalling
- Types of sanction: financial penalties, restrictions, public censure
- Proportionality as the guiding sanctioning principle
- The role of published sanctioning guidelines
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: sanctioning approaches from participants' own jurisdictions

**Session 2: Sanctioning frameworks in practice (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply a sanctioning framework and weigh aggravating/mitigating factors*
- Structured sanctioning frameworks: seriousness, harm, culpability
- Aggravating factors: repeat conduct, concealment, senior involvement
- Mitigating factors: self-reporting, remediation, cooperation
- Financial penalty calculation methodologies
Activities:
- Group exercise: apply a sanctioning framework to a sample case

**Session 3: Precedent and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess consistency with precedent and formulate a sanction*
- Using precedent to maintain sanctioning consistency
- Distinguishing genuinely comparable cases from superficially similar ones
- Case study: determining a sanction for a sample enforcement case
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a proportionate sanction, referencing precedent

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: purpose, framework, aggravating/mitigating factors, precedent
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The purpose of sanctions (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Sanctioning frameworks in practice (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Precedent and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Proportionate sanctioning balances the specific facts of a case against the need for overall consistency
- A structured framework prevents sanctioning decisions from becoming arbitrary or unpredictable
- Precedent should guide, not mechanically dictate, the sanction in a new case

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with enforcement & sanctions is helpful but not essential.
