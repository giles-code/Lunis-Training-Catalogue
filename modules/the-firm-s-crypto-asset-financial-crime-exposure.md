## Aim and Purpose

Where financial crime risk enters crypto-asset, payment and fintech business models, what the FATF standards require of firms, and how to reflect crypto-asset exposure in the business-wide risk assessment.

## Learning Objectives

- Map financial crime risk across the firm's products, channels and fiat on- and off-ramps
- Summarise the FATF obligations on virtual asset service providers and firms dealing with them
- Reflect direct and indirect crypto-asset exposure in the business-wide risk assessment

## Syllabus

- Exchanges, brokers, custodians, wallet providers and payment gateways: where risk arises
- Indirect exposure: payment and e-money firms serving crypto-asset businesses
- FATF Recommendations 15 and 16 and the risk-based approach for firms
- Crypto-asset factors in the business-wide risk assessment and risk appetite
