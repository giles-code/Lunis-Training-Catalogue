## Aim and Purpose

DORA's core requirements for identifying, protecting against, detecting, responding to and recovering from ICT risk, including asset inventories, backup and restoration, and learning from incidents and tests.

## Learning Objectives

- Map the ICT risk management lifecycle to DORA's requirements
- Assess identification, protection, detection, response and recovery capabilities
- Evaluate ICT business continuity and backup arrangements against recovery objectives

## Syllabus

- Identification: information and ICT assets, dependencies and critical or important functions
- Protection and prevention: access, encryption, patching and change management
- Detection, response and recovery, ICT business continuity and backup policies
- Learning, evolving and crisis communication after incidents and tests
