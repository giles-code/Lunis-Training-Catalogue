## Aim and Purpose

The main models of public-private partnership, from strategic forums to tactical taskforces, and the governance, membership and trust arrangements that make them sustainable.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare strategic, tactical and hybrid partnership models
- Design governance, membership and terms of reference
- Build trust and manage expectations between partners

## Syllabus

- Strategic forums, tactical taskforces and hybrid models
- Membership: banks, payment firms, crypto-asset providers and others
- Terms of reference, chairing and decision-making
- Vetting, security and building trust
