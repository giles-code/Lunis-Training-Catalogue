## Aim and Purpose

How to keep fitness under review after appointment, run annual certification and assessments efficiently, and link fitness to performance, conduct breaches and remuneration.

## Learning Objectives

- Design an annual fitness and certification process with clear evidence
- Identify triggers for reassessment during the year
- Link conduct breaches, performance and training records to fitness

## Syllabus

- Annual self-declarations, checks and certification sign-off
- Triggers for reassessment: role changes, incidents and new information
- Conduct rules breaches, training records and remuneration adjustments
- Record-keeping and evidencing assessments to the supervisor
