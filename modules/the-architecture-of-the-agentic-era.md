## Aim and Purpose

A four-layer model of agentic finance, covering governance, decision, asset and settlement layers, and how it helps supervisors locate risk, control and accountability.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the governance, decision, asset and settlement layers of agentic finance
- Analyse how risk and control points shift between the layers
- Map existing regulatory requirements onto each layer

## Syllabus

- Governance layer: mandates, permissions, policies and oversight
- Decision layer: models, reasoning and recommendations
- Asset layer: accounts, wallets, tokenised assets and custody
- Settlement layer: payment rails, stablecoins and programmable money
