## Aim and Purpose

The FIU's place in the national AML/CFT system: its core functions, the administrative, law-enforcement, judicial and hybrid models, and what the FATF standards and effectiveness assessments expect.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the receipt, analysis and dissemination functions of an FIU
- Compare FIU models and their implications for powers and access to information
- Summarise what FATF Recommendation 29 and Immediate Outcome 6 require

## Syllabus

- Core functions: receipt, analysis and dissemination
- Administrative, law-enforcement, judicial and hybrid models
- Operational independence, autonomy and protection of information
- FATF Recommendation 29, Immediate Outcome 6 and common evaluation findings
