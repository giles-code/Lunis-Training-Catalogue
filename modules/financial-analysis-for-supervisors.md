## Aim and Purpose

The quantitative side of risk assessment: reading a firm's financial position, testing the sustainability of earnings and capital, and challenging projections and the assumptions behind them.

## Learning Objectives

- Analyse profitability, capital and liquidity indicators to identify vulnerabilities
- Assess concentration and dependency risks in earnings and funding
- Challenge financial projections and the execution risk behind a strategy

## Syllabus

- Profitability key risk indicators and drivers of earnings
- Capital structure, capital quality and liquidity buffers
- Concentration, dependency and peer comparison
- Why strategies fail: projections, assumptions and execution risk
