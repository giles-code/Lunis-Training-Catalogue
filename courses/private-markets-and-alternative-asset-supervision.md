## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how private equity, private credit and real asset funds are structured and supervised, including the supervisory implications of the retailisation of private markets under regimes such as ELTIF 2.0.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment funds supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the structure and characteristics of private equity, private credit and real asset funds
- Describe how private market funds differ from traditional liquid funds in supervisory terms
- Assess the implications of retailising private markets to less sophisticated investors
- Evaluate liquidity and valuation risk specific to private market funds
- Formulate a supervisory risk assessment for a sample private market fund

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Private market fund structures (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain private market fund structures*
- Private equity, private credit and real asset fund structures
- Illiquidity and long-dated investment horizons
- Fee structures and alignment of interest in private funds
- Growth of private markets and drivers behind it
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: private market growth trends in participants' own markets

**Session 2: Supervisory distinctions and retailisation (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe supervisory distinctions and assess retailisation implications*
- How private fund supervision differs from liquid fund supervision
- ELTIF 2.0 and the retailisation of private markets
- Investor protection considerations for less sophisticated investors
- Distribution and suitability considerations for retail access
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess the retailisation implications of a sample ELTIF structure

**Session 3: Liquidity, valuation and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate liquidity/valuation risk and formulate a risk assessment*
- Liquidity mismatch risk in private market fund structures
- Valuation challenges for illiquid, hard-to-price assets
- Case study: a private market fund facing valuation or liquidity stress
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory risk assessment for a sample private market fund

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: structures, supervisory distinctions, retailisation, liquidity/valuation
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Private market fund structures (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Supervisory distinctions and retailisation (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Liquidity, valuation and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Private market funds require a different supervisory lens than traditional liquid funds, particularly around liquidity and valuation
- Retailisation of private markets significantly raises the investor protection stakes
- Valuation of illiquid assets is inherently more judgement-based and deserves close supervisory scrutiny
