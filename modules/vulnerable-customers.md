## Aim and Purpose

Identifying customers in vulnerable circumstances, adapting products, services and support, and monitoring whether they achieve outcomes as good as other customers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the drivers of vulnerability and how they affect customer decisions
- Design processes to identify and support vulnerable customers
- Monitor outcomes for vulnerable customers compared with others

## Syllabus

- Drivers of vulnerability: health, life events, resilience and capability
- Staff training, recording and data protection
- Adjusting products, journeys and support
- Outcome monitoring for vulnerable customer groups
