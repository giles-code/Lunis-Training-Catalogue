## Aim and Purpose

How to audit the firm's anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing, sanctions, fraud and anti-bribery controls, aligned with the risk-based approach set out in FATF standards.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm's financial crime risk assessment and control framework
- Test customer due diligence, monitoring and reporting effectiveness
- Evaluate sanctions screening and fraud controls, including system tuning

## Syllabus

- Business-wide risk assessment and the risk-based approach
- Customer due diligence, ongoing monitoring and suspicious activity reporting
- Sanctions screening: lists, thresholds, alerts and testing
- Fraud, bribery and the governance of financial crime systems
