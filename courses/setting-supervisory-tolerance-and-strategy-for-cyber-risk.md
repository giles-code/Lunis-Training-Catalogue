## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in establishing a supervisory tolerance for cyber-related harm and prioritising supervisory resource allocation across the regulated population.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept of supervisory risk tolerance as applied to cyber risk
- Describe approaches to prioritising cyber supervisory resource across a population
- Assess proportionality considerations in cyber risk supervision
- Evaluate a sample cyber supervisory strategy
- Formulate a cyber risk tolerance statement for a supervisory authority

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Supervisory tolerance for cyber risk (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain supervisory risk tolerance for cyber risk*
- Why cyber risk requires an explicit tolerance statement
- Balancing zero-tolerance aspirations against operational reality
- Aligning cyber risk tolerance with the authority's overall risk appetite
- International approaches to setting cyber risk tolerance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: how participants' own authorities currently approach cyber risk tolerance

**Session 2: Prioritising resource across the population (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe prioritisation approaches and assess proportionality*
- Approaches to prioritising cyber supervisory resource by firm risk tier
- Proportionality: applying more intensive scrutiny where systemic importance is higher
- Balancing thematic reviews against firm-specific deep dives
- Resourcing constraints specific to cyber expertise
Activities:
- Group exercise: design a cyber resource prioritisation approach for a sample regulated population

**Session 3: Strategy evaluation and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate a strategy and formulate a tolerance statement*
- Criteria for evaluating a cyber supervisory strategy
- Case study: a sample cyber supervisory strategy
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a cyber risk tolerance statement for a supervisory authority

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: risk tolerance, resource prioritisation, proportionality
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Supervisory tolerance for cyber risk (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Prioritising resource across the population (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Strategy evaluation and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- An explicit cyber risk tolerance statement gives supervisory resourcing decisions a clear anchor
- Proportionate cyber supervision concentrates the most intensive scrutiny on the most systemically important firms
- Cyber expertise is scarce, making resource prioritisation decisions especially consequential
