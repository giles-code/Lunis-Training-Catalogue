## Aim and Purpose

Risk-based supervision and enforcement of payment firms under the current and reformed regimes, including stronger powers, data and EBA convergence work, ending with a transition planning workshop for supervisors or firms.

## Learning Objectives

- Design risk-based supervision of payment and e-money institutions
- Apply supervisory, enforcement and cross-border cooperation tools, including for agents and passporting firms
- Plan the transition to PSD3 and the PSR

## Syllabus

- Risk indicators: growth, complaints, fraud data, safeguarding and partner withdrawal
- Home–host cooperation, passporting and EBA convergence work
- Enforcement powers, penalties and the reform's more harmonised sanctions
- Workshop: a PSD3 and PSR transition roadmap
