## Aim and Purpose

A compliance view of how blockchains, crypto-assets and their intermediaries create risk, and how international standards and comprehensive crypto-asset regimes respond to it.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the real control points and failure modes in blockchain-based activities
- Explain the FSB, IOSCO and FATF standards for crypto-asset activities
- Map the common building blocks of comprehensive crypto-asset regimes to the firm

## Syllabus

- Control points and failure modes: upgrades, forks, oracles, smart contracts
- The ecosystem: issuers, validators, venues, custodians and end users
- FSB recommendations, IOSCO policy recommendations and FATF Recommendation 15
- Comprehensive crypto-asset regimes: objectives, scope and layered rulebooks
