## Aim and Purpose

How investigators and service providers trace funds across public ledgers, cluster and attribute addresses, and how far analytics outputs can be relied on as intelligence or evidence.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main tracing techniques and clustering heuristics
- Interpret risk scores, exposure reports and attribution data critically
- Assess the evidential value and limits of analytics outputs

## Syllabus

- Traceability on public ledgers: following inputs and outputs
- Clustering, attribution and labelling of services and illicit actors
- Risk scoring, false positives and gaps in coverage
- From analytics to evidence: documentation, reproducibility and expert testimony
