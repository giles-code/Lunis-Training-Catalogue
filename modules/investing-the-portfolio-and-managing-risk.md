## Aim and Purpose

How funds buy and sell assets, the investment restrictions and techniques they use, and the main investment, liquidity and operational risks funds face during their life.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how funds trade traditional and other assets
- Describe investment restrictions, leverage and efficient portfolio management techniques
- Identify the main risks a fund faces and how they are managed

## Syllabus

- The investment process, trading and settlement
- Diversification limits, eligible assets and borrowing restrictions
- Derivatives, leverage, securities lending and collateral
- Market, liquidity, counterparty and operational risk
