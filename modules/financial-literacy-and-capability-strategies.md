## Aim and Purpose

How to design a national strategy to improve financial literacy and capability, what evidence shows about what works, and how to reach different audiences effectively.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the OECD framework for national financial literacy strategies
- Assess the evidence on effective financial education interventions
- Design programmes for schools, adults, small businesses and vulnerable groups

## Syllabus

- National strategy design, governance and stakeholder roles
- Measuring financial literacy and capability
- Behavioural insights and teachable moments
- Digital financial literacy and scam awareness
