## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a case exercise in which teams respond to a cross-border scam, freeze and trace funds, support victims and agree prosecution, recovery and disruption actions.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply typologies, tracing and rapid response to a realistic case
- Balance prosecution, recovery and disruption objectives
- Agree a coordinated response across agencies and the private sector

## Syllabus

- Case briefing: a cross-border investment scam and mule network
- Freezing, tracing and evidence-gathering exercise
- Recovery, compensation and disruption options
- Workshop: the coordinated response plan
