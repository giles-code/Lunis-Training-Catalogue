## Aim and Purpose

How to use AI to draft and improve supervisory letters, inspection reports, internal briefings and meeting notes in the authority's style, while keeping findings accurate and defensible.

## Learning Objectives

- Draft supervisory correspondence and reports from structured notes
- Edit drafts for clarity, tone and the authority's house style
- Ensure every finding in an AI-assisted draft is supported by evidence

## Syllabus

- From notes to first draft: findings letters and inspection reports
- Briefing papers and summaries for senior management and committees
- Plain language, tone and consistency with the authority's style
- Exercise: drafting and reviewing a findings letter
