## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the regulatory framework for instant/fast payments and the oversight considerations specific to payment system operators and settlement risk.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in payment services supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how instant payment systems operate technically and operationally
- Describe instant payments regulation and its key requirements
- Assess settlement risk within instant payment systems
- Evaluate the oversight responsibilities specific to payment system operators
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of an instant payment system's resilience

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Instant payment systems (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain instant payment system operation*
- How instant/fast payment systems achieve near-real-time settlement
- Instant payments regulation: scope and key requirements
- Verification of payee requirements and fraud prevention implications
- Growth and adoption trends in instant payments
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: instant payment developments in participants' own markets

**Session 2: Settlement risk and oversight (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess settlement risk and evaluate oversight responsibilities*
- Settlement risk considerations unique to near-instant settlement
- Payment system operator oversight responsibilities
- Liquidity management challenges for participants in instant payment systems
- Systemic importance of payment system infrastructure
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess settlement risk considerations for a sample instant payment system

**Session 3: Resilience assessment and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Formulate a resilience assessment*
- Criteria for assessing an instant payment system's resilience
- Case study: an instant payment system facing an operational disruption
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of an instant payment system's resilience

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: instant payment operation, settlement risk, oversight responsibilities
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Instant payment systems (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Settlement risk and oversight (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Resilience assessment and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Near-instant settlement compresses the time available to detect and prevent fraud, raising the stakes for verification controls
- Payment system operators carry systemic oversight responsibilities distinct from individual participant supervision
- Liquidity management becomes more demanding as settlement cycles compress toward real time

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with payment services is helpful but not essential.
