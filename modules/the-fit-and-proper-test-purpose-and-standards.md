## Aim and Purpose

Why the authority assesses the fitness and propriety of owners and individuals, the components of the test and the international standards that underpin it.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of fit and proper assessments in protecting firms and markets
- Describe the components of the test: integrity, competence, capacity and financial soundness
- Apply the burden and standard of proof to fitness decisions

## Syllabus

- The gateway's role in preventing harm before it occurs
- Honesty, integrity, reputation, competence, capability and financial soundness
- International standards from the Basel Committee, IOSCO, IAIS and FATF
- Burden of proof, proportionality and consistency of decisions
