## Aim and Purpose

The country's obligations to combat money laundering, terrorist financing and sanctions evasion, how they are assessed by the FATF, and what is at stake for the economy.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the FATF standards and the mutual evaluation process
- Describe the consequences of grey-listing for the economy
- Identify the legislative role in beneficial ownership and asset recovery

## Syllabus

- Money laundering, terrorist financing and sanctions explained
- FATF evaluations, grey-listing and its economic impact
- Beneficial ownership transparency and asset recovery laws
- Scams and fraud as a growing financial crime threat
