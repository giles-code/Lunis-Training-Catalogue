## Aim and Purpose

How funds reach investors, how marketing is regulated, and how funds are sold across borders within and outside regional passport regimes.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the channels used to market and distribute funds
- Explain how fund marketing is regulated
- Distinguish passported, private placement and third-country marketing routes

## Syllabus

- Distribution channels, platforms and intermediaries
- Regulation of fund marketing and communications
- Cross-border marketing within a passport regime
- Marketing outside the home region and private placement regimes
