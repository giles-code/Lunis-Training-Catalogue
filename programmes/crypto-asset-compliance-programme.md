## Aim and Purpose

To equip the control functions of crypto-asset service providers and issuers with an advanced, practical command of the obligations that comprehensive crypto-asset regimes impose, from authorisation to wind-down. Participants leave able to design and evidence effective governance, custody, conduct, market integrity, financial crime and resilience controls, and to engage with supervisors the way a well-run firm should.

## What It Covers

International standards and comprehensive crypto-asset regimes, business models and the regulatory perimeter, authorisation, governance and internal controls, prudential resources and wind-down planning, custody and safeguarding, ICT and operational resilience, conflicts of interest and white paper disclosure, market abuse surveillance, AML/CFT and the travel rule, stablecoin issuance, and supervisory engagement and enforcement.

## Intended Audience

- Heads of compliance, compliance officers and compliance monitoring staff at crypto-asset service providers and issuers
- Risk managers, MLROs, financial crime and internal audit professionals covering crypto-asset activities
- Senior managers and legal staff responsible for authorisation, regulatory engagement and remediation

## Learning Objectives

- Apply FSB, IOSCO and FATF standards and the common architecture of comprehensive crypto-asset regimes to the firm's activities
- Prepare a credible authorisation application and evidence real substance, governance and control
- Design custody, safeguarding, prudential and wind-down arrangements that protect clients and the firm
- Implement conflicts, disclosure and market abuse controls that meet conduct and market integrity expectations
- Operate an effective AML/CFT, sanctions and travel rule framework and resilient ICT arrangements
- Engage confidently with supervisors, manage enforcement risk and respond to crises

## Structure and Format

Twelve modules of about three and a half hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as an intensive multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions with pre-reading. Modules combine technical briefings with red-flag case studies, self-assessment exercises and a closing crisis simulation, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's services and jurisdictions.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Working knowledge of crypto-assets and of compliance or risk in a regulated firm is helpful; the Crypto and Blockchain Fundamentals for Firms programme provides a suitable foundation.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets (2023)
- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Decentralized Finance (2023)
- FATF, Updated Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (2021)
- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Global Stablecoin Arrangements (2023)
