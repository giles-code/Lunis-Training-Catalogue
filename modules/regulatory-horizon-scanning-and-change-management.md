## Aim and Purpose

How the firm tracks new and changing rules, interprets what they mean for the business and turns them into owned, tracked implementation projects.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a horizon scanning process proportionate to the firm's size and activities
- Apply a structured method for interpreting and allocating new obligations
- Implement regulatory change projects with clear ownership and evidence of completion

## Syllabus

- Sources of regulatory change: rules, guidance, consultations and supervisory statements
- Obligations registers and mapping rules to policies and controls
- Gap analysis, ownership and regulatory change governance
- Evidencing implementation to the board and the supervisor
