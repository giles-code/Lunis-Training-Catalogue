## Aim and Purpose

What operational resilience means, why it has become a core supervisory objective, and how it builds on business continuity, operational risk management and international standards.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core concepts of operational resilience and their supervisory rationale
- Distinguish operational resilience from business continuity and operational risk management
- Identify the international principles shaping operational resilience regimes

## Syllabus

- From preventing failure to assuming disruption: the resilience mindset
- Operational resilience regimes and their common building blocks
- Basel Committee, FSB, IOSCO and CPMI principles on resilience and cyber
- Proportionality across sectors and firm sizes
