## Aim and Purpose

The operational mechanics of investor dealing and the register, the anti-money laundering obligations attached to them, and how directors oversee investor-facing operations.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain subscription, redemption and register maintenance processes
- Assess investor due diligence, screening and suspicious activity controls
- Oversee investor service quality, complaints and dormant accounts

## Syllabus

- Dealing cycles, cut-off times, late trading and market timing controls
- Investor due diligence, sanctions screening and suspicious activity reporting
- Reliance on distributors and intermediaries for investor identification
- Complaints, unclaimed proceeds and investor communications
