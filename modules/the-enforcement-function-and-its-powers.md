## Aim and Purpose

The purpose of enforcement within the supervisory toolkit, the powers an authority typically holds, and why enforcement requires different skills and decision standards from ongoing supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purposes of enforcement: deterrence, remediation and market confidence
- Describe investigative, sanctioning and remedial powers
- Distinguish enforcement from ongoing supervisory intervention

## Syllabus

- Credible deterrence and the role of enforcement in the authority's strategy
- Types of power: investigative, sanctioning, remedial and restitution
- IOSCO principles for enforcement and international practice
- Where supervision ends and enforcement begins
