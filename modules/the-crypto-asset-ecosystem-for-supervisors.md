## Aim and Purpose

A supervisor's map of the technology, assets and market structure of the crypto ecosystem, focusing on where control really sits, how value and risk flow, and where failures typically originate.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how distributed ledgers, tokens and crypto-asset markets operate in practice
- Identify the true control points, incentives and accountability gaps in the ecosystem
- Analyse the technical and operational failure modes that drive consumer and prudential harm

## Syllabus

- Public, permissioned and hybrid ledgers: design choices and supervisory leverage
- Issuers, validators, venues, custodians, market makers and end users
- Control points: upgrades, forks, admin keys, oracles and listings
- Failure modes: smart contract flaws, oracle manipulation and off-chain dependencies
