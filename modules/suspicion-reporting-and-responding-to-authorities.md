## Aim and Purpose

How to escalate concerns about tax evasion, reach a reasoned judgement on suspicion, report to the national FIU and respond to tax authority and law enforcement requests without tipping off.

## Learning Objectives

- Escalate and investigate tax-related concerns consistently
- Decide on and file usable suspicious activity reports involving tax crime
- Handle information requests and relationship decisions correctly

## Syllabus

- Internal reporting and whistleblowing on facilitation concerns
- Suspicion of tax crime: thresholds, evidence and decision records
- Report quality, tipping off and exiting or continuing relationships
- Responding to tax authority, FIU and law enforcement requests
