## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to equip participants with the skills to translate a supervisory authority's statutory mandate into a strategic plan and prioritised work programme, balancing risk, resource, and stakeholder expectations.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior supervisory staff involved in setting or reviewing their authority's strategy, governance, or resourcing decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the relationship between statutory mandate and strategic priorities
- Apply a risk-based approach to prioritising supervisory work
- Assess resource constraints and their impact on strategic choices
- Evaluate stakeholder expectations and how to balance competing demands
- Formulate a prioritised strategic work programme
- Communicate strategic priorities clearly to internal and external audiences

## Syllabus

**Session 1: From mandate to strategy (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain how mandate translates into strategic priorities*
- Interpreting a statutory mandate
- The role of strategic planning in a supervisory authority
- Common strategic planning frameworks
- Aligning strategy with mandate and public expectations
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: participants' own authority's mandate and how it shapes strategy

**Session 2: Risk-based prioritisation (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply risk-based prioritisation and assess resource constraints*
- Risk-based approaches to prioritising supervisory work
- Resource constraints: budget, headcount, specialist skills
- Trade-offs between breadth and depth of supervisory coverage
- Tools for structured prioritisation
Activities:
- Group exercise: prioritise a sample list of supervisory risks under resource constraints

**Session 3: Stakeholders and strategic communication (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Balance stakeholder expectations and communicate strategy*
- Mapping stakeholder expectations: government, industry, public
- Balancing competing demands in strategy-setting
- Communicating strategic priorities clearly
- Case study: setting priorities amid competing pressures
Activities:
- Case study: draft a prioritised strategic work programme and a communication plan for it

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: mandate, prioritisation, stakeholders, communication
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: From mandate to strategy (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Risk-based prioritisation (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Stakeholders and strategic communication (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Strategy is the bridge between a broad statutory mandate and specific day-to-day priorities
- Risk-based prioritisation makes resource trade-offs explicit rather than accidental
- Clear communication of strategic priorities builds credibility with all stakeholder groups
