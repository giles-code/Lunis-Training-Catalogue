## Aim and Purpose

Practical preparation for an assessment: organising the national team, assembling evidence, writing the technical compliance and effectiveness submissions, and managing the on-site visit and plenary.

## Learning Objectives

- Organise a national evaluation team, timetable and governance
- Build a compelling evidence base of statistics and case studies
- Prepare officials for on-site interviews and plenary discussion

## Syllabus

- Gap analysis and a mock evaluation
- Statistics, case studies and evidence of outcomes
- Drafting the technical compliance and effectiveness submissions
- On-site interviews, draft report review and plenary negotiation
