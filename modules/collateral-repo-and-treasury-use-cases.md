## Aim and Purpose

How firms use tokenised assets and tokenised money market fund shares for collateral, margin, intraday repo and cash management, and the legal, liquidity and operational risks of faster collateral movement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how tokenised assets are mobilised for collateral, margin and repo
- Assess the enforceability of collateral arrangements and close-out on a ledger
- Manage the liquidity and operational risks of faster collateral flows

## Syllabus

- Collateral mobility, intraday repo and margin calls
- Tokenised money market fund shares as collateral and cash management
- Eligibility, haircuts, valuation and close-out netting
- Interaction with central counterparties and margin frameworks
