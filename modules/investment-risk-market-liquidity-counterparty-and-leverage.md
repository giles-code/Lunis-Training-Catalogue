## Aim and Purpose

The main investment risks a fund runs, how each is measured and limited, and the questions directors should ask when a strategy, instrument or market environment changes.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main measures of market, liquidity, counterparty and leverage risk
- Assess whether limits fit each fund's strategy, investors and dealing terms
- Challenge management on concentration, correlation and liquidity mismatch

## Syllabus

- Market risk measures: volatility, tracking error, value at risk and their limits
- Liquidity risk: asset liquidity, investor profile, dealing terms and management tools
- Counterparty and collateral risk in derivatives, financing and securities lending
- Leverage measures, internal limits that trigger before a breach and escalation
