## Aim and Purpose

How internal audit can give assurance on conduct, culture and the fair treatment of customers, moving beyond process testing to evidence of actual outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Design audit approaches to culture and behaviour
- Test whether controls deliver fair customer outcomes in practice
- Report soft-control findings credibly to management and the board

## Syllabus

- Auditing culture: indicators, behavioural evidence and soft controls
- Product governance, sales, complaints and vulnerable customers
- Remuneration, incentives and conduct risk
- Outcome testing and file review techniques
