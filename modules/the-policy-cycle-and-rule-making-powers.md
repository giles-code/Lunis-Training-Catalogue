## Aim and Purpose

Where a supervisory authority's rule-making power comes from, what primary legislation, secondary rules and guidance can each achieve, and how the policy cycle fits together.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the legal hierarchy of legislation, rules and guidance
- Identify the sources and limits of the authority's rule-making powers
- Describe the stages of the policy cycle from problem to review

## Syllabus

- Primary legislation, secondary rules and guidance: what each can do
- Delegated powers, their limits and legal challenge
- Binding rules versus non-binding guidance and supervisory statements
- The policy cycle: define, analyse, consult, decide, implement, review
