## Aim and Purpose

What to do when something goes wrong, from reporting a suspected breach to supporting the firm's continuity and recovery plans during a major disruption.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise security incidents, data breaches and near misses that must be reported
- Report incidents promptly through the correct channels
- Explain their own role in business continuity and the firm's important business services

## Syllabus

- What counts as an incident, and why speed matters
- Internal reporting routes and preserving evidence
- Business continuity plans, critical processes and fallback arrangements
- Important business services, impact tolerances and staff roles in recovery
