## Aim and Purpose

How corporate offences of failing to prevent the facilitation of tax evasion work, who counts as an associated person, and what reasonable prevention procedures look like in practice.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the structure of failure-to-prevent offences and the defence of reasonable procedures
- Identify the firm's associated persons, including employees, agents and introducers
- Design proportionate prevention procedures with clear senior management ownership

## Syllabus

- Criminal facilitation, the corporate offence and cross-border reach
- Associated persons: staff, introducers, distributors and service providers
- Prevention principles: risk assessment, proportionality, commitment, due diligence, communication and monitoring
- Documenting procedures and the evidence a prosecutor or supervisor would expect
