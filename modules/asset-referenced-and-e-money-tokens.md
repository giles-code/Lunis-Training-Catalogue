## Aim and Purpose

How ARTs and EMTs are structured and regulated under MiCA, the reserve and redemption mechanics that determine stability, and the supervisory arrangements for significant tokens.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish ART and EMT designs and their risk profiles
- Evaluate reserve quality, custody and redemption resilience under stress
- Explain significance classification and the EBA's supervisory role

## Syllabus

- Issuer authorisation, white papers and lessons from stablecoin failures
- Reserve composition, custody, audit and redemption at par
- EMTs, electronic money and the interplay with PSD2
- Significant tokens: EBA supervision, colleges and recovery and redemption plans
