## Aim and Purpose

How information-sharing gateways and memoranda of understanding work, what a good MoU contains, and how to make and respond to cooperation requests effectively.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain information-sharing gateways and confidentiality constraints
- Assess whether an MoU is fit for purpose
- Prepare and respond to cross-border information requests

## Syllabus

- Bilateral and multilateral MoUs, including the IOSCO MMoU
- Confidentiality, data protection and onward-sharing limits
- Making a clear, proportionate cooperation request
- Exercise: reviewing a sample MoU for strengths and gaps
