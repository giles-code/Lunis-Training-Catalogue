## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in why regulated firms must hold capital and liquidity, how requirements are set and what the board must monitor and approve.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of capital and liquidity requirements
- Interpret the firm's capital and liquidity position and its buffers
- Recognise the board's role in internal assessments, stress testing and recovery planning

## Syllabus

- Capital resources, requirements and buffers in plain language
- Liquidity risk, funding and client money
- Internal capital and liquidity assessments and board approval
- Stress testing, recovery and wind-down planning
