## Aim and Purpose

How regional crypto-asset regimes and international recommendations approach stablecoin issuers, from authorisation and governance to capital, liquidity, recovery and orderly redemption plans.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the international standards for stablecoin arrangements
- Compare regulatory approaches to single-currency and asset-referenced stablecoins
- Assess an issuer's authorisation application against core requirements

## Syllabus

- FSB high-level recommendations and the Basel Committee prudential treatment
- Authorisation, governance, fit and proper and conflicts of interest
- Own funds, liquidity, recovery and redemption plans
- Enhanced requirements for significant or systemic stablecoins
