## Aim and Purpose

How victims are manipulated into authorising payments to criminals, the main scam types, and the evidence available from victims, banks and communications providers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how authorised push payment fraud differs from unauthorised fraud
- Recognise impersonation, invoice redirection and purchase scams
- Gather evidence from victims, banks and communications providers

## Syllabus

- Authorised versus unauthorised payment fraud
- Impersonation, business email compromise and invoice redirection
- Purchase, advance fee and romance scams
- Evidence sources: victim accounts, payment records and communications data
