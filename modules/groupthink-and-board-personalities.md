## Aim and Purpose

Why some boards drift into consensus at the expense of realistic appraisal, the warning signs, and how recognising common board personalities helps plan interviews without prejudging findings.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the conditions and warning signs of groupthink
- Assess the countermeasures a board uses to protect independent judgement
- Use board personality types as an interview planning aid, not a finding

## Syllabus

- Risk factors: long tenure, social closeness and a dominant chair or chief executive
- Warning signs: quick unanimity, self-censorship and private doubts
- Countermeasures: devil's advocate, anonymous polling, 'what would change our mind?'
- The dominator, silent director, absentee, box-ticker and permanent devil's advocate
