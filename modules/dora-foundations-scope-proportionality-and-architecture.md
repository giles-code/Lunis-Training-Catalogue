## Aim and Purpose

Why DORA harmonises ICT risk rules across the financial sector, which entities it covers, how proportionality and the simplified framework work, and how the Level 1 text is built out through the ESAs' technical standards.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the financial entities and ICT third-party service providers within DORA's scope
- Apply proportionality, including the simplified ICT risk management framework
- Navigate DORA's technical standards, guidelines and interaction with sectoral rules

## Syllabus

- From fragmented sectoral rules to a single digital resilience regulation
- Financial entities in scope, microenterprises and the simplified framework
- Joint ESA technical standards, delegated acts and supervisory convergence
- DORA as sector-specific law alongside NIS2 and outsourcing guidelines
