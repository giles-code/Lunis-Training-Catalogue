## Aim and Purpose

The governance, risk management and capital arrangements a payment or e-money firm needs, and how boards and senior managers evidence sound oversight.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess governance arrangements against supervisory expectations
- Calculate and monitor own funds and capital requirements
- Apply fitness and propriety standards to directors and key function holders

## Syllabus

- Board composition, control functions and three lines of defence
- Own funds calculations, capital planning and liquidity
- Fitness and propriety of directors and qualifying holders
- Management information and regulatory reporting
