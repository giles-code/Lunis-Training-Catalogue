## Aim and Purpose

How crypto custody and the wider ICT estate work in practice, the operational, cyber and third-party risks they create, and how supervisors verify that client assets and critical services are protected.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain custody architectures, including cold storage, hardware security modules, multi-signature and MPC
- Assess safeguarding, segregation and reconciliation of client assets
- Evaluate ICT risk management, incident reporting and third-party dependencies

## Syllabus

- Proprietary custody, third-party custodians and sub-custody chains
- Red flags: commingling, weak key controls and missing independent reconciliation
- Cloud concentration, API dependencies and on-chain/off-chain integration risk
- Incident reporting, resilience testing and exit planning for critical providers
