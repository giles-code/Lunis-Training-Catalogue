## Aim and Purpose

A live simulation in which teams respond to an unfolding firm-distress scenario, making supervisory decisions, escalating and coordinating across functions under time pressure, followed by a structured debrief.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply decision-making frameworks under time pressure and incomplete information
- Coordinate a response across supervision, legal, resolution and communications
- Evaluate decisions made and identify lessons for crisis preparedness

## Syllabus

- Scenario briefing, roles and initial rapid assessment
- Escalation, coordination and stakeholder management as the scenario evolves
- Facilitator-led debrief comparing team decisions
- Workshop: the authority's crisis preparedness action plan
