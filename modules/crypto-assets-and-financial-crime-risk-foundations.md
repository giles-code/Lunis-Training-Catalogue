## Aim and Purpose

The technical and regulatory grounding for financial crime work: how public blockchains, wallets and service providers operate, why they attract criminals, and what the FATF standards require.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain addresses, keys, transactions and public ledgers in plain terms
- Describe the roles of exchanges, custodians, brokers and other service providers
- Summarise the FATF standards and risk-based approach for virtual assets

## Syllabus

- Blockchains, wallets, keys and transaction records
- Service provider business models and points of fiat conversion
- Pseudonymity, speed and cross-border reach as risk factors
- FATF Recommendations 15 and 16 and the risk-based approach
