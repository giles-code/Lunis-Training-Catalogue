## Aim and Purpose

The international principles, standards and forums that shape AI policy, and how government can align domestic policy, influence global standards and manage cross-border divergence.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the OECD AI Principles and related international frameworks
- Explain the work of the FSB, IOSCO, BIS and other standard-setters on AI
- Assess the costs of divergence and options for interoperability

## Syllabus

- OECD AI Principles, G20 commitments and international declarations
- Standard-setter work on AI in banking, securities and insurance
- Management system and risk frameworks: ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST
- Cross-border divergence, mutual recognition and interoperability
