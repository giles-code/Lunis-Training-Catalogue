## Aim and Purpose

Designing and calibrating sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening, including fuzzy matching and natural language processing, and managing evasion risks from ownership structures and trade.

## Learning Objectives

- Calibrate name and payment screening, including fuzzy matching
- Evaluate natural language processing for adverse media monitoring
- Identify sanctions evasion typologies relevant to the firm

## Syllabus

- Screening lists, matching logic and threshold calibration
- Ownership and control, and circumvention through intermediaries
- Adverse media analytics and alert quality
- Testing and assuring screening effectiveness
