## Aim and Purpose

How crypto-asset custody works in practice, from hot and cold storage to sub-custody chains, and how the firm builds and evidences safeguarding that protects client assets in all conditions.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess how custody architectures affect the firm's safeguarding risk
- Identify key management, reconciliation and sub-custodian risks
- Design and evidence segregation, reconciliation and insolvency protection

## Syllabus

- Hot and cold storage, hardware security modules, multi-signature and MPC
- Key management, dual control and backup and recovery
- Segregation, reconciliation and the ban on unauthorised use of client assets
- Red flags and custody assurance: walkthroughs, audits and testing
