## Aim and Purpose

The roles, activities and business models of crypto-asset service providers, how they make money, and how to determine which licences and registrations a business needs.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify core service provider activities and services
- Analyse business models, revenue sources and unit economics in crypto markets
- Determine the perimeter, licensing and registration needs of a business

## Syllabus

- Exchanges, brokers and trading platforms: operating structures
- Custodians, wallet providers, issuers and token platforms
- Infrastructure providers supporting crypto-asset markets
- Hybrid and decentralised models: perimeter and licensing implications
