## Aim and Purpose

How PSD2 and EMD2 shaped EU payments, what the review found, and how the reform splits the framework between PSD3 for licensing and supervision and a directly applicable Payment Services Regulation for conduct rules.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the scope, structure and achievements of PSD2 and EMD2
- Identify the weaknesses the review found in fraud, open banking, enforcement and the level playing field
- Map the PSD3 and PSR reform package and its legislative and transition stages

## Syllabus

- Payment services, e-money and exclusions: the current perimeter
- Lessons from PSD2: fraud, interface quality, divergent application and arbitrage
- PSD3 for licensing and supervision, the PSR for conduct and operational rules
- EBA technical standards, guidelines and Q&As under both regimes
