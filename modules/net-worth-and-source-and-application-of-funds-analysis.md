## Aim and Purpose

Indirect methods for proving unexplained income and wealth, how to build a defensible net-worth or source-and-application-of-funds analysis, and how these support charges and confiscation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain when indirect methods of proof are appropriate
- Build a net-worth and a source-and-application-of-funds statement
- Test the analysis against likely defence explanations

## Syllabus

- Direct versus indirect methods of proof
- Net-worth method: assets, liabilities, expenditure and known income
- Source-and-application-of-funds statements
- Anticipating defences: cash savings, gifts, loans and inheritances
