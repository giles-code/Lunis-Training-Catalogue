## Aim and Purpose

How to read a fund's financial statements, what the external audit does and does not provide, and the board's role in the audit cycle.

## Learning Objectives

- Interpret the key components of fund financial statements
- Explain the audit process, audit opinions and management letters
- Oversee the auditor's appointment, independence and findings

## Syllabus

- Statement of net assets, income statement, portfolio and notes
- The audit cycle: planning, fieldwork, findings and sign-off
- Modified opinions, emphasis of matter and management letter points
- Auditor independence, rotation and non-audit services
