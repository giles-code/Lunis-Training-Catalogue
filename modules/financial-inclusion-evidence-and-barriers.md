## Aim and Purpose

Why financial inclusion matters for growth and resilience, how access and usage are measured, and the economic, social and regulatory barriers that keep people excluded.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the economic and social case for financial inclusion
- Use demand-side and supply-side data to measure access and usage
- Identify the barriers facing women, rural, low-income and other excluded groups

## Syllabus

- Inclusion, growth, resilience and poverty reduction
- Global Findex and national survey data
- Barriers: cost, distance, documentation, trust and capability
- Gender gaps, small businesses and forcibly displaced people
