## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' ability to recognise early warning signs of firm distress and apply a structured intervention ladder before problems become crises.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior staff involved in crisis preparedness, recovery, and resolution planning.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept of an intervention ladder
- Identify common early warning indicators of firm distress
- Assess when escalation up the intervention ladder is warranted
- Apply structured decision thresholds for early intervention
- Formulate an early intervention response for a sample scenario

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Warning signs and the intervention ladder (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the intervention ladder concept*
- The intervention ladder: from monitoring to formal action
- Why early intervention is more effective than late intervention
- Common early warning indicators across risk types
- Behavioural versus quantitative warning signs
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: early warning signs participants have observed

**Session 2: Escalation thresholds (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess escalation triggers and apply decision thresholds*
- Deciding when to escalate up the intervention ladder
- Balancing false alarms against late intervention risk
- Structured decision thresholds for common warning signs
- Documenting the basis for escalation decisions
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's warning signs and decide on an escalation point

**Session 3: Applying early intervention (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Formulate an early intervention response*
- Options available at each rung of the intervention ladder
- Case study: a firm showing early signs of distress
Activities:
- Case study: formulate an early intervention response for a sample firm

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: warning signs, escalation thresholds, intervention
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Warning signs and the intervention ladder (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Escalation thresholds (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Applying early intervention (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Early intervention is almost always more effective and less costly than late intervention
- A structured intervention ladder makes escalation decisions consistent and defensible
- Behavioural warning signs often precede quantitative deterioration and deserve equal attention
