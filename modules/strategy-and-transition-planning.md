## Aim and Purpose

How boards test whether the firm's transition plan is credible, consistent with its strategy and capable of delivery, and how climate targets fit into business decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Challenge the ambition, assumptions and deliverability of the transition plan
- Assess whether targets and interim milestones are embedded in business decisions
- Recognise the strategic opportunities and risks of the transition

## Syllabus

- Elements of a credible transition plan: targets, levers, metrics and governance
- Financed and insured emissions and the limits of the data
- Client engagement versus divestment
- Questions to ask of a transition plan before board approval
