## Aim and Purpose

Account information and payment initiation services under PSD2, the reform's improvements to dedicated interfaces, permission dashboards and prohibited obstacles, and the wider move towards open finance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the rights and obligations of account servicing providers and third-party providers
- Assess dedicated interface performance, fallback arrangements and prohibited obstacles
- Evaluate customer permissions, data use and liability between providers

## Syllabus

- Account information and payment initiation services: rights and conditions
- Dedicated interfaces, performance requirements and fallback mechanisms
- Permission dashboards, consent and interaction with data protection rules
- From open banking to open finance: emerging proposals
