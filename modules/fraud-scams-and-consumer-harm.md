## Aim and Purpose

The rapid growth of authorised push payment fraud, investment scams and online financial harm, and the policy tools to prevent fraud, allocate liability and support victims.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the main fraud and scam typologies affecting consumers
- Evaluate prevention, reimbursement and liability allocation policies
- Design coordination between regulators, law enforcement, telecoms and online platforms

## Syllabus

- Investment, romance, impersonation and authorised push payment scams
- Reimbursement and liability models for payment fraud
- Online platforms, telecoms and advertising controls
- National fraud strategies and victim support
