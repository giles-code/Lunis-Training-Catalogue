## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the regulatory foundations for authorising and supervising payment institutions and e-money issuers, including current PSD3/PSR transition considerations.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in payment services supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the categories of authorisation under PSD2/PSD3
- Describe e-money issuance requirements
- Assess licensing requirements for different payment institution categories
- Evaluate the practical implications of the PSD3/PSR transition
- Formulate an authorisation approach for a sample payment institution application

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Authorisation categories (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain authorisation categories under PSD2/PSD3*
- Categories of payment institution authorisation
- E-money institution authorisation and its distinguishing features
- Registered versus authorised status for smaller providers
- Capital and safeguarding requirements by category
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: payment institution authorisation experiences from participants

**Session 2: Licensing requirements in practice (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess licensing requirements for different categories*
- Assessing licensing applications against category-specific requirements
- Common application weaknesses for payment institutions
- Safeguarding arrangements and their verification
- Governance and fit-and-proper expectations for payment institutions
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample payment institution licence application

**Session 3: PSD3/PSR transition and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate the PSD3/PSR transition and formulate an approach*
- Key changes introduced by PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation
- Practical transition considerations for existing authorised institutions
- Case study: an authorisation application under the transitioning framework
Activities:
- Case study: formulate an authorisation approach for a sample application

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: authorisation categories, licensing requirements, PSD3/PSR transition
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Authorisation categories (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Licensing requirements in practice (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: PSD3/PSR transition and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Payment institution authorisation categories carry meaningfully different regulatory requirements that must be matched carefully to the applicant's actual business model
- Safeguarding arrangements are a critical, frequently under-scrutinised element of payment institution authorisation
- The PSD3/PSR transition creates practical challenges for both new applicants and existing authorised institutions
