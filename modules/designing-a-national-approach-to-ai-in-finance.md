## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a policy approach that links financial sector AI policy with national AI strategy, and anticipates emerging issues from generative AI and autonomous agents.

## Learning Objectives

- Align financial sector AI policy with national AI and digital strategy
- Anticipate policy issues from generative AI and autonomous agents
- Produce a policy action plan for AI in the financial sector

## Syllabus

- Linking AI, data, competition and financial services policy
- Sandboxes, pilots and public-private collaboration
- Agentic AI, accountability and liability questions
- Workshop: a national AI in finance policy action plan
