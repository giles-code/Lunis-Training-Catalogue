## Aim and Purpose

How funds value their assets and calculate net asset value, why valuation of illiquid and hard-to-value assets demands close scrutiny, and how supervisors test valuation governance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain valuation and pricing standards for collective investment schemes
- Assess valuation governance, independence and conflicts of interest
- Identify red flags in valuation of illiquid and hard-to-value assets

## Syllabus

- Net asset value calculation, pricing errors and investor compensation
- Valuation policies, committees and the independence of the valuation function
- Hard-to-value assets: models, stale prices and manager discretion
- Case study: valuation red flags in a fund under stress
