## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together: cooperative oversight of cross-border FMIs, the risks of DLT-based and tokenised infrastructures, and a supervisory workshop on an FMI under stress.

## Learning Objectives

- Operate cooperative oversight arrangements and crisis cooperation across borders
- Analyse how the PFMI apply to DLT-based infrastructures and stablecoin arrangements
- Apply the programme to a supervisory case involving an FMI under stress

## Syllabus

- Responsibility E: colleges, crisis management groups and information sharing
- DLT settlement, tokenised assets and atomic settlement risks
- Stablecoin arrangements and the PFMI
- Workshop: a CCP member default with cross-border spillovers
