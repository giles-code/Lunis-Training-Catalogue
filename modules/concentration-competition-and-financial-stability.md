## Aim and Purpose

How platform scale, data advantages and partnership networks can concentrate risk and market power, and when platform finance becomes a financial stability concern.

## Learning Objectives

- Analyse concentration risks in distribution, data and infrastructure
- Assess the interaction between competition and financial stability objectives
- Identify indicators that a platform has become systemically relevant

## Syllabus

- Market power, lock-in, self-preferencing and data advantages
- Rapid deposit and credit flows through platform channels
- Interconnections with banks, funds and payment systems
- Coordination with competition authorities
