## Aim and Purpose

The technology risks specific to tokenised markets, including fragmented ledgers, cross-chain bridges, oracles and smart contract vulnerabilities, and the evidence supervisors should request from firms.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess interoperability between ledgers and with traditional infrastructure
- Evaluate smart contract risk, code audits and upgrade governance
- Identify operational, cyber and third-party risks in DLT arrangements

## Syllabus

- Fragmentation, cross-chain bridges and messaging standards
- Smart contract vulnerabilities, audits and emergency controls
- Oracles, data feeds and node operator dependencies
- Red flags and supervisory questions on DLT technology risk
