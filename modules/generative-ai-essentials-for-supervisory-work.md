## Aim and Purpose

A practical introduction to how generative AI and large language models work, what they can and cannot do, and where they add value in supervisory work.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain in plain terms how large language models generate text
- Identify strengths and limits, including hallucination and outdated knowledge
- Recognise the supervisory tasks where AI tools add most value

## Syllabus

- How large language models work: training, prompts and probabilistic output
- Chat assistants, document tools, analytics tools and AI built into office software
- Typical failure modes: hallucination, omission, overconfidence and bias
- Mapping the supervisory day: where AI helps and where it should not be used
