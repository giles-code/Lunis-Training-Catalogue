## Aim and Purpose

How ARTs and EMTs are structured and regulated under MiCA Titles III and IV, the reserve and redemption mechanics that determine stability, and the EBA's role for significant tokens.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish fiat-backed, collateralised and algorithmic designs and their risk profiles
- Evaluate reserve quality, liquidity and redemption resilience under stress
- Explain significance classification and the supervisory handover to the EBA

## Syllabus

- Stablecoin models and lessons from algorithmic stablecoin collapses
- Reserve composition, custody, audit and redemption at par on demand
- EMTs and electronic money: interplay with PSD2 and the payments framework
- Significant tokens: EBA supervision, colleges and recovery and redemption plans
