## Aim and Purpose

Why the EU legislated horizontally for AI, the definition of an AI system, material and territorial scope, the risk tiers, and how the Act borrows its architecture from EU product safety law.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the AI Act's objectives and product-safety architecture
- Apply the definition of an AI system and the main scope exclusions
- Distinguish prohibited, high-risk, transparency-risk and minimal-risk systems

## Syllabus

- Horizontal AI regulation, fundamental rights and the internal market
- The AI system definition, Commission guidelines and traditional statistical models
- Providers, deployers, importers, distributors and extraterritorial reach
- The risk tiers and the AI literacy obligation
