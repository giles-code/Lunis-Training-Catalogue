## Aim and Purpose

How open banking and open finance frameworks enable customer-permissioned data sharing, the design choices behind them, and the competition, privacy and security trade-offs supervisors need to understand.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the design of open banking and open finance frameworks
- Assess consent, liability and security arrangements for data sharing
- Evaluate the risk that data access entrenches rather than reduces platform power

## Syllabus

- Open banking to open finance: scope and data categories
- APIs, standards, consent dashboards and liability models
- Data protection, fraud and cybersecurity risks in data sharing
- Reciprocity and data flows between big tech and incumbents
