## Aim and Purpose

How targeted financial sanctions work, the obligations they place on firms and the risks of breach and evasion.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and types of financial sanctions
- Describe firms' screening, freezing and reporting obligations
- Recognise common sanctions evasion techniques

## Syllabus

- International, regional and national sanctions regimes in outline
- Asset freezes, prohibitions and ownership and control tests
- Screening, alert handling and reporting obligations
- Evasion: front companies, trade routes and crypto-assets
