## Aim and Purpose

How to plan and run effective supervisory meetings with chairs, directors and senior managers, set the agenda, test understanding and leave with clear outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan a supervisory meeting with clear objectives and lines of enquiry
- Engage senior individuals with authority, courtesy and challenge
- Record outcomes and agree next steps that can be followed up

## Syllabus

- Purpose of meetings: risk assessment, findings and relationship building
- Preparation: briefing packs, agendas and question sets
- Chairing and steering the meeting while testing understanding
- Meeting notes, actions and confirming outcomes in writing
