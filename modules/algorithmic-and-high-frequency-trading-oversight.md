## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in supervising firms' algorithmic and high-frequency trading controls, including pre-trade risk controls and market-making obligations.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment services/capital markets supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain algorithmic and high-frequency trading and their market role
- Describe pre-trade risk control requirements
- Assess a firm's algorithmic trading governance and testing framework
- Evaluate market-making obligations and their supervision
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of an algorithmic trading control framework

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Algorithmic and HFT fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain algo/HFT trading and its market role*
- How algorithmic and high-frequency trading strategies operate
- Benefits and risks: liquidity provision versus flash-crash risk
- Regulatory concerns specific to algorithmic trading
- The evolving market share of algorithmic trading
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: algorithmic trading incidents participants are aware of

**Session 2: Pre-trade controls and governance (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe pre-trade controls and assess governance/testing*
- Pre-trade risk control requirements: limits, kill switches, circuit breakers
- Algorithm testing and approval governance
- Assessing whether controls are genuinely effective, not just present
- Common control failures in algorithmic trading
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's algorithmic trading governance framework

**Session 3: Market-making obligations and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate market-making obligations and formulate an assessment*
- Market-making obligations and their supervision
- Assessing genuine liquidity provision versus opportunistic behaviour
- Case study: an algorithmic trading control framework under review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of an algorithmic trading control framework

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: algo/HFT role, pre-trade controls, market-making obligations
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Algorithmic and HFT fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Pre-trade controls and governance (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Market-making obligations and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Algorithmic trading controls must be tested for genuine effectiveness, not merely confirmed to exist
- Pre-trade risk controls are the first line of defence against algorithmic trading incidents
- Market-making obligations should be assessed against actual liquidity provision behaviour, not just formal commitments

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with investment services & capital markets is helpful but not essential.
