## Aim and Purpose

To build a shared, practical understanding of anti-money laundering and
financial crime risk — useful both to firms managing their own compliance
obligations and to supervisors assessing firms' controls.

## Intended Audience

Compliance officers and MLROs at regulated firms, and supervisory staff at
regulatory authorities responsible for assessing financial crime controls.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

- Explain the core stages of money laundering and typical red flags
- Describe the key elements of an effective AML control framework
- Identify common weaknesses supervisors look for when assessing firms

## Syllabus

1. Money laundering: stages and typologies
2. Customer due diligence and ongoing monitoring
3. Suspicious activity reporting
4. Governance and the three lines of defence
5. What supervisors look for in an AML assessment

## Structure and Format

A half-day session (4 hours), available either fully online or as a hybrid
session with an in-person option for local participants.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

No formal prerequisites. Suitable as an introduction for those new to AML,
or as a refresher for more experienced participants.
