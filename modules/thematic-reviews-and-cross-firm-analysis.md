## Aim and Purpose

When and how to use thematic reviews to examine a risk across many firms, how to scope them tightly, and how to use the results in both firm-level and sector-wide supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what drives a thematic review and the main types of review
- Design a tightly scoped review that avoids over-reach
- Use thematic findings to inform firm ratings, policy and industry communication

## Syllabus

- Triggers for thematic work: emerging risks, outliers and policy questions
- Scoping, sampling and avoiding the over-scoped 'Christmas tree' review
- Managing moral hazard and expectations created by thematic findings
- Thematic governance: panel composition, packs and reporting
