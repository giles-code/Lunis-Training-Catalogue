## Aim and Purpose

How to set the team's priorities when demand always exceeds capacity, make the case for resources, and say no to low-value work without leaving the firm exposed.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply risk-based prioritisation to competing advisory, monitoring and regulatory demands
- Build a credible case for headcount, skills and tools
- Agree with senior management what the function will not do and record the risk

## Syllabus

- Prioritisation tools linked to the compliance and risk assessment
- Regulatory change, incidents and business requests: managing the peaks
- Making the resourcing case: workload data, risk and supervisory expectations
- Saying no and accepting residual risk transparently
