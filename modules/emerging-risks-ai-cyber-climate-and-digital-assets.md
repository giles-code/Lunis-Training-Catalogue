## Aim and Purpose

How new technologies and structural trends change the risk profile of funds and managers, and how the board keeps its risk framework current without chasing every headline.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the risks from AI, cyber threats, climate change and digital assets
- Assess how these risks enter the fund through investments, providers and operations
- Build emerging risks into horizon scanning, appetite and board agendas

## Syllabus

- AI in portfolio management and operations: model, data and dependency risk
- Cyber threats, ransomware and resilience of the operating chain
- Climate and sustainability risks in portfolios and disclosures
- Digital assets, tokenised fund units and custody risk
