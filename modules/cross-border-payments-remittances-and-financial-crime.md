## Aim and Purpose

Why cross-border payments remain slow and costly, the G20 programme to improve them, and the financial crime, sanctions and resilience risks that new payment channels create.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the frictions in cross-border payments and remittances
- Describe the G20 targets and the national actions they require
- Assess fraud, money laundering and operational resilience risks in payments

## Syllabus

- Correspondent banking, de-risking and remittance costs
- The G20 roadmap: targets, building blocks and payment system links
- Authorised push payment fraud, scams and reimbursement policy
- Cyber resilience, outages and critical payment infrastructure
