## Aim and Purpose

The economic case for financial regulation, the objectives regulators pursue, and the tensions between stability, consumer protection, market integrity, competition and growth.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the market failures that justify financial regulation
- Distinguish prudential, conduct, market integrity and financial crime objectives
- Assess the trade-offs between regulatory objectives and economic growth

## Syllabus

- Information asymmetry, externalities, moral hazard and contagion
- Statutory objectives and how they are framed in legislation
- Competition and growth objectives: benefits and risks
- Lessons from crises on the cost of regulatory failure
