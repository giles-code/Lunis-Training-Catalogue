## Aim and Purpose

How jurisdictions are regulating AI, how horizontal AI rules interact with existing financial services requirements, and what overlapping regimes mean for supervisory practice and coordination.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main models of AI regulation and the obligations they create
- Analyse how AI rules overlay conduct, prudential and consumer protection requirements
- Evaluate coordination and perimeter issues between financial supervisors and other authorities

## Syllabus

- Approaches to AI regulation: risk-based laws, sectoral rules and supervisory guidance
- International standards and frameworks: OECD, NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, FSB, IOSCO and BIS
- High-risk financial use cases and existing sectoral obligations
- Coordination with data protection, competition and AI-specific authorities
