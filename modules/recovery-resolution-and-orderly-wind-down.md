## Aim and Purpose

How FMIs plan for recovery from extreme losses, the tools for allocating uncovered losses and liquidity shortfalls, and how resolution authorities maintain critical services when recovery fails.

## Learning Objectives

- Challenge FMI recovery plans, triggers and loss-allocation tools
- Assess general business risk and orderly wind-down resources
- Explain resolution powers and planning for systemically important FMIs

## Syllabus

- Recovery tools: cash calls, variation margin gains haircutting and tear-ups
- Non-default losses: investment, custody, operational and business losses
- Liquid net assets for business risk and orderly wind-down plans
- Resolution planning, continuity of critical services and the Key Attributes
