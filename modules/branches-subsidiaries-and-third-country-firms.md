## Aim and Purpose

The supervisory trade-offs between branches and subsidiaries, how cross-border access and passporting regimes allocate responsibility, and how hosts assess third-country firms and equivalence reliance.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare the supervisory risks of branches and subsidiaries
- Assess third-country firms and reliance on home supervision
- Identify when a host should require subsidiarisation or additional safeguards

## Syllabus

- Branch versus subsidiary: capital, liquidity, governance and resolvability
- Passporting, cross-border services and regional access regimes
- Equivalence, deference and reliance on home supervisors
- Host safeguards for systemically important branches
