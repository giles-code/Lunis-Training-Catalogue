## Aim and Purpose

Assessing whether the price customers pay is reasonable relative to the benefits they receive, and identifying pricing practices that cause harm.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a fair value assessment methodology
- Assess pricing practices, charges and differential treatment of customer groups
- Act on fair value findings through product or pricing change

## Syllabus

- Fair value frameworks: price, benefits, costs and quality
- Loyalty penalties, price walking and hidden charges
- Distribution costs and remuneration in the value chain
- Documenting and evidencing fair value assessments
