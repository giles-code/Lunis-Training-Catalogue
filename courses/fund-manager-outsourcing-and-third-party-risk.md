## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how fund managers structure delegation and outsourcing arrangements, and how supervisors assess effective oversight of third-party arrangements.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment funds supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the regulatory framework governing fund manager delegation
- Describe common outsourcing and delegation structures in asset management
- Assess whether a manager retains effective oversight of delegated functions
- Identify concentration and continuity risks in third-party arrangements
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a manager's outsourcing framework

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Delegation and outsourcing regulation (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the delegation regulatory framework*
- Why delegation and outsourcing are common in fund management
- Regulatory framework governing delegation (AIFMD, UCITS)
- Distinguishing delegation from outsourcing
- Substance requirements and the 'letter-box entity' concern
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: delegation structures participants have encountered

**Session 2: Assessing effective oversight (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess whether managers retain effective oversight*
- Criteria for assessing effective oversight of a delegate
- Common weaknesses: inadequate monitoring, resource gaps at the manager
- Reviewing service level agreements and oversight reporting
- Red flags indicating a manager has lost effective control
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample delegation arrangement for effective oversight

**Session 3: Concentration risk and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify concentration/continuity risk and formulate an assessment*
- Concentration risk from reliance on a small number of key service providers
- Business continuity considerations for outsourced functions
- Case study: a manager's outsourcing framework under review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a manager's outsourcing framework

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: delegation framework, effective oversight, concentration risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Delegation and outsourcing regulation (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing effective oversight (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Concentration risk and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Delegation does not delegate accountability — the manager remains responsible for outcomes
- Effective oversight requires genuine monitoring capability at the manager, not just contractual language
- Concentration in a small number of key service providers is itself a risk worth assessing explicitly
