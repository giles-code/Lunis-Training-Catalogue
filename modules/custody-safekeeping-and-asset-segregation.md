## Aim and Purpose

How custody changes when assets are held as tokens, from private key management and wallet architecture to segregation, reconciliation and the protection of client assets on insolvency.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain custody models for tokenised assets and the role of private keys
- Assess segregation, reconciliation and client asset protection on a ledger
- Evaluate key management controls and the risk of loss or theft

## Syllabus

- Custody models: direct holding, omnibus and third-party custodians
- Private key management, multi-signature and threshold signatures
- Reconciliation between on-chain records and books and records
- Insolvency protection and return of client assets
