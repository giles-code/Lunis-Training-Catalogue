## Aim and Purpose

How to supervise firms' conduct of business arrangements, including client categorisation, disclosure, suitability and appropriateness assessments, and fair treatment of customers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core conduct of business obligations owed to clients
- Assess suitability and appropriateness processes and file quality
- Identify conduct risks arising from advice and distribution models

## Syllabus

- Client categorisation, disclosure and fair, clear and not misleading communications
- Suitability and appropriateness: process, evidence and file reviews
- Advice and distribution models and their conduct risks
- Sample testing of client files: what good and poor look like
