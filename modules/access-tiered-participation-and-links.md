## Aim and Purpose

How FMIs set fair and open access criteria, the risks hidden in tiered participation and client clearing, and the exposures created by links between infrastructures.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate participation requirements for fairness, risk basis and openness
- Identify risks from indirect participants and client clearing
- Assess the risks of links and interoperability arrangements

## Syllabus

- Risk-based access criteria and suspension or exit of participants
- Tiered participation: concentration on large direct participants
- Client clearing, segregation and portability
- CCP links, CSD links and interoperability exposures
