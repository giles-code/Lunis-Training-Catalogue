## Aim and Purpose

How the authority cooperates with the national FIU, law enforcement and foreign counterparts, and the legal and practical considerations involved.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of the national FIU and how suspicious activity reports are used
- Describe the gateways for domestic and international information exchange
- Recognise the authority's own reporting and confidentiality obligations

## Syllabus

- The FIU's functions: receipt, analysis and dissemination
- Domestic cooperation and national coordination arrangements
- International cooperation and memoranda of understanding
- Tipping off, confidentiality and data protection
