## Aim and Purpose

Why most firms depend on vendors, payment schemes and market infrastructures to migrate, and the particular challenges quantum computing poses for blockchains, wallets and digital asset custody.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess firms' reliance on vendors and infrastructures for post-quantum migration
- Analyse coordination challenges in payment systems and market infrastructure
- Evaluate quantum risks to blockchain signatures, wallets and custody

## Syllabus

- Vendor roadmaps, contractual commitments and supply chain assurance
- Coordinated migration across payment schemes and settlement systems
- Quantum exposure of blockchain signatures and exposed public keys
- Implications for crypto-asset custody and tokenised assets
