## Aim and Purpose

To give supervisors a practical understanding of how firms build and run their compliance arrangements, and how to test them on the supervisor's side of the table. Participants leave able to judge whether arrangements are effective, spot the red flags of paper compliance and frame proportionate supervisory responses.

## What It Covers

The compliance function and monitoring programmes, AML/CFT compliance, conduct of business and suitability, complaints handling, outsourcing, governance and fitness and propriety, regulatory reporting, conflicts of interest, data protection and record-keeping, and compliance culture.

## Intended Audience

- Supervisors and inspectors responsible for assessing firms' compliance and control arrangements
- AML/CFT, conduct and governance specialists who review firms' policies, procedures and testing
- Newer supervisors and staff moving into supervision from compliance roles in industry

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what effective compliance arrangements look like across the main areas of firm activity
- Assess the independence, resourcing and effectiveness of the compliance function
- Evaluate firms' policies, monitoring and testing against how they operate in practice
- Identify red flags of paper compliance, weak controls and poor customer outcomes
- Challenge senior management and control functions using targeted supervisory questions
- Formulate proportionate findings and supervisory responses for compliance weaknesses

## Structure and Format

Ten half-day masterclasses of about three and a half hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, as a complete series or as standalone sessions. Each masterclass combines a short briefing with case studies, sample file exercises and a supervisory question set, and masterclasses can be selected or tailored to the authority's priorities.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of supervision or of compliance in a regulated firm is helpful. Each masterclass is self-contained, so no prior attendance is required.

## Core Reading

- Basel Committee, Compliance and the Compliance Function in Banks (2005)
- IOSCO, Compliance Function at Market Intermediaries (2006)
- FSB, Strengthening Governance Frameworks to Mitigate Misconduct Risk: A Toolkit for Firms and Supervisors (2018)
