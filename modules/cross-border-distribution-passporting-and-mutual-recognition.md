## Aim and Purpose

How funds and managers operate across borders through regional passports, bilateral mutual recognition and private placement, and how home and host authorities share responsibility for investor protection.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare regional fund passports, mutual recognition and private placement routes
- Explain home and host responsibilities and supervisory cooperation arrangements
- Assess the entry conditions and marketing rules that apply to foreign funds

## Syllabus

- Regional fund passports: design features and conditions for access
- Bilateral mutual recognition and equivalence-based arrangements
- Private placement, reverse solicitation and pre-marketing
- Information sharing, the IOSCO MMoU and host supervision
