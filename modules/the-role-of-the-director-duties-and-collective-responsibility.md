## Aim and Purpose

The legal duties a director owes, the different roles of executive, non-executive and independent directors and the chair, and how individual responsibility sits alongside the board's collective decision-making.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core duties of a director and to whom they are owed
- Compare the roles of executive, non-executive and independent directors
- Balance individual responsibility with the board's collective accountability

## Syllabus

- Statutory and fiduciary duties: skill, care, diligence and acting in good faith
- Is there a job description for a director of a fund or a management company?
- Executive, non-executive, independent and chair roles on the board and its committees
- Individual versus collective responsibility and protecting against liability
