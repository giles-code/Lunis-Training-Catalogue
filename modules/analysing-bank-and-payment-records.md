## Aim and Purpose

Techniques for reading and analysing account statements, payment data and supporting documents to identify patterns, links and anomalies that test the investigation's hypotheses.

## Learning Objectives

- Read account opening, statement and payment records accurately
- Apply spreadsheet and analytical techniques to large transaction sets
- Identify patterns indicating laundering, fraud or hidden assets

## Syllabus

- Account opening files, statements, payment messages and card data
- Cleaning, normalising and reconciling transaction data
- Pattern analysis: round sums, pass-through activity and structuring
- Charting money flows and counterparties
