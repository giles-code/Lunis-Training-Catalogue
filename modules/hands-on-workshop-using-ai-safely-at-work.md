## Aim and Purpose

A practical workshop in which participants apply what they have learned to realistic tasks from their own roles, using the firm's approved tools.

## Learning Objectives

- Use AI effectively on realistic tasks from participants' own roles
- Verify and improve AI outputs before use
- Agree personal good-practice commitments for AI use

## Syllabus

- Exercise: summarising a policy or regulatory document
- Exercise: drafting and checking a client or internal communication
- Spot the error: reviewing flawed AI outputs
- Workshop: my safe AI use checklist
