## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining sustainability-related fund disclosures and greenwashing, fund tokenisation, and the regulatory topics shaping fund supervision, ending with a supervisory planning workshop.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess sustainability claims, fund labels and disclosures for greenwashing risk
- Explain how tokenisation and distributed ledgers change fund operations and risk
- Prioritise emerging fund risks in a supervisory plan

## Syllabus

- Sustainability disclosures, fund categorisation and naming rules
- Greenwashing and greenbleaching: evidence, red flags and supervisory response
- Tokenised fund units: issuance, transfer agency, custody and settlement
- Workshop: a twelve-month supervisory plan for the funds sector
