## Aim and Purpose

How cases reach enforcement, how referrals are assessed and how the authority decides which matters merit a formal investigation.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess a referral against the authority's case selection criteria
- Distinguish matters for investigation from supervisory or alternative outcomes
- Record the rationale for opening or declining a case

## Syllabus

- Sources of cases: supervision, whistleblowers, complaints and market surveillance
- Case selection criteria: seriousness, harm, deterrence and resources
- Alternatives to investigation and early resolution
- Documenting the referral decision
