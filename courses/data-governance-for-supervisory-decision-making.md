## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of data governance principles and their application to supervisory decision-making, ensuring that decisions are supported by reliable, well-governed data.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior staff involved in the authority's own data and technology infrastructure.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the principles of good data governance
- Describe common data quality issues affecting supervisory decisions
- Assess data lineage and its importance for defensible decisions
- Evaluate the governance of decision-support systems
- Formulate data governance improvements for a sample scenario

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Principles of data governance (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain data governance principles*
- What data governance means for a supervisory authority
- Data ownership, stewardship, and accountability
- Data quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, timeliness
- Consequences of poor data governance for supervisory decisions
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: data quality issues participants have encountered

**Session 2: Data lineage and decision defensibility (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess data lineage and its role in defensible decisions*
- What data lineage is and why it matters
- Tracing a supervisory decision back to its underlying data
- Governance of decision-support and analytics systems
- Documenting data provenance for audit and challenge
Activities:
- Exercise: trace the data lineage behind a sample supervisory decision

**Session 3: Applying data governance (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Formulate governance improvements for a scenario*
- Common data governance weaknesses in supervisory authorities
- Practical steps to strengthen data governance
- Case study: a decision challenged due to poor data governance
Activities:
- Case study: propose data governance improvements for a sample authority

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: governance principles, lineage, applying improvements
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Principles of data governance (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Data lineage and decision defensibility (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Applying data governance (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Good data governance is a precondition for defensible supervisory decisions, not a bureaucratic add-on
- Data lineage lets an authority show its working when a decision is challenged
- Decision-support systems need the same governance rigour as the decisions they inform
