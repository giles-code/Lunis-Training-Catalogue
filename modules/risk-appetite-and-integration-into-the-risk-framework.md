## Aim and Purpose

How climate and nature risk are embedded in the risk management framework, risk appetite statement and capital planning, and what management information the board needs.

## Learning Objectives

- Set climate and nature risk appetite with measurable limits and triggers
- Evaluate how climate risk is integrated across the three lines of defence
- Specify the management information and indicators the board needs

## Syllabus

- Drafting climate risk appetite: sectors, concentrations and limits
- Integration into credit, underwriting, investment and capital processes
- Key risk indicators and the core board information set
- Supervisory expectations for the management of climate-related financial risk
