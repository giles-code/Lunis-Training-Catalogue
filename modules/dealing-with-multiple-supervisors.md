## Aim and Purpose

How home and host supervisors divide responsibilities, share information and coordinate through colleges, and how the group and its local entities manage engagement, consistency and conflicting demands.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain home and host responsibilities and how supervisors share information
- Coordinate consistent, candid engagement across the group's supervisors
- Handle conflicting supervisory demands and joint reviews or college engagement

## Syllabus

- Home and host roles, information-sharing and memoranda of understanding
- Supervisory colleges and joint risk assessment from the firm's side
- One message, many regulators: coordinating notifications and responses
- Exercise: responding to conflicting requests from two supervisors
