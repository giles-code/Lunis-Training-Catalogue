## Aim and Purpose

How conflicts of interest arise in everyday work, how the firm identifies and manages them, and the rules on gifts, hospitality and outside interests.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify actual and potential conflicts of interest
- Apply the firm's gifts, hospitality and outside interests rules
- Record and escalate conflicts appropriately

## Syllabus

- What a conflict of interest is and why it matters
- Conflicts between the firm, its staff and its customers
- Gifts, hospitality, inducements and registers
- Outside business interests and personal relationships
