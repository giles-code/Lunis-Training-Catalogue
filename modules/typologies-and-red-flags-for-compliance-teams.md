## Aim and Purpose

The principal ways criminals use crypto-asset services, from scams and ransomware to mule networks and sanctions evasion, and the customer and transaction red flags front-line and compliance staff should recognise.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main predicate offences and typologies involving crypto-assets
- Recognise red flags in customer profiles, behaviour and transaction patterns
- Translate typologies into monitoring scenarios and staff guidance

## Syllabus

- Investment and romance scams, money mules and victims as unwitting launderers
- Ransomware, darknet markets, hacks and cyber-enabled crime
- Sanctions evasion, terrorist financing and proliferation financing
- From red-flag indicators to scenarios, guidance and training
