## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in using a firm's own testing metrics and internal audit findings as core supervisory data sources for assessing cyber resilience.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the types of cyber testing firms typically conduct
- Describe how internal audit findings inform cyber resilience assessment
- Assess the quality and coverage of a firm's testing programme
- Evaluate whether testing findings are being adequately remediated
- Formulate a supervisory assessment based on testing and audit data

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Types of cyber testing (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain cyber testing types*
- Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, red-team exercises
- The role of internal audit in cyber assurance
- Why testing data is a rich, underused supervisory data source
- Limitations of testing: coverage gaps, timing, scope constraints
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: use of testing data in participants' own supervisory work

**Session 2: Assessing testing quality and coverage (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess testing programme quality and coverage*
- Criteria for assessing whether a testing programme is comprehensive
- Common coverage gaps in testing programmes
- Assessing the independence and rigour of testing
- Comparing testing scope against the firm's actual risk profile
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample testing programme for quality and coverage

**Session 3: Remediation tracking and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate remediation and formulate an assessment*
- Assessing whether identified findings are remediated in a timely way
- Red flags: repeat findings, slow remediation, downgraded severity
- Case study: testing and audit data revealing a resilience gap
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment based on sample testing and audit data

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: testing types, coverage assessment, remediation tracking
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Types of cyber testing (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing testing quality and coverage (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Remediation tracking and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- A firm's own testing data is one of the richest sources of supervisory insight into cyber resilience
- Repeat findings across testing cycles are a strong signal of inadequate remediation culture
- Testing coverage should be assessed against the firm's actual risk profile, not accepted at face value
