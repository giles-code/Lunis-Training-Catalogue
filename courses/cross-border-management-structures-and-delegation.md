## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how cross-border management structures and delegation arrangements operate, and how supervisors assess substance and guard against 'letter-box' entity risk.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment management supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain common cross-border management company structures
- Describe substance requirements for management companies
- Assess whether a management company has sufficient local substance
- Identify indicators of 'letter-box' entity risk
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a cross-border management structure

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Cross-border management structures (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain cross-border management company structures*
- Why fund managers use cross-border management company structures
- Management company passporting
- Typical delegation arrangements within these structures
- Regulatory concerns about substance and letter-box entities
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: cross-border structures participants have reviewed

**Session 2: Substance requirements (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe and assess substance requirements*
- What 'substance' means in a management company context
- Staffing, decision-making authority, and local presence expectations
- Assessing substance in practice: what to look for
- Regulatory guidance on minimum substance expectations
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample management company's substance against regulatory expectations

**Session 3: Letter-box risk and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify letter-box risk and formulate an assessment*
- Indicators of letter-box entity risk
- Consequences of insufficient substance for investor protection and supervision
- Case study: a management company with substance concerns
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a cross-border management structure

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: cross-border structures, substance, letter-box risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Cross-border management structures (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Substance requirements (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Letter-box risk and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Substance requirements exist to ensure real decision-making capability sits where regulatory responsibility sits
- Letter-box entity risk undermines both investor protection and effective supervision
- Assessing substance requires looking past organisational charts to actual staffing and decision-making authority
