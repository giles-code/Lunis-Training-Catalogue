## Aim and Purpose

How two major North Asian distribution markets regulate domestic funds, their managers and the offering of offshore funds, including local agent and presence requirements.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the regulators, legislation and fund vehicles in Taiwan and South Korea
- Explain the requirements for registering offshore funds and appointing local agents
- Compare public and private fund regimes and recent reforms in both markets

## Syllabus

- Taiwan: the Financial Supervisory Commission, securities investment trust enterprises and domestic funds
- Taiwan: offshore fund approval, master agents and local presence incentives
- South Korea: the FSC and FSS, the Capital Markets Act, investment trusts and foreign fund registration
- Recent developments: Korean private fund reform and regional asset management hub ambitions
