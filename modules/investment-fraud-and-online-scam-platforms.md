## Aim and Purpose

How fake investment platforms, crypto-asset scams and boiler rooms operate, how they move victim funds, and how investigators identify the people and infrastructure behind them.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the features of investment fraud and fake trading platforms
- Trace victim funds through payment and crypto-asset channels
- Identify the infrastructure and actors behind scam operations

## Syllabus

- Boiler rooms, clone firms and fake trading platforms
- Crypto-asset and relationship investment scams
- Payment routes, intermediary accounts and off-ramps
- Websites, domains, advertising and scam infrastructure
