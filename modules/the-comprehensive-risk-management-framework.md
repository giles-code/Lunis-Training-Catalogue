## Aim and Purpose

How an infrastructure builds an integrated framework covering credit, liquidity, operational, business, custody and investment risk, and the role of the second and third lines in testing that it works.

## Learning Objectives

- Design an integrated risk framework, appetite and policies across all risk types
- Organise the risk, compliance and internal audit functions for an infrastructure
- Manage general business, custody and investment risk and the capital to cover them

## Syllabus

- Risk appetite, policies and model governance
- Independent risk, compliance and internal audit functions
- General business risk, custody and investment risk
- Model validation and independent review
