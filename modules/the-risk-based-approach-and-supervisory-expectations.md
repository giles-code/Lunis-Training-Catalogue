## Aim and Purpose

The foundations of an effective financial crime framework: FATF standards, the risk-based approach, the MLRO's role and how supervisors now judge effectiveness rather than technical compliance alone.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain FATF standards and the risk-based approach as they apply to the firm
- Define the accountabilities of the board, senior management and MLRO
- Distinguish technical compliance from effectiveness in supervisory assessment

## Syllabus

- FATF Recommendations, immediate outcomes and national risk assessments
- Governance, MLRO independence, resourcing and accountability
- How supervisors assess financial crime frameworks
- Seeing the firm's controls the way a supervisor does
