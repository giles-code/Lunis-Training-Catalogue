## Aim and Purpose

How safeguarding protects user funds under PSD2 and EMD2, the changes proposed in the reform, and how supervisors and firms test safeguarding, reconciliation and wind-down readiness.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply segregation, insurance and guarantee safeguarding methods correctly
- Assess reconciliation, concentration risk and safeguarding audits
- Evaluate wind-down plans and the return of funds on failure

## Syllabus

- Relevant funds, timing, safeguarding accounts and secure low-risk assets
- Reform changes: concentration risk and safeguarding with central banks
- Daily reconciliation, shortfalls and safeguarding audits
- Case study: safeguarding failures revealed on insolvency
