## Aim and Purpose

How firms should govern the design, deployment and use of AI, the ethical principles governance must address, and how supervisors assess a firm's governance maturity.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core components of an effective AI governance framework
- Assess how accountability for AI-driven decisions is allocated within a firm
- Evaluate a firm's AI governance against supervisory expectations

## Syllabus

- Board and senior management accountability for AI systems
- AI policies, risk appetite statements and model inventories
- Ethical principles in practice: fairness, transparency and human oversight
- The three lines of defence and model validation applied to AI
