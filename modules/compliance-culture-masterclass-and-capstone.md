## Aim and Purpose

Brings the series together by examining how compliance culture shows itself in behaviour, incentives and tone from the top, and applies the full series to an integrated firm case study.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess indicators of a firm's compliance culture
- Integrate findings across compliance areas into an overall view
- Formulate proportionate supervisory responses and follow-up

## Syllabus

- Tone from the top and the middle: culture in practice
- Incentives, speaking up and breach reporting as cultural indicators
- From individual weaknesses to an overall compliance risk assessment
- Workshop: integrated firm case study and supervisory action plan
