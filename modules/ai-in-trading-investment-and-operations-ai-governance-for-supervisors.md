## Aim and Purpose

The use of AI in trading, investment management and back-office processing, and the supervisory risks that arise when AI systems act on markets and infrastructure at speed and scale.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI is used in trading, portfolio construction and operations
- Analyse the market integrity risks of AI-driven trading
- Evaluate controls over AI systems operating with limited human intervention

## Syllabus

- AI in algorithmic and high-frequency trading strategies
- Portfolio construction, signal generation and AI-driven risk tools
- Correlated behaviour, flash events and unintended algorithmic collusion
- Kill-switches, human oversight and escalation for autonomous systems
