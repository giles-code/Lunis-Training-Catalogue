## Aim and Purpose

To give internal auditors a practical grounding in auditing the risks that matter most to supervisors, from regulatory compliance and conduct to financial crime, AI and operational resilience. Participants leave able to plan risk-based coverage, test controls and outcomes rigorously, and report in a way that drives remediation and stands up to supervisory scrutiny.

## What It Covers

The role and independence of internal audit, risk-based audit planning, auditing regulatory compliance, conduct and culture, financial crime controls, models and AI, operational resilience and third parties, reporting to the audit committee, follow-up and working with supervisors.

## Intended Audience

- Heads of internal audit, audit managers and auditors in banks, insurers, investment firms, fund managers and payment firms
- Co-sourced and outsourced audit providers delivering assurance to regulated firms
- Audit committee members and compliance and risk staff who rely on internal audit work

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role, independence and standards of internal audit in a regulated firm
- Design a risk-based audit plan aligned with the firm's regulatory exposure
- Apply audit techniques to compliance, conduct, culture and financial crime controls
- Assess models, AI, operational resilience and third-party arrangements
- Report findings to the audit committee and track remediation to closure
- Engage constructively with supervisors and see the firm the way a supervisor does

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Modules combine short briefings with case studies, audit planning exercises and report-writing practice, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's sector and audit plan.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of internal audit, risk or compliance in a regulated firm is helpful. No specialist technical background is required.

## Core Reading

- Institute of Internal Auditors, Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)
