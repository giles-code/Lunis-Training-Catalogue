## Aim and Purpose

The components of the fit and proper test, which roles fall within it, and why the authority expects the firm, not the regulator, to be the first and most rigorous assessor.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain honesty, integrity, competence, capability and financial soundness as applied by authorities
- Identify the roles that require pre-approval, certification or notification
- Explain the firm's primary responsibility and the consequences of weak assessments

## Syllabus

- Components of the test and international standards from IOSCO, the Basel Committee and FATF
- Pre-approved, certified and notified roles under accountability regimes
- Proportionality by firm size, role and business model
- Regulatory findings and enforcement cases on poor appointment practice
