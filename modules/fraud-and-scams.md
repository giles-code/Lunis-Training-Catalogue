## Aim and Purpose

The main types of fraud affecting financial firms and their customers, how fraud links to money laundering and what firms should do to prevent and detect it.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main internal and external fraud types
- Explain how authorised push payment fraud and investment scams operate
- Assess firms' fraud prevention and detection arrangements

## Syllabus

- Internal fraud, insider threat and misconduct
- Investment scams, impersonation and authorised push payment fraud
- Money mules and the link between fraud and laundering
- Prevention, detection and customer protection measures
