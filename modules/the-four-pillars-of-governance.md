## Aim and Purpose

What supervisors expect from the board, senior management, the risk architecture and internal audit, and how these pillars work together, including over outsourced activities.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm against each governance pillar
- Identify gaps between documented and actual governance
- Evidence oversight of outsourced and technology services

## Syllabus

- The board: composition, skills, committees and oversight
- Senior management and effective delegation
- Risk architecture: strategy, risk appetite, risk function and three lines
- Internal audit, outsourcing and what the board still owns
