## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how payment fraud, including authorised push payment scams, is regulated and prevented, and the supervisory considerations around liability allocation and fraud reporting.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in payment services supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the distinguishing features of authorised push payment fraud
- Describe liability allocation frameworks for APP scam losses
- Assess a firm's fraud prevention controls for effectiveness
- Evaluate fraud reporting data as a supervisory tool
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's APP fraud prevention framework

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Understanding APP fraud (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain APP fraud characteristics*
- What distinguishes authorised push payment fraud from unauthorised fraud
- Common APP fraud scenarios: impersonation, invoice fraud, romance scams
- Why APP fraud has grown alongside faster payment methods
- The psychological techniques used in APP scams
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: APP fraud trends participants have observed

**Session 2: Liability allocation and prevention controls (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe liability frameworks and assess prevention controls*
- Liability allocation frameworks for APP scam losses between sending and receiving firms
- Confirmation of payee and other preventive control mechanisms
- Assessing whether a firm's warnings and friction points are effective without being excessive
- International approaches to APP fraud liability
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's APP fraud prevention controls

**Session 3: Fraud reporting and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate fraud reporting data and formulate an assessment*
- Using fraud reporting data as a supervisory early-warning tool
- Assessing data quality and consistency in fraud reporting
- Case study: a firm's APP fraud framework under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's APP fraud prevention framework

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: APP fraud characteristics, liability allocation, prevention and reporting
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Understanding APP fraud (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Liability allocation and prevention controls (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Fraud reporting and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- APP fraud exploits authorised payment authorisation itself, making it structurally different from traditional unauthorised fraud
- Liability allocation frameworks shape firms' incentives to invest in prevention on both the sending and receiving side
- Fraud reporting data is a valuable early-warning tool, but only if reporting is consistent and complete across the market
