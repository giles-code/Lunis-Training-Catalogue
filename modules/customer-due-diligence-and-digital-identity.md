## Aim and Purpose

Proportionate customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence in a digital onboarding world, including beneficial ownership, politically exposed persons and the reliability of remote identity verification.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply risk-based CDD, EDD and ongoing monitoring
- Evaluate digital identity verification and its vulnerabilities
- Identify beneficial ownership and politically exposed person risks

## Syllabus

- CDD, simplified and enhanced due diligence and periodic review
- Beneficial ownership, complex structures and PEP screening
- Remote onboarding, biometrics and FATF digital identity guidance
- Perpetual KYC and event-driven review
