## Aim and Purpose

How expanding board responsibilities collide with fixed meeting time, and how to build an agenda that gives the most important matters the attention they need.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the signs of agenda overload and its effect on scrutiny
- Apply practical rules for prioritising and sequencing board business
- Evaluate the trade-offs in longer, more frequent or larger board meetings

## Syllabus

- Agenda overload: routine reporting crowding out strategy
- Sequencing, timing and protecting time for unscheduled discussion
- Consent agendas, committees and deciding what not to discuss
- Directors' time commitment and the risk of over-boarding
