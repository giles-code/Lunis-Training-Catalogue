## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how a supervisory authority makes rules, covering the legal hierarchy between primary legislation, secondary rules, and guidance, and the core principles of clear, enforceable regulatory drafting.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level policy officers drafting or contributing to rules, guidance, and consultation papers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the legal hierarchy of primary legislation, secondary rules and guidance
- Describe the sources of rule-making power for a supervisory authority
- Apply principles of clear, enforceable regulatory drafting
- Distinguish between binding rules and non-binding guidance
- Identify common drafting pitfalls that create ambiguity or unintended loopholes
- Evaluate a draft rule against clarity and enforceability criteria

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The legal hierarchy of rule-making (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the legal hierarchy and sources of rule-making power*
- Primary legislation, secondary rules, and guidance: what each can and cannot do
- Sources of a supervisory authority's rule-making power
- Delegated powers and their limits
- Binding rules versus non-binding guidance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: mapping the rule-making hierarchy in participants' own jurisdictions

**Session 2: Principles of clear drafting (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply drafting principles and identify common pitfalls*
- Principles of clear, enforceable regulatory drafting
- Common drafting pitfalls: ambiguity, loopholes, inconsistent definitions
- Drafting for different audiences: firms, courts, the public
- The role of legal review in the drafting process
Activities:
- Exercise: redraft a poorly worded sample rule for clarity

**Session 3: Evaluating draft rules (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate a draft rule against clarity and enforceability criteria*
- Criteria for assessing draft rules
- Testing rules against real-world scenarios before finalisation
- Case study: a rule that caused unintended consequences due to poor drafting
Activities:
- Case study: evaluate a draft rule and propose improvements

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: hierarchy, drafting principles, evaluation
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The legal hierarchy of rule-making (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Principles of clear drafting (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Evaluating draft rules (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Understanding the legal hierarchy prevents rules from being drafted with the wrong legal force
- Clear drafting is a skill that directly determines how enforceable a rule will be
- Testing rules against real-world scenarios before finalisation catches problems that desk review misses
