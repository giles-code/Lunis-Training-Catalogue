## Aim and Purpose

How to plan and conduct effective interviews with directors and senior managers, use questioning techniques well, evaluate what is said and turn interview evidence into supervisory conclusions.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan an interview with a clear objective, topics, probes and evidence requests
- Use open, probing, evidence-testing and closed questions to obtain reliable information
- Evaluate the credibility and consistency of answers and manage evasive or defensive interviewees

## Syllabus

- Interview planning: objective, sequence of topics and the interviewer's mindset
- Question types: open, walk-through, probe, evidence and closed questions
- Recognising evasion, using silence and handling difficult interviewees
- Keeping a reliable interview record and linking evidence to supervisory action
