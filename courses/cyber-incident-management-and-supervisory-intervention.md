## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in assessing a firm's cyber incident recovery procedures and disclosure protocols, and understanding the supervisory intervention tools available when incidents occur.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the phases of cyber incident response
- Describe disclosure protocols and obligations following a cyber incident
- Assess the adequacy of a firm's incident recovery procedures
- Evaluate the supervisory intervention tools available during an incident
- Formulate a supervisory response to a simulated cyber incident

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Incident response phases (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain incident response phases*
- Phases of incident response: detection, containment, eradication, recovery
- The role of an incident response plan and playbooks
- Disclosure obligations to supervisors and, where relevant, the public
- Timing considerations in incident disclosure
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: incident response experiences from participants' own sector

**Session 2: Assessing recovery procedures (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess the adequacy of recovery procedures*
- Criteria for assessing incident recovery procedure adequacy
- Testing recovery procedures through exercises and simulations
- Common weaknesses in recovery planning
- Recovery time objectives and their supervisory relevance
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's incident recovery procedures

**Session 3: Supervisory intervention and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate intervention tools and formulate a response*
- Supervisory intervention tools available during a live incident
- Balancing intervention against not impeding a firm's own recovery efforts
- Case study: a simulated cyber incident requiring supervisory response
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory response to a simulated cyber incident

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: incident response phases, recovery assessment, intervention tools
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Incident response phases (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing recovery procedures (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Supervisory intervention and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Disclosure timing during a cyber incident is a genuine judgement call balancing transparency against operational stability
- Recovery procedures should be tested through exercises, not just documented on paper
- Supervisory intervention during a live incident must support, not obstruct, the firm's own recovery efforts
