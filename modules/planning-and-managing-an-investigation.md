## Aim and Purpose

How to scope and plan an investigation, resource it with the right skills, and manage a caseload so that cases progress without drift or backlog.

## Learning Objectives

- Scope an investigation with clear hypotheses and lines of enquiry
- Plan resources, timelines and use of external support
- Manage a growing or backlogged caseload

## Syllabus

- Investigation plans: scope, hypotheses, lines of enquiry and milestones
- Skills, time and external support: forensic accountants and experts
- Case governance, progress review and decisions to discontinue
- Managing caseload pressure and resource constraints
