## Aim and Purpose

The typology of crypto-assets and the structure of crypto markets, following the money to understand how value is created, transferred and traded across centralised and decentralised platforms.

## Learning Objectives

- Classify the main types of crypto-asset
- Analyse market structures, incentives and trading dynamics
- Evaluate risks associated with different market participants

## Syllabus

- Cryptocurrencies, utility, governance and security tokens
- Exchanges, brokers, market makers and institutional participants
- Centralised exchanges versus decentralised trading platforms
- Liquidity, price formation and market fragmentation risks
