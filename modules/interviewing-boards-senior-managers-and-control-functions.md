## Aim and Purpose

How to tailor interviews to chairs, non-executives, chief executives and heads of risk, compliance and internal audit, and what each interview should reveal about governance and control.

## Learning Objectives

- Tailor questioning to the role and accountabilities of the interviewee
- Assess board challenge, executive accountability and control function independence
- Identify governance red flags from interview evidence

## Syllabus

- Interviewing chairs and non-executive directors on challenge and oversight
- Interviewing executives on strategy, risk and accountability
- Interviewing heads of risk, compliance and internal audit on independence and resources
- Governance red flags: dominant individuals, weak challenge and information gaps
