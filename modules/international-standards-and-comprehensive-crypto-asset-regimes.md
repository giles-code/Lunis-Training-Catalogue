## Aim and Purpose

The international standards for crypto-assets and how comprehensive regimes translate them into perimeter, authorisation, conduct, prudential and disclosure requirements, including where gaps and ambiguities create supervisory risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the objectives and content of the main international crypto-asset standards
- Analyse how comprehensive regimes classify crypto-assets, issuers and service providers
- Evaluate gaps between formal compliance documentation and actual firm behaviour

## Syllabus

- FSB high-level recommendations and global stablecoin recommendations
- IOSCO crypto and DeFi policy recommendations; FATF Recommendation 15
- Basel Committee standard on banks' crypto-asset exposures
- Regime architecture: primary law, technical standards, guidance and white papers
