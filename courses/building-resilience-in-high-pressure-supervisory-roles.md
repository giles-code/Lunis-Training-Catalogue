## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' practical techniques for managing the unique pressures of supervisory work, including high-profile casework, public scrutiny, and sustained workload, to support long-term resilience and wellbeing.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisory staff developing core professional judgement skills.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the specific pressures faced in high-profile supervisory roles
- Identify personal early warning signs of stress and burnout
- Apply practical resilience-building techniques
- Evaluate workload and support structures for sustainability
- Formulate a personal resilience plan

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The pressures of supervisory work (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain specific supervisory pressures*
- High-profile casework and public scrutiny as distinct stressors
- Sustained workload and its cumulative effect
- Why supervisory roles carry unique psychological demands
- Organisational versus individual responsibility for resilience
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: pressures participants have experienced in their roles

**Session 2: Recognising warning signs (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Identify personal warning signs of stress*
- Early warning signs of stress and burnout
- Distinguishing normal pressure from unsustainable strain
- The role of peer and management support in early recognition
- When and how to seek support
Activities:
- Individual reflection exercise: personal warning signs and triggers

**Session 3: Resilience techniques and planning (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Apply resilience techniques and formulate a plan*
- Practical resilience-building techniques for high-pressure roles
- Evaluating workload and support structures
- Building a sustainable personal resilience plan
Activities:
- Exercise: draft a personal resilience plan

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: pressures, warning signs, resilience techniques
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The pressures of supervisory work (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Recognising warning signs (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Resilience techniques and planning (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Supervisory work carries distinct pressures that generic resilience training does not always address
- Recognising personal early warning signs allows action before strain becomes unsustainable
- Resilience is supported by organisational structures as well as individual technique
