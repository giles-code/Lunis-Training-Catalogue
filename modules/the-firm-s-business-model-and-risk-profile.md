## Aim and Purpose

How to understand quickly what the firm does, how it makes money, where its key risks sit and how a supervisor would view its business model.

## Learning Objectives

- Analyse how the firm generates revenue and where its key dependencies lie
- Identify the firm's principal risks and how they connect to its strategy
- Assess the business model through a supervisor's eyes

## Syllabus

- Business model, value chain and key dependencies
- Principal risks and the risk management framework
- Risk appetite: what the board has agreed and why
- How supervisors assess business model viability and sustainability
