## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in planning and resourcing a supervisory workforce with the right mix of generalist and specialist skills, addressing recruitment and retention challenges specific to regulatory bodies.

## Intended Audience

Managers and learning & development leads responsible for building supervisory capability within the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the workforce planning challenges specific to regulatory authorities
- Apply a skills-gap analysis methodology
- Assess build-versus-buy options for specialist capability
- Evaluate recruitment and retention strategies for public-sector regulators
- Formulate a workforce plan for a sample specialist function

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Workforce planning challenges (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain regulatory workforce planning challenges*
- Why regulatory authorities face distinct workforce challenges
- Competing with industry for specialist skills
- Public-sector pay and career structure constraints
- The cost of skills gaps in specialist supervisory functions
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: workforce challenges participants' own authorities face

**Session 2: Skills-gap analysis and build-versus-buy (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply skills-gap analysis and assess build-versus-buy options*
- Methodology for conducting a skills-gap analysis
- Build (train existing staff) versus buy (recruit externally) trade-offs
- Using secondments and partnerships to plug specialist gaps
- Forecasting future skills needs as risks evolve
Activities:
- Group exercise: conduct a skills-gap analysis for a sample specialist function

**Session 3: Recruitment, retention and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate recruitment/retention strategies and formulate a plan*
- Recruitment strategies suited to public-sector regulators
- Retention levers beyond pay: mission, development, flexibility
- Case study: building a specialist function from a skills gap
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a workforce plan for a sample specialist regulatory function

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: workforce challenges, skills-gap analysis, recruitment and retention
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Workforce planning challenges (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Skills-gap analysis and build-versus-buy (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Recruitment, retention and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Regulatory authorities face structural disadvantages in the market for specialist technical skills
- Build-versus-buy decisions should be made deliberately, function by function, rather than by default
- Retention often depends more on mission and development opportunities than on matching industry pay
