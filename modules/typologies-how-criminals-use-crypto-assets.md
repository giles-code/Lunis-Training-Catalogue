## Aim and Purpose

The principal ways crypto-assets are used in crime, from fraud and ransomware to sanctions evasion and terrorist financing, with the red flags supervisors and investigators should look for.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main predicate offences and typologies involving crypto-assets
- Recognise red flags in customer behaviour and transaction patterns
- Assess emerging typologies and their implications for national risk assessments

## Syllabus

- Investment and romance scams, fraud and money mule networks
- Ransomware, darknet markets and cyber-enabled crime
- Sanctions evasion, proliferation and terrorist financing
- Red-flag indicators and feeding typologies into risk assessment
