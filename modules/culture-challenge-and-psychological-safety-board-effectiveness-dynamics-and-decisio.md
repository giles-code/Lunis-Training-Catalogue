## Aim and Purpose

How boardroom culture shapes the quality of discussion, why effective challenge depends on psychological safety, and how to tell constructive challenge from confrontation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how unwritten norms and relationships shape board discussion
- Distinguish effective challenge from confrontation and from avoidance
- Identify the will, skill and knowledge each director needs to challenge well

## Syllabus

- Boardroom culture: norms, assumptions and the default to collegiality
- Psychological safety and the signs of its absence
- Effective challenge: evidence-based, focused and persistent
- Oversight without micromanagement: where the board's role ends
