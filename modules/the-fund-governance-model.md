## Aim and Purpose

How investment funds and fund management companies are structured and governed, where the board sits among the parties involved, and what fund product and fund manager rules expect of directors.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main fund structures and the parties involved in running a fund
- Define the duties of fund and management company directors
- Distinguish the board's responsibilities from those of its delegates

## Syllabus

- Fund structures: corporate, contractual and partnership vehicles
- Self-managed funds, management companies and external fund managers
- Directors' duties, independence and acting in investors' best interests
- International standards, including the IOSCO principles for collective investment schemes
