## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining how private market products are reaching retail and wealth investors, the investor protection risks this creates, and the authority's supervisory response.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess semi-liquid and long-term vehicles offered to retail investors
- Evaluate distribution, suitability and disclosure for private market products
- Produce a supervisory plan for managers and private markets

## Syllabus

- Evergreen and semi-liquid funds, feeder structures and tokenised access
- Distribution through wealth managers, platforms and retirement savings
- Disclosure of fees, liquidity limits and valuation uncertainty
- Workshop: a supervisory plan for private markets retailisation
