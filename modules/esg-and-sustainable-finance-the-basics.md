## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in ESG, sustainable finance and the key terms staff will hear from clients, colleagues and the media.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain environmental, social and governance factors with practical examples
- Distinguish ESG integration, sustainable investment and impact investment
- Use core sustainability terminology accurately

## Syllabus

- What ESG means and how it developed
- ESG integration, exclusions, best-in-class, thematic and impact approaches
- Net zero, transition, nature and social factors explained
- ESG ratings and data: uses and limitations
