## Aim and Purpose

Using stewardship, engagement and voting to manage ESG risks and deliver sustainability commitments, and evidencing the outcomes achieved.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a stewardship policy aligned with the firm's sustainability commitments
- Apply engagement, escalation and voting strategies
- Evidence stewardship outcomes credibly

## Syllabus

- Stewardship principles and policy design
- Engagement priorities, escalation and collaborative engagement
- Proxy voting and voting disclosure
- Reporting stewardship activities and outcomes
