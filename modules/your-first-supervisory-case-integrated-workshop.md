## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a facilitated case study in which participants assess a firm, form a view of its risks and propose a proportionate supervisory response.

## Learning Objectives

- Gather and interpret information on a firm's business and risks
- Form an initial risk assessment and identify key concerns
- Propose and justify a proportionate supervisory response

## Syllabus

- Case briefing: the firm, its business and the information available
- Identifying red flags and framing supervisory questions
- Agreeing a risk view and proposed actions in small groups
- Workshop: presenting the case to a mock supervisory panel
