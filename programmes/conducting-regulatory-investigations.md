## Aim and Purpose

To give investigators and case teams a practical, step-by-step method for conducting regulatory investigations that are focused, fair and capable of withstanding challenge. Participants leave able to scope and plan a case, gather and review evidence, conduct compelled interviews, prepare findings and warning notices, and feed lessons back into supervision.

## What It Covers

Case selection and referral, scoping and investigation planning, information-gathering powers, evidence handling and document review, interviews under compulsion, findings and warning notices, settlement and decision-making, and lessons for supervision.

## Intended Audience

- Enforcement investigators, case managers and lawyers
- Supervisory staff seconded to or working alongside enforcement teams
- Staff moving into enforcement from supervision, authorisation or other agencies

## Learning Objectives

- Apply case selection criteria and assess referrals consistently
- Scope and plan an investigation with clear hypotheses, milestones and resources
- Use information-gathering powers lawfully, proportionately and effectively
- Handle, review and analyse evidence to an evidential standard
- Plan and conduct compelled interviews that are fair and productive
- Prepare robust findings, manage settlement and feed lessons back into supervision

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a four-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. The modules follow the life of a single case, combining short briefings with a running investigation exercise, interview role-play and a mock case conference, and can be selected or tailored to the authority's powers and procedures.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of enforcement, supervision or legal casework is helpful. No prior investigative training is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO (2015), Credible Deterrence in the Enforcement of Securities Regulation
- IOSCO (2017), Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation
- IOSCO (2016), Enhanced Multilateral Memorandum of Understanding Concerning Consultation and Cooperation and the Exchange of Information
