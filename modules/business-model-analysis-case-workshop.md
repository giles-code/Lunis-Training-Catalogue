## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a facilitated case in which participants analyse a firm's business model and financial statements and present their supervisory concerns.

## Learning Objectives

- Analyse a firm's business model, financial position and key risks
- Prioritise concerns and draft supervisory questions for management
- Present and defend an initial view of the firm's viability

## Syllabus

- Case briefing: firm profile, accounts and regulatory returns
- Ratio analysis, peer comparison and identifying outliers
- Drafting questions for a meeting with the finance director
- Workshop: presenting the business model assessment
