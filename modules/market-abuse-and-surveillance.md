## Aim and Purpose

The forms of market abuse, how inside information is controlled, and how to build surveillance that detects and reports suspicious orders and transactions.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise insider dealing, unlawful disclosure and manipulation typologies
- Control inside information through insider lists and personal account dealing
- Design and calibrate trade and communications surveillance

## Syllabus

- Inside information, insider lists, wall-crossing and personal account dealing
- Manipulation typologies: spoofing, layering, ramping and cross-market abuse
- Surveillance calibration, alert handling and communications monitoring
- Suspicious order and transaction reports: quality and timeliness
