## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how the EU AI Act's requirements interact with existing sectoral financial regulation, including where AI Act high-risk classifications apply to financial services AI systems.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in AI-related supervision and internal AI use.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the risk-based structure of the EU AI Act
- Describe which financial services AI use cases fall into the high-risk category
- Assess the interplay between AI Act obligations and existing financial sector regulation
- Evaluate potential overlaps or gaps between the two regulatory frameworks
- Formulate a supervisory approach to AI Act compliance in a financial services context

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The AI Act's risk-based structure (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the AI Act's structure*
- The EU AI Act's risk tiers: prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, minimal-risk
- Obligations attached to high-risk AI systems
- Governance and conformity assessment requirements
- Timeline for AI Act implementation
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: AI Act awareness and preparation in participants' own sector

**Session 2: High-risk classification in financial services (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe high-risk use cases and assess interplay with sectoral regulation*
- Which financial services AI use cases are classified as high-risk (e.g. creditworthiness assessment)
- How AI Act obligations interact with existing sectoral regulation (e.g. consumer credit rules)
- Avoiding duplicative or conflicting compliance obligations
- Coordination between AI Act competent authorities and financial supervisors
Activities:
- Group exercise: map a sample financial AI use case against AI Act and sectoral obligations

**Session 3: Overlaps, gaps and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate overlaps/gaps and formulate a supervisory approach*
- Identifying overlaps and gaps between AI Act and financial regulation
- Case study: a financial AI system navigating both regulatory frameworks
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory approach to AI Act compliance for a sample AI system

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: AI Act structure, high-risk classification, interplay with sectoral rules
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The AI Act's risk-based structure (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: High-risk classification in financial services (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Overlaps, gaps and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- The AI Act and existing financial sector regulation apply concurrently, requiring active coordination to avoid gaps or duplication
- Several common financial AI use cases, notably creditworthiness assessment, fall squarely within the AI Act's high-risk category
- Supervisors will need to work with AI Act competent authorities as this framework beds in
