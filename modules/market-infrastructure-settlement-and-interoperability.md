## Aim and Purpose

How tokenised trading, clearing and settlement work, how they interact with existing infrastructure, and the settlement finality and interoperability questions market infrastructures and their users must resolve.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain on-chain trading, clearing and delivery versus payment
- Assess settlement finality, cash leg and collateral mobility issues
- Evaluate interoperability between tokenised and traditional infrastructure

## Syllabus

- Delivery versus payment and atomic settlement on shared ledgers
- The cash leg: central bank money, tokenised deposits or stablecoins
- PFMI principles applied to tokenised arrangements
- Bridges, messaging standards and fragmentation risk
