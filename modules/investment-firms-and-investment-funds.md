## Aim and Purpose

How brokers, asset managers, advisers and funds make money, the role of service providers and depositaries, and the conduct, liquidity and operational risks they carry.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish the business models of brokers, managers, advisers and funds
- Explain the roles of fund managers, depositaries, administrators and distributors
- Identify key risks including client asset, liquidity mismatch and conflict risks

## Syllabus

- Brokerage, execution, asset management and advice revenue models
- Fund structures, fees and the fund service provider chain
- Client money and assets, liquidity mismatch and valuation risk
- Conflicts of interest and the supervisory question set
