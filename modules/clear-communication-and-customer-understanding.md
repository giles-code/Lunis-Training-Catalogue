## Aim and Purpose

How to communicate with customers fairly, clearly and without misleading them, so that they understand what they are buying and can make informed decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the fair, clear and not misleading standard to written, verbal and digital communications
- Recognise misleading framing, omissions and pressure selling
- Check customer understanding at key points in the customer journey

## Syllabus

- Fair, clear and not misleading in practice
- Risk warnings, fees and charges: balance and prominence
- Digital journeys, choice architecture and dark patterns
- Testing and evidencing customer understanding
