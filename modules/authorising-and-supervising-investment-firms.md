## Aim and Purpose

How the authority assesses investment firm applications and supervises firms on an ongoing basis, covering governance, fit and proper requirements, prudential resources and orderly wind-down.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess an investment firm application against authorisation criteria
- Evaluate governance, compliance and risk management arrangements
- Judge whether capital, liquidity and wind-down planning are adequate

## Syllabus

- Authorisation: business model, people, systems and controls
- Governance, fit and proper assessment and the compliance function
- Prudential requirements for investment firms and the risk of harm to clients and markets
- Wind-down planning and orderly exit
