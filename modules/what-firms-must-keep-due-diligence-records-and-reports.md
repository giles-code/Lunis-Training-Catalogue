## Aim and Purpose

The AML/CFT obligations that shape what firms know about their customers, how long records are kept, and how internal alerts and suspicious transaction reports are produced.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence obligations
- Describe record-keeping requirements and retention periods
- Understand how monitoring alerts and suspicious transaction reports are generated

## Syllabus

- Customer due diligence, beneficial ownership and enhanced checks
- Record-keeping: what must be kept and for how long
- Transaction monitoring, alerts and internal investigations
- Suspicious transaction reporting and tipping-off constraints
