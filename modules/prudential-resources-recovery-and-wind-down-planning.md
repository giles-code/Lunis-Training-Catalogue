## Aim and Purpose

Managing capital, liquidity and financial resilience through crypto market cycles, and preparing credible recovery and orderly wind-down plans that protect clients if the business fails.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess own funds and liquidity against the firm's risk profile
- Identify prudential red flags and early warning indicators
- Design recovery and orderly wind-down plans that return client assets

## Syllabus

- Capital requirements, stressed positions and revenue volatility
- Liquidity stress testing and contingency funding
- Red flags: affiliate funding, thin capital and untested projections
- Recovery options, wind-down triggers and client asset return
