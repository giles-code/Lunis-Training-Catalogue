## Aim and Purpose

Why regulatory quality and independence are a competitive asset, how reputation is built and lost, and how government should frame the regulator's role in growth without compromising its independence.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why international firms and investors value a credible regulator
- Assess the risks of competitiveness or growth objectives for regulators
- Define a constructive relationship between promotion bodies and the regulator

## Syllabus

- Regulatory reputation as the foundation of market access
- Growth and competitiveness objectives: design and safeguards
- Separation of promotion and supervision functions
- Case studies of reputational damage from regulatory failure
