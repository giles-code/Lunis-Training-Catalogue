## Aim and Purpose

What money laundering and terrorist financing are, how they work in practice and why financial firms are both vulnerable to them and central to preventing them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the stages of money laundering and the sources of criminal proceeds
- Distinguish terrorist and proliferation financing from money laundering
- Recognise why the financial system is attractive to criminals

## Syllabus

- Placement, layering and integration
- Predicate offences and the scale of illicit finance
- Terrorist financing and proliferation financing compared
- The harm caused and the authority's role in preventing it
