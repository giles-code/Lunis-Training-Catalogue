## Aim and Purpose

How the authority assesses fund and manager applications, the product rules that protect retail investors, and the safekeeping and oversight role of the depositary.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess a fund or manager application against authorisation criteria
- Explain eligible asset, diversification and concentration rules for retail funds
- Evaluate whether the depositary provides effective safekeeping and independent oversight

## Syllabus

- Authorisation of managers and funds: programme of operations, people and resources
- Eligible assets, diversification limits and borrowing restrictions
- Depositary duties: safekeeping, cash monitoring and oversight of the manager
- Red flags in fund applications and depositary arrangements
