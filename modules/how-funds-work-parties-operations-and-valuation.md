## Aim and Purpose

The fund value chain in practice: who manages, safeguards and administers a fund, how assets are valued and units priced, and how delegation and service providers create jobs and risks.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the roles of the fund manager, depositary, administrator and other parties
- Explain how net asset value is calculated and units are priced
- Identify where delegation and outsourcing create operational and oversight risk

## Syllabus

- Fund governance: boards, management companies and investment managers
- Depositaries, custody, administration and transfer agency
- Valuation, net asset value and fees
- Delegation, outsourcing and substance requirements
