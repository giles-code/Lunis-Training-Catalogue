## Aim and Purpose

What fair treatment of customers means day to day, from clear communications to complaints and the needs of vulnerable customers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the principles of fair treatment and good customer outcomes
- Recognise the signs of customer vulnerability and respond appropriately
- Identify and escalate complaints and potential customer harm

## Syllabus

- Fair, clear and not misleading communications
- Suitable products, fair value and avoiding mis-selling
- Vulnerable customers and reasonable adjustments
- Recognising and handling complaints
