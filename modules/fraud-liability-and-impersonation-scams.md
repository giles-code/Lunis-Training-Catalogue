## Aim and Purpose

The shift from unauthorised fraud to authorised push payment scams, the reform's liability rules including impersonation of payment service provider staff, payee verification and fraud data sharing, and how fraud controls are assessed.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish unauthorised transactions, authorised push payment fraud and impersonation fraud
- Apply liability, refund and gross negligence rules under the current and reformed regimes
- Assess transaction monitoring, payee verification and fraud data sharing

## Syllabus

- Unauthorised transactions, gross negligence and refund timelines
- IBAN and name verification under the Instant Payments Regulation and the reform
- Impersonation fraud refunds and cooperation with electronic communications providers
- Fraud monitoring, data sharing, spending limits and customer education
