## Aim and Purpose

An introduction to business model analysis, the main ways financial firms earn revenue and why supervisors need to understand where profits and risks come from.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why business model analysis is central to supervision
- Identify the main revenue drivers of different types of financial firm
- Recognise business models that are unsustainable or depend on excessive risk

## Syllabus

- Interest margin, fees, commissions, spreads and principal risk-taking
- Value chains, distribution and dependence on intermediaries
- Viability and sustainability: the supervisor's two questions
- Signs of a stretched model: rapid growth, outlier returns and concentration
