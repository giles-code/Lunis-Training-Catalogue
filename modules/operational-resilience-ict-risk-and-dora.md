## Aim and Purpose

How DORA applies to CASPs alongside MiCA's organisational requirements, the ICT risks specific to crypto operations, and how supervisors assess resilience and use incident reports as early warning.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain DORA's ICT risk, incident reporting, testing and third-party requirements for CASPs
- Identify crypto-specific ICT risks in key management, cloud and on-chain/off-chain links
- Assess resilience arrangements and respond when standards are not met

## Syllabus

- DORA applied to CASPs and its interaction with MiCA
- Key management, hot and cold wallet segregation and cloud concentration
- Incident classification and reporting as a supervisory early warning tool
- Resilience testing, register of information and third-party exit provisions
