## Aim and Purpose

The main sources of investor harm and systemic risk in funds and asset management, and how international standards and typical fund regulatory regimes are designed to mitigate them.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the key supervisory risks in funds and asset management
- Explain the IOSCO principles and standard-setter work relevant to collective investment schemes
- Distinguish retail fund product rules from regimes for alternative fund managers

## Syllabus

- Risk mapping: conflicts, investment, liquidity, operational and conduct risk
- IOSCO Objectives and Principles and the collective investment scheme standards
- Product-regulated retail funds versus manager-regulated alternative funds
- Workshop: mapping supervisory risk areas to the regulatory schema
