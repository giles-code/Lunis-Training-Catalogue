## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a workshop in which teams design or improve a partnership for their own context, covering model, legal basis, safeguards, priorities and measures.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the programme to design or reform a partnership
- Identify legal and practical obstacles and how to address them
- Produce a partnership plan with priorities and measures

## Syllabus

- Assessing the current information-sharing landscape
- Choosing the model, members and priority threats
- Legal, data protection and governance checklist
- Workshop: the partnership design and twelve-month plan
