## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by redesigning everyday supervisory tasks around AI, measuring the benefit, sharing good practice and agreeing each participant's practical action plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Redesign recurring supervisory tasks as AI-assisted workflows
- Measure time saved, quality and risk in AI-assisted work
- Produce a personal and team plan for productive, safe AI use

## Syllabus

- Mapping recurring tasks and choosing the right tool for each step
- Reusable templates, prompt libraries and team guidance
- Feedback to the authority's AI governance and SupTech teams
- Workshop: personal and team AI action plans
