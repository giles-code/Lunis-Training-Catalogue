## Aim and Purpose

How open-ended funds manage the mismatch between dealing frequency and asset liquidity, how stress testing and liquidity management tools work, and what happens when a fund suspends dealing.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain liquidity mismatch and the FSB and IOSCO recommendations on fund liquidity
- Assess liquidity stress testing, redemption assumptions and liquidity management tools
- Evaluate a manager's handling of a suspension or gating event

## Syllabus

- Liquidity mismatch, first-mover advantage and dilution of remaining investors
- Liquidity stress testing: redemption modelling and asset liquidity assumptions
- Liquidity management tools: swing pricing, anti-dilution levies, notice periods and gates
- Case study: an open-ended fund suspension and lessons for supervisors
