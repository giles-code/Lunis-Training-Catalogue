## Aim and Purpose

How boards decide where AI should and should not be used, test management's business cases, and set a risk appetite that balances innovation against conduct, prudential and operational risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Challenge management's AI strategy and claimed benefits
- Set an AI risk appetite with measurable limits and escalation triggers
- Weigh innovation against conduct, prudential and operational risk

## Syllabus

- Questions to ask of an AI strategy: purpose, benefit, cost, dependency and exit
- Drafting a risk appetite statement: in-scope uses, prohibited uses and limits
- Avoiding hype and paralysis: pilots and staged adoption
- Investment, capability and talent decisions for board approval
