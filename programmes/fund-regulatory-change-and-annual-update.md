## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a concise annual briefing on the regulatory developments that matter for funds and their managers, together with a practical framework for overseeing regulatory change and regulatory risk. Participants leave able to challenge management's change programme, prioritise board attention and evidence timely, proportionate implementation to supervisors.

## What It Covers

Legal and regulatory touch points for funds and managers, regulatory change and regulatory risk at board level, the year's international regulatory agenda, operational resilience, tokenisation and AI in funds, sustainability and disclosure developments, and the board's regulatory change plan.

## Intended Audience

- Independent and non-executive directors of investment funds and fund management companies
- Executive directors, conducting officers and designated persons responsible for regulatory compliance
- Heads of compliance, legal and risk, and company secretaries supporting fund boards

## Learning Objectives

- Map the legal and regulatory touch points that apply to a fund, its manager and its service providers
- Explain the board's role in overseeing regulatory change and managing regulatory risk
- Identify the year's main international regulatory developments affecting funds and managers
- Assess the implications of operational resilience, tokenisation and AI developments for the fund range
- Evaluate sustainability and disclosure developments and the risk of misleading claims
- Design a board regulatory change plan with clear priorities, owners and assurance

## Structure and Format

Six modules of about ninety minutes each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a one-day annual update or as a series of short board briefing sessions. Content is refreshed every year, each module combines a briefing with board questions, and modules can be selected or tailored to the jurisdictions and fund types the board oversees.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Experience as a fund director or in fund management, compliance or legal roles is helpful. No legal qualification is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation (2017)
- FSB, Revised Policy Recommendations to Address Structural Vulnerabilities from Liquidity Mismatch in Open-Ended Funds (2023)
- FSB, The Financial Stability Implications of Tokenisation (2024)
- IOSCO, Recommendations on Sustainability-Related Practices, Policies, Procedures and Disclosure in Asset Management (2021)
