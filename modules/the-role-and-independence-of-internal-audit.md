## Aim and Purpose

What internal audit is for in a regulated firm, how it sits within the three lines of defence, and the structural safeguards that keep it independent and credible with supervisors.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of internal audit and its place in the three lines of defence
- Assess the independence, reporting lines and resourcing of the function
- Identify what supervisors expect of internal audit and why it matters to them

## Syllabus

- Internal audit's mandate, charter and unrestricted access
- Independence, objectivity and the head of audit's reporting lines
- Professional standards, quality assurance and external assessment
- How supervisors use internal audit work and judge its quality
