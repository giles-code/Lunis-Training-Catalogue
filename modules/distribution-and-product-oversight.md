## Aim and Purpose

The distribution life cycle from the investor's and the product's perspective, and how the board oversees who is buying the fund, what they are buying and how it is sold.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the board's role in distribution strategy and distributor oversight
- Apply product governance questions before launch, during the fund's life and at closure
- Assess distribution data, marketing materials and value for investors

## Syllabus

- Distribution models, platforms and bipartite versus tripartite agreements
- Target market, stress testing and product reviews
- Distribution data: who is buying, through which channel and why
- Marketing communications and value for investors
