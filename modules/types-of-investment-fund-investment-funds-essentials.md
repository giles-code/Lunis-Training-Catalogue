## Aim and Purpose

The main families of fund, from traditional retail funds and money market funds to exchange traded funds, hedge funds, private equity and real estate funds.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish traditional, alternative and exchange traded funds
- Explain how open-ended and closed-ended funds differ
- Describe the investors, assets and fees typical of each fund type

## Syllabus

- Equity, fixed income, multi-asset and money market funds
- Exchange traded funds and listed investment companies
- Hedge funds, private equity, venture capital and real estate funds
- Open-ended versus closed-ended funds, gates, lock-ups and side pockets
