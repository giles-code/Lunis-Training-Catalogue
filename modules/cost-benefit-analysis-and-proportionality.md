## Aim and Purpose

Practical techniques for assessing the costs and benefits of proposed rules, testing proportionality across firms of different sizes, and anticipating unintended consequences before rules are finalised.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply cost-benefit techniques to a proposed rule
- Assess proportionality and distributional effects across firm sizes
- Identify unintended consequences and market reactions in advance

## Syllabus

- Impact assessment frameworks and data sources
- Quantifying compliance costs and valuing hard-to-measure benefits
- Proportionality: is the intervention justified by the risk?
- Case study: a rule with significant unintended consequences
