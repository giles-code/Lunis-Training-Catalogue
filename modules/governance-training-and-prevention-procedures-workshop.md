## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining governance, training and monitoring of tax crime controls, ending with a workshop in which teams test and improve the firm's prevention procedures.

## Learning Objectives

- Specify management information, monitoring and testing for tax crime controls
- Design targeted training for staff and associated persons
- Produce a gap analysis and action plan for the firm's prevention procedures

## Syllabus

- Senior management ownership and board reporting
- Training, attestations and contractual terms for associated persons
- Monitoring, internal audit and periodic review of procedures
- Workshop: prevention procedures gap analysis and action plan
