## Aim and Purpose

How to audit the firm's ability to withstand and recover from disruption, including important business services, cyber security, outsourcing and reliance on critical third parties.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the mapping of important business services and impact tolerances
- Audit cyber, incident management and scenario testing arrangements
- Evaluate outsourcing and third-party risk management across the lifecycle

## Syllabus

- Important business services, mapping and impact tolerances
- Cyber security, incident response and business continuity, including ISO 22301
- Outsourcing and third-party due diligence, contracts, oversight and exit
- Concentration risk and reliance on intragroup and cloud providers
