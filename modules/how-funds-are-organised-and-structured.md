## Aim and Purpose

The parties to a fund, the difference between self-managed and externally managed funds, and the common structures and legal forms funds take.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main parties to an investment fund
- Describe stand-alone, umbrella, master-feeder and fund of funds structures
- Explain why funds use different legal forms and share classes

## Syllabus

- Management company, investment manager, depositary and administrator
- Self-managed versus externally managed funds
- Umbrella and sub-funds, master-feeder and fund of funds structures
- Corporate, contractual and partnership forms and share classes
