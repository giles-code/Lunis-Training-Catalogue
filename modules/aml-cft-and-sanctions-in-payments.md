## Aim and Purpose

The financial crime risks specific to payment and e-money firms and how to build a risk-based AML/CFT and sanctions framework aligned with FATF standards.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess money laundering and terrorist financing risks in payments
- Design customer due diligence, monitoring and travel rule controls
- Implement sanctions screening for payments and customers

## Syllabus

- Business-wide risk assessment for payment and e-money models
- Customer due diligence, simplified due diligence and e-money thresholds
- Transaction monitoring, payment transparency and suspicious activity reporting
- Sanctions screening, payment filtering and money mule risk
