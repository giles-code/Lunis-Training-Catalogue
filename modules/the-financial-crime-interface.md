## Aim and Purpose

How the compliance function works alongside the MLRO and financial crime teams, avoiding gaps and overlaps in anti-money laundering, sanctions, fraud, bribery and market abuse controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the allocation of financial crime responsibilities between compliance and the MLRO
- Identify gaps and overlaps in the firm's financial crime control framework
- Apply compliance monitoring to financial crime controls

## Syllabus

- Roles of compliance, the MLRO and the second line for financial crime
- Anti-money laundering, sanctions, anti-bribery and fraud risk
- Market abuse surveillance and personal account dealing
- Joint monitoring, management information and escalation
