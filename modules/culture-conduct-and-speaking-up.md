## Aim and Purpose

How tone from the top, incentives and the board's own behaviour shape whether staff report concerns and whether commercial pressure overrides financial crime controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm's financial crime culture and the signals the board sends
- Evaluate whether incentives and commercial pressure undermine controls
- Identify how the board oversees whistleblowing and speaking up

## Syllabus

- Tone from the top, the middle and the front line
- Remuneration, sales incentives and accountability for breaches
- Whistleblowing arrangements and the board's champion
- Training and awareness that changes behaviour
