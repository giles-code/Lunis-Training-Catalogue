## Aim and Purpose

How companies, trusts, foundations and nominee arrangements are used to conceal income and assets from tax authorities, and the red flags wealth managers and advisers should recognise.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise structures commonly used to conceal income and assets
- Identify beneficial owners, controllers and tax-relevant persons
- Apply red flags to client instructions and account activity

## Syllabus

- Personal investment companies, trusts, foundations and nominees
- Beneficial ownership, controlling persons and register limitations
- Red flags: hold mail, cash, secrecy requests and unexplained transfers
- Mapping exercise: unpicking a multi-layered client structure
