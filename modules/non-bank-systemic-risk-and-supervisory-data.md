## Aim and Purpose

How authorities identify and monitor systemic risk outside the banking sector, close data gaps, and use macroprudential and supervisory tools in line with FSB and IOSCO recommendations.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain FSB and IOSCO recommendations on non-bank leverage and liquidity
- Use supervisory data to monitor concentration, leverage and liquidity risk
- Evaluate macroprudential and supervisory tools for non-bank risk

## Syllabus

- Systemic risk channels: leverage, liquidity, interconnectedness and concentration
- Data gaps and use of fund, manager and transaction reporting
- Stress testing and system-wide exercises for non-bank finance
- Macroprudential tools and cross-authority coordination
