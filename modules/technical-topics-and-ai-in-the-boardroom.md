## Aim and Purpose

How directors without specialist backgrounds can oversee cyber, technology and other complex risks, and what the use of AI by management and the board means for oversight.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess whether the board has enough knowledge to test technical statements and assumptions
- Challenge technical presentations that inform without enabling scrutiny
- Evaluate the benefits and risks of AI tools in board preparation and decision-making

## Syllabus

- Cyber, technology and other complex topics: what directors need to understand
- Getting the most from technical presentations and specialist advisers
- AI-assisted board packs, analysis and preparation: benefits and limits
- Accountability for decisions does not move to the tool
