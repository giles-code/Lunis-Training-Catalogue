## Aim and Purpose

How to deliver adverse findings and unwelcome decisions clearly and constructively, handle defensive or aggressive reactions, and stay firm without becoming inflexible.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why firms react defensively to adverse findings
- Apply techniques for delivering difficult messages clearly
- Manage pushback while protecting credibility for future engagement

## Syllabus

- Types of difficult supervisory conversation and how to prepare
- Structuring the delivery of an adverse finding
- Handling challenge, lobbying and appeals to seniority
- Role-play: delivering a finding to a senior manager
