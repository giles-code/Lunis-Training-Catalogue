## Aim and Purpose

How criminals take over genuine accounts or create fictitious customers, including the use of synthetic identities, deepfake audio and video, and AI-generated documents.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain account takeover methods, including phishing, SIM swap and malware
- Recognise synthetic identity and document forgery at onboarding
- Assess identity verification and authentication controls against deepfake attacks

## Syllabus

- Credential theft, SIM swap, remote access and device compromise
- Synthetic identities, bust-out fraud and mule account opening
- Deepfakes and generative AI in onboarding and impersonation
- Strong authentication, liveness checks and digital identity
