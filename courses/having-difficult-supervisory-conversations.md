## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in delivering difficult messages to regulated firms — including adverse findings and escalation decisions — while managing pushback constructively and maintaining a professional, credible supervisory relationship.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisory staff developing core professional judgement skills.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what makes a supervisory conversation difficult
- Apply techniques for delivering adverse findings clearly and constructively
- Manage pushback and defensive reactions from firms
- Maintain professional credibility while holding a firm position
- Practise a difficult supervisory conversation in a simulated scenario

## Syllabus

**Session 1: What makes a conversation difficult (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the sources of difficulty in supervisory conversations*
- Common types of difficult supervisory conversation
- Why firms react defensively to adverse findings
- The tension between relationship management and regulatory firmness
- Preparing for a difficult conversation in advance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: difficult conversations participants have had to hold

**Session 2: Delivering adverse findings (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply techniques for delivering findings clearly*
- Structuring the delivery of an adverse finding
- Being clear without being unnecessarily confrontational
- Anticipating and preparing for likely pushback
- Written versus verbal delivery of difficult messages
Activities:
- Exercise: draft the opening of a difficult supervisory conversation for a sample finding

**Session 3: Managing pushback and practice (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Manage pushback and practise a difficult conversation*
- Techniques for managing defensive or aggressive reactions
- Staying firm without becoming inflexible
- Maintaining credibility for future engagements
Activities:
- Role-play: hold a difficult supervisory conversation with a firm representative

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: sources of difficulty, delivery technique, managing pushback
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: What makes a conversation difficult (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Delivering adverse findings (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Managing pushback and practice (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Preparation is the single most effective way to improve a difficult supervisory conversation
- Clarity and firmness do not require confrontation — tone matters as much as content
- Credibility built through fair, professional difficult conversations pays off in every future engagement with a firm
