## Aim and Purpose

What SupTech is, how it differs from firms' RegTech, and why the quality of an authority's evidence base increasingly determines the quality of its supervisory judgement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain SupTech and RegTech and how they interact
- Identify the main categories of SupTech tools and their purposes
- Assess realistic expectations of what SupTech can and cannot do

## Syllabus

- SupTech versus RegTech: whose tools and whose data
- Categories: data collection, analytics, reporting automation and surveillance
- International experience: BIS, FSB and authority surveys
- The link between data quality and supervisory judgement
