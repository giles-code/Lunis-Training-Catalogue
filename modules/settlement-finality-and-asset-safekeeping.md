## Aim and Purpose

How settlement finality, money settlement, delivery versus payment and CSD safekeeping remove principal and custody risk, including the questions raised by DLT-based and tokenised settlement.

## Learning Objectives

- Establish the legal basis and moment of settlement finality
- Manage DvP and PvP models, money settlement assets and settlement bank risk
- Protect participants' assets through account structures and reconciliation

## Syllabus

- Legal certainty and the moment of finality
- DvP, PvP and central bank versus commercial bank money
- Settlement fails, buy-ins and integrity of the issue
- DLT settlement, tokenised assets and stablecoin arrangements under the PFMI
