## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together to examine how tokenisation, stablecoins and DeFi are reshaping markets and regulation, and how firms can position themselves to grow while meeting rising expectations.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the trends shaping the future of digital finance
- Compare regulatory approaches and international standards across jurisdictions
- Evaluate strategic options, licensing choices and readiness for regulatory change

## Syllabus

- Comprehensive crypto-asset regimes, convergence and divergence across jurisdictions
- International standards: FSB, IOSCO, FATF and the Basel Committee
- Institutional adoption, stablecoins in payments and tokenised infrastructure
- Workshop: a growth and regulatory readiness plan for a crypto-asset business
