## Aim and Purpose

Why one-way reporting alone is not enough, what public-private partnerships add to financial intelligence, and what FATF work and international experience say about their effectiveness.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the limits of one-way suspicious transaction reporting
- Describe the benefits and risks of public-private partnerships
- Summarise FATF work on private sector information sharing

## Syllabus

- From one-way reporting to two-way sharing
- Benefits: better targeting, faster response and improved reporting
- Risks: legal exposure, de-risking and unequal participation
- FATF guidance and international experience
