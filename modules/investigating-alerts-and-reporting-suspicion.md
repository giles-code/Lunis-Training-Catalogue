## Aim and Purpose

How to triage and investigate on-chain and customer alerts, reach and record a reasoned judgement on suspicion, and write reports that the national FIU and law enforcement can act on.

## Learning Objectives

- Run a structured investigation combining on-chain and off-chain information
- Reach and document a defensible decision on suspicion
- Write suspicious activity reports with usable crypto-asset detail

## Syllabus

- Alert triage, escalation and case management
- Investigation steps: customer profile, tracing, counterparties and open sources
- Decision records, tipping off and restricting or exiting relationships
- Report quality: addresses, transaction hashes, attribution and narrative
