## Aim and Purpose

The NIST post-quantum standards, what a realistic multi-year migration programme involves, and why crypto-agility is as important as the choice of algorithm.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the NIST post-quantum cryptography standards and their intended uses
- Assess the credibility, sequencing and resourcing of a firm's migration plan
- Evaluate a firm's approach to crypto-agility and hybrid deployment

## Syllabus

- NIST post-quantum standards: ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA
- Migration roadmaps, milestones and testing
- Hybrid schemes, performance impacts and interoperability
- Crypto-agility as a design principle for future change
