## Aim and Purpose

Why AI outputs must be checked, how to verify them efficiently and how to keep human judgement at the centre of decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the warning signs of hallucinated or unreliable output
- Apply proportionate verification to AI-assisted work
- Retain personal responsibility for work produced with AI

## Syllabus

- Fabricated facts, citations, figures and regulatory references
- Verification techniques: source checks, cross-checks and reasonableness
- Over-reliance and automation bias
- When AI should not be used at all
