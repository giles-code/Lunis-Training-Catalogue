## Aim and Purpose

How the authority maps and manages expectations of government, industry, consumers and international peers, communicates its strategy, and engages with standard-setters and cooperation frameworks.

## Learning Objectives

- Map stakeholder expectations and balance competing demands
- Communicate strategic priorities clearly to internal and external audiences
- Identify practical steps to strengthen the authority's international engagement

## Syllabus

- Stakeholder mapping: government, industry, consumers, media and peers
- Strategic communication, public reporting and transparency
- Engagement with IOSCO, BCBS, FSB and other standard-setters
- Resourcing international engagement in a smaller authority
