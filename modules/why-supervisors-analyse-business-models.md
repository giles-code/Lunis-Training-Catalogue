## Aim and Purpose

How strategy and business model risk fit into risk-based supervision, what supervisors want to learn and how their conclusions shape the firm's supervisory engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the link between business model weakness and other risks
- Describe what a supervisory business model review covers
- Anticipate the questions supervisors will ask about the firm's strategy

## Syllabus

- Strategy, business model and the value chain
- Viability now and sustainability over the plan period
- Proportionality: what prompts a deeper review
- How business model findings feed risk ratings and remediation
