## Aim and Purpose

How to assess whether a firm's recovery plan would genuinely restore viability under severe stress, and the common weaknesses supervisors should challenge.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and content of a recovery plan
- Assess recovery indicators, options and scenarios for credibility
- Challenge unrealistic assumptions and untested options

## Syllabus

- Recovery plans: governance, indicators, options and communication
- Severe but plausible scenarios and the capacity of recovery options
- Common weaknesses: optimistic timing, value and executability
- Exercise: assessing a sample recovery plan
