## Aim and Purpose

How assessors rate technical compliance and effectiveness, what the immediate outcomes test, and the procedures and timeline of the current round of mutual evaluations.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish technical compliance ratings from effectiveness ratings
- Explain the immediate outcomes and the core issues assessors examine
- Describe the mutual evaluation process, follow-up and listing triggers

## Syllabus

- Technical compliance criteria and ratings
- The immediate outcomes, core issues and levels of effectiveness
- Evaluation procedures: questionnaires, on-site visit and plenary
- Follow-up, enhanced follow-up and the listing process
