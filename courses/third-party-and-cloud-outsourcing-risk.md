## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in assessing firms' reliance on critical third-party and cloud providers, including concentration risk and the adequacy of exit strategies.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the risks associated with third-party and cloud outsourcing
- Describe concentration risk arising from reliance on a small number of providers
- Assess the adequacy of a firm's third-party risk management framework
- Evaluate exit strategies for critical outsourced services
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's third-party risk exposure

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Third-party and cloud risk fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain third-party and cloud outsourcing risks*
- Why third-party and cloud reliance has grown across financial services
- Risks: loss of control, shared responsibility model misunderstanding, vendor lock-in
- Regulatory frameworks addressing critical third-party risk (e.g. DORA)
- Distinguishing critical from non-critical outsourced functions
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: third-party risk episodes participants are aware of

**Session 2: Concentration risk (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe and assess concentration risk*
- Concentration risk from a small number of dominant cloud/critical providers
- Systemic implications of sector-wide reliance on the same providers
- Assessing a firm's third-party risk management framework
- Due diligence expectations for critical third parties
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess concentration risk for a sample sector's cloud provider reliance

**Session 3: Exit strategies and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate exit strategies and formulate an assessment*
- What a credible exit strategy for a critical outsourced service requires
- Common weaknesses in exit planning
- Case study: a firm's exit strategy under review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's third-party risk exposure and exit strategy

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: outsourcing risks, concentration risk, exit strategies
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Third-party and cloud risk fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Concentration risk (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Exit strategies and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Cloud and third-party concentration risk is increasingly a sector-wide, not just firm-specific, supervisory concern
- A credible exit strategy must be genuinely actionable, not just a theoretical fallback
- Due diligence on critical third parties should be treated with the same rigour as due diligence on the firm itself
