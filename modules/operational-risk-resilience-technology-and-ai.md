## Aim and Purpose

How operational risk is identified and managed across the service provider chain, how operational and digital resilience is tested, and how boards oversee new technology including AI.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe an effective operational risk framework for the fund and its delegates
- Evaluate cyber, third-party and supply chain resilience
- Appraise the opportunities and threats of technology and AI in fund operations

## Syllabus

- Risk and control self-assessment, risk registers and operational risk appetite
- Operational and digital resilience: important services, impact tolerances and testing
- Supply chain security, third-party concentration and exit plans
- Transformation programmes and AI as both asset and threat
