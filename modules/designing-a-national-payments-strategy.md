## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining how a national payments strategy is set, how legislation and institutions are aligned behind it, and how progress is measured.

## Learning Objectives

- Set national payments objectives for efficiency, inclusion and resilience
- Align legislation, institutions and public-private governance
- Produce a national payments strategy action plan

## Syllabus

- Payments councils and public-private governance
- Digital public infrastructure, identity and government payments
- Measuring cost, access, usage and resilience
- Workshop: a national payments and digital money strategy
