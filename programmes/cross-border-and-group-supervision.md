## Aim and Purpose

To give supervisors the frameworks and practical skills to oversee cross-border groups, branches and third-country firms without gaps or duplication. Participants leave able to assess group structures and intra-group risk, use cooperation arrangements and colleges effectively, and protect their own jurisdiction's interests in normal times and in a crisis.

## What It Covers

Home and host responsibilities, consolidated and group-wide supervision, intra-group exposures and complex structures, MoUs and information sharing, supervisory colleges, branches, subsidiaries and third-country firms, cross-border crisis cooperation, and overcoming obstacles to cooperation.

## Intended Audience

- Supervisors responsible for cross-border groups, branches, subsidiaries or third-country firms
- International affairs, legal and cooperation staff managing MoUs and information requests
- Senior supervisors and managers who represent the authority in colleges and crisis management groups

## Learning Objectives

- Explain home and host responsibilities under international standards such as the Basel Core Principles
- Assess group structures, intra-group exposures and contagion risk on a consolidated basis
- Evaluate MoUs and information-sharing arrangements and use them effectively
- Contribute to joint risk assessments and coordinated decisions in supervisory colleges
- Assess the risks of branches, subsidiaries and third-country firms in the authority's market
- Apply cooperation principles to cross-border crisis management and resolution

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a briefing on international standards with case studies and practical exercises, ending in a cross-border simulation, and modules can be selected or tailored to the authority's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of prudential or conduct supervision is helpful. No prior involvement in colleges or international cooperation is required.

## Core Reading

- Basel Committee, Principles for Effective Supervisory Colleges (2014)
- IOSCO, Multilateral Memorandum of Understanding (MMoU) and Enhanced MMoU (2016)
- FSB, Key Attributes of Effective Resolution Regimes for Financial Institutions (2014)
- IAIS, Insurance Core Principles and Common Framework for the Supervision of Internationally Active Insurance Groups (ComFrame)
