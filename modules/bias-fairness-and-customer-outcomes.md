## Aim and Purpose

Building bias and fairness testing into the model development lifecycle, weighing fairness metrics and trade-offs, and meeting consumer protection and non-discrimination obligations in the firm's own decisioning.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how historical data, proxies and feedback loops introduce bias
- Apply fairness metrics and evaluate the trade-offs between them
- Design bias testing and outcome monitoring across the model lifecycle

## Syllabus

- Sources of bias: historical data, proxy discrimination and feedback loops
- Fairness metrics in credit scoring, insurance pricing and targeting
- Fair treatment and non-discrimination obligations applied to AI decisions
- Case studies of AI bias harms and the governance failures behind them
