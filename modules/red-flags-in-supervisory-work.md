## Aim and Purpose

How financial crime risks surface in authorisation applications, supervisory reviews and firm data, and how staff outside specialist teams can spot them.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise financial crime red flags in applications and firm information
- Use data and returns to identify unusual patterns
- Decide when concerns should be referred to specialist colleagues

## Syllabus

- Red flags at the gateway: ownership, source of funds and business plans
- Red flags in supervision: growth, customer base and geography
- Unusual transactions, products and delivery channels
- Escalation routes within the authority
