## Aim and Purpose

Why custody is the critical risk in digital assets, how client assets are held and protected, and what assurance the board needs that client holdings are safe and reconciled.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how private keys, wallets and custody arrangements protect or expose client assets
- Evaluate segregation, reconciliation and safeguarding controls
- Judge the adequacy of independent assurance over client holdings

## Syllabus

- Hot, warm and cold storage, multi-signature and key management
- Segregation of client assets and treatment on insolvency
- Reconciliation, proof of reserves and their limits
- Sub-custody and third-party custodian due diligence
