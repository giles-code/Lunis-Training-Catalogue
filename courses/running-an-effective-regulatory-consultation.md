## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in designing and running an effective public consultation on proposed rules, including stakeholder engagement strategy and how to analyse and respond to consultation feedback transparently.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level policy officers drafting or contributing to rules, guidance, and consultation papers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and value of regulatory consultation
- Design an effective consultation process and timeline
- Develop a stakeholder engagement strategy for a consultation
- Apply techniques for analysing consultation responses systematically
- Draft a transparent feedback statement responding to consultation input

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Purpose and design of consultation (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain consultation purpose and design an effective process*
- Why consultation improves rule quality and legitimacy
- Designing a consultation timeline and document
- Consultation questions: open versus closed, avoiding leading questions
- Statutory versus voluntary consultation requirements
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: consultation experiences, good and bad, from participants

**Session 2: Stakeholder engagement strategy (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Develop a stakeholder engagement strategy*
- Identifying and mapping consultation stakeholders
- Engagement channels: written responses, roundtables, targeted outreach
- Ensuring smaller or less-resourced stakeholders are heard
- Balancing industry input against other public interests
Activities:
- Group exercise: design a stakeholder engagement plan for a sample consultation

**Session 3: Analysing and responding to feedback (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Analyse consultation responses and draft a feedback statement*
- Systematic approaches to analysing consultation responses
- Identifying themes versus outlier views
- Drafting a transparent feedback statement
- Handling contentious or high-volume responses
Activities:
- Exercise: analyse a set of sample consultation responses and draft key feedback points

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: purpose, design, stakeholder engagement, feedback analysis
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Purpose and design of consultation (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Stakeholder engagement strategy (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Analysing and responding to feedback (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Good consultation design shapes the quality of the input an authority receives
- Stakeholder engagement should actively seek out less-resourced voices, not just respond to the loudest
- A transparent feedback statement builds trust even when not every response can be accommodated
