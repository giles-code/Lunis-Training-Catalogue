## Aim and Purpose

How large technology platforms enter financial services, the data, network and activity loop that drives their advantage, and how their models differ from traditional financial firms.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the economics of platforms and the data-network-activities loop
- Distinguish the routes by which big tech enters payments, credit, insurance and wealth
- Identify where platform models create new supervisory challenges

## Syllabus

- Network effects, ecosystems and cross-subsidisation
- Entry routes: own licence, partnership or acquisition
- Platform finance in advanced and emerging economies
- BIS and FSB analysis of big tech in finance
