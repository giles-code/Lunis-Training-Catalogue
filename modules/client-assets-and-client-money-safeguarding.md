## Aim and Purpose

How firms protect client assets and money through segregation, reconciliations and governance, and what happens when things go wrong or the firm fails.

## Learning Objectives

- Design segregation and trust arrangements for client assets and money
- Operate reconciliations and resolve discrepancies promptly
- Govern client asset arrangements and prepare for insolvency

## Syllabus

- Segregation, trust status, acknowledgement letters and sub-custody
- Internal and external reconciliations, shortfalls and breach reporting
- Title transfer collateral, securities lending and use of client assets
- Client asset oversight, audit reporting and resolution packs
