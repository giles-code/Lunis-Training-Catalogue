## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a practical supervisory approach to platforms and partnerships, from data and early warning indicators to cooperation with other authorities and the authority's policy stance.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply a supervisory assessment framework to a platform partnership
- Evaluate cooperation arrangements with competition, data protection and consumer authorities
- Design priorities for the authority's approach to platform finance

## Syllabus

- Mapping partnership chains and accountability
- Early warning indicators and thematic reviews of partnerships
- Cross-authority and cross-border cooperation
- Workshop: supervisory review of an embedded finance partnership
