## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how investment performance is measured, attributed, and disclosed, and how supervisors assess the accuracy and fairness of performance claims used in marketing.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment management supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain standard performance measurement methodologies
- Describe performance attribution and its role in explaining returns
- Assess the accuracy of a sample performance disclosure
- Evaluate marketing performance claims for fairness and compliance
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's performance disclosure practice

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Performance measurement methodologies (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain performance measurement standards*
- Standard performance measurement methodologies (e.g. GIPS)
- Time-weighted versus money-weighted returns
- Gross versus net-of-fee performance presentation
- Why standardisation matters for comparability
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: performance disclosure issues participants have encountered

**Session 2: Attribution and disclosure accuracy (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe attribution and assess disclosure accuracy*
- Performance attribution: explaining sources of return
- Common inaccuracies in performance reporting
- Benchmark selection and its effect on perceived performance
- Verifying disclosed performance against underlying data
Activities:
- Group exercise: verify a sample performance disclosure against underlying portfolio data

**Session 3: Marketing claims and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate marketing claims and formulate an assessment*
- Assessing marketing performance claims for fairness and compliance
- Cherry-picking and misleading presentation techniques
- Case study: a marketing claim raising fair-presentation concerns
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's performance disclosure practice

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: measurement standards, attribution, disclosure accuracy, marketing claims
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Performance measurement methodologies (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Attribution and disclosure accuracy (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Marketing claims and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Standardised performance measurement makes genuine comparison possible; deviations should raise questions
- Benchmark selection can materially affect how good or bad a performance record appears
- Marketing performance claims deserve the same scrutiny as any other investor-facing disclosure
