## Aim and Purpose

The information a board needs to oversee fund risk, how to read risk reports critically, and how to distinguish genuine assurance from volume of data.

## Learning Objectives

- Specify the core risk information set for a fund board
- Interpret key risk indicators, trends and breach reports
- Identify gaps, stale data and comfort without evidence

## Syllabus

- The core risk information set: profile, appetite, limits, breaches and actions
- Leading and lagging indicators, thresholds and trend analysis
- Exception reporting, overdue actions and risk acceptances
- Common weaknesses in risk reports and what to ask for instead
