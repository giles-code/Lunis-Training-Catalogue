## Aim and Purpose

How managers delegate portfolio management, risk management and administration while retaining the substance, expertise and oversight that supervisors expect.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess whether the manager retains sufficient substance and decision-making
- Design due diligence and ongoing oversight of delegates
- Identify letter-box risks and how supervisors test for them

## Syllabus

- Delegation models and the limits on what can be delegated
- Substance: people, expertise, resources and decision-making
- Delegate due diligence, service levels and ongoing monitoring
- Intragroup and cross-border delegation
