## Aim and Purpose

How central bank money, commercial bank money and new private digital money relate to each other, and why programmable ledgers are prompting firms to rethink payments and settlement.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish the forms of money by issuer, claim and protection
- Explain the singleness of money and why it matters to firms
- Identify where tokenisation changes payments and settlement economics

## Syllabus

- Central bank, commercial bank and private digital money
- Singleness of money, convertibility at par and confidence
- Shared ledgers, programmability and atomic settlement
- The BIS and CPMI vision of a tokenised unified ledger
