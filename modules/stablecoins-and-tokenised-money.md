## Aim and Purpose

The rise of stablecoins and tokenised deposits as payment instruments, their risks to consumers, monetary sovereignty and stability, and the policy options for regulating them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how stablecoins and tokenised deposits differ from existing money
- Assess risks to monetary sovereignty, payments and financial stability
- Evaluate reserve, redemption and issuer requirements

## Syllabus

- Stablecoin designs and de-pegging case studies
- Global stablecoin arrangements and FSB recommendations
- Currency substitution in smaller and emerging economies
- Tokenised deposits and settlement in central bank money
