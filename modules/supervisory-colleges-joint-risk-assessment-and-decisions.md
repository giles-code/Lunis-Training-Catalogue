## Aim and Purpose

How supervisory colleges are governed, how members build a shared risk picture across jurisdictions, and how to reach coordinated decisions and manage disagreement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose, governance and outputs of supervisory colleges
- Contribute effectively to a joint risk assessment
- Manage disagreement and unequal engagement among college members

## Syllabus

- College governance: chair, secretariat, membership and workplans
- Building and reconciling a joint risk assessment
- Coordinated decisions, constructive challenge and escalation
- Case study: a college facing a coordination challenge
