## Aim and Purpose

How to draft the authorisation regime: the general prohibition, threshold conditions, fit and proper tests, conditions and variations, and the powers to refuse, suspend and withdraw a licence.

## Learning Objectives

- Draft a general prohibition and its criminal and civil consequences
- Specify threshold conditions and fitness and propriety requirements
- Provide for conditions, variation, suspension and withdrawal of licences

## Syllabus

- The general prohibition and unenforceability of agreements
- Threshold conditions, controllers and change of control
- Fit and proper requirements and senior manager accountability regimes
- Licence conditions, variation, suspension and cancellation
