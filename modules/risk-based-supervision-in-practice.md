## Aim and Purpose

How to design and run a risk-based supervisory approach to crypto firms: prioritising resources, monitoring between engagements, conducting inspections and using graduated powers to drive remediation.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a risk-based framework that allocates resources by impact and probability
- Conduct ongoing monitoring using returns, market intelligence and early warning indicators
- Plan, execute and follow up inspections of crypto-asset firms

## Syllabus

- Firm risk assessment, supervisory planning and resource allocation
- Red flags triggering enhanced engagement: rapid growth, turnover and incidents
- Inspection methodology: document requests, interviews and walkthroughs
- Graduated supervisory powers and tracking remediation
