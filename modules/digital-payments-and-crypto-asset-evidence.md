## Aim and Purpose

Obtaining and interpreting evidence from payment firms, e-money issuers, online platforms and crypto-asset service providers, and integrating it with traditional financial records.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the records held by payment, e-money and crypto-asset service providers
- Integrate digital payment and blockchain data into the financial picture
- Recognise the evidential limits of analytics outputs

## Syllabus

- Payment firms, e-money accounts and online marketplaces
- Crypto-asset service provider records and blockchain tracing basics
- Device, communications and other digital financial evidence
- Attribution, reliability and continuity of digital evidence
