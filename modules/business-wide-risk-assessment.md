## Aim and Purpose

Building a business-wide risk assessment that genuinely drives controls and resourcing, drawing on data rather than assertion and covering money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions, proliferation financing and fraud.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a business-wide risk assessment methodology linked to controls
- Use customer, product, channel and geographic data to evidence inherent risk
- Assess control effectiveness and residual risk credibly

## Syllabus

- Inherent risk factors: customers, products, channels and geographies
- Data-driven risk scoring and its limitations
- Control effectiveness testing and residual risk
- Linking the assessment to risk appetite, policy and resourcing
