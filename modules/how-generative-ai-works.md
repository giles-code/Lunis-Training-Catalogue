## Aim and Purpose

A plain-language introduction to generative AI and large language models: what they do well, where they fail and why that matters in a regulated firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain in plain terms how large language models generate answers
- Distinguish suitable from unsuitable tasks for generative AI
- Recognise the main risks of using generative AI at work

## Syllabus

- Training data, prediction and why outputs sound confident
- Strengths: drafting, summarising, analysis and coding support
- Limits: hallucination, out-of-date knowledge and inconsistency
- Approved tools, consumer tools and why the difference matters
