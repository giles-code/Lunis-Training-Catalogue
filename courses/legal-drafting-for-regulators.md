## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build core legal drafting skills for producing rules and guidance that are clear, internally consistent, and enforceable in practice.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level policy officers drafting or contributing to rules, guidance, and consultation papers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core principles of legislative and regulatory drafting
- Apply techniques for consistent use of defined terms
- Identify and resolve internal inconsistencies within a draft
- Draft provisions that are enforceable and testable in practice
- Evaluate a draft rule for statutory interpretation risk

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Principles of legal drafting (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain core drafting principles*
- Precision, clarity and consistency as drafting objectives
- Structuring rules logically: definitions, obligations, exceptions
- Common statutory interpretation principles courts apply
- Drafting for different legal traditions
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: drafting challenges participants have faced

**Session 2: Defined terms and consistency (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply techniques for consistent defined-term usage and resolve inconsistencies*
- Creating and using a defined terms schedule
- Common consistency failures across long documents
- Cross-referencing and version control in drafting
- Techniques for reviewing a draft for internal consistency
Activities:
- Exercise: identify and correct inconsistent term usage in a sample draft rule

**Session 3: Enforceability and interpretation risk (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Draft enforceable provisions and evaluate interpretation risk*
- Drafting obligations that are testable and enforceable
- Avoiding vague standards that invite litigation
- Case study: a provision successfully challenged due to drafting ambiguity
Activities:
- Case study: redraft a vulnerable provision to reduce interpretation risk

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: principles, consistency, enforceability
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Principles of legal drafting (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Defined terms and consistency (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Enforceability and interpretation risk (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Precise, consistent defined terms are the foundation of enforceable regulatory drafting
- Ambiguous standards create litigation risk that undermines a rule's practical effect
- Reviewing a draft specifically for interpretation risk catches problems before they reach a court
