## Aim and Purpose

How law enforcement and FIUs work with banks, payment and e-money firms and crypto-asset service providers to freeze, trace and repatriate fraud proceeds within hours rather than weeks.

## Learning Objectives

- Use rapid-response and freezing mechanisms with financial institutions
- Request and interpret payment, account and device data
- Build effective working relationships and escalation routes with the private sector

## Syllabus

- Rapid freezing, recall and repatriation of funds
- Payment, account, device and login data from institutions
- Single points of contact and escalation routes
- Data-sharing gateways and public-private fraud partnerships
