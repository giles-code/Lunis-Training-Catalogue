## Aim and Purpose

How to assess whether firms identify, prevent and manage conflicts of interest across their business, including inducements, personal account dealing and group relationships.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main sources of conflicts of interest in regulated firms
- Evaluate conflicts policies, registers and management arrangements
- Assess when disclosure is an inadequate response to a conflict

## Syllabus

- Sources of conflict: remuneration, inducements, group and personal interests
- Conflicts policies, registers and information barriers
- Personal account dealing, gifts and hospitality
- Disclosure as a last resort: when it is not enough
