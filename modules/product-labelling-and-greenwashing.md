## Aim and Purpose

Making sustainability claims about products and the firm that are clear, fair and substantiated, and meeting product labelling and disclosure regimes.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the forms of greenwashing and how supervisors detect them
- Apply substantiation standards to sustainability claims and labels
- Design product governance controls for sustainability-related products

## Syllabus

- Greenwashing, greenhushing and misleading claims
- Sustainable product labels and disclosure regimes
- Substantiation, evidence and marketing review
- Case studies of greenwashing enforcement
