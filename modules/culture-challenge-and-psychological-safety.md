## Aim and Purpose

How board culture shows itself in meetings, minutes and interviews, the role of the chair in enabling challenge, and how to tell effective challenge from confrontation or avoidance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why challenge depends on psychological safety
- Read the signs of board culture: airtime, silence, interruptions and openness
- Assess the effectiveness of the chair in enabling challenge

## Syllabus

- Psychological safety and how its absence shows up
- Observable signs of culture in meetings and minutes
- Effective and ineffective chairs
- Challenge, confrontation and avoiding conflict to keep the peace
