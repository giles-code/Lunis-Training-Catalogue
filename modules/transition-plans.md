## Aim and Purpose

Designing a transition plan that sets credible targets, links them to business strategy and capital allocation, and can be monitored and evidenced.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the components of a credible transition plan
- Design targets, metrics and interim milestones
- Assess the plan's credibility from a supervisor's and investor's perspective

## Syllabus

- Transition plan frameworks and elements
- Financed emissions, targets and interim milestones
- Linking plans to strategy, products and capital allocation
- Red flags of a non-credible transition plan
