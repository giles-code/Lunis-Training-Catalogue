## Aim and Purpose

How the firm identifies the risks capital must cover, assesses how much capital it needs over and above regulatory minimums, and documents the assessment for the board and supervisor.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of an internal capital adequacy self-assessment
- Assess capital needed for risks not fully captured by regulatory minimums
- Document the assessment in a way the board owns and supervisors can review

## Syllabus

- Regulatory minimums and the case for additional internal capital
- Quantifying credit, market, operational and business risk
- Capital planning, buffers and the business plan
- Common weaknesses supervisors find in self-assessments
