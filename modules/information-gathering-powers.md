## Aim and Purpose

The powers available to the authority to require information, documents and attendance, and how to use them lawfully and effectively.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the information-gathering powers available and their limits
- Draft clear and proportionate information requirements
- Respond to privilege claims, non-compliance and delay

## Syllabus

- Requirements for information, documents and attendance
- Drafting notices: scope, deadlines and format
- Legal professional privilege, self-incrimination and confidentiality
- On-site inspections, third parties and requests to other authorities
