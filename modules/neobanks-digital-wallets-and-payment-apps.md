## Aim and Purpose

The economics of app-based banks, e-money wallets and payment apps, the customer funds and safeguarding risks they carry, and why rapid growth often outpaces controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain neobank and wallet revenue models and the path to profitability
- Assess safeguarding, reconciliation and the protection of customer funds
- Evaluate control maturity against customer growth and transaction volumes

## Syllabus

- Deposit-taking neobanks versus e-money and payment licences
- Interchange dependency, float income and premium subscriptions
- Safeguarding, reconciliation and reliance on partner banks
- Red flags: onboarding surges, complaint spikes and account freezes
