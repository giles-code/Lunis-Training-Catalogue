## Aim and Purpose

How the management company organises its managerial functions, the substance and time commitment the authority expects, and how the board oversees the people who perform them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the managerial functions and how they are allocated
- Assess substance, resourcing and time commitment of designated persons
- Evaluate reporting from designated persons to the board

## Syllabus

- Core managerial functions: investment, risk, distribution, operations and compliance
- Allocation, capability and time commitment of designated persons
- Substance requirements and avoiding letterbox arrangements
- Designated persons' reporting and the board's monitoring plan
