## Aim and Purpose

To equip staff with the distinct skills enforcement demands over and above ongoing supervision: evidence-gathering to a legal standard, procedural fairness and sanctioning judgement. Participants leave able to take a case from referral to outcome in a way that is proportionate, consistent and able to withstand challenge.

## What It Covers

Enforcement powers and purpose, the decision to investigate and case triage, investigation planning, evidential standards and disclosure, chain of custody and digital evidence, investigative interviews and witness statements, procedural fairness, proportionate sanctions, settlement, criminal referral and cooperation, and tribunal and court proceedings.

## Intended Audience

- Enforcement investigators, case managers and lawyers
- Supervisors who refer cases to enforcement or work alongside enforcement teams
- Members of decision-making committees and senior staff responsible for sanctioning and settlement decisions

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the enforcement function, its powers and how it differs from supervisory intervention
- Apply criteria and a triage framework to decide which cases to investigate
- Gather, preserve and present evidence to a standard that will withstand legal challenge
- Design and apply decision-making processes that meet procedural fairness standards
- Determine proportionate, consistent sanctions and judge when settlement serves the public interest
- Coordinate effectively with prosecutors and domestic and foreign authorities

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Modules combine short input with case studies, evidence exercises and negotiation role-play, ending in an end-to-end case workshop, and can be selected or tailored to the authority's own powers and procedures.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of supervision, investigations or regulatory legal work is helpful. No formal legal qualification is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Credible Deterrence in the Enforcement of Securities Regulation (2015)
- IOSCO, Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation (2017)
- IOSCO, Multilateral Memorandum of Understanding (MMoU) and Enhanced MMoU (2016)
