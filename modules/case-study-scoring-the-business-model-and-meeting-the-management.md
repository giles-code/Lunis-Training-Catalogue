## Aim and Purpose

Supervisory teams analyse contrasting case-study firms, reach a view on viability and sustainability, draft a risk rating and test their concerns in a short role-played meeting with management.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify a firm's three key vulnerabilities and support each with evidence
- Conclude on viability over twelve months and sustainability over three years
- Test the analysis through clear, simple questions to management

## Syllabus

- Group analysis: SWOT, key vulnerabilities and dependencies
- Scoring business model risk with an analytical, not descriptive, rationale
- Role play: meeting the management to test the supervisory team's concerns
- Debrief: comparing conclusions across firms and questions for the on-site visit
