## Aim and Purpose

The open, closed and restricted sources investigators can use to build a financial profile, and how to assess their reliability and the conditions attached to their use.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify open, closed and restricted sources of financial information
- Assess reliability, completeness and handling conditions
- Build an initial financial profile of a subject

## Syllabus

- Public registers: companies, land, vehicles and court records
- FIU intelligence and tax, customs and law-enforcement holdings
- Open-source research and lifestyle indicators
- Building the subject's financial profile
