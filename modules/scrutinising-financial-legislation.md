## Aim and Purpose

How financial bills are structured, what to look for in the powers and safeguards they contain, and how to test impact assessments and industry claims.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main components of a financial services bill
- Assess delegated powers, safeguards and review clauses
- Test impact assessments, consultation responses and lobbying claims

## Syllabus

- Perimeter, licensing, enforcement and transitional provisions explained
- Delegated powers and parliamentary oversight of rules
- Reading an impact assessment critically
- Engaging with industry and consumer representations
