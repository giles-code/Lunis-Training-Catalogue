## Aim and Purpose

How home and host authorities cooperate when a cross-border group comes under stress, from early warning and information sharing to crisis management groups and resolution.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the FSB Key Attributes and cross-border resolution cooperation
- Assess recovery and resolution planning for cross-border groups
- Identify the conflicts of interest that arise between authorities in a crisis

## Syllabus

- Crisis management groups and institution-specific cooperation agreements
- Single and multiple point of entry resolution strategies
- Ring-fencing incentives and host protection in stress
- Coordinated communication with firms, markets and the public
