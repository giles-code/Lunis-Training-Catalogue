## Aim and Purpose

What a realistic multi-year migration programme involves, from roadmap and resourcing to testing, sequencing and integration with the firm's wider technology change and refresh cycles.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a migration roadmap with milestones, dependencies and resourcing
- Sequence migration to protect high-value data and critical services first
- Manage testing, change control and rollback for cryptographic change

## Syllabus

- Roadmaps, milestones and alignment with technology refresh cycles
- Business case, budget and skills for a multi-year programme
- Testing, change management and rollback
- Measuring progress: coverage, exceptions and residual risk
