## Aim and Purpose

A briefing on the year's main regulatory and supervisory developments for funds and managers, drawing on the work of international standard-setters and recurring supervisory priorities.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the year's main international policy developments for funds and managers
- Explain how standard-setter work is translated into national requirements
- Assess which developments matter most for the board's own fund range

## Syllabus

- IOSCO and FSB work on fund liquidity, leverage and non-bank intermediation
- Supervisory priorities: fees and costs, valuation, governance and delegation
- Private markets, retail access and product governance developments
- Enforcement themes and lessons from recent supervisory findings
