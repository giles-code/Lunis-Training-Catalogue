## Aim and Purpose

How to turn risk findings into a national AML/CFT strategy, design an effective coordination body, and ensure the legal and institutional framework meets the standards.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a national AML/CFT strategy linked to the risk assessment
- Establish coordination mechanisms with clear mandates and accountability
- Assess gaps in criminal, preventive and administrative legislation

## Syllabus

- Strategy design: priorities, owners, resources and milestones
- National coordination committees: membership, mandate and secretariat
- Criminalisation of money laundering and terrorist financing
- Domestic cooperation, information sharing and data protection
