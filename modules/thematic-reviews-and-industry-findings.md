## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors run thematic reviews, what they mean for firms in and outside the sample, and how to use published findings to strengthen the firm's own controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how thematic reviews are scoped and run
- Respond effectively when selected for a thematic review
- Apply published thematic findings to the firm

## Syllabus

- Why supervisors run thematic reviews
- Responding to questionnaires, visits and feedback
- Reading industry reports: complying with intent, not just wording
- Workshop: a gap analysis against a thematic report
