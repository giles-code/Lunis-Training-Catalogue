## Aim and Purpose

The standards of competence, honesty, integrity and financial soundness that directors must meet, how regulators act as gatekeepers at appointment, and how firms assess fitness on an ongoing basis.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply fitness and probity standards to directors and other key roles
- Prepare for a regulatory fitness and probity assessment or interview
- Recognise the circumstances that call fitness or probity into question

## Syllabus

- Competent and capable: qualifications, experience, competence and capacity
- Honest, ethical and acting with integrity, including non-financial misconduct
- Financial soundness and the firm's due diligence and certification
- Regulatory gatekeeping, interviews, refusals and lessons from case law
