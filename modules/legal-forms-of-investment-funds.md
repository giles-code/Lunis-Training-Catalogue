## Aim and Purpose

The four main legal forms a fund can take, how each holds assets and treats investors, and why a sponsor would choose one over another.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe corporate funds, unit trusts, contractual funds and limited partnerships
- Explain how legal form affects investor rights, governance and tax
- Assess the rationale for choosing a particular legal form

## Syllabus

- Corporate investment funds and the role of shareholders
- Unit trusts and the trustee relationship
- Contractual funds and co-ownership models
- Limited partnerships: general and limited partners
