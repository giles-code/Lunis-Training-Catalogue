## Aim and Purpose

Identifying and managing the structural conflicts in multi-service crypto-asset firms, and producing white papers, marketing and client disclosures that are fair, clear and not misleading.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify structural conflicts in combined service models and manage them
- Prepare white papers and disclosures that meet content and liability standards
- Review marketing communications and customer journeys for fair treatment

## Syllabus

- Conflicts in exchange, custody, market making and proprietary trading
- White papers: content, accuracy, updates and responsibility
- Marketing communications, risk warnings and complaints handling
- Listing and admission policies and token due diligence
