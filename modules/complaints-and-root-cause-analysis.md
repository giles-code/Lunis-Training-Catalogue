## Aim and Purpose

Handling complaints fairly and using complaint data to identify and fix the root causes of customer harm across the firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a complaints process that delivers fair and timely outcomes
- Apply root cause analysis to complaints and other customer data
- Identify when wider remediation or redress is required

## Syllabus

- Complaint handling standards and timelines
- Root cause analysis techniques
- From individual complaints to systemic issues
- Customer remediation and redress programmes
