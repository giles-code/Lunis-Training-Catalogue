## Aim and Purpose

How to assess a firm's business-wide risk assessment, customer due diligence, monitoring and reporting arrangements against the FATF Recommendations and the risk-based approach.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate a firm's business-wide and customer risk assessments
- Assess customer due diligence, transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting
- Identify the red flags of an ineffective AML/CFT framework

## Syllabus

- The risk-based approach and the business-wide risk assessment
- Customer due diligence, enhanced due diligence and ongoing monitoring
- Suspicious activity reporting to the national FIU and the MLRO role
- Sample testing of customer files and alert handling
