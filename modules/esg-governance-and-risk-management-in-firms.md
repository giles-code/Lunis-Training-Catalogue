## Aim and Purpose

How boards and management should govern sustainability risk, embed it in risk appetite and the three lines of defence, and develop credible transition plans.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess board oversight and accountability for sustainability risk
- Evaluate how sustainability risk is embedded in risk appetite, policies and controls
- Challenge the credibility of a firm's transition plan and targets

## Syllabus

- Board skills, accountability and remuneration links
- Risk appetite, limits and integration into the three lines of defence
- Transition plans: targets, interim milestones and implementation
- Red flags and supervisory questions on ESG governance
