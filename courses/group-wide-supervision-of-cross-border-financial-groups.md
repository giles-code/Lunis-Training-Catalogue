## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to equip participants with the skills to supervise financial groups operating across multiple jurisdictions on a consolidated, group-wide basis, including assessing intra-group exposures and the risks of complex group structures.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior supervisors involved in cross-border/group supervision and international cooperation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the concept and purpose of group-wide, consolidated supervision
- Describe common cross-border group structures and their risks
- Assess intra-group exposures and contagion risk
- Evaluate the adequacy of group risk management and governance
- Identify the risks posed by complex or opaque group structures
- Formulate a group-wide supervisory risk assessment

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Foundations of group-wide supervision (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain consolidated supervision and group structures*
- Why supervisors look at groups, not just legal entities
- Common cross-border group structures
- Consolidated versus solo supervision
- Regulatory perimeter issues in group supervision
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: mapping a complex group structure

**Session 2: Intra-group exposures and contagion risk (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess intra-group exposures and contagion risk*
- Types of intra-group exposure: funding, guarantees, shared services
- Contagion channels within a group
- Assessing group risk management and governance
- Group-level versus entity-level risk appetite
Activities:
- Exercise: map intra-group exposures for a sample group and identify contagion channels

**Session 3: Complex structures and supervisory application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify structural risks and produce a group-wide assessment*
- Risks of opaque or unnecessarily complex structures
- Ring-fencing and structural subordination
- Case study: a complex cross-border group
Activities:
- Case study: produce a group-wide supervisory risk assessment

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: consolidated supervision, intra-group exposures, structural risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Foundations of group-wide supervision (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Intra-group exposures and contagion risk (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Complex structures and supervisory application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Group-wide supervision looks through legal entities to the risks of the group as a whole
- Intra-group exposures can transmit risk quickly across borders and legal entities
- Structural complexity is itself a risk factor, not just a description of the group
