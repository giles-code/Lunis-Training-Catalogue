## Aim and Purpose

MiCA's market abuse regime and crypto-native manipulation, illicit finance typologies in crypto markets, and the AML/CFT and Travel Rule obligations that supervisors test at CASPs.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain MiCA's prohibitions on insider dealing, unlawful disclosure and manipulation
- Assess CASP surveillance, suspicious order reporting and AML/CFT controls
- Evaluate Travel Rule compliance, including transfers involving self-hosted wallets

## Syllabus

- Wash trading, pump-and-dump schemes, spoofing and MEV
- Laundering typologies: mixers, mules, rapid layering and sanctions evasion
- The Transfer of Funds Regulation and the EU AML package for CASPs
- Blockchain analytics and coordination with the national FIU
