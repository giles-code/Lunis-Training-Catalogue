## Aim and Purpose

How to develop a financial centre strategy grounded in evidence, choose credible niches and align ministries, agencies, the regulator and industry behind it.

## Learning Objectives

- Diagnose the jurisdiction's current position using data and stakeholder evidence
- Select target sectors and niches that build on genuine strengths
- Design governance for the strategy across government, regulator and industry

## Syllabus

- Diagnostic tools: sector mapping, benchmarking and competitor analysis
- Niche selection: funds, insurance, fintech, digital assets and sustainable finance
- Public-private partnership models and industry bodies
- Avoiding over-reliance on a single sector or source of business
