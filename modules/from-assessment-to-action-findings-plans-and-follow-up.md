## Aim and Purpose

Brings the toolkit together: choosing proportionate supervisory tools, writing findings that firms understand and act on, setting a supervisory plan and tracking remediation to closure.

## Learning Objectives

- Select proportionate supervisory tools and interventions for the risks identified
- Write clear, evidenced findings and a risk mitigation programme
- Track remediation and decide when to close or escalate

## Syllabus

- The supervisory toolkit: from letters to formal requirements
- Writing findings: issue, evidence, risk, required action and deadline
- Supervisory plans, follow-up and validating remediation
- Workshop: end-to-end risk assessment of a case-study firm
