## Aim and Purpose

Why valuation of private assets relies heavily on judgement, how fees and conflicts can distort valuations and investor outcomes, and how supervisors challenge valuation governance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain valuation methods for private equity and private credit assets
- Assess valuation governance, independence and use of third-party valuers
- Identify red flags such as stale marks and valuation smoothing

## Syllabus

- Valuation methods and the treatment of stressed and amended loans
- Valuation committees, independent valuers and audit
- Fees, expenses and conflicts in transactions between funds
- Case study: challenging a private credit portfolio valuation
