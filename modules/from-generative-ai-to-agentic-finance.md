## Aim and Purpose

What AI agents are, how they differ from chatbots and traditional automation, and where they are beginning to act and transact in financial services.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI agents plan, use tools and act on behalf of users
- Distinguish assistive, delegated and autonomous agent models
- Identify current and near-term agentic use cases in the firm's sector

## Syllabus

- Agents, tools, memory and multi-agent systems
- From advice to action: agents that execute, trade and pay
- The layers of agentic finance: governance, decision, assets and settlement
- Use cases across banking, payments, investment and insurance
