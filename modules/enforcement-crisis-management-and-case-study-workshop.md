## Aim and Purpose

Using MiCA's graduated supervisory and enforcement measures, managing a CASP in distress through to orderly wind-down, and applying the whole programme to a realistic case.

## Learning Objectives

- Select proportionate supervisory measures, sanctions and public warnings
- Manage a CASP crisis from early distress to orderly wind-down
- Apply the programme to a realistic CASP supervision case

## Syllabus

- From supervisory measures to fines and withdrawal of authorisation
- Lessons from crypto exchange and crypto lender failures
- Return of client assets and cross-border coordination in a failure
- Workshop: supervising a troubled cross-border CASP
