## Aim and Purpose

The legal tools for obtaining financial records and information, how to draft applications that are precise and justified, and how to work effectively with the institutions that must comply.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main powers to obtain financial information and their thresholds
- Draft focused, justified applications for production and disclosure orders
- Manage institution responses, deadlines and data formats

## Syllabus

- Production, disclosure, account monitoring and customer information orders
- Search, seizure and powers to obtain electronic records
- Drafting applications: necessity, proportionality and specificity
- Working with institutions: contacts, formats and confidentiality
