## Aim and Purpose

How the board of a regulated subsidiary discharges its own duties while operating within group strategy, policies and reporting lines, and what supervisors mean by a local board that is not a rubber stamp.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the duties of subsidiary directors to the entity, its customers and its regulators
- Evaluate the balance between group policies, matrix reporting and local decision-making
- Apply practical tests of whether the local board genuinely governs

## Syllabus

- Directors' duties in a subsidiary: whose interests come first
- Group policies, local adoption and documented deviations
- Matrix management, dual-hatted executives and independent directors
- Board question set: is our board governing or ratifying
