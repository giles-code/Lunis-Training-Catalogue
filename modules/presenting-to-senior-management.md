## Aim and Purpose

How to prepare papers and presentations for senior management and decision-making committees that lead with the message, frame the decision and handle questions with confidence.

## Learning Objectives

- Structure a paper or presentation around the decision required
- Deliver key messages with clarity, brevity and confidence
- Handle challenging questions from senior decision-makers

## Syllabus

- Writing for decision: the one-page summary and recommendation
- Structuring a presentation: message first, evidence second
- Presence, delivery and managing nerves
- Skills practice: a short briefing to a senior panel
