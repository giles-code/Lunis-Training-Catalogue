## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors assess whether a manager is properly governed and genuinely in control of the activities it delegates, including in cross-border and platform structures.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate board composition, independence and oversight of investment risk
- Assess substance, delegation and cross-border management structures
- Identify letter-box and white-label red flags

## Syllabus

- Governance, fit and proper requirements and key function holders
- Delegation of portfolio and risk management and retained oversight
- Cross-border management structures and substance tests
- Host platforms and white-label managers: supervisory concerns
