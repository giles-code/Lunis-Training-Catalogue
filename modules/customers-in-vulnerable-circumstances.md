## Aim and Purpose

How to recognise customers who may be in vulnerable circumstances, respond with care and flexibility, and record and share information appropriately.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the drivers and signs of vulnerability
- Respond to vulnerable customers with appropriate support and adjustments
- Record and share information sensitively and in line with data protection rules

## Syllabus

- Drivers of vulnerability: health, life events, resilience and capability
- Spotting signs in conversations, behaviour and account activity
- Practical adjustments, third-party support and powers of attorney
- Financial abuse, scams and when to escalate
