## Aim and Purpose

How to prepare and deliver concise, persuasive presentations to internal committees, panels and senior decision-makers, and how to handle questions.

## Learning Objectives

- Prepare a clear, evidence-based paper and presentation
- Present recommendations persuasively and concisely
- Respond to challenging questions from senior colleagues

## Syllabus

- Knowing the audience and the decision required
- Structuring papers and slides for decision-makers
- Delivery, timing and confident body language
- Exercise: presenting a case to a mock committee
