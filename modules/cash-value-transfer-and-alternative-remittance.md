## Aim and Purpose

How criminal cash is moved, stored and converted, how informal value transfer and alternative remittance operate, and the evidence investigators can obtain about them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how cash proceeds are placed and moved
- Describe informal value transfer and alternative remittance systems
- Identify evidence sources for cash-based and informal transfers

## Syllabus

- Cash-intensive businesses and cash deposits
- Cross-border cash movements and declarations
- Informal value transfer and alternative remittance
- Evidencing cash: seizures, ledgers and communications
