## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by preparing the firm for risk-based supervision, inspections and enforcement, and for managing a crisis through to recovery or orderly exit.

## Learning Objectives

- Prepare for and host supervisory inspections and information requests
- Respond proportionately to supervisory measures and enforcement action
- Lead the firm's crisis response, drawing on lessons from major failures

## Syllabus

- How risk-based supervision allocates attention and what triggers escalation
- Inspection readiness, management interviews and remediation tracking
- Enforcement tools, rights of defence and lessons from major failures
- Workshop: crisis simulation and the firm's compliance action plan
