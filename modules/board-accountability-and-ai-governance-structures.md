## Aim and Purpose

Where accountability for AI sits, what a sound governance framework looks like, and how oversight is divided between the board, its committees and management.

## Learning Objectives

- Define the board's responsibilities for AI and distinguish them from management's
- Evaluate the firm's AI governance framework, policies and model inventory
- Allocate oversight across committees and the three lines of defence

## Syllabus

- Directors' duties and senior executive accountability for AI-driven decisions
- Elements of a governance framework: policy, inventory, ownership and escalation
- Committee mandates: board, risk, audit and technology
- Common governance failures and their consequences
