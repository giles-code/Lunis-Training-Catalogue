## Aim and Purpose

Why complex AI models are hard to interpret, what explainability techniques can realistically deliver, and how model risk management should extend to machine learning and generative AI.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the trade-off between model complexity and explainability
- Apply model risk management principles to AI and machine learning
- Evaluate a firm's model documentation, validation and monitoring

## Syllabus

- Black-box models and interpretation techniques, from feature importance to counterfactuals
- Explainability expectations by use case, from credit decisions to trading
- Extending model risk frameworks to machine learning and generative AI
- Supervisory questions on drift, retraining and independent validation
