## Aim and Purpose

How operational analysis is applied to the cases FIUs see most often, from fraud and corruption to drug trafficking, tax crime and terrorist financing, and the financial indicators each tends to show.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise the financial footprint of the main predicate offences
- Apply case-specific indicators to prioritise and develop analysis
- Identify when a case needs specialist or multi-agency input

## Syllabus

- Fraud, corruption and politically exposed persons
- Drug trafficking, organised crime and cash-intensive laundering
- Tax crime, trade-based laundering and professional enablers
- Terrorist financing and sanctions-related reports
