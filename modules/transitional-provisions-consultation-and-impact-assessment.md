## Aim and Purpose

How to move from an old regime to a new one without disruption, and how consultation and impact assessment improve legislation and its legitimacy.

## Learning Objectives

- Draft transitional, grandfathering and commencement provisions
- Plan consultation with industry, regulators and the public
- Prepare and use impact assessments and post-implementation reviews

## Syllabus

- Commencement, staged implementation and transitional periods
- Grandfathering, deemed authorisation and wind-down regimes
- Consultation design and handling of responses
- Regulatory impact assessment and post-implementation review
