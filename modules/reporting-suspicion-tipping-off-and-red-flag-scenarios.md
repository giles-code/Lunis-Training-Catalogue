## Aim and Purpose

How to report a suspicion internally, what happens next, how to avoid tipping off, and a closing set of role-based red-flag scenarios.

## Learning Objectives

- Report suspicions internally through the correct channel and at the right time
- Explain tipping off and how to handle customer contact after a report
- Apply the programme to realistic scenarios across front office, operations and support roles

## Syllabus

- Internal suspicious activity reports: what to include and when
- The money laundering reporting officer, the national FIU and what happens next
- Tipping off, prejudicing an investigation and managing customer contact
- Workshop: red-flag scenarios by role, what would you do?
