## Aim and Purpose

The SCA regime and its exemptions, how the reform clarifies scope, accessibility and responsibilities, and the operational and security risk requirements that now sit alongside DORA.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply SCA requirements, exemptions and transaction risk analysis
- Explain reform changes to SCA scope, accessibility and outsourcing of authentication
- Assess the interaction between payment security rules and DORA

## Syllabus

- SCA elements, dynamic linking and the main exemptions
- Merchant-initiated and mail and telephone order transactions
- Accessibility for persons with disabilities and customers without smartphones
- Payment security rules, DORA incident reporting and technical service providers
