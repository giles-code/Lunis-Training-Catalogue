## Aim and Purpose

How the firm supports applications by proposed owners and controllers, maps its ownership chain, and evidences source of wealth and source of funds to the authority's satisfaction.

## Learning Objectives

- Map ownership chains and identify controllers and beneficial owners
- Prepare controllers for the authority's assessment of reputation and financial soundness
- Evidence source of wealth and source of funds credibly

## Syllabus

- Control thresholds, acting in concert and indirect holdings
- Mapping ownership and beneficial ownership for the authority
- Source of wealth narratives and supporting documents
- Red flags the authority looks for: nominees, layered structures and secrecy
