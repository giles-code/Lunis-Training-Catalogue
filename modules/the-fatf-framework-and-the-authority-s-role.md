## Aim and Purpose

The international AML/CFT standards set by the FATF, how they are implemented nationally and what they require of supervisors.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the FATF Recommendations and the risk-based approach
- Explain the authority's responsibilities as an AML/CFT supervisor
- Recognise the importance of mutual evaluations and national risk assessments

## Syllabus

- The FATF Recommendations, Immediate Outcomes and methodology
- National risk assessments and the risk-based approach
- Supervisory obligations: licensing, monitoring and sanctions
- Mutual evaluations, follow-up and the consequences of grey-listing
