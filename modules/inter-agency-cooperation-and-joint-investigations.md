## Aim and Purpose

Models of cooperation between tax administrations, FIUs, police and prosecutors, from information-sharing gateways to joint investigation teams, and how to make them work in practice.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare models of domestic inter-agency cooperation
- Use the legal gateways between tax, FIU and law-enforcement agencies
- Plan a joint investigation with clear governance and roles

## Syllabus

- Models of cooperation: gateways, secondments and joint task forces
- Tax access to FIU information and reporting of suspicions by tax officials
- Joint investigation teams and international tax crime cooperation
- Governance, confidentiality and case ownership
