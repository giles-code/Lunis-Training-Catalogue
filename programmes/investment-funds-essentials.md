## Aim and Purpose

To give staff at fund managers, administrators, depositaries and other service providers a practical, end-to-end grounding in investment funds and the industry that services them. Participants leave able to explain how a fund operates, who does what, why the rules exist and how their own work contributes to good investor outcomes.

## What It Covers

Investing and asset classes, fund structures and legal forms, types of investment fund, why and how funds are regulated, governance and delegation, the main service providers, fund administration, dealing and valuation, portfolio investment and risk, distribution and sustainable investment, and the fund lifecycle and careers in the industry.

## Intended Audience

- New joiners and graduates at fund managers, administrators, depositaries and other fund service providers
- Operations, client service, finance, technology and support staff who need a grounding in funds
- Staff moving into the funds industry from banking, insurance or other sectors

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what investment funds are, how they work and why investors use them
- Describe the main fund structures, legal forms and types of fund
- Identify the parties to a fund and the role each service provider plays
- Explain why funds are regulated and how governance and delegation work in practice
- Describe how funds are administered, valued, invested, distributed and wound up
- Recognise the risks, trends and career paths across the funds industry

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about ninety minutes each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two-day induction programme or as a series of short sessions. Modules combine short briefings with real-world examples and quick quizzes, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's business and fund range.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

No prior knowledge of investment funds is required. The programme is suitable for new joiners, graduates and staff moving into the funds industry.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation (2017)
- IOSCO, Principles for the Valuation of Collective Investment Schemes (2013)
- IOSCO, Recommendations for Liquidity Risk Management for Collective Investment Schemes (2018)
- IOSCO, Recommendations on Sustainability-Related Practices, Policies, Procedures and Disclosure in Asset Management (2021)
