## Aim and Purpose

How terrorist and proliferation financing differ from money laundering, why small and legitimate-looking sums matter, and the indicators staff should be aware of.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how terrorist financing differs from money laundering
- Recognise indicators of terrorist and proliferation financing
- Describe why small and apparently legitimate transactions can be significant

## Syllabus

- Sources of terrorist finance: legitimate income, crime and abuse of charities
- Small sums, crowdfunding and informal value transfer systems
- Proliferation financing and dual-use goods in plain terms
- Indicators, and why staff should not dismiss low-value concerns
