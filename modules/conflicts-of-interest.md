## Aim and Purpose

How conflicts arise for boards and individual directors, including in group structures, smaller firms and multiple directorships, and how they should be identified, managed, monitored and disclosed.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify conflicts of interest at board, group and individual level
- Apply a conflicts policy, register and management arrangements
- Judge when a conflict should be disclosed, mitigated or avoided altogether

## Syllabus

- Sources of conflict: group links, delegates, fees and personal interests
- Multiple directorships and full-time roles elsewhere in the group
- Smaller firms and condensed structures: when senior staff wear several hats
- Conflicts registers, board minutes and evidencing that conflicts are managed
