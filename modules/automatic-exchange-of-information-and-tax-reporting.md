## Aim and Purpose

What automatic exchange of information under the OECD Common Reporting Standard requires of financial institutions, how arrangements to avoid it work, and how reporting extends to crypto-assets.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain due diligence and reporting obligations under the Common Reporting Standard
- Recognise arrangements designed to avoid automatic exchange
- Assess the accuracy and governance of the firm's tax reporting

## Syllabus

- Reporting financial institutions, reportable accounts and controlling persons
- Self-certification, indicia searches and change in circumstances
- Avoidance arrangements, mandatory disclosure and the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework
- Data quality, reconciliations, penalties and assurance over reporting
