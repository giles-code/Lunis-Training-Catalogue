## Aim and Purpose

Why a board can have the right composition, committees and terms of reference and still fail to oversee the firm, and what the board's dynamics reveal about its effectiveness.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish governance structures from the behaviours that make them work
- Explain the board's role in strategy, risk appetite and holding management to account
- Identify the common impediments to board effectiveness

## Syllabus

- Form versus substance: what a structural governance review misses
- The board's core tasks: direction, oversight and accountability
- Common impediments: poor information, weak challenge and ineffective chairing
- Seeing your board the way a supervisor does
