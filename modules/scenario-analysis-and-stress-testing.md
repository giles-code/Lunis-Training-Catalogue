## Aim and Purpose

What climate scenario analysis can and cannot tell the board, how to read the results critically and how to use them in strategic and capital decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how climate scenarios are built and the main scenario families
- Interpret results and identify weaknesses in data, models and assumptions
- Use scenario outcomes to inform strategy, risk appetite and capital planning

## Syllabus

- Orderly, disorderly and hot-house world scenarios
- Time horizons, data gaps and model limitations
- Supervisory climate stress tests and what they reveal
- Turning scenario results into board decisions
