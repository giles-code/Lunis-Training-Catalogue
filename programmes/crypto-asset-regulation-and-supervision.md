## Aim and Purpose

To equip supervisors with the practical tools to apply a comprehensive crypto-asset regime across the full supervisory lifecycle, from authorisation and risk-based supervision to enforcement and crisis management. Participants leave able to test business models, governance, prudential resources, custody and financial crime controls, and to look beneath firms' documentation to how they actually operate.

## What It Covers

The crypto-asset ecosystem, international standards and comprehensive crypto-asset regimes, service provider business models, governance and internal controls, prudential resources, authorisation, risk-based supervision, custody and operational resilience, market abuse, AML/CFT and sanctions, stablecoins and systemic risk, and cross-border cooperation, enforcement and crisis management.

## Intended Audience

- Supervisors and authorisation staff responsible for crypto-asset service providers and stablecoin issuers
- Risk, prudential, enforcement and financial crime specialists covering digital finance
- Senior managers responsible for the authority's crypto-asset supervisory strategy

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the international standards for crypto-assets and how comprehensive regimes apply them to issuers and service providers
- Assess business models, governance, internal controls and prudential resources of crypto-asset firms
- Evaluate authorisation applications and operate a risk-based approach to ongoing supervision and inspection
- Assess custody, safeguarding and operational resilience arrangements for crypto-asset operations
- Evaluate market abuse, money laundering, terrorist financing and sanctions risks and the controls firms use to manage them
- Apply supervisory, enforcement and crisis management tools, including cross-border cooperation and stablecoin oversight

## Structure and Format

Twelve modules of about four hours each, grouped into five pathways and delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, as a multi-week programme or as a series of half-day sessions with pre-reading. Each module combines a technical briefing with case studies, red-flag exercises and practical supervisory tools, and pathways or individual modules can be selected or tailored to the authority's regime.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Completion of Crypto and Blockchain Fundamentals, or equivalent experience of crypto-asset markets, is helpful. Some experience of authorisation, supervision or enforcement work is recommended but not mandatory.

## Core Reading

- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Crypto-Asset Activities and Markets (2023)
- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Global Stablecoin Arrangements (2023)
- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets (2023)
- FATF, Updated Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (2021)
