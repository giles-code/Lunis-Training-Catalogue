## Aim and Purpose

How to assess board composition, challenge and accountability, and whether the firm's arrangements for assessing the fitness and propriety of key individuals are robust and ongoing.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess board composition, committee structure and quality of challenge
- Evaluate the firm's own fitness and propriety assessments of key individuals
- Identify governance weaknesses that undermine compliance

## Syllabus

- Board structure, independence, committees and senior manager accountability
- Board papers, minutes and evidence of effective challenge
- Fitness and propriety: competence, integrity, financial soundness and time commitment
- Red flags: dominant individuals, weak challenge and rubber-stamping
