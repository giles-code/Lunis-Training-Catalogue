## Aim and Purpose

The use of natural language processing, network analysis and machine learning in supervision, what these techniques add to traditional analysis, and where they fall short.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how text analytics, network analysis and machine learning are applied in supervision
- Evaluate the explainability, bias and validation challenges of supervisory AI
- Identify suitable and unsuitable use cases for advanced analytics

## Syllabus

- Natural language processing of documents, returns and complaints
- Network analysis of ownership, transactions and interconnectedness
- Machine learning and generative AI for supervisory tasks
- Explainability, bias and validation of supervisory models
