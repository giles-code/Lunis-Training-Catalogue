## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors gather and weigh evidence, test management's assertions rather than accept them, and decide when the evidence is sufficient to support a conclusion.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the reliability, relevance and sufficiency of supervisory evidence
- Apply professional scepticism to firms' assertions and management information
- Challenge firms constructively while keeping an open mind

## Syllabus

- Types of evidence: documents, data, interviews and observation
- Corroboration, triangulation and testing assertions
- Red flags: comfort without evidence and over-polished narratives
- When is enough evidence enough to conclude
