## Aim and Purpose

Managing ICT third-party risk across the contract lifecycle, the mandatory contractual provisions, concentration and exit, and the register of information as both a compliance tool and a supervisory dataset.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply DORA's principles for ICT third-party risk and the third-party risk strategy
- Assess contracts against the key provisions, especially for critical or important functions
- Maintain and use the register of information, including subcontracting chains

## Syllabus

- Pre-contract due diligence, concentration risk and intragroup providers
- Key contractual provisions, audit rights, termination and exit strategies
- Register of information: structure, data quality and submission to authorities
- Subcontracting of ICT services supporting critical or important functions
