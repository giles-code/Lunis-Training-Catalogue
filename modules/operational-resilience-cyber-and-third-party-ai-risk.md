## Aim and Purpose

How AI systems fail and can be attacked, how reliance on a few AI vendors concentrates risk, and whether the firm can withstand and recover from an AI failure.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise how AI fails, including drift, data poisoning and prompt injection
- Assess concentration and dependency on third-party AI providers
- Evaluate resilience testing and incident escalation to the board

## Syllabus

- Drift, silent degradation and late detection of AI problems
- Adversarial attacks and the board's cyber oversight role
- Vendor and foundation-model dependency: due diligence and exit planning
- Operational resilience and outsourcing expectations applied to AI
