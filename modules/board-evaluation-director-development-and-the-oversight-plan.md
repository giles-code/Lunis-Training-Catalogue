## Aim and Purpose

How boards evaluate their own effectiveness and develop their directors, ending with each participant drafting a risk-based oversight plan for their own board.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a proportionate board and director evaluation process
- Plan induction, continuing development and succession for the board
- Produce a draft risk-based oversight plan for the participant's board

## Syllabus

- Internal and external evaluations, and tracking actions to completion
- Induction, continuing professional development and succession planning
- Due diligence before joining a fund board
- Workshop: drafting the board's risk-based oversight plan
