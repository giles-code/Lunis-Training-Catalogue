## Aim and Purpose

A map of where cryptography protects the financial system, from customer channels and payment messaging to hardware security modules, certificates and digital signatures on contracts and ledgers.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main uses of cryptography across financial services
- Analyse which systems and data carry the highest quantum exposure
- Explain why cryptography is often invisible and poorly documented within firms

## Syllabus

- Secure communications, certificates and public key infrastructure
- Payment cards, messaging networks and hardware security modules
- Digital signatures, identity and authentication
- Embedded cryptography in legacy and vendor systems
