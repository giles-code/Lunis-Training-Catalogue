## Aim and Purpose

How boards evaluate themselves, what regulators look for in board effectiveness reviews, how directors keep their skills current, and a capstone in which each participant builds a board effectiveness action plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Design an internal or externally facilitated board evaluation
- Plan ongoing development for the board and individual directors
- Produce a practical board effectiveness action plan

## Syllabus

- Board evaluation: scope, methods, frequency and acting on findings
- What supervisors look for in board effectiveness reviews
- Director development, upskilling and moving from executive to non-executive
- Capstone: a board effectiveness action plan for each participant
