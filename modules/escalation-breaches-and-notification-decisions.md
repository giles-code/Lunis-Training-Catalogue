## Aim and Purpose

How to recognise when an issue must be escalated, write an escalation note that gets a decision, and reason through whether a breach is significant enough to notify the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify escalation triggers and the right route in the firm
- Assess the significance of a breach and the case for notification
- Write an escalation note that sets out facts, risk and the decision required

## Syllabus

- Escalation triggers, routes and timing
- Assessing a breach: cause, impact, customers affected and recurrence
- Notification judgements and the cost of late or partial disclosure
- Exercise: escalating an emerging issue to senior management
