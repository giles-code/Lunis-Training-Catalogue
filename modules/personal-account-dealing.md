## Aim and Purpose

The rules on staff dealing for themselves and connected persons, why pre-clearance and holding periods exist, and how to avoid conflicts and front running.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and scope of the firm's personal account dealing policy
- Apply pre-clearance, holding period and reporting requirements
- Recognise dealing that creates conflicts of interest or market abuse risk

## Syllabus

- Accounts, instruments and connected persons covered by the rules
- Pre-clearance, restricted lists, holding periods and disclosure
- Front running, derivatives, crypto-assets and other grey areas
- Monitoring, breaches and what happens when things go wrong
