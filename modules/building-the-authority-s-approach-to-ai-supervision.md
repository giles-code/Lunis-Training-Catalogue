## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a strategic view of how the authority should supervise AI risk, balancing innovation against conduct, prudential and systemic risk, and building lasting capability.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the strategic choices facing authorities as AI adoption accelerates
- Compare international approaches and coordination initiatives on AI in finance
- Design options for AI supervisory capability, policy and resourcing

## Syllabus

- International coordination on AI: FSB, IOSCO and BIS workstreams
- Embedding AI oversight across existing supervisory functions
- Generative AI and autonomous agents: emerging supervisory questions
- Workshop: the authority's AI supervision roadmap
