## Aim and Purpose

A practical framework for judging how a decision was made rather than how it turned out, and how boards learn by revisiting major decisions and feeding lessons into future practice.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply a structured test to the process behind significant board decisions
- Evaluate whether minutes record the rationale for a decision, not just the outcome
- Design a routine for revisiting major decisions and learning from them

## Syllabus

- Why, why now, alternatives, assumptions and downside
- Success measures and how the board will judge the decision later
- Recording rationale and dissent in the minutes
- Post-decision reviews and acting on repeat findings
