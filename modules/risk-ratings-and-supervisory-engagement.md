## Aim and Purpose

What impact and risk ratings mean, how they drive the intensity of supervision and what the firm can do to understand and influence its risk profile.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish impact from assessed risk
- Explain how ratings shape supervisory engagement
- Identify the drivers of the firm's risk profile

## Syllabus

- Impact, risk and the intensity of supervision
- How ratings are communicated to the firm
- Correcting factual errors and providing context
- Improving the risk profile over time
