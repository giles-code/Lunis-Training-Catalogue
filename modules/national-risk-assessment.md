## Aim and Purpose

How to plan, conduct and use a national assessment of money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing risks, including sectoral and thematic assessments.

## Learning Objectives

- Design the scope, methodology and governance of a national risk assessment
- Gather and analyse threat, vulnerability and consequence data
- Translate risk findings into policy priorities and resource allocation

## Syllabus

- Methodologies, data sources and private sector input
- Threats, vulnerabilities and consequences by sector and product
- Sectoral assessments: legal persons, virtual assets and non-profit organisations
- Communicating results and keeping the assessment current
