## Aim and Purpose

To give FIU and law-enforcement managers a practical understanding of how financial information-sharing partnerships work and what makes them effective. Participants leave able to choose an appropriate partnership model, put lawful gateways and safeguards in place, run tactical and strategic sharing, and measure and demonstrate results.

## What It Covers

The case for public-private partnerships, partnership models and governance, legal gateways for sharing, data protection and confidentiality safeguards, tactical and strategic information sharing, private-to-private sharing and collaborative analytics, and measuring results.

## Intended Audience

- FIU heads, partnership managers and senior analysts
- Law-enforcement leads for economic crime, fraud and terrorist financing
- Policy, legal and data protection officers advising on information-sharing arrangements

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purposes and benefits of public-private financial information sharing
- Compare partnership models and choose one suited to the national framework
- Identify the legal gateways that permit sharing and the limits they impose
- Apply data protection, confidentiality and tipping-off safeguards
- Run tactical and strategic sharing that produces actionable results
- Measure and demonstrate the partnership's outcomes and value

## Structure and Format

Six modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a briefing with case studies of partnership models and design exercises, ending in a partnership planning workshop, and modules can be selected or tailored to the national legal framework.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of financial intelligence, investigation or AML/CFT policy is helpful. No legal or data protection expertise is required.

## Core Reading

- FATF (2017), Guidance on Private Sector Information Sharing
- FATF (2021), Stocktake on Data Pooling, Collaborative Analytics and Data Protection
- FATF (2022), Partnering in the Fight Against Financial Crime: Data Protection, Technology and Private Sector Information Sharing
- Egmont Group (2013), Principles for Information Exchange Between Financial Intelligence Units
