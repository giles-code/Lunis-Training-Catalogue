## Aim and Purpose

Building and evidencing ICT risk management and operational resilience for the hybrid on-chain and off-chain architecture of crypto-asset firms, including cloud concentration and complex outsourcing chains.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify ICT and operational risks specific to crypto-asset operations
- Design ICT risk management, incident reporting and resilience testing
- Manage third-party and cloud dependencies with credible exit arrangements

## Syllabus

- Key infrastructure, API dependencies and smart contract upgrade risk
- ICT incident classification, reporting and internal escalation
- Business continuity, penetration testing and scenario exercises
- Third-party ICT contracts, concentration and exit planning
