## Aim and Purpose

How platform design, personalisation and data use shape consumer decisions, the risks of harm and exclusion, and the evidence supervisors should seek on outcomes in embedded journeys.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify consumer harms from choice architecture, dark patterns and frictionless credit
- Assess disclosure, suitability and complaints handling in embedded journeys
- Evaluate the financial inclusion benefits and risks of platform finance

## Syllabus

- Choice architecture, defaults and dark patterns
- Embedded credit and buy now, pay later products
- Personalised pricing, profiling and fair treatment
- Who owns the complaint: redress across the partnership chain
