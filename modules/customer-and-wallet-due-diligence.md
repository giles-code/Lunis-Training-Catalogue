## Aim and Purpose

How to onboard and keep under review customers whose activity involves crypto-assets, including source of funds and wealth, ownership of self-hosted wallets and risk-based enhanced due diligence.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a customer risk assessment that reflects crypto-asset activity
- Verify source of funds and wealth derived from crypto-assets
- Apply proportionate controls to self-hosted wallets and peer-to-peer transfers

## Syllabus

- Customer risk factors for crypto-asset activity and business customers
- Source of funds and wealth: using on-chain history as evidence
- Self-hosted wallets: proof of ownership, risk scoring and limits
- Enhanced due diligence, periodic review and trigger events
