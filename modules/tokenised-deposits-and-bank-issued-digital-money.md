## Aim and Purpose

How banks can represent deposits on a ledger, how tokenised deposits differ from stablecoins, and the strategic, prudential and interoperability choices facing banks and their corporate clients.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how tokenised deposits work and how they differ from stablecoins
- Evaluate use cases in treasury, trade finance and wholesale settlement
- Assess the prudential, deposit protection and interoperability implications

## Syllabus

- Tokenised deposits versus stablecoins: claim, regulation and protection
- Programmable payments, corporate treasury and intraday liquidity
- Interbank interoperability and settlement in central bank money
- Basel Committee treatment of tokenised traditional assets
