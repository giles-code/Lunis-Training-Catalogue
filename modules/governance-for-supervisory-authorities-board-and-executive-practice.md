## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the governance structures and practices that support effective decision-making and accountability within a supervisory authority's own board and executive.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior supervisory staff involved in setting or reviewing their authority's strategy, governance, or resourcing decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the governance structures typical of supervisory authorities
- Describe the respective roles of board and executive
- Assess board effectiveness against recognised good-practice criteria
- Evaluate executive accountability mechanisms
- Identify governance risks specific to public regulatory bodies

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Governance structures for supervisory authorities (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain governance structures and board/executive roles*
- Typical governance models for regulatory bodies
- The board's role: oversight versus operational involvement
- The executive's role and accountability to the board
- Distinguishing regulatory governance from commercial corporate governance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: comparing governance models across participants' own authorities

**Session 2: Assessing board effectiveness (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess board effectiveness and executive accountability*
- Good-practice criteria for board effectiveness
- Board composition, skills and independence
- Executive accountability mechanisms
- Common governance weaknesses in public bodies
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample board's effectiveness against good-practice criteria

**Session 3: Governance risks and case study (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify governance risks specific to regulatory bodies*
- Governance risks: political interference, capture, groupthink
- Managing conflicts of interest at board level
- Case study: a governance failure in a regulatory body
Activities:
- Case study: analyse a governance failure and propose safeguards

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: structures, effectiveness, accountability, risks
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Governance structures for supervisory authorities (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing board effectiveness (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Governance risks and case study (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- A supervisory authority's own governance quality shapes the credibility of everything it does
- Clear separation between board oversight and executive operation is essential
- Regulatory bodies face distinct governance risks, particularly around independence and capture

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with governance, strategy & leadership is helpful but not essential.
