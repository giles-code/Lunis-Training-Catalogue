## Aim and Purpose

How a supervisory authority interprets its statutory mandate, ranks its primary and secondary objectives, and turns them into a credible strategic plan and work programme.

## Learning Objectives

- Interpret a statutory mandate and the hierarchy of its objectives
- Explain the role of strategic planning in a supervisory authority
- Align strategy with mandate, international standards and public expectations

## Syllabus

- Interpreting statutory objectives: primary, secondary and implicit duties
- IOSCO and Basel principles relating to the regulator
- Strategic planning frameworks for public authorities
- From strategy to annual business plan and work programme
