## Aim and Purpose

How to gather client information, assess suitability for advice and portfolio management, apply appropriateness tests for non-advised business, and evidence good outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply suitability requirements to advice and discretionary management
- Apply appropriateness tests and warnings for non-advised services
- Test advice quality through file reviews and outcome data

## Syllabus

- Client objectives, risk tolerance, capacity for loss and knowledge
- Suitability reports, ongoing suitability and portfolio drift
- Appropriateness tests, warnings and execution-only business
- Exercise: reviewing a sample of advice files
