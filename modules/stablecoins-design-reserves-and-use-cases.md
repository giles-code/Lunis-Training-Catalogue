## Aim and Purpose

How stablecoins are structured and used, what determines whether they hold their value, and the questions a firm should ask before issuing, distributing, holding or accepting them.

## Learning Objectives

- Classify stablecoin designs and stabilisation mechanisms
- Evaluate reserve quality, redemption rights and run risk
- Assess use cases in payments, trading and cross-border settlement

## Syllabus

- Single-currency, multi-asset and algorithmic designs
- Reserve composition, custody, attestation and redemption
- De-pegging episodes and lessons for firms
- FSB recommendations on global stablecoin arrangements
