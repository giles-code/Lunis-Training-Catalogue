## Aim and Purpose

How lawyers, accountants, tax advisers, trust and company service providers and financial professionals facilitate tax crime and laundering, and the tools available to investigate and disrupt them.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the roles professional enablers play in tax and laundering schemes
- Recognise indicators of complicit or negligent professional involvement
- Apply criminal, civil, regulatory and disruptive responses to enablers

## Syllabus

- Typologies of professional enablement
- Legal professional privilege and access to professional records
- Working with professional supervisors and self-regulatory bodies
- Disruption strategies: prosecution, penalties and deterrence
