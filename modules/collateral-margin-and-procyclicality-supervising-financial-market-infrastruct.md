## Aim and Purpose

How initial and variation margin and collateral policies protect FMIs, the trade-off between prudence and procyclicality, and how supervisors assess margin models and haircuts.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate initial margin models, calibration and look-back periods
- Assess eligible collateral, haircuts and concentration limits
- Analyse procyclicality and the transparency of margin calls in stress

## Syllabus

- Initial and variation margin: purpose, methods and calibration
- Collateral eligibility, haircuts, concentration limits and reuse
- Procyclicality tools, anti-procyclicality buffers and margin transparency
- Lessons from margin spikes in commodity and bond market stress
