## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in applying regulatory liquidity stress-testing frameworks in practice, assessing redemption assumptions, liquidity management tools, and the adequacy of a fund's liquidity risk management.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment funds supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of fund liquidity stress testing
- Describe ESMA/ESRB liquidity stress-testing guidelines
- Apply redemption modelling techniques to a sample fund
- Assess the adequacy of liquidity management tools deployed by a fund
- Formulate a supervisory view on a fund's liquidity stress-testing framework

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Purpose of liquidity stress testing (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the purpose of liquidity stress testing*
- Why liquidity mismatches pose systemic and investor protection risk
- ESMA/ESRB guidelines on fund liquidity stress testing
- Key components of a liquidity stress test
- Regulatory reporting expectations for stress-testing results
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: liquidity stress episodes participants are aware of

**Session 2: Redemption modelling (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply redemption modelling techniques*
- Modelling redemption assumptions under stress scenarios
- Historical redemption behaviour versus stressed assumptions
- Asset-side liquidity assessment alongside liability-side redemption modelling
- Common modelling weaknesses
Activities:
- Exercise: apply redemption stress assumptions to a sample fund's liability profile

**Session 3: Liquidity management tools and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess liquidity management tools and formulate a supervisory view*
- Liquidity management tools: swing pricing, redemption gates, suspension
- Assessing whether tools are calibrated and usable in practice
- Case study: a fund's liquidity stress-testing framework under review
Activities:
- Case study: assess a sample fund's liquidity stress-testing framework and formulate a supervisory view

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: purpose, guidelines, redemption modelling, liquidity tools
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Purpose of liquidity stress testing (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Redemption modelling (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Liquidity management tools and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Liquidity stress testing must model genuinely stressed redemption behaviour, not just historical averages
- Liquidity management tools are only useful if they are actually usable when a fund needs them
- Both sides of the balance sheet — asset liquidity and redemption liability — must be assessed together
