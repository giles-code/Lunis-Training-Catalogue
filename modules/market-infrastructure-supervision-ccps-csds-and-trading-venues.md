## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the regulatory framework for central counterparties, central securities depositories, and trading venues, and the supervisory considerations specific to critical market infrastructure.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment services/capital markets supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of CCPs, CSDs and trading venues in market infrastructure
- Describe the key regulatory requirements for CCPs and CSDs
- Assess the systemic importance of market infrastructure entities
- Evaluate risk management arrangements at a sample market infrastructure entity
- Formulate a supervisory risk assessment for market infrastructure

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Market infrastructure fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the role of CCPs, CSDs and trading venues*
- The role of CCPs in central clearing and counterparty risk mitigation
- CSDs and securities settlement
- Trading venues: regulated markets, MTFs, OTFs
- Why market infrastructure requires distinct regulatory treatment
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: market infrastructure risk episodes participants are aware of

**Session 2: CCP and CSD requirements and systemic importance (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe key requirements and assess systemic importance*
- Key regulatory requirements for CCPs: margining, default management, capital
- Key regulatory requirements for CSDs: settlement discipline, capital
- Why market infrastructure entities are considered systemically critical
- Interconnectedness risk between infrastructure entities
Activities:
- Group exercise: map the systemic importance of a sample market infrastructure entity

**Session 3: Risk management and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate risk management and formulate an assessment*
- Assessing default management and margining arrangements
- Operational resilience considerations for market infrastructure
- Case study: a market infrastructure entity under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory risk assessment for a sample market infrastructure entity

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: infrastructure roles, CCP and CSD requirements, systemic importance, risk management
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Market infrastructure fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: CCP and CSD requirements and systemic importance (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Risk management and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Market infrastructure entities require a systemic risk lens, not just an entity-level supervisory approach
- Default management and margining arrangements are the core of CCP risk management and deserve close scrutiny
- Interconnectedness between infrastructure entities can transmit risk quickly across the whole market system

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with investment services & capital markets is helpful but not essential.
