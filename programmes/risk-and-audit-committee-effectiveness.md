## Aim and Purpose

To give committee chairs and members a practical understanding of how effective risk and audit committees work and the tools to oversee risk management, compliance, internal audit and external audit. Participants leave able to sharpen their committee's mandate and agenda, challenge the control functions and auditors, and evidence effective oversight to the board and supervisors.

## What It Covers

Committee mandates and composition, risk appetite frameworks, overseeing the chief risk officer, compliance and internal audit, external audit and financial reporting judgements, assurance mapping, reading risk MI, chairing the committee, reporting to the board and committee evaluation.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs and members of board risk and audit committees
- Non-executive and independent directors preparing to join a risk or audit committee
- Heads of risk, compliance and internal audit, and company secretaries supporting committees

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the distinct mandates of the risk and audit committees and how they interact
- Evaluate the firm's risk appetite framework and its use in decision-making
- Oversee the chief risk officer, compliance and internal audit, including their independence and resourcing
- Challenge external audit and significant financial reporting judgements
- Use assurance mapping and risk MI to identify gaps in oversight
- Assess committee effectiveness and agree an improvement plan

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two-day programme or as a series of committee briefing sessions. Each module combines a short briefing with case studies and a committee question set, and modules can be selected or tailored to the committee's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Board or senior management experience in financial services is helpful. No accounting or audit qualification is required.

## Core Reading

- Basel Committee, Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015)
- FSB, Principles for an Effective Risk Appetite Framework (2013)
- Basel Committee, The Internal Audit Function in Banks (2012)
- Institute of Internal Auditors, Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)
