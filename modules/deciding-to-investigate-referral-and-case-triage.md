## Aim and Purpose

How cases reach enforcement, the criteria for opening an investigation, and how triage directs limited resource to the cases with the greatest impact.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe referral routes from supervision and other sources into enforcement
- Apply criteria for deciding whether to open an investigation
- Prioritise a caseload using severity, evidential strength and public interest

## Syllabus

- Referral sources: supervision, whistleblowers, complaints and other authorities
- Statutory thresholds and the decision to investigate as a judgement
- Triage: severity, harm, evidential strength and public interest
- Exercise: triaging a set of sample referrals
