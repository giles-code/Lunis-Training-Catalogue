## Aim and Purpose

How mixers and tumblers, privacy-enhancing coins and self-hosted wallets obscure the audit trail, what can still be seen, and the controls and supervisory responses they call for.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how mixing, tumbling and coin-joining break transaction links
- Describe how privacy coins and privacy features limit traceability
- Assess service providers' policies on anonymity-enhanced transfers and unhosted wallets

## Syllabus

- Custodial and non-custodial mixers, tumblers and coin-joining
- Privacy-enhancing coins and privacy features: what remains visible
- Unhosted wallets and peer-to-peer transfers: risk and controls
- Red flags, enhanced due diligence and restrictions on exposure
