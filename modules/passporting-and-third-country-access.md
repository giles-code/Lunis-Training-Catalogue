## Aim and Purpose

How regional passporting, reliance and third-country regimes allow firms to serve clients across borders, the conditions and limits attached to them, and the governance needed to stay within them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main models of cross-border market access in generic terms
- Assess the regulatory perimeter risks of serving clients from outside their jurisdiction
- Design governance and controls that keep cross-border activity within its permissions

## Syllabus

- Passporting, equivalence, reliance and third-country regimes
- Reverse solicitation, delegation and other routes and their limits
- Losing access: planning for a change in regime or recognition
- Controls over cross-border marketing, onboarding and servicing
