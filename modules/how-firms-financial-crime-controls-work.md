## Aim and Purpose

The building blocks of a firm's financial crime framework and what good and poor controls look like in practice.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the main components of a firm's AML/CFT framework
- Distinguish effective controls from paper compliance
- Frame supervisory questions to test controls

## Syllabus

- Business-wide risk assessment, policies and governance
- Customer due diligence, beneficial ownership and enhanced due diligence
- Transaction monitoring, suspicious activity reporting and record-keeping
- The MLRO, compliance function, training and independent testing
