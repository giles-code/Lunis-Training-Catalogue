## Aim and Purpose

How fund governance is structured, how management companies are organised, and how the board and key personnel oversee delegated activities.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the governance structure of a fund and its management company
- Explain the role of the fund board and its directors
- Explain how key personnel and assurance functions oversee delegates

## Syllabus

- Governance structure of an investment fund
- Organisation and substance of management companies
- Fund board composition, duties and independence
- Key management personnel, assurance functions and delegate oversight
