## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together: recovery planning and loss allocation, orderly wind-down and resolution, preparing for supervisory assessment and cooperative oversight, and a workshop on an infrastructure under stress.

## Learning Objectives

- Design recovery plans, triggers and tools for uncovered losses and liquidity shortfalls
- Prepare orderly wind-down plans and support resolution planning
- Evidence PFMI compliance to supervisors and overseers in assessments and crises

## Syllabus

- Recovery tools: cash calls, variation margin gains haircutting and tear-ups
- Orderly wind-down, liquid net assets and the Key Attributes
- Self-assessments, cooperative oversight and crisis management groups
- Workshop: a participant default with cross-border spillovers
