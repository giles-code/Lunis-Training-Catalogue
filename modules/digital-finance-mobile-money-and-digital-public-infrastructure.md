## Aim and Purpose

How mobile money, agent networks, digital identity and fast payment systems have transformed access, and the regulatory choices that enable them while managing new risks.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how digital financial services expand access
- Assess enabling regulation for e-money, agents and digital credit
- Evaluate the role of digital identity and proportionate customer due diligence

## Syllabus

- Mobile money, agent networks and interoperability
- Digital credit, buy now pay later and over-indebtedness
- Digital identity, tiered due diligence and FATF guidance on inclusion
- Digital public infrastructure and government-to-person payments
