## Aim and Purpose

Why a governance assessment can confirm the right structures and still miss how the board actually works, and the supervisor's framework for assessing dynamics systematically.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish governance form from governance substance
- Apply the supervisor's eye: look for, concerns, evidence, questions and conclusion
- Identify the evidence sources available for assessing board dynamics

## Syllabus

- Composition, committees and policies versus behaviour and challenge
- How weak board dynamics have contributed to firm failures
- Evidence sources: packs, minutes, evaluations, observation and interviews
- The five questions of the supervisor's eye
