## Aim and Purpose

How Japan regulates investment trusts and their managers, the role of its self-regulatory organisations and the policy push to grow household investment and attract new asset managers.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the roles of the Japanese Financial Services Agency, SESC and self-regulatory organisations
- Explain registration of investment management businesses and of domestic and foreign funds
- Assess the distribution environment for domestic and foreign investment trusts

## Syllabus

- The Financial Instruments and Exchange Act and the Act on Investment Trusts and Investment Corporations
- Vehicles: contractual investment trusts and investment corporations
- Notification and distribution of foreign funds and the Asia Region Funds Passport
- Recent developments: expanded tax-advantaged savings accounts and customer-oriented business conduct
