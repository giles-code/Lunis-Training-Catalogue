## Aim and Purpose

How a firm defines what an agent is permitted to do, on whose behalf and within what limits, whether the agent is the firm's own or a customer's.

## Learning Objectives

- Define the scope, limits and duration of authority given to an agent
- Design mandates and permissions for firm-operated and customer agents
- Identify where delegated authority creates legal and conduct uncertainty

## Syllabus

- Principals, agents and mandates: who is acting for whom
- Permissions, spending limits, scopes and expiry
- Customer consent, revocation and changes of instruction
- Agents acting across firms and platforms
