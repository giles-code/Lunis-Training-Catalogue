## Aim and Purpose

How insider dealing and market manipulation are committed and detected, how firms and venues report suspicious activity, and how transaction reporting data supports surveillance and investigation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main forms of insider dealing and market manipulation
- Assess firms' surveillance systems and suspicious order and transaction reporting
- Apply transaction reporting data to identify and escalate potential abuse

## Syllabus

- Inside information, insider lists and disclosure delays
- Manipulation typologies: spoofing, layering, ramping and cross-market abuse
- Suspicious order and transaction reports: quality, volume and follow-up
- Transaction reporting data quality and its use in surveillance
