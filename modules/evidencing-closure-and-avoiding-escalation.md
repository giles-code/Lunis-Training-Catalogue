## Aim and Purpose

What evidence supervisors need before closing an action, how to report progress and how to handle slippage without losing credibility.

## Learning Objectives

- Assemble evidence that an action is complete and effective
- Report remediation progress to the board and supervisor
- Manage slippage and changes to agreed actions

## Syllabus

- Completion versus effectiveness: testing the fix
- Assurance from the second and third lines
- Progress reporting and early warning of slippage
- Case study: six months on
