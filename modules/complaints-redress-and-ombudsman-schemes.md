## Aim and Purpose

How to give consumers effective routes to redress, from internal complaints handling to independent ombudsman schemes, and how complaints data can inform policy and supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Set standards for internal complaints handling by firms
- Design an independent, accessible and fair ombudsman scheme
- Use complaints data to identify emerging harm

## Syllabus

- Internal dispute resolution standards
- Ombudsman scheme design: independence, jurisdiction, funding and binding decisions
- Mass redress, collective actions and compensation schemes
- Complaints data as an early warning system
