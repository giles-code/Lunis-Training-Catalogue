## Aim and Purpose

Building risk reporting that is accurate, timely and decision-useful, from data aggregation to dashboards that tell the board what it needs to know.

## Learning Objectives

- Design risk management information aligned to appetite and principal risks
- Apply principles of risk data aggregation and reporting quality
- Produce risk reports that support decisions rather than reassure

## Syllabus

- Risk data aggregation, quality and lineage
- Dashboards, key risk indicators and trend analysis
- Writing the CRO report: insight, judgement and recommendations
- Case study: reporting that failed to warn the board
