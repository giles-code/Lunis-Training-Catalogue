## Aim and Purpose

The business models of payment, e-money and crypto-asset firms, how they earn revenue and why safeguarding, operational and financial crime risks dominate.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how payment, e-money and crypto-asset firms generate revenue
- Assess safeguarding, custody and operational dependency risks
- Identify financial crime and consumer harm red flags specific to these sectors

## Syllabus

- Payment flows, interchange, float income and e-money issuance
- Crypto-asset exchanges, custody, staking, lending and token issuance
- Safeguarding, segregation and the risk of customer shortfalls
- Rapid growth, thin capital and dependence on banking partners
