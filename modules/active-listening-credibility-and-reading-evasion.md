## Aim and Purpose

How to listen for what is said and not said, assess the credibility and consistency of answers, and recognise and respond to evasion, deflection and rehearsed responses.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply active listening and summarising techniques during an interview
- Evaluate the consistency and plausibility of responses against other evidence
- Recognise verbal signs of evasion and follow them up

## Syllabus

- Active listening: silence, summarising and reflecting back
- Testing accounts for consistency, plausibility and corroboration
- Evasion patterns: deflection, generalisation, scripted and partial answers
- Limits of non-verbal cues and avoiding false conclusions
