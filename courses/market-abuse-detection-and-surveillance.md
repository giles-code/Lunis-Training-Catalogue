## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how market abuse is detected through surveillance systems, and how supervisors assess the adequacy of firms' own surveillance and monitoring capability.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory and financial crime specialist staff assessing firms' financial crime controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main categories of market abuse
- Describe how trade surveillance systems detect suspicious trading patterns
- Assess cross-market and cross-asset surveillance challenges
- Evaluate the adequacy of a firm's surveillance framework
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a market abuse case

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Categories of market abuse (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain market abuse categories*
- Insider dealing, market manipulation, and unlawful disclosure
- Evolving typologies: spoofing, layering, cross-market manipulation
- Why market abuse undermines market integrity and confidence
- The supervisor's role in market surveillance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: market abuse cases participants are aware of

**Session 2: Trade surveillance systems (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe surveillance systems and assess cross-market challenges*
- How trade surveillance systems detect suspicious patterns
- Alert generation, tuning, and false-positive management
- Cross-market and cross-asset surveillance challenges
- Data and technology requirements for effective surveillance
Activities:
- Group exercise: review sample surveillance alerts and assess plausibility of abuse

**Session 3: Evaluating frameworks and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate a surveillance framework and formulate an assessment*
- Criteria for assessing a firm's surveillance framework adequacy
- Case study: a suspected market abuse case
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a market abuse case

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: abuse categories, surveillance systems, framework assessment
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Categories of market abuse (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Trade surveillance systems (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Evaluating frameworks and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Market abuse typologies continue to evolve, particularly around cross-market and algorithmic manipulation
- Effective surveillance requires both good technology and skilled analysts to interpret alerts
- Supervisors should assess a firm's surveillance framework as rigorously as its other control functions
