## Aim and Purpose

The fraud threats facing firms and their customers, especially authorised push payment scams and money mules, and how behavioural analytics and real-time scoring help to stop losses.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify fraud typologies, including authorised push payment scams and mule networks
- Evaluate real-time fraud scoring and behavioural analytics
- Align fraud and AML functions to share intelligence and protect customers

## Syllabus

- Investment, romance, impersonation and purchase scams
- Money mule detection and account takeover
- Behavioural biometrics, device intelligence and payee verification
- Customer protection, reimbursement and fraud and AML convergence
