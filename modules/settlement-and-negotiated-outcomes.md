## Aim and Purpose

The benefits and risks of settlement, when it is and is not appropriate, negotiation technique specific to enforcement, and the public interest test every settlement must meet.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the rationale for settlement and common discount mechanisms
- Assess when settlement should be pursued or resisted
- Evaluate a proposed settlement against deterrence and the public interest

## Syllabus

- Speed, certainty and resource efficiency versus perceived leniency
- Settlement discounts, timing and transparency of settled cases
- Negotiation technique and power dynamics between authority and firm
- Role-play: negotiating a settlement
