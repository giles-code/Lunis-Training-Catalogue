## Aim and Purpose

The governance, capital, regulatory and operational trade-offs between serving a market through a subsidiary, a branch or cross-border services, and how the choice shapes supervisory expectations.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare subsidiaries, branches and cross-border services as routes to market
- Assess the governance and resourcing consequences of each structure
- Anticipate when a host authority may require a subsidiary or added safeguards

## Syllabus

- Branch versus subsidiary: capital, governance, liability and resolvability
- Cross-border services without a local presence
- Host expectations of systemically or retail-significant branches
- Case study: restructuring a branch network into a regulated subsidiary
