## Aim and Purpose

Plain-language grounding in how physical, transition and nature-related risks arise, how they transmit through credit, market, insurance, liquidity and operational risk, and why they matter to the board.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain physical, transition and liability risks and their time horizons
- Trace how climate and nature risks transmit into existing financial risk categories
- Identify where the firm is most exposed across its balance sheet, clients and operations

## Syllabus

- Physical, transition and liability risk in plain language
- Transmission channels into credit, market, underwriting and operational risk
- Nature loss, biodiversity and dependency on ecosystem services
- International work by the FSB, NGFS, Basel Committee and IAIS
