## Aim and Purpose

How portfolio managers construct and manage portfolios against mandates and risk budgets, and how supervisors test the independence and effectiveness of investment risk oversight.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain mandate-driven portfolio construction and risk budgeting
- Assess pre-trade and post-trade mandate compliance controls
- Evaluate the independence of investment risk management

## Syllabus

- Investment process, mandates and risk budgets
- Mandate breaches, investment restrictions and compliance monitoring
- Trade allocation, cross trades and dealing errors
- Exercise: testing mandate compliance on a sample portfolio
