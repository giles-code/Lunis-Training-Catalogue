## Aim and Purpose

How investors and lenders use stewardship, voting and engagement to manage climate and nature risk, and how the board oversees whether stewardship activity is genuine and effective.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and tools of stewardship and engagement
- Assess whether stewardship policies are applied in practice and produce outcomes
- Oversee escalation, voting and reporting on stewardship

## Syllabus

- Stewardship codes and principles in outline
- Engagement, escalation, voting and collaborative initiatives
- Evidencing stewardship outcomes rather than activity
- Conflicts of interest and consistency with stated policy
