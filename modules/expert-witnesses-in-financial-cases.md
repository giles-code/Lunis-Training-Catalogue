## Aim and Purpose

When expert evidence is needed in financial cases, how to instruct and test experts, the duties experts owe the court, and how judges manage conflicting expert opinion.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify issues that require expert rather than factual evidence
- Instruct experts with clear questions and appropriate material
- Test expert methodology, independence and the limits of opinion

## Syllabus

- Forensic accountants, market specialists and blockchain analysts
- Letters of instruction, independence and the expert's duty to the court
- Testing methodology, assumptions and conclusions
- Managing competing experts and joint statements
