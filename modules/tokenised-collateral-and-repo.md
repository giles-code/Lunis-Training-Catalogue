## Aim and Purpose

How tokenisation is being used to mobilise collateral, support intraday repo and margining, and the legal, operational and liquidity risks that faster collateral movements create.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the use of tokenised assets in collateral management and repo
- Assess legal enforceability of collateral arrangements on a ledger
- Evaluate liquidity and procyclicality risks of faster collateral mobility

## Syllabus

- Collateral mobility, intraday repo and margin calls
- Collateral eligibility, haircuts and valuation of tokenised assets
- Enforceability, close-out netting and default management
- Interaction with central counterparties and margin frameworks
