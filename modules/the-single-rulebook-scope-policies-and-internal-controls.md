## Aim and Purpose

The scope of the AML Regulation, including newly covered sectors, and its harmonised requirements for business-wide risk assessment, internal policies, compliance roles and group-wide arrangements.

## Learning Objectives

- Determine which entities and activities fall within the AML Regulation
- Apply the requirements for business-wide risk assessment, policies and internal controls
- Assess the roles of the management body, compliance manager and compliance officer

## Syllabus

- Obliged entities, including crypto-asset service providers and newly covered sectors
- Business-wide risk assessment, documented methodology and link to risk appetite
- Management body accountability, compliance manager and compliance officer roles
- Group-wide policies, outsourcing limits and third-country branches and subsidiaries
