## Aim and Purpose

How to write papers and reports for executive committees, risk and audit committees and the board that lead with the message, frame the decision and give an honest view of risk.

## Learning Objectives

- Structure a paper or report around the decision or assurance required
- Present an honest view of risk and control weaknesses without alarm or comfort
- Handle challenging questions from executives and directors

## Syllabus

- Writing for decision: the one-page summary and recommendation
- Function reports that inform: themes, trends and risk appetite rather than activity
- Presenting to committees: message first, evidence second
- Skills practice: a short briefing to a mock risk committee
