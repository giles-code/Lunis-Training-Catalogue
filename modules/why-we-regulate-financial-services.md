## Aim and Purpose

The economic and public interest case for financial regulation, the market failures it addresses and the costs and limits of intervention.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the market failures that justify regulating financial services
- Distinguish prudential, conduct, market integrity and financial crime objectives
- Recognise the costs, trade-offs and limits of regulation

## Syllabus

- Information asymmetry, externalities, moral hazard and systemic risk
- Prudential, conduct and market integrity objectives compared
- Lessons from financial crises and regulatory failures
- The costs of regulation: proportionality and unintended consequences
