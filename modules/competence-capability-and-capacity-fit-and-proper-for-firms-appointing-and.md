## Aim and Purpose

How to test whether a candidate has the knowledge, experience and time the role requires, using evidence rather than assertion, and how to close gaps without lowering the standard.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess competence against the role profile using documentary and interview evidence
- Judge capacity, other commitments and conflicts of interest
- Design development plans and conditions where gaps exist

## Syllabus

- Evidence of experience, qualifications and track record
- Structured competency interviews and scenario questions
- Time commitment, multiple directorships and conflicts of interest
- Closing gaps: induction, development plans and conditional appointments
