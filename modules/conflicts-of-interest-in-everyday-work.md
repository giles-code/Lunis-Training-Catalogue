## Aim and Purpose

How conflicts of interest arise between the firm, its staff and its customers, and the practical steps staff take to identify, record and manage them.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify actual, potential and perceived conflicts of interest
- Apply the firm's conflicts policy, including disclosure and recording
- Escalate conflicts that cannot be managed at team level

## Syllabus

- Types of conflict: firm and customer, customer and customer, staff and firm
- Remuneration, sales incentives and targets
- Personal relationships, outside interests and misuse of position
- Managing conflicts: avoidance, controls, disclosure and the conflicts register
