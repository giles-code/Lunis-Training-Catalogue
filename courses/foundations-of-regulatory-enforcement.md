## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of a supervisory authority's enforcement function — what powers exist, how the decision to open an investigation is made, and how cases are triaged and prioritised.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level enforcement investigators and case officers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the range of enforcement powers available to a supervisory authority
- Describe the criteria used to decide whether to open an investigation
- Apply a case triage framework to prioritise enforcement matters
- Distinguish enforcement from ongoing supervisory intervention
- Evaluate the resourcing implications of an enforcement caseload

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Enforcement powers and purpose (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the range of enforcement powers*
- Types of enforcement power: investigative, sanctioning, remedial
- The purpose of enforcement within the broader supervisory toolkit
- Distinguishing enforcement from ongoing supervisory intervention
- Statutory thresholds for opening an investigation
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: enforcement's role relative to supervision in participants' own authorities

**Session 2: Deciding to investigate (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe investigation criteria and apply a triage framework*
- Criteria for deciding whether to open an investigation
- Case triage: severity, evidential strength, public interest
- Balancing enforcement priorities against limited resource
- Escalation routes from supervision into enforcement
Activities:
- Group exercise: triage a set of sample cases for investigation priority

**Session 3: Resourcing and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate resourcing implications and apply learning to a scenario*
- Resourcing an enforcement caseload: skills, time, external support
- Managing a growing or backlogged caseload
- Case study: a resourcing-constrained enforcement decision
Activities:
- Case study: propose a triage and resourcing plan for a sample caseload

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: powers, investigation decisions, triage, resourcing
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Enforcement powers and purpose (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Deciding to investigate (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Resourcing and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Enforcement is a distinct function from supervision, requiring different powers, skills, and decision standards
- Case triage makes limited enforcement resource go where it has the most impact
- The decision to investigate is itself a significant supervisory judgement, not just a procedural trigger
