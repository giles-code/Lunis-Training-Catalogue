## Aim and Purpose

How financial firms' reliance on platforms for distribution, cloud, data and customer interfaces creates operational dependency, and how supervisors assess resilience and exit options.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess firms' operational dependency on platform partners and providers
- Evaluate contractual rights, oversight and exit planning for critical platform relationships
- Apply operational resilience principles to embedded and platform-based services

## Syllabus

- Distribution dependency and loss of the customer interface
- Cloud, data and technology dependencies on platform groups
- Contractual rights, audit access and substitutability
- Links to critical third-party oversight regimes
