## Aim and Purpose

A map of where cryptography protects a financial services firm, from client channels and trading links to payment messaging, hardware security modules, certificates and signatures on ledgers.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main uses of cryptography in the firm's business services
- Recognise cryptography embedded in legacy, cloud and vendor systems
- Link cryptographic dependencies to important business services and client data

## Syllabus

- Secure communications, certificates and public key infrastructure
- Client portals, trading connectivity and payment messaging
- Hardware security modules, key management and digital signatures
- Cryptography hidden in vendor products, libraries and cloud services
