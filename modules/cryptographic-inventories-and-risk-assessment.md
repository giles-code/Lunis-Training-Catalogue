## Aim and Purpose

How firms discover and record where and how cryptography is used, prioritise exposed systems and data, and how supervisors judge whether an inventory is complete and decision-useful.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and contents of a cryptographic inventory
- Assess the completeness of a firm's inventory, including third-party and embedded uses
- Evaluate a firm's prioritisation of systems by exposure and data shelf life

## Syllabus

- Discovery tools, manual review and the cryptographic bill of materials
- Prioritising by data sensitivity, shelf life and system criticality
- Governance: ownership, board reporting and risk appetite
- Red flags: partial inventories and no named owner
