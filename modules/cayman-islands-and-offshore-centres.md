## Aim and Purpose

How the leading offshore fund domicile regulates hedge, private equity and other alternative funds, and how offshore funds are distributed into onshore markets.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the Cayman Islands fund vehicles and the role of the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority
- Explain the registration regimes for open-ended and closed-ended funds
- Assess governance, AML and substance expectations for offshore funds and their onshore distribution

## Syllabus

- Vehicles: exempted companies, segregated portfolio companies, unit trusts and exempted limited partnerships
- Registration of mutual funds and private funds, audit, valuation and custody requirements
- Distribution into onshore markets through private placement regimes
- Recent developments: AML, beneficial ownership and economic substance, and other offshore centres
