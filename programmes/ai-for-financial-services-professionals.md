## Aim and Purpose

To give staff a practical, jargon-free understanding of how generative AI works, how to get good results from it and where it can go wrong. Participants leave able to use approved AI tools effectively, verify outputs, protect data and follow the firm's AI policy with confidence.

## What It Covers

How generative AI works, prompting techniques, verification and hallucination, confidentiality and data protection, the firm's AI policy, AI in client communications, bias, records and accountability, and a hands-on workshop.

## Intended Audience

- Staff at all levels who use or plan to use generative AI tools in their work
- Front-office, operations, compliance, risk and support teams adopting approved AI tools
- Managers responsible for supervising AI use within their teams

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how generative AI works and where it is and is not suitable
- Apply prompting techniques to produce useful, well-structured outputs
- Verify AI outputs and identify hallucination and over-reliance
- Protect client, personal and confidential information when using AI
- Apply the firm's AI policy, conduct standards and record-keeping requirements
- Use AI safely on realistic tasks and agree personal good-practice commitments

## Structure and Format

Six modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two-day programme or as a series of short sessions. Modules combine live demonstrations with hands-on exercises using the firm's approved tools, and can be selected or tailored to the firm's AI policy and use cases.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

No prior experience of AI is required. Familiarity with the firm's approved tools and AI policy is helpful.

## Core Reading

- OECD, Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence (2019, revised 2024)
- NIST, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) (2023)
- IOSCO, The Use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning by Market Intermediaries and Asset Managers (2021)
