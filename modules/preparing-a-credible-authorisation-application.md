## Aim and Purpose

How to build an application dossier that meets the authority's completeness and quality thresholds, and to pressure-test the firm's governance and operations as a supervisor would before submission.

## Learning Objectives

- Build a complete application dossier and programme of operations
- Evidence local substance and manage group and outsourcing dependencies
- Pressure-test the business plan, customer journeys and regulatory history

## Syllabus

- Core dossier elements: governance, suitability, policies, ICT and safeguarding
- Business plans that do not outpace compliance capability
- Substance, intragroup agreements and avoiding letter-box structures
- Customer journeys, risk warnings and unresolved regulatory history
