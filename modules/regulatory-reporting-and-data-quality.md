## Aim and Purpose

How to assess the governance, controls and data lineage behind firms' regulatory returns, and why late, inaccurate or inconsistent reporting signals deeper control weaknesses.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the supervisory reliance placed on regulatory returns
- Assess governance, reconciliation and sign-off controls over reporting
- Identify data quality red flags and frame a supervisory response

## Syllabus

- Regulatory returns and their role in risk-based supervision
- Reporting governance: ownership, review and sign-off
- Data lineage, reconciliations and end-user computing risk
- Resubmissions, errors and breaches: supervisory follow-up
