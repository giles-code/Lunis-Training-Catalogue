## Aim and Purpose

How law enforcement, prosecutors and asset recovery bodies pursue money laundering, terrorist financing and the proceeds of crime, and what assessors expect to see in results.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess whether investigations and prosecutions match the risk profile
- Evaluate confiscation, asset recovery and asset management arrangements
- Identify practical obstacles in parallel financial investigations

## Syllabus

- Parallel financial investigations and stand-alone money laundering cases
- Prosecution strategies, sentencing and alternative measures
- Freezing, seizure, confiscation and non-conviction based forfeiture
- Terrorist financing investigations and targeted financial sanctions
