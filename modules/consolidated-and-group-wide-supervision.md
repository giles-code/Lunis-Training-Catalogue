## Aim and Purpose

Why supervisors look at groups rather than single legal entities, how consolidated supervision works, and how to assess group governance and risk management.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain consolidated versus solo supervision
- Assess group governance, risk management and risk appetite
- Identify perimeter issues in group supervision

## Syllabus

- Common cross-border group structures across banking, insurance and investment
- Scope of consolidation, holding companies and unregulated entities
- Group-level versus entity-level governance and risk appetite
- Financial conglomerates and cross-sector groups
