## Aim and Purpose

How to handle personal and confidential information lawfully and securely throughout its lifecycle, from collection to sharing, retention and disposal.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply data protection principles to everyday handling of information
- Share, store and dispose of data securely and on a need-to-know basis
- Respond correctly to requests from individuals and third parties for information

## Syllabus

- Personal data, special category data and confidential information
- Lawful use, minimisation, accuracy and retention
- Secure sharing: email, file transfer, printing and clear desk
- Individuals' rights and handling requests for information
