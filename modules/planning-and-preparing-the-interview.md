## Aim and Purpose

How to set objectives, research the interviewee and the firm, select documents, build lines of enquiry and agree roles within the interview team.

## Learning Objectives

- Define interview objectives linked to the supervisory risk assessment
- Prepare an interview plan with lines of enquiry and supporting evidence
- Organise the interview team, logistics and introductions

## Syllabus

- Setting objectives and identifying the right interviewees
- Desk research: board papers, management information and prior findings
- Building the interview plan and question map
- Roles: lead questioner, note-taker and observer
