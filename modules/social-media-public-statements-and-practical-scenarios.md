## Aim and Purpose

How personal and professional use of social media can create market abuse, confidentiality, financial promotion and reputational risk, closing with practical market conduct scenarios.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the firm's social media and public statements policy
- Recognise how online posts can spread rumours, disclose information or amount to promotions
- Apply the programme to realistic market conduct scenarios

## Syllabus

- Personal and professional accounts: what you can and cannot say
- Rumours, market commentary and amplifying unverified information
- Financial promotions, endorsements and online influencers
- Workshop: what would you do? Market conduct dilemmas
