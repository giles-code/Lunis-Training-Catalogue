## Aim and Purpose

How fund units, including money market fund shares, are being tokenised, the effect on transfer agency, valuation and distribution, and the supervisory questions for fund managers and depositaries.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how tokenised fund units are issued, transferred and redeemed
- Assess the implications for transfer agency, valuation and investor registers
- Evaluate liquidity and conduct risks of tokenised money market fund shares

## Syllabus

- Tokenised share classes and on-chain transfer agency
- NAV calculation, dealing cycles and redemption on a ledger
- Tokenised money market fund shares as collateral and settlement assets
- Responsibilities of managers, depositaries and distributors
