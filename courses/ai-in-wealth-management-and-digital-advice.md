## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how AI-driven digital advisory and wealth management platforms operate, and the supervisory considerations specific to automated investment advice.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in AI-related supervision and internal AI use.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how robo-advisory platforms construct and manage portfolios
- Describe the algorithmic techniques underlying digital advice platforms
- Assess the suitability assessment process within a digital advice platform
- Evaluate the adequacy of human oversight in automated advice models
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a digital advice platform

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Robo-advisory fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain robo-advisory platform operation*
- How robo-advisory platforms construct and rebalance portfolios
- Algorithmic techniques: risk profiling algorithms, portfolio optimisation
- Business models: pure robo-advice versus hybrid human-AI models
- Growth drivers and market context for digital advice
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: digital advice platforms participants have reviewed

**Session 2: Suitability in a digital context (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess suitability assessment within digital platforms*
- How digital platforms gather and use client information for suitability
- Risks of oversimplified or poorly designed digital risk-profiling questionnaires
- Assessing whether digital suitability assessment meets regulatory standards
- Comparing digital and traditional advice suitability processes
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample digital risk-profiling questionnaire for suitability adequacy

**Session 3: Human oversight and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate human oversight and formulate an assessment*
- The role of human oversight in automated advice models
- Escalation triggers for referring clients to human advisers
- Case study: a digital advice platform under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a digital advice platform

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: robo-advisory operation, digital suitability, human oversight
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Robo-advisory fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Suitability in a digital context (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Human oversight and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Digital advice platforms must meet the same suitability standards as traditional advice, delivered through different means
- Oversimplified risk-profiling questionnaires are a common weakness in digital advice suitability
- Clear escalation triggers to human advisers are an important safeguard even in largely automated models
