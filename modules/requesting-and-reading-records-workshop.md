## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a practical exercise in which participants frame requests to different firms, read the records produced and identify gaps and lines of enquiry.

## Learning Objectives

- Draft precise, proportionate requests for financial records
- Read statements, payment messages and onboarding files accurately
- Identify gaps, inconsistencies and follow-up enquiries

## Syllabus

- Choosing the right firm, power and point of contact
- Drafting requests: scope, period, format and confidentiality
- Reading sample records from banks, payment and crypto-asset firms
- Workshop: from records to lines of enquiry
