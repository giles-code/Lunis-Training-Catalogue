## Aim and Purpose

The features that distinguish fund types, how funds are classified by regulators, and a closer look at exchange traded funds and money market funds.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the factors that distinguish different types of fund
- Describe regulatory classifications of retail and alternative funds
- Explain how exchange traded funds and money market funds work

## Syllabus

- Asset class, strategy, liquidity and investor type
- Regulatory classification of funds
- Exchange traded funds: creation, redemption and secondary trading
- Money market funds: types, liquidity rules and stability
