## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how stablecoin issuers structure reserves and redemption mechanisms under MiCA, and how supervisors assess run risk, reserve adequacy, and the operational resilience of EMT/ART issuers.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in digital assets/crypto-asset regulation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the distinction between e-money tokens and asset-referenced tokens under MiCA
- Describe reserve management requirements for stablecoin issuers
- Assess redemption rights and their practical operation
- Evaluate run risk specific to stablecoin structures
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of an EMT/ART issuer's resilience

## Syllabus

**Session 1: EMTs, ARTs and MiCA requirements (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain EMT/ART distinctions and reserve requirements*
- Distinguishing e-money tokens from asset-referenced tokens under MiCA
- Reserve management requirements: composition, segregation, custody
- Issuance and redemption rules under MiCA
- Significant EMT/ART additional requirements
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: stablecoin structures participants are aware of

**Session 2: Redemption rights and run risk (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess redemption rights and evaluate run risk*
- How redemption rights are meant to operate in practice
- Run risk dynamics specific to stablecoins
- Historical stablecoin de-pegging episodes and their lessons
- Liquidity of reserve assets under stress
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess the run-risk profile of a sample stablecoin structure

**Session 3: Supervisory assessment and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Formulate a resilience assessment*
- Supervisory criteria for assessing EMT/ART issuer resilience
- Case study: an EMT/ART issuer facing reserve or redemption pressure
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a sample issuer's resilience

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: EMT/ART distinctions, reserves, redemption, run risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: EMTs, ARTs and MiCA requirements (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Redemption rights and run risk (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Supervisory assessment and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Reserve adequacy and redemption mechanism design together determine a stablecoin's real resilience
- Run risk in stablecoins shares features with traditional bank runs and money market fund runs
- Historical de-pegging episodes offer concrete lessons for what to test in a supervisory assessment
