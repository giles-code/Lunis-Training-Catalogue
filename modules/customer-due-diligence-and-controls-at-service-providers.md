## Aim and Purpose

What effective AML/CFT controls look like at crypto-asset service providers, and how supervisors test customer due diligence, wallet screening, transaction monitoring and travel rule compliance.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess customer due diligence and ongoing monitoring for crypto-asset customers
- Evaluate wallet screening, transaction monitoring and sanctions controls
- Test travel rule implementation and counterparty service provider due diligence

## Syllabus

- Customer risk assessment, onboarding and source of funds and wealth
- Wallet screening, transaction monitoring scenarios and alert handling
- The travel rule: originator and beneficiary data and counterparty checks
- Supervisory testing: sample selection, walk-throughs and common deficiencies
