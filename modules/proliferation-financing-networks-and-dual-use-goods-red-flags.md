## Aim and Purpose

How proliferation networks procure weapons-related and dual-use goods, conceal their activity through front companies, shipping and intermediaries, and the red flags that may surface in a firm's customers and payments.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how procurement networks source goods and move funds
- Recognise dual-use goods, trade and corporate red flags
- Apply enhanced due diligence to customers with trade or technology exposure

## Syllabus

- Procurement networks, front companies and third-country diversion
- Dual-use goods, end-user and routing red flags in payments and invoices
- Exposure through investors, portfolio companies, payment flows and crypto-assets
- Enhanced due diligence on technology, logistics and trading customers
