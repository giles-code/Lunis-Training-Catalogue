## Aim and Purpose

The design choices behind retail and wholesale CBDCs, the role central banks expect intermediaries to play, and what CBDC adoption could mean for deposits, payments revenue and operations.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish retail and wholesale CBDC models and their design choices
- Identify the roles intermediaries may play in distribution and services
- Assess the impact on the firm's deposits, liquidity and payments business

## Syllabus

- Retail and wholesale CBDCs: purpose, architecture and privacy
- Two-tier models, holding limits and intermediary obligations
- Deposit disintermediation and liquidity implications
- Cross-border CBDC projects and multi-currency platforms
