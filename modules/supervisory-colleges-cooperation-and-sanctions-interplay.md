## Aim and Purpose

How AML/CFT supervisors, FIUs, prudential authorities and AMLA cooperate across borders through colleges and information exchange, and how AML/CFT controls interact with EU restrictive measures and targeted financial sanctions.

## Learning Objectives

- Operate AML/CFT supervisory colleges and home–host cooperation effectively
- Coordinate AML/CFT findings with prudential supervisors, FIUs and resolution authorities
- Assess how firms integrate targeted financial sanctions into AML/CFT controls

## Syllabus

- AML/CFT colleges, information exchange and cooperation with prudential supervisors
- AML/CFT considerations in authorisation, qualifying holdings and withdrawal
- Targeted financial sanctions and screening obligations under the AML Regulation
- Sanctions evasion typologies and criminal enforcement of restrictive measures
