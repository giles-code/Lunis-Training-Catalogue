## Aim and Purpose

How to develop, govern and implement a national financial inclusion strategy, setting priorities and targets and aligning ministries, regulators and the private sector.

## Learning Objectives

- Design the structure, priorities and targets of a national strategy
- Establish governance and coordination for implementation
- Monitor progress with a results framework

## Syllabus

- Diagnostics, stakeholder engagement and priority setting
- Governance: steering committees, working groups and secretariats
- Public sector levers: government payments, postal networks and state banks
- Results frameworks, targets and reporting
