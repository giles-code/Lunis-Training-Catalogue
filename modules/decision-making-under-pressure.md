## Aim and Purpose

How managers make and communicate sound decisions when time is short and information incomplete, recognise bias in themselves and the team, and own the outcome.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply structured decision-making techniques under time pressure
- Recognise individual and group biases in management decisions
- Communicate decisions and their rationale clearly to the team

## Syllabus

- Process-based thinking and structured decision-making
- Bias, groupthink and stress effects on judgement
- Deciding with incomplete information: options, trade-offs and reversibility
- Simulation: a time-pressured supervisory management decision
