## Aim and Purpose

How boards test whether digital assets fit the firm's strategy, challenge management's business case and recognise the business model risks that have caused failures in the sector.

## Learning Objectives

- Challenge the strategic rationale and claimed benefits of a digital asset proposition
- Assess revenue concentration, volatility and dependency in digital asset business models
- Recognise the business model weaknesses behind high-profile sector failures

## Syllabus

- Business models: trading, custody, issuance, payments, tokenisation and infrastructure
- Questions to ask of a business case: demand, cost, capability, dependency and exit
- Conflicts of interest in vertically integrated platforms
- Case studies of sector failures and the governance lessons
