## Aim and Purpose

How FinTechs fail, what an orderly wind-down requires for customers and their funds, and a workshop applying the programme to a fast-growing firm heading into difficulty.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify failure patterns in FinTech firms and their early signals
- Assess solvent wind-down plans and the protection of customer funds and data
- Apply the programme to a supervisory case involving a FinTech under stress

## Syllabus

- Failure patterns: funding withdrawal, partner exit, fraud losses and control breakdown
- Wind-down planning, client money return and continuity of critical services
- Supervisory intervention, communication and coordination with other authorities
- Workshop: a fast-growing FinTech loses its funding and partner bank
