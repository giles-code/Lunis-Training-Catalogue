## Aim and Purpose

A grounding in how AI and machine learning systems are built, trained and deployed in financial services, how they differ from rules-based and statistical models, and why firms are adopting them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how machine learning models are developed, trained and deployed
- Distinguish traditional statistical models, machine learning and generative AI
- Identify the drivers and patterns of AI adoption across financial sectors

## Syllabus

- Supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning in financial applications
- The AI lifecycle: data, training, validation, deployment and monitoring
- Generative AI and large language models: capabilities and limits
- In-house build versus vendor AI: supervisory implications
