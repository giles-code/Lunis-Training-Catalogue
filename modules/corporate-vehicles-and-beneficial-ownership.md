## Aim and Purpose

How companies, trusts, foundations and nominees are used to conceal ownership and move funds, and how investigators trace control through registers, records and cooperation.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise how legal persons and arrangements are misused to conceal ownership
- Trace control and beneficial ownership using registers and institution records
- Identify the role of professional enablers

## Syllabus

- Shell, shelf and front companies, trusts and nominee arrangements
- Beneficial ownership registers, service provider records and their limits
- Mapping ownership and control across jurisdictions
- Professional enablers: lawyers, accountants and company service providers
