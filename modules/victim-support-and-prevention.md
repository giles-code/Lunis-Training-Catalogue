## Aim and Purpose

How investigators support victims through reporting, investigation and recovery, reduce repeat victimisation, and contribute to prevention and public awareness.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply a victim-centred approach throughout the investigation
- Identify vulnerable and repeat victims and refer them for support
- Contribute to prevention, warnings and disruption campaigns

## Syllabus

- Victim needs, vulnerability and trauma-informed engagement
- Repeat victimisation and recovery scams
- Reimbursement, compensation and restitution routes
- Prevention: alerts, warnings and takedowns
