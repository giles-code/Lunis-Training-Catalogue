## Aim and Purpose

The threat actors, attack vectors and sector-specific vulnerabilities facing regulated firms and market infrastructure, and how threat intelligence should shape supervisory priorities.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main categories of cyber threat actor and their motivations
- Identify sector-specific vulnerabilities in payments, trading, custody and core systems
- Assess how threat intelligence should inform supervisory priorities

## Syllabus

- Threat actors: nation-state, organised crime, insiders and opportunists
- Attack vectors: phishing, ransomware, supply chain compromise and data extortion
- Legacy systems and technical debt as vulnerability drivers
- Sources and supervisory use of threat intelligence
