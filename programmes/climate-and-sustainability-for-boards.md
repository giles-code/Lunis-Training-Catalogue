## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a practical, jargon-free grounding in how climate change and nature loss translate into financial risk for the firm, and the governance tools to oversee it. Participants leave able to challenge transition plans, scenario analysis and disclosures, and evidence effective oversight to supervisors, auditors and investors.

## What It Covers

Climate and nature-related financial risk, transition planning, scenario analysis, ISSB and TCFD-type disclosure, greenwashing and conduct risk, stewardship, risk appetite, and board skills and incentives.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs, non-executive and independent directors and board committee members, especially on risk, audit and sustainability committees
- Executive directors and senior managers accountable for strategy, risk, sustainability and disclosure
- Company secretaries and heads of governance supporting board oversight of sustainability

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how physical, transition and nature-related risks become financial risks for the firm
- Challenge the credibility of the firm's transition plan and its targets
- Interpret scenario analysis and stress testing results and their limitations
- Oversee sustainability disclosures and product claims to avoid greenwashing
- Assess stewardship, risk appetite and the integration of climate risk into the risk framework
- Evaluate the board's own skills and the role of incentives, and agree an action plan

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two-day programme or as a series of board briefing sessions. Each module combines a short briefing with case studies and a board question set, and modules can be selected or tailored to the board's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Board or senior management experience in financial services is helpful. No technical background in climate science or sustainability reporting is required.

## Core Reading

- ISSB, IFRS S1 General Requirements and IFRS S2 Climate-related Disclosures (2023)
- TCFD, Recommendations of the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (2017)
- Basel Committee, Principles for the Effective Management and Supervision of Climate-related Financial Risks (2022)
- TNFD, Recommendations of the Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (2023)
