## Aim and Purpose

The shift from preventing disruption to assuming it will happen, and the building blocks of an operational resilience framework the board should expect to see.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain operational resilience and how it differs from business continuity
- Evaluate the firm's resilience framework against international standards
- Identify how resilience links to strategy, risk appetite and change

## Syllabus

- From business continuity to operational resilience
- International principles, including BCBS and ISO 22301
- Resilience by design: change, legacy technology and investment decisions
- Resilience risk appetite and the board's role in setting it
