## Aim and Purpose

The legal foundations that make digital money and tokenised assets work, including the nature of the holder's claim, custody and insolvency protection, and the enforceability of smart contracts.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the legal nature of tokens and the holder's rights
- Evaluate custody models and insolvency protection for tokenised assets
- Identify smart contract and governance risks requiring legal and technical control

## Syllabus

- Legal characterisation of tokens and linkage to the underlying asset
- Custody, segregation and protection in insolvency
- Smart contract enforceability, upgrades and administrative keys
- Permissioned versus public ledgers: control and accountability
