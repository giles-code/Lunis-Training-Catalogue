## Aim and Purpose

The international bodies that set standards for financial regulation, how their standards are applied nationally and how the authority is assessed against them.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the main international standard-setters and their remits
- Explain how international standards are adopted and assessed
- Recognise the importance of cross-border cooperation and information exchange

## Syllabus

- The FSB, Basel Committee, IOSCO, IAIS, FATF, CPMI and IOPS
- Core principles and how they translate into national frameworks
- Peer reviews, mutual evaluations and international assessments
- Supervisory colleges, memoranda of understanding and information sharing
