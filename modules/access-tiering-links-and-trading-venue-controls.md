## Aim and Purpose

How to set fair, risk-based access criteria, manage the risks of tiered participation, client clearing and links, and operate the trading controls and surveillance expected of a venue.

## Learning Objectives

- Set risk-based, fair and open participation requirements and exit procedures
- Manage risks from indirect participants, client clearing and links
- Operate trading venue controls that support orderly markets

## Syllabus

- Access criteria, monitoring and suspension of participants
- Tiered participation, client segregation and portability
- CCP links, CSD links and interoperability exposures
- Trading venue rules, circuit breakers and market surveillance
