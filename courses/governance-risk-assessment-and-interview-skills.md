## Aim and Purpose

Combines two linked supervisory disciplines: assessing governance risk in regulated firms, and conducting effective supervisory interviews with directors and senior managers.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisory staff conducting day-to-day risk assessment and firm engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Governance Risk Assessment Session Explain what governance is and describe the subcategories
- Interview Skills Session Explain how to conduct effective interviews with directors and

## Syllabus

**Governance Risk | —** — Governance Risk Assessment Session Explain what governance is and describe the subcategories of governance risk Discuss the purpose of the governance assessments that supervisors undertake Describe how to undertake a governance assessment Identify how to draw conclusions from a governance assessment

**Interview Skills | —** — Interview Skills Session Explain how to conduct effective interviews with directors and senior managers Discuss questioning techniques and other methods to maximise the effectiveness of supervisory interviews Evaluate the credibility, consistency and reliability of interview responses, including recognising evasion Draw supervisory conclusions from interview evidence and link them to appropriate action Apply techniques for managing difficult, evasive or defensive interviewees Recognise how mindset affects an interview and adopt the right supervisory perspective
