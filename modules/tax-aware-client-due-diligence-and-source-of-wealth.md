## Aim and Purpose

How to build tax transparency into onboarding and review, from tax residence self-certifications and source of wealth to legacy clients and requests for evidence of tax compliance.

## Learning Objectives

- Integrate tax residence and tax compliance checks into due diligence
- Assess source of wealth and source of funds for plausibility
- Manage legacy and high-risk clients, including remediation and exit

## Syllabus

- Tax residence self-certifications and reasonableness checks
- Source of wealth and source of funds: evidence and plausibility
- When to ask for evidence of tax compliance or regularisation
- Legacy book reviews, remediation and relationship exit
