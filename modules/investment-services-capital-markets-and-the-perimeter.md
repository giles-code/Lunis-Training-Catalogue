## Aim and Purpose

How capital markets raise and allocate capital, the investment services and activities that require authorisation, and how the IOSCO Objectives and Principles shape the supervisory framework.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain primary and secondary markets and the participants in each
- Identify regulated investment services, activities and financial instruments
- Relate the IOSCO Objectives and Principles to the authority's supervisory approach

## Syllabus

- Market participants: issuers, investors, intermediaries, venues and infrastructures
- Investment services: reception and execution, dealing, advice and portfolio management
- Financial instruments, derivatives and perimeter judgements
- IOSCO Objectives: investor protection, fair and efficient markets, systemic risk
