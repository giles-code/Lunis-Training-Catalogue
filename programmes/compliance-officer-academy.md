## Aim and Purpose

To equip compliance professionals with the frameworks, tools and judgement to run a compliance function that is independent, risk-based and respected by the business, the board and the supervisor. Participants leave able to assess compliance risk, design monitoring and advisory work that targets it, and handle supervisory engagement with confidence.

## What It Covers

The role and independence of compliance, regulatory horizon scanning, compliance risk assessment, monitoring programmes, advisory and training, conduct and consumer outcomes, the financial crime interface, regulatory reporting and breaches, dealing with the supervisor, and reporting to the board.

## Intended Audience

- Heads of compliance, compliance officers and deputies in regulated firms
- Compliance analysts and monitoring staff moving into more senior or advisory roles
- Risk, legal and business managers who work closely with the compliance function

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role, mandate and independence of the compliance function within the firm's governance
- Design a compliance risk assessment and a risk-based monitoring programme that targets the firm's real exposures
- Apply horizon scanning and regulatory change management to keep the firm's obligations current
- Evaluate conduct, consumer outcome and financial crime risks alongside the business and other control functions
- Manage regulatory reporting, breaches and supervisory engagement in a way that builds credibility with the authority
- Produce board reporting that gives directors a clear, honest view of compliance risk and agree an action plan for the function

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines practical tools with case studies, group exercises and a supervisor's-eye view of the function, and modules can be selected or tailored to the firm's business model.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of working in or alongside a compliance function in a regulated firm is helpful. No legal qualification is required.

## Core Reading

- Basel Committee, Compliance and the Compliance Function in Banks (2005)
- IOSCO, Compliance Function at Market Intermediaries (2006)
- FSB, Strengthening Governance Frameworks to Mitigate Misconduct Risk: A Toolkit for Firms and Supervisors (2018)
