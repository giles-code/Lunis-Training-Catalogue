## Aim and Purpose

How to plan and conduct interviews with proposed directors and key function holders that test competence and understanding rather than recitation.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan an approval interview with clear objectives and lines of questioning
- Use open and probing questions to test knowledge and judgement
- Record the interview and its conclusions as evidence

## Syllabus

- Preparing the interview plan from the application file
- Scenario-based and probing questions for different roles
- Assessing answers: depth, ownership and red flags
- Role-play: interviewing a proposed chief executive and compliance officer
