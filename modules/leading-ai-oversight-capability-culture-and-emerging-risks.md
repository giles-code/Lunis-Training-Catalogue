## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board action plan covering the board's own capability, culture, emerging risks from generative AI and agents, and keeping governance current.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the board's AI literacy and plan to close gaps
- Anticipate governance issues from generative AI and autonomous agents
- Produce a board action plan for AI oversight

## Syllabus

- Board skills, external advice and how much expertise is enough
- Culture and speaking up on AI concerns
- Generative AI and autonomous agents: accountability and control questions
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month AI oversight plan
