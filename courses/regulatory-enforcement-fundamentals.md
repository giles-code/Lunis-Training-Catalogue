## Aim and Purpose

To give staff at regulatory authorities a practical grounding in enforcement
powers, investigative process, and the principles underpinning fair and
proportionate enforcement decision-making.

## Intended Audience

Supervisors, investigators, and case officers at regulatory authorities who
are new to, or want to refresh their understanding of, enforcement work.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

- Identify when a matter should be referred for enforcement action
- Describe the key stages of an enforcement investigation
- Apply principles of proportionality and procedural fairness to case decisions

## Syllabus

1. The purpose and scope of enforcement powers
2. Referral and case selection
3. Conducting an investigation
4. Decision-making and sanctions
5. Procedural fairness and rights of representation

## Structure and Format

A single in-person day combining case studies, group discussion, and a
simulated case-decision exercise.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

No formal prerequisites. Familiarity with the regulatory authority's
supervisory framework is helpful but not required.
