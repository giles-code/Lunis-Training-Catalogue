## Aim and Purpose

How to implement the travel rule in practice, from collecting and transmitting originator and beneficiary information to due diligence on counterparty service providers and handling missing or inconsistent data.

## Learning Objectives

- Implement originator and beneficiary data requirements across transfer types
- Conduct risk-based due diligence on counterparty service providers
- Handle missing data, unhosted wallets and counterparties in jurisdictions without the rule

## Syllabus

- FATF Recommendation 16 for virtual assets: data, timing and thresholds
- Messaging solutions, interoperability and the uneven pace of adoption
- Counterparty due diligence: licensing, controls and nested services
- Missing or mismatched information: return, reject, review or report
