## Aim and Purpose

How blockchain analytics tools trace funds, cluster and attribute addresses and score risk, and how to configure, tune and govern them within the firm's wider transaction monitoring framework.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain tracing, clustering and attribution in plain terms
- Set exposure rules, thresholds and scenarios that fit the firm's risk
- Govern analytics tools: selection, validation, coverage gaps and documentation

## Syllabus

- Following funds on public ledgers: inputs, outputs and hops
- Direct and indirect exposure, risk categories and scoring
- Deposit and withdrawal screening, thresholds, tuning and false positives
- Selecting, validating and overseeing an analytics provider
