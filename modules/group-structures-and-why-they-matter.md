## Aim and Purpose

How regulated firms organise across borders through holding companies, subsidiaries and branches, and why supervisors look at the group as well as the licensed entity.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain common cross-border group structures in investment, funds, payments and crypto-asset businesses
- Identify where regulated and unregulated entities create gaps or risks
- Explain how consolidated and group-wide supervision affects the local firm

## Syllabus

- Holding companies, operating subsidiaries, branches and service companies
- Why groups grow complex: tax, licences, acquisitions and legacy
- Consolidated and group-wide supervision from the firm's side
- Mapping the group: legal entities, licences, people and money flows
