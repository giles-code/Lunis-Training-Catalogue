## Aim and Purpose

A practical framework for identifying how AI systems fail, are manipulated or behave unexpectedly, and the evidence supervisors should request to test a firm's resilience.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify common AI vulnerabilities and failure modes
- Analyse the operational, cyber and third-party risks specific to AI
- Apply a structured framework to probe firms on AI resilience

## Syllabus

- Drift, data degradation and silent failure in production systems
- Adversarial attacks, data poisoning and prompt injection
- Vendor dependency and sector-wide concentration on AI providers
- Supervisory questions and evidence requests on AI resilience
