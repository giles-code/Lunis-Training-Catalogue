## Aim and Purpose

How bias enters AI systems and produces unfair customer outcomes, how fairness is measured and tested, and the supervisory tools for assessing fairness in AI-driven products and services.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the sources of bias in data, model design and deployment
- Analyse the fair-treatment and non-discrimination implications of AI decisions
- Evaluate a firm's bias testing and fairness monitoring

## Syllabus

- Historical data bias, proxy discrimination and feedback loops
- Fairness metrics and their trade-offs in credit scoring and insurance pricing
- Bias testing methodologies and ongoing outcome monitoring
- Case studies: AI bias, supervisory findings and remediation
