## Aim and Purpose

The firm's exposure to money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions breaches, fraud and bribery, and the controls and reporting the board should expect.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main financial crime risks and the firm's legal obligations
- Assess the firm's financial crime risk assessment and control framework
- Oversee the MLRO's reporting and the resources given to financial crime compliance

## Syllabus

- FATF standards and the risk-based approach
- Customer due diligence, monitoring and suspicious activity reporting
- Sanctions, fraud, bribery and corruption
- The MLRO's annual report and board-level financial crime MI
