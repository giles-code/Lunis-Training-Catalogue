## Aim and Purpose

How infrastructures measure and cover credit and liquidity exposures to participants, the cover standards and stress testing behind them, and how to defend model assumptions under supervisory challenge.

## Learning Objectives

- Measure current and potential future exposures to participants
- Design stress testing, cover standards and qualifying liquid resources
- Defend scenarios, model assumptions and backtesting results to the authority

## Syllabus

- Current and potential future exposure; cover 1 and cover 2 standards
- Liquidity needs, qualifying resources and settlement bank dependencies
- Stress scenario design, reverse stress testing and backtesting
- Weaknesses supervisors look for: optimistic scenarios, wrong-way risk and stale models
