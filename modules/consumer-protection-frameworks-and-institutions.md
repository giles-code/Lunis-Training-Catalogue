## Aim and Purpose

The legal and institutional foundations of financial consumer protection, the international principles that guide them, and the choices in assigning responsibilities to regulators.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the G20/OECD High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection
- Compare institutional models for financial consumer protection
- Assess gaps in disclosure, fair treatment, product and conduct rules

## Syllabus

- Disclosure, transparency of costs and fair treatment
- Responsible lending, product governance and suitability
- Institutional models: integrated, twin peaks and dedicated agencies
- Data protection, privacy and vulnerable consumers
