## Aim and Purpose

What governance risk is, how supervisors assess board effectiveness, accountability and culture, and how to reach a well-supported conclusion on the quality of a firm's governance.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the sub-categories of governance risk
- Plan and carry out a governance assessment using documents, observation and interviews
- Draw and evidence conclusions on governance effectiveness

## Syllabus

- Governance risk: board, management, accountability, culture and group influence
- Evidence sources: board packs, minutes, evaluations and committee papers
- Red flags: dominant individuals, weak challenge and unclear accountability
- Rating governance and linking it to other risk categories
