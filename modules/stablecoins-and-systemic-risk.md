## Aim and Purpose

How stablecoins are structured and supervised, the reserve, redemption and governance risks that determine whether a peg holds, and how stress can transmit to the wider financial system.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish stablecoin structures and how design drives risk and regulatory classification
- Evaluate reserve quality, liquidity and redemption resilience under stress
- Assess systemic risk, bank exposures and supervisory tools for significant stablecoins

## Syllabus

- Fiat-backed, asset-referenced, crypto-collateralised and algorithmic designs
- Reserve composition, custody, attestation and redemption rights
- Red flags: affiliated reserves, redemption frictions and unchecked policy changes
- Runs, contagion and enhanced oversight of systemically significant stablecoins
