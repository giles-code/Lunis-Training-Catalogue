## Aim and Purpose

The business-line uses of AI with the greatest conduct and market integrity risk, and the controls, kill-switches and escalation arrangements the board should expect.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI is used in trading, investment and client-facing advice
- Assess market integrity, suitability and mis-selling risks
- Judge the adequacy of controls, human oversight and escalation protocols

## Syllabus

- Algorithmic trading, portfolio construction and signal generation
- Market integrity risks: correlated behaviour and flash events
- Robo-advice, chatbots and the guidance versus advice boundary
- Kill-switches and escalation: what the board should require
