## Aim and Purpose

An introduction to how money moves through the financial system, the main types of firm and how they are regulated, and why understanding them makes investigators more effective.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the main sectors and types of financial services firm
- Explain how payment, clearing and settlement systems connect firms
- Outline how firms are licensed and supervised

## Syllabus

- The main sectors: banking, payments, investment, funds and fiduciary services
- How money moves: payment systems, clearing and settlement
- Licensing, supervision and the role of the supervisor
- Why knowing the firm matters to an investigation
