## Aim and Purpose

The behavioural foundations of high-performing teams: trust, psychological safety, constructive conflict and shared ownership, and the routines that sustain them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the conditions that enable teams to perform well
- Use cognitive diversity and constructive conflict to improve decisions
- Design team routines that build cohesion and shared ownership

## Syllabus

- Trust and psychological safety in supervisory teams
- Constructive conflict and collaborative decision-making
- Team structures, roles and routines
- Building a team charter
