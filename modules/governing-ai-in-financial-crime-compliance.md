## Aim and Purpose

Applying model risk management to AI-driven financial crime tools, so that the firm can explain, validate, monitor and defend them, and avoid unfair or discriminatory outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply model risk management to AML, screening and fraud models
- Evaluate explainability, human oversight and audit trails
- Assess bias and de-risking effects on customers

## Syllabus

- Model inventory, validation and performance monitoring
- Explainability for investigators, auditors and supervisors
- Vendor tools, data quality and third-party dependency
- Bias, financial exclusion and unwarranted de-risking
