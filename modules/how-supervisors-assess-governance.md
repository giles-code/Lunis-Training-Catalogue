## Aim and Purpose

Why governance is a core supervisory risk, how a governance assessment is scoped and carried out, and how its conclusions affect the firm's ratings and supervisory engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of a supervisory governance assessment
- Describe the stages from desk review to conclusions
- Anticipate how governance findings are rated and followed up

## Syllabus

- Governance risk and why supervisors assess it
- Proportionality and materiality in governance reviews
- Desk analysis, interviews and conclusions
- What strong, adequate and weak governance look like
