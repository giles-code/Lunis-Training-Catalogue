## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together: reporting findings to the audit committee, tracking remediation to closure, and building a constructive relationship with supervisors, ending with an audit improvement plan.

## Learning Objectives

- Write audit reports and committee papers that drive action
- Run an effective issue-tracking and validation process
- Engage confidently with supervisors and agree an improvement plan

## Syllabus

- Report writing, ratings and the head of audit's annual opinion
- Issue tracking, validation of remediation and escalation of overdue actions
- Supervisory meetings, information requests and regulator-commissioned reviews
- Workshop: an improvement plan for the internal audit function
