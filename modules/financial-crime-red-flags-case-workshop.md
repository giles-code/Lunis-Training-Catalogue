## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a case study in which participants review a firm's profile and information, identify financial crime risks and agree the supervisory response.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify financial crime risks and red flags in a case scenario
- Assess the adequacy of the firm's controls
- Agree and justify an appropriate supervisory response and escalation

## Syllabus

- Case briefing: firm profile, customer base and transaction data
- Identifying red flags and control weaknesses in small groups
- Deciding on escalation and referral
- Workshop: presenting findings and the proposed supervisory response
