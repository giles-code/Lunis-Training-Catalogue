## Aim and Purpose

Why supervision depends on judgement rather than rule-checking alone, where uncertainty comes from, and what accountability for a judgement requires of the individual supervisor and the authority.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role of judgement in risk-based, forward-looking supervision
- Identify the main sources of uncertainty in supervisory decisions
- Distinguish judgement from discretion and describe what accountability requires

## Syllabus

- From compliance checking to judgement-led supervision
- Sources of uncertainty: incomplete information, evolving risk and ambiguous rules
- The cost of over- and under-cautious judgements
- Judgement, discretion and the supervisor's accountability
