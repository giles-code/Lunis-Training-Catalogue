## Aim and Purpose

How asset, wealth and private market managers fit into the financial system, how their business models have changed, and why non-bank financial intermediation is now a supervisory priority.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the business models and revenue drivers of investment managers
- Distinguish the regulation of managers, products and client services
- Describe the growth of non-bank financial intermediation and private markets

## Syllabus

- Traditional, alternative, wealth and private market managers
- Fee pressure, consolidation and the shift to passive and private assets
- Manager, product and service regulation: where the perimeter sits
- FSB and IOSCO work on non-bank financial intermediation
