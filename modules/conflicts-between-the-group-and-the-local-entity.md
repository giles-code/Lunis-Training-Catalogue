## Aim and Purpose

Where the interests of the group and a regulated subsidiary diverge, from capital and liquidity transfers to pricing, client allocation and strategy, and how directors identify, manage and record those conflicts.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify common conflicts between group and local entity interests
- Apply a structured approach to managing and recording group conflicts
- Challenge group decisions that disadvantage the entity, its customers or its regulators

## Syllabus

- Intragroup funding, dividends, guarantees and transfer pricing
- Client allocation, product shelf and revenue sharing across entities
- Local directors' conflicts when they also hold group roles
- Case study: a group restructuring the local board does not support
