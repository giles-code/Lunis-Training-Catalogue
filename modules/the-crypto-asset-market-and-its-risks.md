## Aim and Purpose

How the crypto-asset market is structured, who the main participants are, and the consumer, market integrity and financial stability risks revealed by recent market failures.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe exchanges, brokers, custodians, issuers and DeFi protocols
- Explain the risks of conflicts of interest, commingling and market manipulation
- Assess the links between crypto-asset markets and the wider financial system

## Syllabus

- Market structure: centralised platforms, vertical integration and DeFi
- Consumer harm: fraud, mis-selling, custody losses and platform failures
- Case studies of exchange and lender collapses
- Financial stability: interconnectedness and spillover channels
