## Aim and Purpose

How control of crypto-assets rests on cryptographic keys, the custody models firms use to safeguard client assets, and the operational and governance controls clients, auditors and supervisors will test.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how private keys determine control of assets
- Evaluate custody models and their risks
- Assess operational and cyber risks in custody arrangements

## Syllabus

- Public and private keys: how asset control works
- Hot, cold, hardware, multi-signature and custodial wallets
- Segregation, reconciliation and safeguarding of client assets
- Key loss, hacks and system failure: lessons from custody incidents
