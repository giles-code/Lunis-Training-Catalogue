## Aim and Purpose

How corporate sustainability reporting standards compare, the quality and limits of ESG data, ratings and scores, and what this means for firms relying on them.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare the ISSB standards with other international sustainability reporting frameworks
- Evaluate the reliability and limits of ESG data, ratings and scoring methodologies
- Assess how firms govern their use of third-party ESG data

## Syllabus

- ISSB standards and their foundations in TCFD
- Regional reporting standards and interoperability
- ESG ratings and scores: methodologies, divergence and conflicts
- Data gaps, estimates and assurance of sustainability information
