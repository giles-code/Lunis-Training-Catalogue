## Aim and Purpose

The international bodies that set financial standards, how those standards bind or influence national policy, and how assessments and peer reviews judge a country's framework.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the roles of the G20, FSB, BCBS, IOSCO, IAIS, CPMI and FATF
- Explain how standards are developed and how government can influence them
- Prepare for IMF and World Bank assessments, FSB peer reviews and FATF evaluations

## Syllabus

- The international financial architecture and its standard-setters
- Core principles for banking, insurance, securities and market infrastructure
- Financial sector assessments, peer reviews and mutual evaluations
- Implementing recommendations and managing reputational consequences
