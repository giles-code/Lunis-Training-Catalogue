## Aim and Purpose

What the compliance function is for, where it sits in the firm's governance and what independence, authority and resources it needs to be credible to the business and the supervisor.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the mandate of compliance and its place in the three lines model
- Assess the independence, standing and resourcing of the firm's compliance function
- Identify the personal responsibilities and accountability of the compliance officer

## Syllabus

- The compliance charter, mandate and reporting lines
- Independence, access to the board and conflicts of interest
- Resourcing, skills and the use of outsourced compliance support
- How supervisors judge whether a compliance function is effective
