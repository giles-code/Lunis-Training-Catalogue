## Aim and Purpose

How funding, guarantees and shared services link entities within a group, how stress spreads between them, and the supervisory risks of opaque or complex structures.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess types of intra-group exposure and dependency
- Identify contagion channels within a cross-border group
- Evaluate the risks of opaque or unnecessarily complex structures

## Syllabus

- Intra-group funding, guarantees, liquidity and capital flows
- Shared services, outsourcing and operational dependencies
- Ring-fencing, structural subordination and trapped resources
- Case study: assessing a complex cross-border group
