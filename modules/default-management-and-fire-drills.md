## Aim and Purpose

How the infrastructure prepares for and handles a participant default, from declaration, close-out and auction to the default waterfall, and how fire drills test that procedures work under pressure.

## Learning Objectives

- Design default rules, procedures and the sequence of the default waterfall
- Size the default fund and the infrastructure's own resources at risk
- Plan and run default management fire drills and auctions

## Syllabus

- Declaring a default, porting client positions and close-out
- Default waterfall: margin, default fund, own resources and assessments
- Auction design, participant obligations and hedging
- Fire drill design, findings and remediation
