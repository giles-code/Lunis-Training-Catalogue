## Aim and Purpose

How the authority defines the risks it will and will not tolerate, accepts that a non-zero-failure regime is inevitable, and allocates scarce supervisory resources accordingly.

## Learning Objectives

- Define a risk appetite for the authority and its tolerance for firm failure
- Apply risk-based prioritisation to supervisory coverage and resourcing
- Evaluate trade-offs between breadth and depth of supervision

## Syllabus

- The non-zero-failure regime and communicating it honestly
- Drafting the authority's risk appetite and tolerance statements
- Budget, headcount, specialist skills and value for money
- Exercise: prioritising a supervisory risk register under constraints
