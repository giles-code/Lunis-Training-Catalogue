## Aim and Purpose

How insider dealing and manipulation occur in crypto markets, from wash trading to maximal extractable value, and how supervisors assess surveillance and use suspicious order reports as intelligence.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify insider dealing and unlawful disclosure risks in token issuance and trading
- Detect traditional and crypto-native manipulation schemes
- Assess firms' trade surveillance and suspicious transaction and order reporting

## Syllabus

- Market abuse prohibitions adapted to crypto-asset markets
- Wash trading, spoofing, pump-and-dump schemes and MEV exploitation
- Surveillance systems, alert calibration and investigation workflows
- Investigative techniques: order book analysis and on-chain tracing
