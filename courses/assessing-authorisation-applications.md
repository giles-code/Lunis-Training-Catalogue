## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build a structured methodology for assessing new licence applications, including scrutinising business plans, financial projections, and organisational readiness.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level authorisations/licensing caseworkers assessing new-entrant applications.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and standard of assessment for authorisation applications
- Apply a structured framework for reviewing a business plan
- Assess the credibility of financial projections
- Evaluate organisational readiness, including systems and controls
- Formulate an authorisation recommendation based on assessed evidence

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The authorisation assessment standard (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the purpose and standard of assessment*
- The threshold conditions for authorisation
- Assessment as gatekeeping: balancing market access and risk
- Sources of evidence in an application
- Common application weaknesses
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: authorisation assessment experiences

**Session 2: Business plan and financial projections (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply a business plan framework and assess financial projections*
- Structured business plan review: market, model, viability
- Assessing the credibility of financial projections
- Common red flags in projections
- Stress-testing an applicant's growth assumptions
Activities:
- Group exercise: review a sample business plan and financial projections

**Session 3: Organisational readiness and recommendation (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate organisational readiness and formulate a recommendation*
- Assessing systems, controls and staffing readiness
- Weighing readiness gaps against conditions or phased authorisation
- Case study: a marginal authorisation application
Activities:
- Case study: assess a sample application and formulate an authorisation recommendation

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: assessment standard, business plan review, readiness
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The authorisation assessment standard (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Business plan and financial projections (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Organisational readiness and recommendation (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Authorisation assessment is a gatekeeping function that shapes the risk profile of the whole regulated population
- Financial projections should be stress-tested, not simply accepted at face value
- Organisational readiness gaps can sometimes be addressed through conditions rather than outright refusal
