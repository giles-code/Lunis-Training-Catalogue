## Aim and Purpose

The personal standards expected of a supervisor, the threats to independence and objectivity, and the habits that underpin credible supervisory judgement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the ethical duties and conduct standards that apply to regulatory staff
- Recognise conflicts of interest, capture and other threats to independence
- Apply professional scepticism and sound judgement in everyday work

## Syllabus

- Confidentiality, conflicts of interest, gifts and hospitality
- Regulatory capture and maintaining independence from industry
- Professional scepticism, challenge and willingness to act
- Accountability for decisions and recording the rationale
