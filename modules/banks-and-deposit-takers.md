## Aim and Purpose

How banks and deposit-takers create credit, manage maturity transformation and earn margin, and the credit, liquidity and concentration risks that most often cause failure.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain maturity transformation, credit creation and net interest margin
- Identify the main credit, liquidity and interest rate risks of banks
- Frame supervisory questions on lending, funding and asset quality

## Syllabus

- Deposit-taking, lending and the net interest margin
- Credit risk, provisioning and non-performing exposures
- Funding concentration, deposit runs and interest rate risk
- Lessons from recent bank failures for supervisors
