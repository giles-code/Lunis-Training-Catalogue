## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in communicating effectively with the public, industry, and government during a financial crisis or firm failure, building on general media communication skills with crisis-specific technique.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior staff involved in crisis preparedness, recovery, and resolution planning.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the distinct demands of crisis communication versus routine communication
- Apply techniques for drafting clear public statements under time pressure
- Assess the risks of premature or excessive disclosure during a crisis
- Evaluate stakeholder communication needs during a crisis
- Formulate a crisis communication plan for a sample scenario

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The demands of crisis communication (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain how crisis communication differs from routine communication*
- Why crisis communication requires distinct skills and speed
- Balancing transparency against financial stability risk
- The role of the authority's spokesperson during a crisis
- Learning from past crisis communication successes and failures
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: crisis communication examples participants have observed

**Session 2: Drafting under pressure (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply techniques for drafting statements under time pressure*
- Structuring a clear, accurate public statement quickly
- Avoiding common crisis communication mistakes
- Managing media questions and follow-up
- Coordinating messaging across multiple authorities
Activities:
- Exercise: draft a public statement for a simulated crisis scenario against the clock

**Session 3: Disclosure risk and stakeholder needs (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess disclosure risk and evaluate stakeholder needs*
- Risks of premature or excessive disclosure during a crisis
- Differentiated messaging for public, industry, and government audiences
- Case study: a firm failure requiring coordinated crisis communication
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a full crisis communication plan for a sample scenario

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: crisis communication demands, drafting, disclosure risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The demands of crisis communication (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Drafting under pressure (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Disclosure risk and stakeholder needs (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Crisis communication requires speed and clarity that routine communication practice does not train for
- Getting the balance between transparency and financial stability right is a genuine judgement call, not a formula
- Different stakeholder audiences need different messages delivered through coordinated channels
