## Aim and Purpose

How a net asset value is produced each dealing day, the process flow from trade capture to publication, and the break points and controls the board should understand.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the fund accounting and NAV production process step by step
- Identify the key risk areas and controls in the NAV cycle
- Interpret administrator reporting and controls reports

## Syllabus

- Trade capture, reconciliations, pricing, accruals and fee calculations
- NAV review, sign-off and publication
- Common break points: stale prices, corporate actions and late trades
- Controls reports and what they do not cover
