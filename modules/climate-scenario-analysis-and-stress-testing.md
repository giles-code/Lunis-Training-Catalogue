## Aim and Purpose

Using climate scenarios to explore the resilience of the firm's business model and portfolios, and turning results into decisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the design and use of NGFS climate scenarios
- Apply scenario analysis to portfolios and the business model
- Translate scenario results into risk management and strategy

## Syllabus

- NGFS scenarios: orderly, disorderly and hot house world
- Time horizons, data gaps and modelling limitations
- Portfolio and business model scenario analysis
- Using results in capital planning and strategy
