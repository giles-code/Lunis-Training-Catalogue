## Aim and Purpose

The question types that elicit full and accurate accounts, how to sequence them using the funnel technique, and the questioning habits that undermine an interview.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply open, probing and closed questions for the right purpose
- Use the funnel technique to move from broad accounts to specific facts
- Avoid leading, multiple and assumptive questions

## Syllabus

- Open questions to obtain a free account
- Probing and clarifying questions to test detail and gaps
- Funnel sequencing: from general to specific to confirmation
- Exercise: rewriting weak questions into effective ones
