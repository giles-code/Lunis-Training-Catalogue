## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build practical techniques for assessing fund valuation governance and methodology, with particular focus on hard-to-value and illiquid assets and the conflicts of interest that can arise in valuation processes.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment funds supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the regulatory framework for fund valuation
- Describe common valuation methodologies for hard-to-value assets
- Assess the governance arrangements surrounding a fund's valuation process
- Identify conflicts of interest that can arise in valuation
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a fund's valuation practice

## Syllabus

**Session 1: The valuation framework (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the regulatory valuation framework*
- Regulatory expectations for fair and independent valuation
- The role of the valuer: internal versus external, and independence requirements
- Valuation frequency and its relationship to fund liquidity terms
- Consequences of valuation errors for investors
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: valuation challenges participants have encountered

**Session 2: Valuation methodologies for hard-to-value assets (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe methodologies for hard-to-value assets*
- Common methodologies: comparable transactions, discounted cash flow, net asset value adjustments
- Particular challenges of valuing illiquid or unique assets
- Assessing the reasonableness of key valuation assumptions
- Red flags in valuation methodology
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample valuation methodology for a hard-to-value asset

**Session 3: Governance, conflicts and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Assess governance, identify conflicts, and formulate an assessment*
- Governance arrangements: valuation committees, independent oversight
- Conflicts of interest: manager influence over valuation outcomes
- Case study: a fund with a valuation governance weakness
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a fund's valuation practice

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: framework, methodologies, governance, conflicts
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: The valuation framework (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Valuation methodologies for hard-to-value assets (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Governance, conflicts and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Valuation of hard-to-value assets is inherently judgement-based, making governance and independence critical
- Conflicts of interest in valuation are a persistent risk wherever the manager benefits from higher valuations
- Valuation frequency should be assessed against a fund's actual liquidity terms, not treated as a fixed standard
