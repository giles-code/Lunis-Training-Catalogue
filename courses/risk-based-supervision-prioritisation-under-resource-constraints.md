## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in designing and applying a risk-based supervisory approach that allocates limited supervisory resource to the areas of greatest risk, addressing the common challenge of unclear mandates and resource constraints.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisory staff conducting day-to-day risk assessment and firm engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the principles of risk-based supervision
- Apply a risk appetite framework to prioritise supervisory work
- Assess supervisory intensity models and their trade-offs
- Evaluate resource allocation decisions against risk priorities
- Formulate a risk-based supervisory work plan under resource constraints

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Principles of risk-based supervision (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain risk-based supervision principles*
- Why risk-based supervision replaced compliance-based approaches
- Risk appetite frameworks for supervisory authorities
- The relationship between risk appetite and resource allocation
- International practice on risk-based supervisory models
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: resource constraints participants face in their own authorities

**Session 2: Supervisory intensity models (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Apply a risk appetite framework and assess intensity models*
- Models for varying supervisory intensity by risk tier
- Trade-offs: breadth of coverage versus depth of scrutiny
- Dynamic reprioritisation as risks change
- Tools for structured resource allocation
Activities:
- Group exercise: design a supervisory intensity model for a sample population of firms

**Session 3: Resource allocation and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate resource allocation and formulate a work plan*
- Evaluating whether resource allocation matches stated risk priorities
- Managing stakeholder expectations when resource is constrained
- Case study: reprioritising a supervisory work plan mid-year
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a risk-based work plan under a constrained resource scenario

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: principles, intensity models, resource allocation
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Principles of risk-based supervision (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Supervisory intensity models (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Resource allocation and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Risk-based supervision is fundamentally about making resource trade-offs explicit and defensible
- Supervisory intensity should vary deliberately across the regulated population, not uniformly
- A good risk-based work plan can be reprioritised quickly as new risks emerge
