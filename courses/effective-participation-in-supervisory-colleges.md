## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to prepare participants to participate effectively in supervisory colleges, building skills in joint risk assessment and coordinated decision-making with fellow supervisors from other jurisdictions.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior supervisors involved in cross-border/group supervision and international cooperation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and governance of supervisory colleges
- Describe the typical structure and cadence of college meetings
- Contribute effectively to a joint risk assessment
- Apply techniques for coordinated supervisory decision-making
- Identify common challenges in college dynamics and how to address them

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Supervisory colleges: purpose and governance (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the purpose and governance of colleges*
- Why supervisory colleges exist
- College governance: chair, secretariat, membership
- The college's role alongside home-host bilateral relationships
- College outputs: joint risk assessments, joint decisions
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: participants' experience of multilateral coordination

**Session 2: Joint risk assessment in practice (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Contribute effectively to a joint risk assessment*
- Building a shared risk picture across jurisdictions
- Reconciling differing national risk assessments
- Techniques for constructive challenge within a college
- Producing a joint risk assessment output
Activities:
- Group exercise: draft a joint risk assessment section for a sample group

**Session 3: Coordinated decision-making and college dynamics (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Apply coordinated decision-making and manage college challenges*
- Reaching coordinated supervisory decisions
- Managing disagreement between college members
- Common challenges: unequal engagement, language, resourcing
- Case study: a college facing a coordination challenge
Activities:
- Role-play: a college discussion requiring coordinated decision-making

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: college purpose, joint assessment, coordinated decisions
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Supervisory colleges: purpose and governance (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Joint risk assessment in practice (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Coordinated decision-making and college dynamics (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Colleges exist to build a shared risk picture that no single supervisor could construct alone
- Effective participation requires active contribution, not passive attendance
- Coordinated decisions are stronger and more defensible than isolated national ones
