## Aim and Purpose

Why opaque legal persons and arrangements are a core vulnerability, the FATF requirements on beneficial ownership, and the policy choices in designing and operating registers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the FATF requirements for legal persons and legal arrangements
- Evaluate register models, verification and access arrangements
- Assess whether competent authorities obtain accurate information in a timely way

## Syllabus

- Recommendations 24 and 25 and the multi-pronged approach
- Register design: scope, thresholds, verification and sanctions
- Access for authorities, obliged entities and the public
- Trusts, nominees, bearer shares and foreign legal persons
