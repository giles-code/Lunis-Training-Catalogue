## Aim and Purpose

Controls for AI used in algorithmic trading, portfolio construction and operational processing, the market integrity risks it can introduce, and the kill-switches and escalation the firm must build.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI is used in trading, portfolio construction, signal generation and processing
- Assess market integrity risks, including correlated behaviour and flash events
- Design kill-switches, human oversight and escalation for AI-driven systems

## Syllabus

- AI in algorithmic and high-frequency trading
- AI-driven portfolio construction, risk tools and reconciliation
- Market integrity risks: correlated behaviour, flash events and algorithmic collusion
- Designing kill-switches, limits and escalation protocols
