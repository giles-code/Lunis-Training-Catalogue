## Aim and Purpose

The board's oversight of how fund assets are valued and how liquidity is managed, with particular focus on hard-to-value assets and stressed market conditions.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess valuation policies, committees and the treatment of hard-to-value assets
- Evaluate liquidity risk management, stress testing and available tools
- Challenge management on matching fund liquidity to redemption terms

## Syllabus

- Valuation governance, pricing sources and fair value adjustments
- Liquidity mismatch, stress testing and investor concentration
- Liquidity management tools: swing pricing, gates and suspensions
- Board decisions under stress: lessons from fund suspensions
