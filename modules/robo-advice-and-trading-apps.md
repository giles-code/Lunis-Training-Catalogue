## Aim and Purpose

How automated advice and commission-free trading apps make money, how digital design shapes investor behaviour, and what supervisors examine in suitability, order handling and gamification.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain robo-advice and trading app revenue models, including payment for order flow
- Assess suitability algorithms, risk profiling and portfolio rebalancing
- Evaluate gamification, leverage and complex product access for retail investors

## Syllabus

- Hybrid and fully automated advice models
- Commission-free trading, spreads, securities lending and order routing
- Gamification, push notifications and copy trading
- Supervisory testing of algorithms and customer journeys
