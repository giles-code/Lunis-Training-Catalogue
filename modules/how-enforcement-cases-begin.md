## Aim and Purpose

How authorities use enforcement within the supervisory toolkit, where cases come from and how triage decides which matters become formal investigations, so the firm can recognise its own exposure early.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose of enforcement and the principle of credible deterrence
- Identify the routes by which issues at the firm reach the enforcement function
- Recognise the early warning signs that a supervisory matter may escalate

## Syllabus

- Credible deterrence and where supervision ends and enforcement begins
- Referral sources: supervision, whistleblowers, complaints, surveillance and other authorities
- Triage criteria: seriousness, harm, evidential strength and public interest
- Warning signs: escalating requests, thematic outliers and repeat findings
