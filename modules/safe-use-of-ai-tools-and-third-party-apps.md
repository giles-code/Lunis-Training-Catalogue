## Aim and Purpose

How to use AI tools, cloud services and third-party apps safely within the firm's policies, avoid shadow IT and data leakage, and apply the programme to practical scenarios.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the firm's policies on AI tools, cloud services and third-party apps
- Recognise the data leakage, accuracy and security risks of unapproved tools
- Apply the programme to realistic cyber, data and resilience scenarios

## Syllabus

- Approved versus unapproved tools: shadow IT and personal accounts
- What never goes into an AI tool: customer, confidential and personal data
- Browser extensions, messaging apps and file-sharing services
- Workshop: what would you do? Cyber, data and disruption scenarios
