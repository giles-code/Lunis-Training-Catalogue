## Aim and Purpose

Who does what in a fund, from the management company and investment manager to the depositary, administrator and the wider circle of service providers.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the roles of the management company and investment manager
- Explain the safekeeping, oversight and administration functions
- Identify the other parties involved and how they connect

## Syllabus

- How a fund is organised and the flow of responsibilities
- Management company and investment manager
- Depositary and fund administrator: custody, oversight, valuation and transfer agency
- Distributors, auditors, legal advisers, brokers and other parties
