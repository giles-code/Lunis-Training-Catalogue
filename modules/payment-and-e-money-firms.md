## Aim and Purpose

How payment institutions, e-money issuers, money remitters and card schemes work, how they differ from banks, and the transaction and device data they hold.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the business models of payment, e-money and remittance firms
- Describe the roles of card issuers, acquirers, schemes and payment processors
- Identify the transaction, account and device data these firms hold

## Syllabus

- Payment institutions, e-money issuers and money remitters
- Cards: issuers, acquirers, schemes and merchants
- Agents, distributors and safeguarded customer funds
- Records: transaction logs, IP and device data and merchant files
