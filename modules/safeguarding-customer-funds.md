## Aim and Purpose

Why safeguarding is the single most important customer protection in payments, the methods available and how firms design, reconcile and audit their arrangements.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the purpose and methods of safeguarding
- Design safeguarding reconciliations and controls
- Prepare for safeguarding audits and supervisory reviews

## Syllabus

- Segregation, insurance and guarantee methods
- Relevant funds, timing and designated safeguarding accounts
- Daily reconciliations, breaks and escalation
- Safeguarding audits, attestations and failures in insolvency
