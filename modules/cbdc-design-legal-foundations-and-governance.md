## Aim and Purpose

The design and legal choices a CBDC requires, the roles of the central bank, government and private intermediaries, and the legislative and accountability questions for government and legislators.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main CBDC architecture and distribution models
- Identify the legal basis, legal tender and central bank mandate questions
- Define the decision rights of government, legislature and central bank

## Syllabus

- Direct, intermediated and hybrid architectures
- Offline functionality, programmability and interoperability
- Legal basis, legal tender status and central bank law amendments
- Democratic accountability and the decision to launch
