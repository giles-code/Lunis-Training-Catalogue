## Aim and Purpose

How to delegate control work with clear outcomes and quality standards, set meaningful objectives for second-line staff, and address underperformance fairly and early.

## Learning Objectives

- Delegate work with clear outcomes, authority, checkpoints and sign-off standards
- Set objectives that reward quality of judgement rather than volume of activity
- Manage underperformance and difficult people issues fairly and early

## Syllabus

- Effective delegation: what, to whom and how much review
- Objectives and measures for control function staff
- Performance conversations, including underperformance
- Quality assurance of the team's work without rewriting it
