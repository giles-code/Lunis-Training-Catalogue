## Aim and Purpose

How tokenisation, distributed ledgers and new technology are changing fund issuance, dealing and servicing, and the regulatory and operational questions this raises.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how tokenised fund units are issued, held and transferred
- Assess custody, record-keeping and settlement risks in tokenised models
- Identify how existing fund rules apply to new technology

## Syllabus

- Tokenised fund units and on-chain registers
- Custody, transfer agency and settlement on distributed ledgers
- Tokenised money market funds as collateral and settlement assets
- Technology, AI and operational resilience in fund servicing
