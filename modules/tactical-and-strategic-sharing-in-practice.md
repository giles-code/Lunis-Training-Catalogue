## Aim and Purpose

How partnerships share information on specific cases and subjects and on threats and typologies, and how outputs feed investigations, reporting and firms' controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Run tactical sharing on live cases securely and lawfully
- Produce strategic threat and typology products with partners
- Turn shared intelligence into investigative and preventive action

## Syllabus

- Tactical sharing: requests, responses and targeted reporting
- Strategic sharing: threat assessments, typologies and alerts
- Handling, dissemination and onward use of shared information
- Feeding results into investigations and firms' monitoring
