## Aim and Purpose

MiCA's market abuse regime and how it adapts established market abuse concepts to crypto, the manipulation schemes specific to crypto markets, and how supervisors assess surveillance and use order reports as intelligence.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain MiCA's prohibitions on insider dealing, unlawful disclosure and manipulation
- Detect crypto-native manipulation, including wash trading, spoofing and MEV extraction
- Assess CASP surveillance systems and suspicious transaction and order reporting

## Syllabus

- Inside information in token issuance, listings and CASP operations
- Wash trading, pump-and-dump schemes, spoofing and MEV
- Surveillance calibration, investigation workflows and STORs as intelligence
- Cross-venue analysis, enforcement referral and cross-border cooperation
