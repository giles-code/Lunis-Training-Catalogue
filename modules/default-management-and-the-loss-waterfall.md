## Aim and Purpose

How FMIs handle a participant default, from close-out, hedging and auction to the sequence of resources in the default waterfall, and how supervisors test readiness through fire drills.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain default rules, procedures and the sequence of the default waterfall
- Assess the adequacy of skin in the game and default fund sizing
- Evaluate default management fire drills and auction design

## Syllabus

- Declaring a default, porting client positions and close-out
- Default waterfall: margin, default fund, own resources and assessments
- Auction design, participant obligations and hedging
- Lessons from clearing member defaults and fire drill findings
