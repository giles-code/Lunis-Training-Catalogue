## Aim and Purpose

Building governance, risk and compliance functions that are genuinely effective rather than merely documented, and using self-assessment to find weaknesses before a supervisor does.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a management body, oversight functions and three lines of defence that work in practice
- Operate risk appetite, compliance monitoring and internal audit with real independence
- Detect governance red flags and apply proportionate remediation

## Syllabus

- Board composition, fitness and propriety and segregation of duties
- Risk appetite, risk registers and independence of control functions
- Compliance monitoring plans and internal audit scope
- Red flags: founder dominance, nominal independence and marginalised risk functions
