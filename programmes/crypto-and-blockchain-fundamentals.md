## Aim and Purpose

To give supervisors a comprehensive, practical grounding in how blockchain technology works and how crypto-asset markets, products and service providers operate. Participants leave able to analyse business models and their economic function, identify the key prudential, conduct and financial crime risks, and apply risk-based judgement to digital finance scenarios.

## What It Covers

Blockchain technology, crypto-asset typology and market structure, wallets and custody, virtual asset service providers, tokenisation, stablecoins and unbacked crypto-assets, DeFi and emerging technologies, supervisory risks, financial crime and the travel rule, and digital finance strategy and policy.

## Intended Audience

- Supervisors and authorisation staff responsible for, or moving into, oversight of virtual asset service providers
- Risk, policy, legal and financial crime specialists working on digital finance
- Senior managers shaping the authority's approach to crypto-assets and digital finance

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how blockchain and distributed ledger technology operate within digital finance
- Analyse crypto-asset types and business models to determine their economic function and risk profile
- Evaluate the supervisory risks of custody, tokenisation and stablecoins
- Assess the activities, governance and controls of virtual asset service providers
- Apply AML/CFT requirements, including the travel rule, to crypto-asset activity
- Evaluate emerging trends and policy choices that balance innovation with effective risk management

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about four hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines technical explanation with case studies, red flags and supervisory question exercises, and modules can be selected or tailored to the authority's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of financial supervision, authorisation or financial crime work is helpful. No technical background in blockchain or crypto-assets is required.

## Core Reading

- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Crypto-Asset Activities and Markets (2023)
- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets (2023)
- FATF, Updated Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (2021)
- Basel Committee, Prudential Treatment of Cryptoasset Exposures (2022)
