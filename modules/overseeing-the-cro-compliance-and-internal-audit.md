## Aim and Purpose

How committees ensure the control functions have the independence, standing and resources to provide effective challenge and assurance, and how they get the most from their heads.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the independence, resourcing and reporting lines of the control functions
- Assess risk, compliance and internal audit plans and their coverage
- Identify warning signs of weak or captured control functions

## Syllabus

- The chief risk officer's role, access and protection from dismissal
- Compliance monitoring plans, findings and regulatory relationships
- Internal audit charter, risk-based plan, quality and follow-up
- Private sessions, appraisal and appointment of function heads
