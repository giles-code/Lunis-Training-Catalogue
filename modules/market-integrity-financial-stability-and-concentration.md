## Aim and Purpose

The systemic risks AI may create, from herding and correlated trading to dependence on a few technology providers, and the international work assessing them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AI can amplify herding, procyclicality and market volatility
- Assess concentration and third-party dependency on cloud and model providers
- Evaluate the financial stability analysis of the FSB, BIS and IMF

## Syllabus

- Algorithmic trading, correlated behaviour and flash events
- Cloud and foundation-model concentration and critical third parties
- Cyber risk, AI-enabled fraud and deepfakes
- International financial stability assessments of AI
