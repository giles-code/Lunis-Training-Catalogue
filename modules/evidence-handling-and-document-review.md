## Aim and Purpose

How to collect, preserve and review large volumes of documents and data so that evidence is reliable, admissible and well organised.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply sound practice in evidence collection, continuity and storage
- Plan and manage document review, including electronic review tools
- Analyse evidence and build a chronology against the elements of the breach

## Syllabus

- Chain of custody, forensic copies and data integrity
- Electronic disclosure, search terms and review workflows
- Chronologies, evidence matrices and gap analysis
- Disclosure obligations and unused material
