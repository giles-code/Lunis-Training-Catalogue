## Aim and Purpose

Brings the update together as participants prioritise the year's developments for a fund range and draft the board's regulatory change plan and reporting.

## Learning Objectives

- Prioritise regulatory developments by impact, deadline and risk
- Allocate oversight across the board, committees and management
- Produce a board regulatory change plan with milestones and assurance

## Syllabus

- Impact and urgency ranking of the year's developments
- Allocating owners, committee oversight and provider dependencies
- Evidence the board should hold for supervisors and auditors
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month regulatory change plan
