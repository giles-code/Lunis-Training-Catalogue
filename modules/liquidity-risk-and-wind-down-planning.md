## Aim and Purpose

Assessing the firm's liquidity needs under normal and stressed conditions, maintaining a contingency funding plan and preparing for an orderly wind-down if needed.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the sources of liquidity risk in the firm's business model
- Assess liquidity adequacy under business-as-usual and stressed conditions
- Design a contingency funding plan and an orderly wind-down plan

## Syllabus

- Liquidity risk drivers, metrics and limits
- Internal liquidity adequacy self-assessment
- Contingency funding plans and early warning indicators
- Wind-down planning: triggers, costs and timelines
