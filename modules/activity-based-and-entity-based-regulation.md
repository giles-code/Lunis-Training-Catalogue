## Aim and Purpose

Why the perimeter struggles with platforms that combine regulated and unregulated activities, and the arguments for activity-based rules, entity-based requirements or a combination of the two.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare activity-based and entity-based approaches to regulating platforms
- Analyse perimeter gaps created by unregulated activities within platform groups
- Evaluate group-level and holding company options for large platforms

## Syllabus

- Same activity, same risk, same regulation: strengths and limits
- Entity-based requirements for systemically relevant platforms
- Mixed-activity groups and intragroup dependencies
- FSB and BIS work on regulatory approaches to big tech
