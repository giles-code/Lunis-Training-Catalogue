## Aim and Purpose

How the board tests whether fees and costs are justified, whether investors receive value, and whether the fund is treating them fairly.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the components of fund fees and costs
- Assess value for investors against performance, service and comparable products
- Challenge undue costs and evidence the board's conclusions

## Syllabus

- Management, performance and transaction fees, and other charges
- Undue costs and supervisory expectations on fund fees
- Value assessment: performance, service, comparable funds and share classes
- Fair treatment, disclosure and closet tracking
