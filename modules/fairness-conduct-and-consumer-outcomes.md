## Aim and Purpose

The board's responsibility for fair outcomes where AI makes or influences decisions: how bias arises, how fairness is tested, and how the board gains assurance on customer treatment.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how bias and proxy discrimination enter AI-driven decisions
- Evaluate whether bias testing and outcome monitoring give real assurance
- Identify the conduct, legal and reputational exposure of unfair outcomes

## Syllabus

- Sources of bias: historical data, proxies and feedback loops
- Fairness measures in credit, insurance pricing and targeting
- Consumer protection, fair treatment and non-discrimination obligations applied to AI
- Case studies of AI bias harms and remediation
