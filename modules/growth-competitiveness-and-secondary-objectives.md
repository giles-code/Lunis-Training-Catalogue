## Aim and Purpose

How authorities with growth, competitiveness or market development objectives can support a thriving financial sector while preserving safety, consumer protection and market integrity.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the rise of growth and competitiveness objectives for regulators
- Assess where growth objectives align with, and conflict with, primary objectives
- Identify regulatory levers that support growth without lowering standards

## Syllabus

- Secondary objectives on growth, competitiveness and market development
- Proportionality, speed of authorisation and regulatory burden as growth levers
- Innovation, market access and international attractiveness
- Guarding against capture and a race to the bottom
