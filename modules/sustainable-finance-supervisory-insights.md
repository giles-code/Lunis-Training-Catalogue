## Aim and Purpose

Discussed sustainability risk assessments and their implications for supervised entities, equipping participants to address sustainability risk and integrate sustainable finance into day-to-day supervision.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in sustainable finance/ESG supervision.

- Sustainable Finance Risks and EU Regulation Explain the key environmental, social and
- Sustainable Finance Reporting, Governance and Risk Management Describe the European Sustainability Reporting

## Syllabus

**Workshop 1** — Sustainable Finance Risks and EU Regulation Explain the key environmental, social and governance risks that impact financial products and services Describe the process of undertaking a sustainability risk assessment Identify greenwashing and greenbleaching practices Discuss the EU’s regulatory framework for sustainable finance, including the SFDR, CSRD and CSDDD

**Workshop 2** — Sustainable Finance Reporting, Governance and Risk Management Describe the European Sustainability Reporting Standards and contrast these with other international reporting frameworks Explain how to supervise ESG risk management and product governance in firms Identify the implications of sustainability risk for supervised entities Discuss how sustainable finance can be integrated into day-to-day supervision Explore future changes to the EU’s regulatory framework for sustainable finance

## Structure and Format

Delivered as a two-workshop series. Format: class discussion and case studies.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with sustainable finance in practice is helpful but not essential.
