## Aim and Purpose

The legal duties every director owes, how regulation adds to them, and the distinct roles of the chair, executive and non-executive directors.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the core fiduciary and statutory duties of directors
- Distinguish the roles of chair, executive and non-executive directors
- Recognise how regulatory obligations extend directors' responsibilities

## Syllabus

- Duties of care, skill, diligence, loyalty and independent judgement
- Collective board responsibility and individual accountability
- Conflicts of interest and related-party matters
- Group structures: duties to the firm versus the parent
