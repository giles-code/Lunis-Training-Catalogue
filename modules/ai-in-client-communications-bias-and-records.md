## Aim and Purpose

The conduct risks of using AI in client-facing work, how bias can affect outputs, and the record-keeping and accountability that apply to AI-assisted work.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply fair, clear and not misleading standards to AI-drafted communications
- Recognise bias in AI outputs and its effect on customers
- Keep appropriate records of AI-assisted work and decisions

## Syllabus

- Client communications, marketing and the advice boundary
- Bias, stereotyping and vulnerable customers
- Record-keeping, audit trails and disclosure of AI use
- Accountability: who signs off and who is responsible
