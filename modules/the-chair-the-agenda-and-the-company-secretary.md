## Aim and Purpose

The pivotal role of the chair in setting the tone and running effective meetings, how to manage agenda overload and time, and the role of the company secretary and pre-board engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the responsibilities and attributes of an effective chair
- Design an agenda that prioritises strategy, risk and key decisions
- Use the company secretary and pre-board engagement to strengthen meetings

## Syllabus

- The chair's responsibilities, attributes and relationship with the chief executive
- Agenda overload: frequency, duration, committees and priorities
- Agenda rules: important topics first, timed items and clear decisions
- The company secretary, pre-board meetings and minutes that evidence challenge
