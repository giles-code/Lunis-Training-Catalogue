## Aim and Purpose

How to measure the outputs, outcomes and wider impact of a partnership, avoid vanity metrics, and report results credibly to government, partners and assessors.

## Learning Objectives

- Define meaningful measures of partnership performance
- Collect and attribute results across agencies and firms
- Report outcomes credibly, including to mutual evaluation assessors

## Syllabus

- Outputs, outcomes and impact: what to measure
- Attributing arrests, freezes and reporting improvements
- Qualitative evidence and case studies
- Reporting to government, partners and assessors
