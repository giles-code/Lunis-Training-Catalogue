## Aim and Purpose

How to turn analysis into well-evidenced findings and a risk mitigation programme with specific, measurable actions, owners and deadlines, and how to track remediation to closure.

## Learning Objectives

- Formulate findings that link evidence, root cause and risk
- Design a risk mitigation programme with measurable actions and deadlines
- Assess whether remediation is complete, effective and sustained

## Syllabus

- Anatomy of a good finding: issue, evidence, root cause and impact
- Designing actions, owners, deadlines and evidence of completion
- Tracking remediation and validating closure
- Red flags: repeat findings, slippage and cosmetic remediation
