## Aim and Purpose

How an authority sets its own risk appetite, varies supervisory intensity across the regulated population and makes explicit, defensible trade-offs when resources are limited.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply a supervisory risk appetite to prioritise firms and issues
- Assess intensity models and the trade-off between breadth and depth
- Formulate a risk-based work plan that can be reprioritised as risks change

## Syllabus

- The authority's risk appetite and tolerance for firm failure
- Supervisory intensity tiers and minimum engagement models
- Allocating scarce resource and explaining the trade-offs
- Exercise: reprioritising a work plan mid-year
