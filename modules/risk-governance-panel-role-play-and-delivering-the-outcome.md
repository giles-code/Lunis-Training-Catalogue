## Aim and Purpose

Groups take turns presenting their full risk assessment and sitting as the panel, then practise explaining the outcome to the firm's board, drawing together all three days.

## Learning Objectives

- Present a supervisory assessment concisely and seek the panel's advice
- Give and respond to constructive, evidence-based challenge
- Communicate ratings and required actions clearly to a firm's board

## Syllabus

- Mock panels: chair, members, secretary and presenting team roles
- Good challenge, fair concession and pushing back on the panel
- Minutes, agreed actions and comparison with a model letter
- Workshop: delivering the risk assessment letter to the board
