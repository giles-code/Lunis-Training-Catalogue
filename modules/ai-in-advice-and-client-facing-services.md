## Aim and Purpose

Designing robo-advice, chatbots and personalisation tools that meet suitability, disclosure and record-keeping obligations, treat customers fairly and stay on the right side of the guidance versus advice boundary.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how automated advice, chatbots and virtual assistants are designed and deployed
- Apply suitability, disclosure and record-keeping requirements to automated processes
- Evaluate personalisation and dynamic pricing against fair treatment obligations

## Syllabus

- Robo-advice and automated portfolio recommendations
- Chatbots and generative AI assistants in service and sales
- Guidance versus regulated advice in AI-enabled client tools
- Fair treatment risks in personalisation and dynamic pricing
