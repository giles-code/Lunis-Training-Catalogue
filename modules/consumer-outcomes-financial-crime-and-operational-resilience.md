## Aim and Purpose

The cross-cutting risks that recur across FinTech models: harm from digital journeys, exploitation by fraudsters and launderers, and fragile technology stacks built for speed rather than resilience.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess consumer outcomes, vulnerability and complaints across digital journeys
- Evaluate onboarding, fraud and transaction monitoring controls at scale
- Judge the resilience of technology stacks, cloud reliance and incident response

## Syllabus

- Frictionless journeys, dark patterns and fair value
- Remote onboarding, synthetic identities, mule accounts and authorised push payment fraud
- Cloud dependency, change management and outage history
- Supervisory data and early warning indicators across the three risks
