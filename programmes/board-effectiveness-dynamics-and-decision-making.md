## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a practical understanding of the dynamics that decide whether a board challenges management, weighs real alternatives and acts when things go wrong. Participants leave able to improve their agenda, papers and meetings, test the quality of board decisions and see their board the way a supervisor does.

## What It Covers

How boards work in practice, the role of the chair, agenda and time management, board papers and information quality, information asymmetry and independent assurance, decision quality and looking back, culture, challenge and psychological safety, group think and bias, technical topics and AI in the boardroom, and board evaluation, succession and skills.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs, non-executive and independent directors and board committee members
- Executive directors and senior managers who present to or sit on the board
- Company secretaries and heads of governance responsible for board processes and evaluation

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why sound governance structures do not guarantee effective board behaviour
- Evaluate the agenda, board papers and information flows against good practice
- Assess the quality of board decisions by their process, not just their outcome
- Challenge management effectively while respecting the line between oversight and management
- Identify group think, bias and unhelpful dynamics, and apply practical counter-measures
- Design a board effectiveness action plan covering evaluation, succession and skills

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two- to three-day programme or as a series of board briefing sessions. Each module combines a short briefing with scenarios, exercises and a board question set, and modules can be selected or tailored to the board's needs.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Board or senior management experience in financial services is helpful. No prior governance training is required.

## Core Reading

- G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance (2023)
- Basel Committee, Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015)
- Amy C. Edmondson, The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace (2018)
- Olivier Sibony, You're About to Make a Terrible Mistake! How Biases Distort Decision-Making (2020)
