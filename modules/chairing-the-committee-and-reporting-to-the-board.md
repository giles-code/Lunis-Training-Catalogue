## Aim and Purpose

How committee chairs set the agenda, run meetings that produce real challenge and give the board a clear, candid account of what the committee has found.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply good practice in committee agenda setting and chairing
- Encourage challenge from members and candour from management
- Report committee conclusions and escalations to the board effectively

## Syllabus

- Annual cycle of business and agenda prioritisation
- Chairing for challenge: drawing out members and managing dominant voices
- Relationships with function heads, auditors and the authority
- Committee reports to the board: what, how and when to escalate
