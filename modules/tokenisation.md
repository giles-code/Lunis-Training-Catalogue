## Aim and Purpose

How traditional financial assets are represented on distributed ledgers, the legal, operational and technological considerations involved, and the supervisory challenges as market structure evolves.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main tokenisation models
- Analyse tokenised asset and fund structures
- Evaluate the legal and operational risks of tokenisation

## Syllabus

- Tokenising real-world assets: equities, bonds and funds
- Smart contracts governing asset behaviour and transfer restrictions
- Claimed benefits: efficiency, transparency and liquidity
- Legal enforceability, settlement finality and custody risks
