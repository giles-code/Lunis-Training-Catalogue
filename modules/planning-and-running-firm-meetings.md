## Aim and Purpose

How to prepare for, conduct and follow up meetings with firms so that each meeting has a clear purpose and produces usable evidence.

## Learning Objectives

- Set objectives and an agenda for a firm meeting
- Use questioning and listening techniques to obtain useful information
- Record outcomes accurately and agree follow-up actions

## Syllabus

- Preparation: objectives, information request and roles
- Opening, questioning, listening and controlling the meeting
- Taking notes and producing an accurate meeting record
- Follow-up letters, actions and deadlines
