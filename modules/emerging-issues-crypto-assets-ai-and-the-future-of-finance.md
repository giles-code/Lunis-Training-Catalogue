## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining emerging issues likely to come before parliament, including crypto-assets, stablecoins, AI, climate risk and cyber threats.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain crypto-assets, stablecoins and AI in plain language
- Assess the main risks and regulatory options for emerging issues
- Prepare a scrutiny agenda for the parliamentary term

## Syllabus

- Crypto-assets and stablecoins: risks and regulatory approaches
- AI in finance: benefits, risks and accountability
- Climate risk, cyber threats and operational resilience
- Workshop: a committee scrutiny agenda on financial services
