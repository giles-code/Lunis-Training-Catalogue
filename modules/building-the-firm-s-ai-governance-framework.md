## Aim and Purpose

How the firm designs and operates an AI governance framework, from board accountability to day-to-day oversight, with the policies, inventory and lines of defence that give credible assurance.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a framework that allocates clear accountability from the board to the business line
- Develop an AI policy, risk appetite statement and firm-wide model inventory
- Structure the three lines of defence for AI risk, including independent validation

## Syllabus

- Board and senior management accountability for AI outcomes
- AI policy, risk appetite, use-case approval and the model inventory
- Principles in practice: fairness, transparency and meaningful human oversight
- Common governance weaknesses and how supervisors spot them
