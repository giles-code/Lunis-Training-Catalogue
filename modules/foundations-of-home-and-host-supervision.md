## Aim and Purpose

Why cross-border groups need cooperating supervisors, how responsibilities are allocated between home and host, and the risks of supervisory gaps and duplication.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the home-host principle in the Basel Core Principles and other sector standards
- Distinguish home and host supervisor roles and responsibilities
- Identify the risks of gaps, duplication and conflicting demands

## Syllabus

- Basel Core Principles, IAIS and IOSCO standards on cross-border cooperation
- Allocation of home and host responsibilities across sectors
- Asymmetry: large home, small host and systemic branches
- Supervisory gaps, regulatory arbitrage and duplication
