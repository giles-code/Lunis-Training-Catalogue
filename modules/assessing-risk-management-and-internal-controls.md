## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors judge whether a firm's risk management framework and control functions actually work, rather than merely exist, and how control quality affects the overall risk rating.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the design and operation of the risk management framework
- Assess the independence, resourcing and influence of the control functions
- Judge whether controls adequately mitigate the inherent risks identified

## Syllabus

- Risk appetite, risk identification and management information
- Risk, compliance and internal audit: independence, stature and coverage
- Testing controls: walk-throughs, sample testing and outcomes evidence
- Net risk: weighing controls against inherent risk
