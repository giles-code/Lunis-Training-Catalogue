## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in applying a risk-based approach to supervising payment institutions, drawing on published supervisory guidance in this area, including how to assess safeguarding of client funds.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in payment services supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the key risk categories relevant to payment institution supervision
- Describe risk-based supervisory approaches for payment institutions
- Assess the adequacy of a firm's safeguarding arrangements for client funds
- Evaluate operational and financial resilience indicators for payment institutions
- Formulate a risk-based supervisory assessment of a payment institution

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Risk categories in payment institution supervision (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain key risk categories*
- Key risk categories: safeguarding, operational, financial crime, conduct
- Why payment institutions pose distinct risks compared to banks
- Published supervisory guidance on risk-based payment institution supervision
- Proportionality considerations given the diversity of payment institution business models
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: risk profiles of payment institutions participants supervise

**Session 2: Assessing safeguarding arrangements (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess safeguarding adequacy*
- How safeguarding of client funds is meant to work in practice
- Common safeguarding failures: commingling, inadequate reconciliation
- Verifying safeguarding through reconciliation and audit evidence
- Consequences of safeguarding failure for consumers
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's safeguarding arrangements for adequacy

**Session 3: Resilience assessment and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate resilience indicators and formulate an assessment*
- Operational and financial resilience indicators for payment institutions
- Case study: a payment institution's risk profile under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a risk-based supervisory assessment of a sample payment institution

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: risk categories, safeguarding assessment, resilience indicators
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Risk categories in payment institution supervision (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing safeguarding arrangements (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Resilience assessment and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Safeguarding of client funds is the single most important consumer protection control in payment institution supervision
- Payment institutions' diverse business models require genuinely proportionate, tailored supervisory approaches
- Reconciliation evidence, not policy statements, is what actually verifies safeguarding adequacy
