## Aim and Purpose

How to turn the inventory into a risk-based view of quantum exposure, ranking systems by data sensitivity and shelf life, business criticality and difficulty of change, within the firm's risk appetite.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess quantum exposure using data shelf life, sensitivity and system criticality
- Prioritise remediation by risk and by time and effort to migrate
- Integrate quantum risk into the firm's cyber, technology and operational risk frameworks

## Syllabus

- Exposure scoring: data shelf life, migration time and threat timeline
- Linking to important business services and impact tolerances
- Risk appetite, risk register entries and key risk indicators
- Case study: prioritising a firm's systems for migration
