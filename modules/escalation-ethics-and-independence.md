## Aim and Purpose

When and how to escalate concerns within the authority, the ethical standards supervisors must uphold, and how to protect independence against capture, conflicts and external pressure.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the triggers and routes for escalating supervisory concerns
- Recognise conflicts of interest, capture and other threats to independence
- Apply ethical standards to grey-area situations in supervisory work

## Syllabus

- Escalation triggers, routes and the escalation paper
- Regulatory capture, familiarity threats and conflicts of interest
- Gifts, hospitality, confidentiality and post-employment considerations
- Ethical dilemmas: case discussions from supervisory practice
