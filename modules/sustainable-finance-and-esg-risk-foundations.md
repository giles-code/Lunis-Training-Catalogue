## Aim and Purpose

An introduction to sustainable finance, the environmental, social and governance risks that affect financial products and services, and why sustainability has become a supervisory concern.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the scope of sustainable finance and the main ESG risk categories
- Distinguish sustainability risks to firms from firms' impacts on sustainability factors
- Identify how ESG risks translate into credit, market, liquidity, operational and conduct risk

## Syllabus

- Sustainable finance, ESG integration and impact investing: key terms
- Environmental, social and governance risk categories with examples
- Single and double materiality
- Green, social and sustainability-linked bonds and loans
