## Aim and Purpose

How to supervise firms' outsourcing and third-party arrangements, from due diligence and contracts to oversight, concentration risk and exit planning, where responsibility stays with the firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why regulatory responsibility cannot be outsourced
- Assess due diligence, contractual safeguards and ongoing oversight
- Evaluate concentration risk, sub-outsourcing and exit strategies

## Syllabus

- Material and critical outsourcing: identification and notification
- Due diligence, contracts, audit and access rights
- Ongoing oversight, performance monitoring and intra-group arrangements
- Concentration, sub-outsourcing and exit planning
