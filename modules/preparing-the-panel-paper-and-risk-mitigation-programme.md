## Aim and Purpose

How supervisory teams build a panel paper that is analytical rather than descriptive, justify risk ratings against the framework and select the most effective tools for each risk mitigation action.

## Learning Objectives

- Structure a panel paper around the firm, key issues, ratings and proposed actions
- Justify ratings against the framework's descriptors and peer firms
- Design outcome-focused risk mitigation actions with proportionate deadlines and escalation triggers

## Syllabus

- Panel pack content: supervisory narrative, supporting information and materiality
- Rating rationale: evidence, descriptors and consistency across firms
- Mitigation tools: board action, audits, skilled person reviews, directions and licence conditions
- The phases of a panel: pre-meeting, discussion, conclusions and follow-up
