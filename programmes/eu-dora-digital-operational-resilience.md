## Aim and Purpose

To give supervisors and practitioners a practical command of DORA and its technical standards, from management body accountability and the ICT risk management framework to incident reporting, resilience testing and third-party risk. Participants leave able to judge whether arrangements work in practice rather than on paper, and to see the firm's digital resilience the way a supervisor does.

## What It Covers

DORA's scope, proportionality and place in the EU rulebook, management body accountability, the ICT risk management framework, incident classification and reporting, digital operational resilience testing and TLPT, ICT third-party risk and the register of information, the oversight framework for critical ICT third-party providers, information sharing, and supervision and enforcement.

## Intended Audience

- Supervisors, inspectors and ICT risk specialists at national competent authorities
- Information security, ICT risk, operational resilience, outsourcing and compliance staff at financial entities
- Senior managers and management body members accountable for digital operational resilience

## Learning Objectives

- Explain DORA's scope, proportionality and relationship with sectoral rules and the NIS2 Directive
- Assess an ICT risk management framework and its governance against DORA and its technical standards
- Apply the incident classification criteria and reporting timelines to major ICT-related incidents
- Evaluate digital operational resilience testing programmes, including threat-led penetration testing
- Assess ICT third-party risk management, contractual provisions and the register of information
- Explain the oversight of critical ICT third-party providers and the supervisory and enforcement toolkit

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a technical briefing with case studies, simulations and supervisory question sets, and modules can be selected or tailored for supervisors or financial entities.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of operational risk, ICT risk or its supervision is helpful. No technical cybersecurity background is required.

## Core Reading

- Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 on digital operational resilience for the financial sector (DORA)
- Directive (EU) 2022/2555 on measures for a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union (NIS2 Directive)
- Basel Committee, Principles for Operational Resilience (2021)
- FSB, Enhancing Third-Party Risk Management and Oversight: A Toolkit for Financial Institutions and Financial Authorities (2023)
