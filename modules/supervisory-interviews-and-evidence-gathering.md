## Aim and Purpose

How to plan and conduct interviews with directors and senior managers, test the credibility of what is said, handle evasive or defensive interviewees and record reliable evidence.

## Learning Objectives

- Plan interviews with clear objectives and an evidence-led question strategy
- Apply questioning techniques and recognise evasion and inconsistency
- Link interview evidence to supervisory conclusions and action

## Syllabus

- Interview planning, roles and the supervisory mindset
- Open, probing and closed questions; funnelling and challenge
- Credibility, consistency and handling difficult interviewees
- Recording interviews and corroborating with documentary evidence
