## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a working set of governance tools, from skills matrices and committee terms of reference to oversight calendars and escalation triggers, built on a risk-based view of the fund and its service providers. Participants apply each tool to their own board and leave with a draft oversight plan they can take back and adopt.

**Coverage:** Board composition, independence and skills, board organisation, meetings and delegation, a risk-based oversight framework across service providers, oversight calendars, management information and escalation, ethics, culture and professional standards, and board evaluation and director development.

## Intended Audience

- Chairs, independent and non-executive directors of investment funds and fund management companies
- Executive directors, conducting officers and designated persons who prepare board business
- Company secretaries and governance professionals who design and maintain board frameworks

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the programme, participants will be able to:

- Assess board composition, independence and skills against the fund's risk profile
- Design board and committee arrangements, agendas and delegations that focus time on what matters
- Apply a risk-based oversight framework across the manager and each service provider
- Build an oversight calendar, management information set and escalation triggers
- Evaluate the ethical standards, culture and conduct expected of fund directors
- Produce a board evaluation approach and a draft oversight plan for the participant's own board

## Syllabus

### Module 1: Board Composition, Independence and Skills Matrix

How to compose a fund board that matches the fund's risks, test independence in substance as well as form, and build a skills matrix that drives appointments and development.

**Objectives**

- Distinguish executive, non-executive and independent directors and test independence
- Build a skills matrix linked to the fund's strategy and risk profile
- Assess diversity, location, time commitment and fitness and probity

**Syllabus**

- Board profile: executive, non-executive and independent roles
- Independence tests, tenure, affiliations and multiple directorships
- Building a skills matrix from the fund's risk profile
- Tool: a completed skills matrix and gap analysis

### Module 2: Board Organisation: Meetings, Agendas, Committees and Delegation

How the board organises its work: meeting frequency and format, agendas that protect decision time, committees with clear mandates and delegations that do not become abdication.

**Objectives**

- Design a meeting schedule, format and agenda structure for a fund board
- Draft committee mandates and a schedule of matters reserved to the board
- Evaluate delegations of authority and how they are monitored

**Syllabus**

- Meeting frequency, attendance, observers and virtual meetings
- Standing agenda items, decision papers and minutes that evidence challenge
- Committees: formal and ad hoc, terms of reference and scope of authority
- Tool: a matters reserved schedule and committee terms of reference

### Module 3: A Risk-Based Oversight Framework for Service Providers

How to build an oversight framework from the risks to the fund and its investors, covering the manager, administrator, transfer agent, depositary, distributors and auditors in proportion to risk.

**Objectives**

- Identify the key risks each service provider presents to the fund
- Apply proportionate oversight intensity based on risk and materiality
- Define the assurance the board needs from each provider

**Syllabus**

- Investor protection as the starting point for the oversight framework
- Risk mapping across portfolio management, administration, depositary and distribution
- Oversight intensity: reporting, reviews, due diligence visits and independent assurance
- Tool: a risk-based service provider oversight matrix

### Module 4: Oversight Calendars, MI and Escalation

How to turn the oversight framework into an annual calendar, a focused management information set and pre-agreed escalation triggers that remove guesswork when something goes wrong.

**Objectives**

- Build an annual oversight calendar aligned to reporting and review cycles
- Specify the management information the board needs and in what form
- Set escalation triggers and internal limits that operate before a breach

**Syllabus**

- The annual cycle: reviews, approvals, audits and provider assessments
- Management information: dashboards, exception reporting and key risk indicators
- Escalation triggers, internal limits and out-of-cycle meetings
- Tool: an oversight calendar and escalation matrix

### Module 5: Ethics, Culture and Professional Standards for Directors

The ethical standards expected of fund directors, how culture and conflicts are overseen in a delegated model, and how directors handle the dilemmas that rules do not resolve.

**Objectives**

- Explain the professional and ethical standards expected of fund directors
- Assess culture, values and conflicts of interest across the fund's delegates
- Apply an ethical decision-making framework to board dilemmas

**Syllabus**

- Duties of loyalty, care, independence of judgement and acting for investors
- Conflicts of interest: identification, registers, disclosure and management
- Overseeing culture in a delegated model: signals and evidence
- Tool: an ethical decision-making framework applied to case dilemmas

### Module 6: Board Evaluation, Director Development and the Oversight Plan

How boards evaluate their own effectiveness and develop their directors, ending with each participant drafting a risk-based oversight plan for their own board.

**Objectives**

- Design a proportionate board and director evaluation process
- Plan induction, continuing development and succession for the board
- Produce a draft risk-based oversight plan for the participant's board

**Syllabus**

- Internal and external evaluations, and tracking actions to completion
- Induction, continuing professional development and succession planning
- Due diligence before joining a fund board
- Workshop: drafting the board's risk-based oversight plan

## Structure and Format

Six workshop modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a three-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a short briefing with hands-on exercises using governance templates, and modules can be selected or tailored to a single board or a mixed group of directors.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Current or prospective experience as a fund or management company director is helpful. No prior governance qualification is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Examination of Governance for Collective Investment Schemes (2006–2007)
- G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance (2023)
- FSB, Enhancing Third-Party Risk Management and Oversight: A Toolkit for Financial Institutions and Financial Authorities (2023)
- FSB, Strengthening Governance Frameworks to Mitigate Misconduct Risk: A Toolkit for Firms and Supervisors (2018)
