## Aim and Purpose

How to analyse information from firms, test it against other sources and reach conclusions that are supported by evidence rather than assertion.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish facts, evidence, opinion and assertion
- Test information for reliability, consistency and completeness
- Draw proportionate conclusions and record the reasoning behind them

## Syllabus

- Sources of evidence: documents, data, interviews and observation
- Corroboration, triangulation and spotting inconsistencies
- Common reasoning errors and cognitive bias
- Recording analysis so another reviewer can follow it
