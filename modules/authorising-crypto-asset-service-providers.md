## Aim and Purpose

A proportionate, risk-based framework for assessing applications from crypto-asset service providers, testing substance, governance, group dependencies and operations before authorisation is granted.

## Learning Objectives

- Operate an authorisation gateway with clear quality thresholds and early challenge
- Assess application dossiers systematically, from programme of operations to ICT security
- Evaluate substance, group dependencies and conflicts to prevent letter-box authorisations

## Syllabus

- Completeness, quality thresholds and risk-based prioritisation of applications
- Programme of operations, business plans, management and shareholder suitability
- Group structures, intragroup outsourcing and local substance
- Regulatory history, customer journeys and exposure to unregulated or DeFi services
