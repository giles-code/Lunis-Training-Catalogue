## Aim and Purpose

Why investment funds are regulated, how rules apply to managers, products and service providers, and how a fund's investors and strategy shape the regime that applies.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the objectives of fund regulation
- Distinguish regulation of fund managers, fund products and service providers
- Identify how investor type and strategy determine the applicable rules

## Syllabus

- Investor protection, market integrity and financial stability
- Fund manager and fund product rules: retail, professional and private funds
- Regulated activities: management, administration, custody and distribution
- International standards, including the IOSCO Objectives and Principles
