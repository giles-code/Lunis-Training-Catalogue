## Aim and Purpose

How directors can oversee a business when management always knows more, by understanding how information is produced and drawing on independent sources of assurance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the causes and consequences of information asymmetry in the boardroom
- Assess the reliance placed on management's own account
- Use the control functions, auditors and outside experts to verify what the board is told

## Syllabus

- Why the board always knows less, and what independent directors add
- Trusting and verifying: healthy scepticism without hostility
- Sources of assurance: risk, compliance, internal and external audit, outside advisers
- Side conversations, pre-meetings and the risk of ratification
