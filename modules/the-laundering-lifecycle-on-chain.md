## Aim and Purpose

How criminal proceeds move through placement, layering and integration using crypto-assets, and where along the chain the best opportunities for detection and disruption lie.

## Learning Objectives

- Map placement, layering and integration to crypto-asset techniques
- Explain chain-hopping, cross-chain bridges and decentralised finance as layering tools
- Identify off-ramps and detection points where intervention is most effective

## Syllabus

- Placement: cash-to-crypto, kiosks, peer-to-peer and over-the-counter brokers
- Layering: rapid hops, chain-hopping, bridges, swaps and decentralised protocols
- Integration: off-ramps, nested services, merchant payments and high-value assets
- Detection points across the lifecycle
