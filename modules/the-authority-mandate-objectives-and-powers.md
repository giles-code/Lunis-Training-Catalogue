## Aim and Purpose

How a regulatory authority is constituted, what it is required to achieve, the powers it holds and how it is held to account.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the authority's statutory objectives and how they are balanced
- Identify the main powers available to the authority and their limits
- Explain how independence and accountability work together

## Syllabus

- Legal basis, governance and statutory objectives of the authority
- Rule-making, authorisation, information-gathering and intervention powers
- Independence, transparency and accountability to government and the public
- Working with other domestic authorities and the national FIU
