## Aim and Purpose

How to respond fairly when adverse information emerges, including investigation, notification to the authority, conditions and removal, followed by a workshop working two cases to decision.

## Learning Objectives

- Respond to adverse information with a fair and timely process
- Decide on notification, conditions, suspension or removal
- Apply the programme to realistic appointment and reassessment cases

## Syllabus

- Investigating adverse information fairly and promptly
- Notification duties, regulatory references on departure and settlement agreements
- Conditions, suspension, removal and the risk of prohibition
- Workshop: a director appointment and a mid-year reassessment to decision
