## Aim and Purpose

How agents can be exploited for fraud, money laundering and sanctions evasion, and how firms manage dependence on third-party models and agent platforms.

## Learning Objectives

- Recognise financial crime typologies involving AI agents
- Adapt transaction monitoring and fraud controls to agent activity
- Assess due diligence, concentration and exit risk for third-party models

## Syllabus

- Agent-enabled fraud, account takeover and synthetic identities
- Laundering and sanctions evasion through automated transactions
- Third-party models, agent platforms and concentration risk
- Contracts, audit rights and exit planning
