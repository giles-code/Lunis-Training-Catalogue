## Aim and Purpose

How suspicious transaction reports become usable intelligence, how crypto-assets are frozen, seized and safely held, and how authorities cooperate across borders and with the private sector.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the quality of crypto-related suspicious transaction reports
- Explain the practicalities of freezing, seizing and managing crypto-assets
- Use domestic and international cooperation channels effectively

## Syllabus

- Suspicious transaction reporting: content, quality and FIU analysis
- Freezing and seizure: private keys, service provider cooperation and custody
- Asset management, valuation and return of seized crypto-assets
- International cooperation, information sharing and public-private partnerships
