## Aim and Purpose

Authorising payment and e-money institutions today, how the reform folds e-money institutions into a single payment institution category, and the reauthorisation, capital and governance implications for existing firms.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess applications against authorisation, capital and fitness requirements
- Explain how e-money issuance becomes a payment service under the reform
- Plan for reauthorisation and transitional arrangements for existing licence holders

## Syllabus

- Programme of operations, business plan, governance and own funds methods
- A single payment institution category with e-money services
- Reauthorisation, transitional periods and grandfathering questions
- Agents, distributors, passporting and the narrowing of exclusions
