## Aim and Purpose

How boards manage the gap between what they know and what management knows, how decisions get made before the meeting, and where oversight ends and micromanagement begins.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess how the board validates information and uses independent assurance
- Recognise when the board meeting has become a ratification exercise
- Judge whether the board is under-engaged, appropriately engaged or micromanaging

## Syllabus

- Independent assurance: internal and external audit, risk function and outside experts
- Pre-meetings and side conversations: legitimate preparation or foregone conclusion
- Reading minutes for recorded debate and challenge
- Oversight versus direction of day-to-day decisions and staff
