## Aim and Purpose

To equip participants with the practical skills to build robust, auditable
three-statement financial models, using techniques and formatting
conventions consistent with industry best practice.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level analysts and associates at regulated firms who need to
build or review financial models as part of their role, but have limited
prior modelling experience.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

- Build an integrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow model
- Apply industry-standard formatting and formula auditing conventions
- Perform basic scenario and sensitivity analysis

## Syllabus

1. Modelling principles and best practice
2. Building the income statement
3. Building the balance sheet
4. Linking the cash flow statement
5. Scenario and sensitivity analysis
6. Model review and error checking

## Structure and Format

Delivered online over two full days, combining short instructor-led
demonstrations with hands-on modelling exercises in Excel.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Basic Excel proficiency (formulas, cell referencing) is assumed. No prior
financial modelling experience is required.
