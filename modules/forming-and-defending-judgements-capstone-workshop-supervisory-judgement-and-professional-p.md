## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a case study in which participants form a judgement on an ambiguous scenario, document it, present it to a review panel and defend it under challenge.

## Learning Objectives

- Formulate a reasoned judgement on an ambiguous supervisory scenario
- Document the reasoning so that it is defensible under later scrutiny
- Defend a judgement before a panel and respond to challenge

## Syllabus

- Recording the rationale: options considered and reasons for the decision
- Hindsight, internal review and external scrutiny of supervisory decisions
- Presenting to a review or risk panel
- Workshop: form, document and defend a supervisory judgement
