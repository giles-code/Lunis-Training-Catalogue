## Aim and Purpose

The legal and regulatory obligations AI creates for the firm and the board: how jurisdictions are regulating AI, how this overlays existing financial services rules, and what supervisors and auditors expect to see.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main approaches to AI regulation and the duties they place on firms
- Identify which of the firm's AI uses are likely to be treated as high-risk and the assurance they require
- Prepare the board to evidence oversight to supervisors and auditors

## Syllabus

- Approaches to AI regulation across jurisdictions: risk-based laws, sectoral rules, supervisory guidance and managing divergence
- International standards and frameworks, including OECD, NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, IOSCO, FSB and BIS
- Interaction with existing conduct, prudential, consumer protection and data protection rules
- Preparing for supervisory engagement: what the board should be able to show
