## Aim and Purpose

How investment firms must protect client money and custody assets, why failures usually surface only on insolvency, and how supervisors test segregation, reconciliation and recordkeeping.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain client money and custody asset protection requirements
- Assess segregation, reconciliation and third-party custody arrangements
- Evaluate the firm's readiness to return client assets on failure

## Syllabus

- Segregation, trust status and acknowledgement letters
- Daily reconciliations, shortfalls and breaches reporting
- Title transfer collateral, securities lending and sub-custody chains
- Case study: client asset shortfalls in an investment firm failure
