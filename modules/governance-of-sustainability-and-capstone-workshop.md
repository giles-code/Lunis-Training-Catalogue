## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together around board oversight, accountability, remuneration and capability for sustainability, with a workshop in which participants assess their own firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Design governance and accountability for sustainability risks and commitments
- Assess the board's capability to oversee sustainability matters
- Produce a prioritised action plan for the firm's sustainability approach

## Syllabus

- Board oversight, committees and management accountability
- Remuneration links and sustainability capability
- Seeing the firm the way a supervisor does: a sustainability self-assessment
- Workshop: the firm's twelve-month sustainability action plan
