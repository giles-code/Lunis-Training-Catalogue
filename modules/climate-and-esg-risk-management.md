## Aim and Purpose

How physical and transition risks and wider ESG factors become credit, market, operational, liquidity and reputational risks, and how to integrate them into the firm's risk framework.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain physical and transition risk transmission channels
- Integrate climate and ESG risks into the risk taxonomy and appetite
- Assess materiality of climate risk for the firm's business model

## Syllabus

- Physical and transition risks and their transmission channels
- Climate risk in credit, market, operational and liquidity risk
- Materiality assessment and risk appetite
- Basel Committee and IAIS expectations for climate risk
