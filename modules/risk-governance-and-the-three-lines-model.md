## Aim and Purpose

How responsibility for risk is allocated between the business, the risk and compliance functions and internal audit, and why supervisors look closely at how this works in practice.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the roles of the first, second and third lines
- Assess the independence, authority and resourcing of the risk function
- Identify common failures in how the three lines operate in practice

## Syllabus

- Risk ownership in the business and the role of the second line
- Internal audit and independent assurance
- Risk committees, terms of reference and escalation routes
- Where the three lines blur: lessons from supervisory findings
