## Aim and Purpose

How countries organise financial regulation, from sectoral and integrated regulators to twin peaks models, and the role of central banks, ministries and other public bodies.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare sectoral, integrated, twin peaks and hybrid models
- Explain the role of the central bank in prudential and macroprudential policy
- Evaluate coordination arrangements between authorities

## Syllabus

- Sectoral, integrated and twin peaks models compared
- Central banks, financial stability committees and deposit insurers
- Memoranda of understanding and inter-agency coordination
- Case studies of architecture reform after a crisis
