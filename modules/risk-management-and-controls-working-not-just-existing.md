## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors judge whether risk management and the control functions actually work, how they test controls, and how control quality changes the net risk rating.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how supervisors move from inherent risk to net risk
- Assess the stature, independence and resourcing of the control functions
- Evidence that controls operate effectively, not merely on paper

## Syllabus

- Risk appetite, risk identification and management information
- Compliance, risk and internal audit: independence, stature and coverage
- Walk-throughs, sample testing and outcomes evidence
- Net risk: weighing controls against inherent risk
