## Aim and Purpose

The management body's ultimate responsibility for ICT risk under DORA, the role of the ICT risk control function, and how supervisors test whether governance is real rather than nominal.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the management body's responsibilities for ICT risk, budgets and policies
- Assess the independence and resourcing of the ICT risk control function
- Challenge board reporting, training and the digital operational resilience strategy

## Syllabus

- Management body duties: approval, oversight, budget and up-to-date ICT knowledge
- Digital operational resilience strategy and ICT risk tolerance
- Three lines of defence, the ICT risk control function and internal audit
- Red flags: delegated accountability, stale strategy and weak board reporting
