## Aim and Purpose

The logic of risk-based supervision, how authorities combine the impact of a firm's failure with the probability of it happening, and how the framework structures every later assessment.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain why risk-based supervision replaced compliance-based approaches
- Distinguish impact and probability and how each is assessed
- Map the risk categories and elements of the authority's supervisory risk framework

## Syllabus

- From compliance checking to risk-based and outcomes-based supervision
- Impact: size, systemic importance, customer reach and substitutability
- Probability: inherent risks, controls, governance and financial resilience
- International principles on risk-based supervision across sectors
