## Aim and Purpose

How internal audit gives assurance over models, data and AI systems, including model risk management, data governance and the particular challenges of machine learning and generative AI.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm's model risk management and AI governance framework
- Audit data quality, model validation and ongoing monitoring
- Use technology and analytics within the audit process itself

## Syllabus

- Model inventory, tiering, validation and change control
- Data governance, lineage and quality controls
- AI-specific risks: explainability, bias, drift and third-party models
- Data analytics and AI tools in audit work
