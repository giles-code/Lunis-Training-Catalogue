## Aim and Purpose

How mule accounts are recruited, controlled and used to layer fraud proceeds, how to distinguish witting from unwitting mules, and how to dismantle the networks behind them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how mule networks are recruited and controlled
- Identify indicators of mule accounts in financial data
- Choose proportionate responses for different types of mule

## Syllabus

- Mule recruitment, herders and controllers
- Indicators of mule activity in account and payment data
- Witting, unwitting and complicit mules
- Network analysis and targeting controllers
