## Aim and Purpose

To give investigators and analysts a practical understanding of how modern fraud and cyber-enabled crime generate and move proceeds at speed and scale. Participants leave able to recognise the main typologies, act quickly with banks and payment firms to freeze and trace funds, dismantle money mule networks, support victims and pursue recovery.

## What It Covers

The fraud and cyber-enabled crime landscape, authorised push payment and impersonation fraud, investment and online scam platforms, money mule networks, ransomware and its proceeds, rapid response with banks and payment firms, victim support and prevention, and recovery and disruption.

## Intended Audience

- Fraud, economic crime and cybercrime investigators
- FIU analysts handling fraud-related reports and urgent requests
- Victim liaison, prevention and disruption officers working with the financial sector

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main fraud and cyber-enabled crime typologies and how proceeds move
- Identify money mule activity and the networks that recruit and control mules
- Trace scam and ransomware proceeds across payment and crypto-asset channels
- Work with banks and payment firms to freeze, repatriate and recover funds quickly
- Support victims effectively and reduce repeat victimisation
- Design disruption and prevention responses alongside prosecution

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a technical briefing with case studies and response exercises, ending in an investigation workshop, and modules can be selected or tailored to the participants' caseload and partners.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of fraud, cybercrime or financial crime investigation is helpful. No technical background in cyber security or crypto-assets is required.

## Core Reading

- FATF (2023), Illicit Financial Flows from Cyber-Enabled Fraud
- FATF (2023), Countering Ransomware Financing
