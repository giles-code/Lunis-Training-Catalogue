## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together in a case study in which participants investigate an ambiguous issue, form and document a judgement, write it up for a committee and defend it under challenge.

## Learning Objectives

- Combine evidence, questioning, judgement and writing skills in a realistic scenario
- Document reasoning so that it is defensible under later scrutiny
- Defend a judgement before a mock committee and set personal development priorities

## Syllabus

- Case briefing, analysis and a fact-finding interview
- Recording the rationale: options considered and reasons for the decision
- Drafting a short committee paper and escalation note
- Workshop: presenting, defending and reflecting on the judgement
