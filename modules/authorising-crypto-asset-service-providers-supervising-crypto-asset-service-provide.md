## Aim and Purpose

Running a consistent, proportionate MiCA authorisation gateway: assessing the application dossier, substance and group dependencies, and resolving material issues before authorisation rather than deferring them to supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the programme of operations, governance, prudential safeguards and ICT arrangements
- Test substance, group dependencies and outsourcing to prevent letter-box entities
- Apply service-specific focus to trading platforms, brokers and custodians

## Syllabus

- Gateway design: completeness thresholds, risk-based prioritisation and early challenge
- Forward-looking review of business plans, growth assumptions and scalability
- Transitional regime, notification route for licensed firms and prior regulatory history
- Authorising a cross-border hub: passporting implications from day one
