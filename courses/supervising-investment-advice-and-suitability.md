## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in supervising the quality of investment advice given to clients, including suitability assessment and product governance controls.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in investment services/capital markets supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the suitability and appropriateness assessment obligations
- Describe how firms should gather and use client information
- Assess the quality of a sample suitability assessment
- Evaluate product governance controls supporting advice quality
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's advice practice

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Suitability and appropriateness obligations (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain suitability and appropriateness requirements*
- The distinction between suitability (advice) and appropriateness (execution-only)
- What information firms must gather from clients
- Common failures in client information gathering
- The link between suitability and product governance
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: suitability failures participants have observed

**Session 2: Assessing suitability quality (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess the quality of a suitability assessment*
- Criteria for assessing whether a suitability assessment is genuine
- Common red flags: template answers, mismatched risk profiles
- Assessing ongoing suitability for advisory relationships
- The role of file review in supervisory assessment
Activities:
- Group exercise: review a sample suitability assessment file for quality

**Session 3: Product governance and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate product governance and formulate an assessment*
- Product governance controls linking target market to advice practice
- Assessing whether products are being sold outside their intended target market
- Case study: an advice practice under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's advice practice

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: suitability/appropriateness, quality assessment, product governance
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Suitability and appropriateness obligations (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing suitability quality (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Product governance and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Genuine suitability assessment requires real client engagement, not template-driven box-ticking
- Product governance and advice quality are two sides of the same investor protection coin
- File review remains one of the most effective supervisory tools for assessing advice quality directly
