## Aim and Purpose

To give the people who run a firm's appointments process a complete, operational method for assessing fitness and propriety, taught by former regulators who show how the authority tests the firm's work. Participants leave able to run defensible due diligence, submit applications that succeed first time, prepare candidates for interview and manage fitness on an ongoing basis.

## What It Covers

The fit and proper standard and the firm's obligations, role design and succession, integrity and reputation checks, competence, capability and capacity, controllers and change in control, applications to the authority, preparing candidates for regulatory interviews, ongoing fitness and annual certification, and handling adverse information.

## Intended Audience

- Heads of compliance, company secretaries and governance teams who manage approved and certified roles
- HR, talent and nominations committee support staff responsible for senior appointments and references
- Senior managers and chairs who sponsor appointments and sign off fitness assessments

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the components of the fit and proper standard and the firm's primary responsibility for meeting it
- Design role profiles and succession plans that define what fitness means for each key function
- Apply proportionate due diligence on integrity, reputation, competence and capacity, and document the judgement
- Prepare complete and consistent applications to the authority, including for owners and controllers
- Prepare candidates for regulatory interviews so they demonstrate understanding rather than recitation
- Manage ongoing fitness, annual certification and adverse information fairly and in line with notification duties

## Structure and Format

Nine modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines practical tools with case studies, exercises and mock interviews, and modules can be selected or tailored to the firm's roles and accountability regime.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of compliance, company secretarial, HR or governance work in a regulated firm is helpful. No prior experience of regulatory applications is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation (2017)
- G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance (2023)
- FSB, Strengthening Governance Frameworks to Mitigate Misconduct Risk: A Toolkit for Firms and Supervisors (2018)
