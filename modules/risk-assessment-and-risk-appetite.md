## Aim and Purpose

How the firm assesses its financial crime exposure across customers, products, channels and geographies, and how the board sets a risk appetite that management can apply.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the quality of the business-wide financial crime risk assessment
- Set a risk appetite with in-scope and prohibited business and measurable limits
- Challenge strategic decisions that change the firm's financial crime exposure

## Syllabus

- Business-wide risk assessment: inherent risk, controls and residual risk
- Drafting a financial crime risk appetite statement
- High-risk customers, jurisdictions, products and new business lines
- Growth, de-risking and the board's strategic choices
