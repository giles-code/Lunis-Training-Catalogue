## Aim and Purpose

The basic idea of pooled investment, how a fund turns investors' money into a managed portfolio, and why investors choose funds over direct investment.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe an investment fund and the pooling of investor money
- Explain the benefits of diversification, professional management and scale
- Identify the main types of investor and how they access funds

## Syllabus

- Pooled investment, units, shares and net asset value
- Why investors subscribe: diversification, expertise, liquidity and cost
- Retail, professional and institutional investors
- Access routes: direct, platforms, advisers and wrappers
