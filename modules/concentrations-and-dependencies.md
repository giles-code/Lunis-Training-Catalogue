## Aim and Purpose

How concentrations in clients, products, distributors and counterparties, and dependence on outsourced providers and technology, can break a business model.

## Learning Objectives

- Map the firm's material concentrations
- Identify critical internal and external dependencies
- Test what happens when a dependency fails

## Syllabus

- Client, product, distribution and counterparty concentrations
- Hidden shared dependencies across concentrations
- Outsourcing, technology and cyber as strategic dependencies
- Linking concentrations to risk appetite
