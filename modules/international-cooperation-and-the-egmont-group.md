## Aim and Purpose

How FIUs exchange information with foreign counterparts through Egmont Group channels and bilateral arrangements, the principles governing exchange, and how to make and answer requests well.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the Egmont Group principles for information exchange
- Prepare clear, well-founded requests and timely responses
- Manage consent, onward dissemination and cooperation with non-counterparts

## Syllabus

- The Egmont Group, its secure channel and membership expectations
- Principles of exchange: reciprocity, prior consent and confidentiality
- Drafting requests and spontaneous disclosures
- Diagonal cooperation and cross-border case coordination
