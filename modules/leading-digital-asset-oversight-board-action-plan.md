## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board action plan covering the board's own capability, emerging developments such as tokenised markets and decentralised finance, and keeping governance current.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the board's digital asset literacy and plan to close gaps
- Anticipate governance issues from tokenisation, decentralised finance and new market infrastructure
- Produce a board action plan for digital asset oversight

## Syllabus

- Board skills, external advice and how much expertise is enough
- Tokenised deposits, securities and funds: what comes next
- Decentralised finance and accountability questions
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month digital asset oversight plan
