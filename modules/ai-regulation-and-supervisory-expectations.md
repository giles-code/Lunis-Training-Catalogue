## Aim and Purpose

How jurisdictions are regulating AI, how AI-specific rules overlay existing financial services obligations, and how the firm evidences compliance when operating across several regimes.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main approaches to AI regulation and the duties they place on providers and deployers
- Identify which of the firm's AI uses are likely to be treated as high-risk
- Evidence compliance to financial supervisors and other authorities

## Syllabus

- Approaches to AI regulation: risk-based laws, sectoral rules and supervisory guidance
- International frameworks: OECD, NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, IOSCO, FSB and BIS
- Overlay with conduct, prudential, consumer and data protection rules
- Managing divergence across jurisdictions and preparing for supervisory reviews
