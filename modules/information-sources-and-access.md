## Aim and Purpose

The information an FIU can draw on, from its own holdings to law-enforcement, administrative and open sources, and the legal and practical limits on obtaining and using it.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the domestic information sources available to analysts
- Request further information from reporting entities effectively
- Use open-source and commercial data lawfully and with appropriate verification

## Syllabus

- FIU holdings and law-enforcement, tax, customs and registry data
- Requests for further information from reporting entities
- Open-source intelligence: value, verification and safeguards
- Data protection, confidentiality and handling restrictions
