## Aim and Purpose

The board's responsibility for treating customers fairly, how poor outcomes arise and how the board gains assurance that products and services deliver good outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain conduct and consumer protection obligations and the shift to outcomes
- Evaluate product governance, pricing, communications and support
- Use conduct MI and complaints data to identify harm

## Syllabus

- Fair treatment, suitability and consumer protection obligations
- Product governance and value
- Vulnerable customers and communications
- Complaints, redress and conduct MI
