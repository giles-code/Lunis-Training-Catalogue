## Aim and Purpose

What the authority expects to see from fund boards, how to prepare for supervisory engagement, and how to run effectiveness reviews that lead to change.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain supervisory expectations of fund and management company boards
- Prepare the board to evidence its oversight to the authority
- Evaluate board effectiveness reviews and act on their findings

## Syllabus

- Supervisory thematic reviews of fund governance: recurring findings
- Fitness and probity, time commitment and directors' interviews
- Board minutes as evidence of challenge and oversight
- Effectiveness reviews, skills matrices and succession
