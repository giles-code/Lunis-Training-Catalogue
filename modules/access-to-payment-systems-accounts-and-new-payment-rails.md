## Aim and Purpose

Non-bank access to payment systems and bank accounts, instant payments, and the interplay between payments rules and MiCA e-money tokens, including dual-licensing questions for crypto-asset service providers.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain non-bank access to payment systems and objective, non-discriminatory account access
- Assess the implications of instant payments for liquidity, fraud and sanctions screening
- Analyse the interplay between e-money tokens, crypto-asset services and payment services

## Syllabus

- Payment and e-money institution access to settlement finality systems
- Bank account access, refusals, reasons and de-risking
- Instant payments: availability, pricing, sanctions screening and payee verification
- E-money tokens under MiCA and the payment services boundary
