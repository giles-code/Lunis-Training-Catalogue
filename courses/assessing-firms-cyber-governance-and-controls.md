## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in evaluating a firm's IT governance, board-level oversight of cyber risk, and the adequacy of its security policies and data integrity controls.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in cyber and operational resilience supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the components of good IT governance for cyber risk
- Describe board-level oversight expectations for cyber risk
- Assess the adequacy of a firm's security policies
- Evaluate data integrity controls within a firm's IT environment
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's cyber governance

## Syllabus

**Session 1: IT governance fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain IT governance components*
- Components of good IT governance: ownership, policy, risk reporting
- The three lines of defence applied to cyber risk
- Board-level oversight expectations for cyber risk
- Common governance weaknesses in firms of varying size
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: cyber governance structures participants have reviewed

**Session 2: Security policies and data integrity (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess security policies and evaluate data integrity controls*
- Assessing the adequacy of security policies against firm-specific risk
- Data integrity controls: access management, encryption, change control
- Common gaps between stated policy and operational practice
- Techniques for testing whether policy is actually followed
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample firm's security policy and data integrity controls

**Session 3: Application to a case study (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Formulate a supervisory assessment of cyber governance*
- Bringing governance, policy, and control assessment together
- Case study: a firm's cyber governance under supervisory review
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory assessment of a firm's cyber governance

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: governance components, board oversight, policy and control assessment
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: IT governance fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Security policies and data integrity (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Application to a case study (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Board-level engagement with cyber risk is a strong indicator of a firm's overall cyber governance maturity
- Policy documents mean little without evidence that they are followed in operational practice
- Data integrity controls deserve as much supervisory attention as perimeter security controls
