## Aim and Purpose

How intelligence is packaged and disseminated spontaneously and on request, how its use is tracked, and how FIUs balance timeliness, source protection and the needs of recipients.

## Learning Objectives

- Prepare intelligence products that recipients can act on
- Apply dissemination rules, handling conditions and source protection
- Track the use and outcomes of disseminated intelligence

## Syllabus

- Spontaneous and on-request dissemination
- Writing for investigators, prosecutors, tax and supervisory recipients
- Handling codes, onward use and protecting reporters
- Tracking use, outcomes and case feedback
