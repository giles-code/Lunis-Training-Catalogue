## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into the authority's strategic response, covering international work on AI, perimeter and liability policy, supervisory tools and the authority's own use of agents.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare international approaches to AI and agentic finance, including FSB work
- Evaluate perimeter, liability and trust framework policy options
- Design a supervisory roadmap for agentic finance

## Syllabus

- International work: FSB, IOSCO, BIS, CPMI, FATF and OECD
- Perimeter questions: when an agent provider becomes a regulated activity
- Sandboxes, industry engagement and supervisory use of agents
- Workshop: the authority's agentic finance roadmap
