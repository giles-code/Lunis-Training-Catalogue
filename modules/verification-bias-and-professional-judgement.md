## Aim and Purpose

How to check AI outputs to the standard supervisory work requires, recognise bias and error, and keep accountability for conclusions and decisions with the supervisor, not the tool.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply structured techniques to verify AI outputs against sources
- Recognise bias, omission and automation bias in AI-assisted work
- Maintain accountability and an evidential trail for supervisory decisions

## Syllabus

- Source checking, citation testing and cross-checking with a second method
- Automation bias and the temptation to over-rely on fluent output
- When AI must not be used: decisions, sanctions and sensitive judgements
- Exercise: finding the errors in an AI-generated analysis
