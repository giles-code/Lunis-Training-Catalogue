## Aim and Purpose

Designing and operating a risk-based supervisory model for CASPs: prioritising resources, monitoring between engagements, running inspections and using MiCA's supervisory powers to drive and verify remediation.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a risk framework that prioritises CASPs by impact and probability of harm
- Use regulatory data and market intelligence to set escalation triggers
- Plan, conduct and follow up on-site inspections of CASPs

## Syllabus

- Firm risk assessment, supervisory planning and resource allocation
- Early warning signals: rapid growth, control function turnover, incidents
- Inspection methodology: document requests, interviews, walkthroughs and customer journeys
- Using MiCA's supervisory powers and keeping a defensible decision trail
