## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in assessing proposed changes of control and complex ownership structures, including cross-border and beneficial ownership considerations.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level authorisations/licensing caseworkers assessing new-entrant applications.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what triggers a change-of-control assessment
- Describe the assessment criteria applied to a proposed new controller
- Assess beneficial ownership in complex or layered structures
- Evaluate cross-border ownership structures for transparency risk
- Formulate a change-of-control assessment outcome

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Change-of-control triggers and criteria (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain triggers and assessment criteria*
- What constitutes a notifiable change of control
- Assessment criteria for a proposed new controller
- Timelines and procedural requirements
- Interaction with fit-and-proper assessment
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: change-of-control cases participants have encountered

**Session 2: Beneficial ownership and complex structures (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess beneficial ownership in complex structures*
- Tracing beneficial ownership through layered structures
- Trusts, nominees, and other opacity-creating structures
- Red flags in ownership structures
- Tools and registries for verifying ownership
Activities:
- Exercise: trace beneficial ownership through a sample layered structure

**Session 3: Cross-border ownership and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate cross-border structures and formulate an assessment*
- Cross-border transparency risk
- Cooperating with foreign authorities on ownership verification
- Case study: a proposed change of control with a complex cross-border structure
Activities:
- Case study: assess a proposed change of control and formulate an outcome

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: triggers, beneficial ownership, cross-border risk
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Change-of-control triggers and criteria (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Beneficial ownership and complex structures (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Cross-border ownership and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Change-of-control assessment is a critical checkpoint for keeping unsuitable owners out of the regulated sector
- Complex or layered ownership structures should themselves be treated as a risk indicator
- Cross-border verification of ownership often requires cooperation with foreign authorities
