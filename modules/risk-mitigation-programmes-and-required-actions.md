## Aim and Purpose

How supervisors structure issues and actions, the range of tools from firm-led actions to formal measures, and how the escalation ladder works.

## Learning Objectives

- Read a supervisory issue and its required actions
- Explain the range of supervisory tools and escalation
- Recognise when a matter risks referral to enforcement

## Syllabus

- Anatomy of a required action: issue, action, owner, date
- Tools that work through the firm and formal supervisory measures
- The escalation ladder and what triggers it
- When findings become enforcement referrals
