## Aim and Purpose

How the EU sustainable finance framework applies to funds and managers, from SFDR classification and principal adverse impacts to fund naming rules, and whether disclosures prevent greenwashing.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain SFDR entity and product disclosures and the Article 6, 8 and 9 categories
- Assess sustainability claims in fund documents, names and marketing
- Evaluate the SFDR review and the case for product categories

## Syllabus

- SFDR, the Taxonomy Regulation and sustainability preferences under MiFID II
- Principal adverse impacts and pre-contractual and periodic templates
- ESMA guidelines on fund names using ESG or sustainability terms
- Break-out: spotting greenwashing and greenbleaching
