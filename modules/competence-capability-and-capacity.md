## Aim and Purpose

How to assess whether directors and key function holders have the knowledge, experience and time to perform their roles, individually and as a board.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess competence against the specific role and the firm's risk profile
- Evaluate time commitment, multiple directorships and conflicts of interest
- Consider the collective suitability of the board and senior team

## Syllabus

- Role profiles, experience and qualifications
- Capacity, other commitments and conflicts of interest
- Collective suitability, board composition and independence
- Key function holders: risk, compliance, MLRO and finance
