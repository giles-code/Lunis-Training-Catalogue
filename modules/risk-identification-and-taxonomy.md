## Aim and Purpose

Building a complete, consistent picture of the firm's risks through a clear taxonomy, structured identification and a risk register that stays current.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a risk taxonomy suited to the firm's business model
- Apply structured techniques to identify current and emerging risks
- Maintain a risk register that links risks, controls and owners

## Syllabus

- Principal risk categories and taxonomy design
- Top-down and bottom-up risk identification
- Risk registers, owners and control mapping
- Business model analysis: seeing risk as a supervisor does
