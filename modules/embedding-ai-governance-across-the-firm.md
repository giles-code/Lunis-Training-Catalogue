## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a firm-wide approach covering capability, culture, generative AI and autonomous agents, keeping the framework current and evidencing it to supervisors.

## Learning Objectives

- Embed AI oversight consistently across business, risk, compliance and technology functions
- Evaluate governance questions raised by generative AI and autonomous agents
- Produce a prioritised action plan for the firm's AI governance

## Syllabus

- Building AI capability: skills, tools and organisational placement
- Generative AI and autonomous agents: accountability and control
- Seeing the firm's AI the way a supervisor does: evidence and readiness
- Workshop: the firm's twelve-month AI governance action plan
