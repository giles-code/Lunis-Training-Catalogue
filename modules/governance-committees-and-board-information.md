## Aim and Purpose

How board and committee arrangements work in a regulated firm, how to read board packs critically and how to challenge management effectively.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the firm's governance structure, committee mandates and reporting lines
- Interpret board information and identify what is missing
- Apply effective challenge techniques in the boardroom

## Syllabus

- Board, risk, audit, remuneration and nomination committees
- The three lines of defence and access to control function heads
- Reading board packs: quality, timeliness and comfort without evidence
- Constructive challenge, minutes and evidencing oversight
