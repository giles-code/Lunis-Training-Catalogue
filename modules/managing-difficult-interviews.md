## Aim and Purpose

Techniques for handling defensive, hostile, evasive or over-talkative interviewees, the involvement of legal advisers, and keeping control of the interview without compromising fairness.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply techniques to manage defensive, hostile and evasive interviewees
- Maintain control and fairness when advisers or colleagues intervene
- Decide when to pause, re-plan or escalate an interview

## Syllabus

- Types of difficult interviewee and why they behave as they do
- De-escalation, re-framing and returning to the question
- Legal advisers, compliance chaperones and interruptions
- When to stop, re-plan or escalate
