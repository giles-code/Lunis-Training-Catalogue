## Aim and Purpose

How cases are resolved by settlement or contested decision, how penalties are determined and how fairness and consistency are maintained.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the separation of investigation and decision-making
- Apply penalty frameworks and settlement principles consistently
- Manage negotiations and communicate final outcomes

## Syllabus

- Independent decision-makers and procedural fairness
- Penalty frameworks: seriousness, aggravation, mitigation and discounts
- Settlement negotiations, agreed facts and admissions
- Final notices, publicity and appeals
