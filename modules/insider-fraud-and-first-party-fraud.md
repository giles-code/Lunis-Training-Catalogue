## Aim and Purpose

Fraud committed by employees, contractors and the firm's own customers, how it is concealed, and the controls, culture and investigation practices that reduce it.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify insider fraud risk across roles, systems and third parties
- Recognise first-party fraud, including false claims and mule activity
- Apply proportionate investigation and response to suspected cases

## Syllabus

- Insider threat: access, opportunity, pressure and rationalisation
- Preventive controls: segregation of duties, access reviews and vetting
- First-party fraud: false claims, refund abuse and complicit mules
- Whistleblowing, investigations and evidence handling
