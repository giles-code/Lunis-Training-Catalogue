## Aim and Purpose

How governance findings are communicated, how to respond to them, and how to turn the review into a lasting improvement in governance.

## Learning Objectives

- Respond to governance findings constructively
- Agree realistic remediation and evidence its completion
- Produce a governance improvement plan

## Syllabus

- Feedback letters and requests for further information
- Responding to findings: accuracy, ownership and context
- Remediation actions and evidencing closure
- Workshop: a governance improvement plan for your firm
