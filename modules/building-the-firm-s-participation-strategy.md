## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a structured decision on whether, where and how the firm should participate, with the governance, controls and supervisory engagement needed to proceed.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate strategic options from observer to issuer or infrastructure provider
- Design governance, risk appetite and staged implementation for participation
- Prepare to evidence readiness to supervisors before launch

## Syllabus

- Strategic options: partner, distribute, hold, issue or operate
- Business case, risk appetite and board approval
- Pilots, sandboxes and engaging the authority early
- Workshop: the firm's digital money and tokenisation roadmap
