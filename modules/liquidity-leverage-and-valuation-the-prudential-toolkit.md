## Aim and Purpose

How international standards address liquidity mismatch, leverage and valuation in funds, the liquidity management tools open-ended funds are expected to have, and the red flags that precede suspensions and losses.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the FSB and IOSCO recommendations on liquidity mismatch and liquidity management tools
- Assess leverage measures, limits and reporting for different fund types
- Identify valuation governance weaknesses, especially for hard-to-value assets

## Syllabus

- Liquidity mismatch, stress testing and fund liquidity classification
- Anti-dilution tools, gates, notice periods and suspensions
- Leverage: the IOSCO framework, gross and commitment measures
- Valuation policies, independence and red flags
