## Aim and Purpose

How governments attract digital asset business without compromising standards, the design of innovation hubs and sandboxes, and the reputational risks of competing on light-touch regulation.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish innovation hubs, regulatory sandboxes and accelerator programmes
- Evaluate the economic case and the reputational risks of attracting digital asset firms
- Define the respective roles of ministries, promotion agencies and regulators

## Syllabus

- Innovation hub and sandbox design: eligibility, safeguards and exit
- Attracting firms: licensing speed, standards and the race to the bottom
- Case studies of financial centre strategies and their consequences
- Protecting regulatory independence in investment promotion
