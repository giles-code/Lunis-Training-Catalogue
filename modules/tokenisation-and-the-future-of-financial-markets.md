## Aim and Purpose

How tokenisation of securities, funds and real-world assets could change market infrastructure, and what legal and regulatory changes government may need to make to enable it safely.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain tokenisation and its potential benefits for issuance, settlement and access
- Identify the legal certainty issues for ownership, settlement finality and insolvency
- Assess the policy case for pilot regimes and legislative change

## Syllabus

- Tokenised bonds, funds and real-world assets
- Distributed ledger market infrastructure and settlement assets
- Legal foundations: property law, finality and custody
- Pilot regimes, sandboxes and public sector issuance
