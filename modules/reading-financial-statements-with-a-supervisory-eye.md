## Aim and Purpose

How to read the balance sheet, income statement and cash flow statement, and how to spot what they reveal and what they conceal.

## Learning Objectives

- Interpret the structure and main lines of the financial statements
- Calculate and use basic ratios to assess performance and position
- Identify accounting judgements and disclosures that warrant challenge

## Syllabus

- Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow and notes to the accounts
- Key ratios: profitability, leverage, cost efficiency and asset quality
- Audit opinions, going concern and related party disclosures
- Red flags: aggressive accounting, unexplained movements and late filing
