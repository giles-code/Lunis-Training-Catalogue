## Aim and Purpose

The EU oversight framework for critical ICT third-party providers: designation, the Lead Overseer, joint examination teams, oversight tools and recommendations, and what it means for financial entities and national authorities.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the criteria and process for designating critical ICT third-party providers
- Describe the Lead Overseer's powers, oversight plans and recommendations
- Assess the implications for financial entities' own third-party risk management

## Syllabus

- Criticality assessment, designation and the role of the ESAs
- Lead Overseer, Joint Oversight Network, Oversight Forum and joint examination teams
- Inspections, recommendations, periodic penalty payments and follow-up by authorities
- Third-country providers, the subsidiary requirement and residual entity responsibility
