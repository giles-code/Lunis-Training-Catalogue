## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining how a national financial services strategy is developed, how emerging issues are anticipated and how policy is translated into action.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the national financial sector's strengths, vulnerabilities and priorities
- Anticipate emerging issues including digital assets, AI, climate and cyber risk
- Produce a policy action plan with clear roles for government and regulators

## Syllabus

- Diagnosing the financial sector: evidence, data and assessments
- Emerging issues: digital assets, AI, sustainable finance and operational resilience
- Sequencing reforms and building institutional capacity
- Workshop: a national financial services policy action plan
