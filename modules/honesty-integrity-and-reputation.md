## Aim and Purpose

How to assess the integrity of individuals and owners, the checks the authority should carry out and how to weigh past conduct and disclosures.

## Learning Objectives

- Design proportionate background and due diligence checks
- Assess the relevance and seriousness of past conduct
- Respond to non-disclosure, misleading information and adverse findings

## Syllabus

- Criminal, regulatory, civil and insolvency checks
- Open-source research and information from other authorities
- Weighing seriousness, recency, pattern and rehabilitation
- Non-disclosure as a fitness issue in its own right
