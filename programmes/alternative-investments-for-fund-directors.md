## Aim and Purpose

To give directors a clear, practical understanding of how hedge fund, private markets, real asset, commodity, structured and digital asset strategies work, how they make money and how they can go wrong. Participants leave able to oversee alternative funds with confidence and challenge managers on due diligence, valuation, liquidity and fees.

## What It Covers

The alternatives landscape, hedge fund strategies, private equity and venture capital, private credit, real estate and infrastructure, commodities and structured products, digital assets and tokenised funds, and the board's questions on due diligence, valuation, liquidity and fees.

## Intended Audience

- Independent and non-executive directors of alternative investment funds and their management companies
- Directors of traditional funds whose portfolios increasingly include alternative assets or strategies
- Fund professionals preparing for board roles, and governance staff supporting alternative fund boards

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main alternative asset classes and strategies, their return sources and their role in portfolios
- Identify the distinctive investment, liquidity, leverage and operational risks of each strategy
- Assess how alternative assets are valued and where valuation judgement is greatest
- Evaluate fee structures, terms and alignment of interest between manager and investors
- Challenge managers on due diligence, strategy drift and the fit between assets and dealing terms
- Apply a board question set to the oversight of an alternative fund

## Structure and Format

Eight modules of about two hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a two-day programme or as a series of board briefing sessions. Each module combines a strategy briefing with worked examples and board questions, ending in a case study workshop, and modules can be selected or tailored to the fund range the board oversees.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Familiarity with investment funds and traditional asset classes is helpful. No prior experience of alternative investments is required.

## Core Reading

- IOSCO, Thematic Analysis: Emerging Risks in Private Finance (2023)
- IOSCO, Principles for the Valuation of Collective Investment Schemes (2013)
- FSB, Global Monitoring Report on Non-Bank Financial Intermediation (annual)
- FSB, The Financial Stability Implications of Tokenisation (2024)
