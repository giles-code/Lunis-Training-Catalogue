## Aim and Purpose

The standards directors must meet on appointment and continuously thereafter, how the authority assesses them and what can lead to withdrawal of approval.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the fitness and propriety criteria: competence, integrity and financial soundness
- Describe the approval process and the authority's interview and assessment practice
- Recognise the ongoing obligations to notify and maintain fitness

## Syllabus

- Competence, capability, honesty, integrity and reputation
- Time commitment, independence and conflicts
- Applications, interviews and how the authority reaches its decision
- Ongoing fitness, notifications and withdrawal of approval
