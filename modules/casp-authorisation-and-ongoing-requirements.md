## Aim and Purpose

Running a proportionate authorisation gateway for CASPs and applying MiCA's ongoing requirements on governance, prudential safeguards, custody, conduct and ICT resilience under DORA.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess a CASP application dossier, business model, substance and group dependencies
- Evaluate governance, prudential, custody and conduct arrangements against MiCA
- Recognise ICT and key management risks and how DORA applies to CASPs

## Syllabus

- Crypto-asset services, business models and the authorisation dossier
- Management body, conflicts, own funds and complaints handling
- Custody, segregation and safeguarding of client crypto-assets
- Red flags: letter-box entities, founder dominance and affiliated sub-custody
