## Aim and Purpose

Who is responsible when an agent gets it wrong, how conduct and consumer protection rules apply to agent actions, and how firms secure good customer outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Analyse liability allocation between the firm, customer and technology provider
- Apply suitability, fair treatment and disclosure obligations to agent actions
- Identify risks of manipulation, herding and harm to vulnerable customers

## Syllabus

- Liability for errors, unauthorised acts and agent failures
- Suitability, best interests and the advice boundary
- Dark patterns, agent-to-agent manipulation and collusion
- Vulnerable customers and outcome monitoring
