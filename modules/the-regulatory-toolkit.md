## Aim and Purpose

The range of tools the authority uses to achieve its objectives, from rules and guidance through supervision to intervention, and how to choose between them.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish rules, principles, guidance and supervisory expectations
- Match supervisory tools to the risk and the desired outcome
- Explain when and how the authority escalates its response

## Syllabus

- Rules-based and principles-based regulation
- Guidance, consultation and communicating expectations to the industry
- Supervisory tools: reviews, visits, thematic work and data returns
- Escalation ladder: from feedback letters to formal intervention
