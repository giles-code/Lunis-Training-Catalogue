## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the Basel Core Principle on home-host relationships and to develop the practical skills needed to cooperate effectively with other supervisory authorities in the consolidated supervision of cross-border banking groups.

## Intended Audience

Mid-to-senior supervisors involved in cross-border/group supervision and international cooperation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the requirements of the Basel Core Principle on home-host relationships
- Distinguish the roles and responsibilities of home and host supervisors
- Describe the information-sharing obligations between home and host supervisors
- Assess the adequacy of cooperation arrangements for a cross-border group
- Identify common obstacles to effective home-host cooperation
- Apply home-host principles to a cross-border supervisory scenario

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Foundations of home-host supervision (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain the Basel Core Principle and the home-host framework*
- The Basel Core Principle on home-host relationships
- Why cross-border consolidated supervision requires cooperation
- Distinguishing home and host supervisor roles
- The risks of supervisory gaps and duplication
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: challenges of supervising a group with cross-border operations

**Session 2: Information-sharing and cooperation in practice (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Describe information-sharing obligations and assess cooperation arrangements*
- Information-sharing gateways and confidentiality considerations
- Memoranda of understanding and their role
- Assessing whether cooperation arrangements are fit for purpose
- IOSCO's cross-sectoral comparison of home/host practice
Activities:
- Group exercise: review a sample cooperation arrangement and identify gaps

**Session 3: Applying home-host principles (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Identify obstacles and apply principles to a live scenario*
- Common obstacles: legal barriers, differing mandates, resource asymmetry
- Escalation when cooperation breaks down
- Case study: a cross-border group facing a supervisory issue
Activities:
- Case study: role-play a home-host coordination scenario and agree an action plan

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: Basel principle, roles, information-sharing, application
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Foundations of home-host supervision (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Information-sharing and cooperation in practice (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Applying home-host principles (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Home-host cooperation is a named Basel Core Principle, not an optional courtesy
- Clear role division between home and host supervisors avoids both gaps and duplication
- Effective cooperation depends on pre-agreed information-sharing arrangements, not ad hoc requests
