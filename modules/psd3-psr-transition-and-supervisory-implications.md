## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of the key changes introduced by PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation compared to PSD2, and the practical implications for supervisors managing the transition.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisors working in payment services supervision.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the key changes introduced by PSD3 and the PSR compared to PSD2
- Describe the rationale behind moving core requirements into a directly applicable Regulation
- Assess the transition timeline and its practical supervisory implications
- Evaluate how existing authorised institutions will be affected by the transition
- Formulate a supervisory transition plan for a sample authority

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Key changes under PSD3/PSR (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain key changes from PSD2 to PSD3/PSR*
- Overview of PSD3 and the accompanying Payment Services Regulation
- Key substantive changes: fraud liability, authentication, open banking enhancements
- Why some requirements move from Directive to Regulation form
- Anticipated impact on payment institutions and e-money issuers
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: PSD3/PSR awareness and preparation among participants

**Session 2: Transition timeline and implications (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess the transition timeline and its implications*
- Anticipated transition timeline and key milestones
- Practical supervisory implications of the Directive-to-Regulation shift
- Managing supervisory resource during the transition period
- Coordinating with other national and EU authorities during transition
Activities:
- Group exercise: map the transition timeline and identify key supervisory action points

**Session 3: Impact on existing institutions and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate impact and formulate a transition plan*
- How existing authorised institutions will need to adapt
- Communicating transition expectations to the regulated population
- Case study: planning a supervisory transition programme
Activities:
- Case study: formulate a supervisory transition plan for a sample authority

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: PSD3/PSR changes, transition timeline, impact on institutions
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Key changes under PSD3/PSR (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Transition timeline and implications (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Impact on existing institutions and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- The shift of core requirements into a directly applicable Regulation is intended to improve consistency across the EU
- Existing authorised institutions will need active supervisory support to navigate the PSD3/PSR transition smoothly
- Early transition planning reduces the risk of supervisory bottlenecks as the deadline approaches

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some familiarity with payment services is helpful but not essential.
