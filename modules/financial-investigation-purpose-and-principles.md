## Aim and Purpose

Why every profit-generating crime should have a financial dimension, the objectives a financial investigation can serve, and what the FATF standards expect of investigative authorities.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the evidential, intelligence and asset recovery purposes of financial investigation
- Distinguish parallel financial investigations from stand-alone money laundering cases
- Summarise the FATF expectations for investigative powers and outcomes

## Syllabus

- Following the money: proving offences, finding assets and mapping networks
- Parallel financial investigation alongside predicate offence cases
- FATF Recommendations 30 and 31 and Immediate Outcome 7
- The financial investigator's role in a multi-disciplinary team
