## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' skills in supervising firms' sanctions compliance frameworks, including screening obligations and assessment of control effectiveness.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory and financial crime specialist staff assessing firms' financial crime controls.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the sanctions regimes relevant to supervised firms
- Describe screening obligations under applicable sanctions regimes
- Assess the effectiveness of a firm's sanctions control framework
- Evaluate a firm's response to a potential sanctions breach
- Formulate a supervisory assessment of sanctions compliance

## Syllabus

**Session 1: Sanctions regimes and screening obligations (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain sanctions regimes and screening obligations*
- Overview of relevant sanctions regimes (UN, EU, domestic, OFAC)
- Screening obligations: customers, transactions, counterparties
- Extraterritorial reach of certain sanctions regimes
- Interaction between sanctions and AML/CFT frameworks
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: sanctions compliance challenges from participants' jurisdictions

**Session 2: Assessing control effectiveness (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess sanctions control framework effectiveness*
- Elements of an effective sanctions control framework
- Screening calibration and list maintenance
- Common control weaknesses in sanctions compliance
- Assessing governance and escalation for sanctions hits
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess a sample sanctions control framework

**Session 3: Breach response and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate breach response and formulate an assessment*
- Assessing a firm's response to a potential sanctions breach
- Reporting obligations following a breach
- Case study: a potential sanctions breach scenario
Activities:
- Case study: evaluate a firm's breach response and formulate a supervisory assessment

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: regimes, screening, control effectiveness, breach response
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: Sanctions regimes and screening obligations (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Assessing control effectiveness (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Breach response and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- Sanctions compliance requires attention to multiple overlapping regimes, some with extraterritorial reach
- Screening calibration and list maintenance are common sources of control failure
- How a firm responds to a potential breach is often as revealing as the breach itself
