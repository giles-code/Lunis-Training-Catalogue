## Aim and Purpose

Prepares supervisory staff to participate effectively in risk governance panels (RGPs) and thematic review panels (TRPs) — internal governance mechanisms used to review and challenge supervisory assessments and thematic findings.

## Intended Audience

Junior to mid-level supervisory staff conducting day-to-day risk assessment and firm engagement.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the role and purpose of thematic review panels and risk governance panels
- Describe the operating procedures for both panel types, including the role of key participants
- Identify the main components of the RGP and TRP report
- Explain the do's and don'ts for panel members and presenters
- Discuss the importance of challenging and open discussion in panels

## Syllabus

**Morning | 09:30–13:00** — What are risk governance and thematic review panels? Purpose and types of panel within the overall supervisory risk framework, including recent risk assurance review findings Thematic review panels: what drives thematic reviews, types of review, how to set one up effectively, avoiding the ‘Christmas tree effect’ of over-scoping Managing moral hazard from thematic reviews; thematic governance panel composition, packs, role and reporting Risk governance panels: purpose, frequency, duration, composition, and how RGPs support supervisors

**Afternoon | 14:00–16:00** — Panel operation, roles and case study The phases of an RGP: pre-panel meeting, panel discussion (preliminary issues, core issues, conclusions), post-panel actions and follow-up Selecting and preparing panel members; role of presenters, panel chair, and panel secretary; meeting minutes and observer etiquette Preparing an RGP pack: key content, supervisory narrative, supporting information, assessing materiality Case study: a firm risk assessment exercise applying the panel process end-to-end
