## Aim and Purpose

How geopolitical tension, economic fragmentation, sanctions and trade restrictions affect financial firms' markets, clients, operations and strategy, and how boards should respond.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the firm's exposure to geopolitical events and fragmentation
- Explain how sanctions and trade restrictions can disrupt business at short notice
- Challenge management's country, client and supply-chain exposure analysis

## Syllabus

- Geopolitical fragmentation, conflict and shifting alliances
- Rapidly expanding sanctions, counter-sanctions and export controls
- Market, credit, operational and conduct transmission channels
- Exiting markets and clients: strategy, cost and customer impact
