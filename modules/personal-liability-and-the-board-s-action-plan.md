## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board action plan, examining directors' personal exposure and how the board evidences effective financial crime oversight.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess directors' personal regulatory, civil and criminal exposure
- Prepare the board to evidence its oversight to supervisors
- Produce a board action plan for financial crime oversight

## Syllabus

- Personal accountability, fitness and probity and enforcement against individuals
- Reasonable steps: what the minutes and papers need to show
- Board skills, training and external advice on financial crime
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month financial crime oversight plan
