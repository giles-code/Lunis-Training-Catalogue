## Aim and Purpose

How to lead a team through organisational change, new supervisory approaches or technology, understand reactions to change and keep the team focused and engaged.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how people respond to change and why change initiatives fail
- Communicate the case for change and involve the team
- Plan and sustain the implementation of change in the team

## Syllabus

- Why change happens in supervisory authorities
- Reactions to change and managing resistance
- Communicating and involving the team in change
- Embedding change: quick wins, feedback and follow-through
