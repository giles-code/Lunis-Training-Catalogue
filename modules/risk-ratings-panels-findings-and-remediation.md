## Aim and Purpose

How supervisory assessments are challenged and calibrated in internal panels, how ratings become findings letters and risk mitigation programmes, and how the firm responds credibly.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how panels calibrate ratings and what drives the outcome
- Interpret findings letters and the supervisory tools that may follow
- Design a remediation response that fixes root causes and closes with evidence

## Syllabus

- Risk governance panels: pack, challenge and calibration of ratings
- Findings letters, risk mitigation programmes and deadlines
- Escalation tools: attestations, skilled person reviews, directions and licence conditions
- Responding to findings: accuracy, root causes and evidence of closure
