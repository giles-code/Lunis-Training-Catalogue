## Aim and Purpose

How supervisory teams turn analysis into ratings and proposed actions, and how internal challenge and governance processes test their conclusions before findings reach the firm.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe how supervisors move from evidence to judgement
- Explain the role of internal challenge in supervisory decisions
- Anticipate what a well-reasoned finding looks like

## Syllabus

- From desk review and visits to key vulnerabilities
- Written rationales and the supervisory audit trail
- Internal challenge and peer review of supervisory conclusions
- Consistency across firms and over time
