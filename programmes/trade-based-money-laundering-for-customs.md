## Aim and Purpose

To give customs officers, FIU analysts and investigators a practical understanding of how international trade is used to move value and disguise criminal proceeds. Participants leave able to spot the red flags in trade and financial data, target high-risk traders and consignments, and build joint cases with FIU, tax and law-enforcement partners.

## What It Covers

How trade-based money laundering works, trade documentation and trade finance, customs data, mis-invoicing and misdescription of goods, phantom and multiple shipments, red-flag indicators and data analysis, free trade zones, and cooperation with FIUs, tax authorities and foreign customs.

## Intended Audience

- Customs officers, trade intelligence analysts and post-clearance audit teams
- FIU analysts and financial investigators handling trade-related intelligence
- Tax investigators and prosecutors working on trade-linked fraud and laundering cases

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how trade transactions are used to move value and launder proceeds
- Interpret customs declarations, trade documents and trade finance records
- Identify mis-invoicing, misdescription and other red flags in trade and financial data
- Assess the vulnerabilities of free trade zones and transhipment
- Analyse trade data to target high-risk traders and consignments
- Build joint investigations with FIUs, tax authorities and foreign partners

## Structure and Format

Six modules of about three hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines a technical briefing with trade-document exercises and data analysis, ending in a case workshop, and modules can be selected or tailored to the participants' agencies and trade profile.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of customs, financial intelligence or financial crime investigation is helpful. No specialist knowledge of trade finance is required.

## Core Reading

- FATF (2006), Trade Based Money Laundering
- FATF (2010), Money Laundering Vulnerabilities of Free Trade Zones
- FATF and Egmont Group (2020), Trade-Based Money Laundering: Trends and Developments
- FATF and Egmont Group (2021), Trade-Based Money Laundering: Risk Indicators
