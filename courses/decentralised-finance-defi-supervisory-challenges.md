## Aim and Purpose

The aim of this workshop is to build participants' understanding of how DeFi protocols operate outside traditional intermediated structures, examining the supervisory and regulatory perimeter challenges this creates and the tools available to address them.

## Intended Audience

Mid-level supervisory staff building capability in digital assets/crypto-asset regulation.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how DeFi protocols differ from intermediated financial services
- Describe common DeFi structures: lending pools, DEXs, yield aggregators
- Assess smart contract risk within DeFi protocols
- Evaluate the regulatory perimeter challenges DeFi presents
- Identify supervisory tools and approaches for addressing DeFi risk

## Syllabus

**Session 1: DeFi fundamentals (09:30–10:45)**
*Objective: Explain DeFi and its disintermediated structure*
- How DeFi removes traditional intermediaries
- Common DeFi structures: lending/borrowing pools, decentralised exchanges, yield aggregators
- Governance tokens and decentralised governance models
- Why disintermediation challenges traditional regulatory concepts
Activities:
- Facilitated discussion: DeFi developments participants are tracking

**Session 2: Smart contract risk (11:00–12:30)**
*Objective: Assess smart contract risk*
- How smart contracts execute DeFi protocol logic
- Common smart contract vulnerabilities: exploits, flash loan attacks
- Assessing audit quality for a DeFi protocol
- Oracle risk and price manipulation vulnerabilities
Activities:
- Group exercise: assess smart contract risk factors for a sample DeFi protocol

**Session 3: Regulatory perimeter and application (13:30–14:45)**
*Objective: Evaluate perimeter challenges and identify supervisory tools*
- Challenges of applying entity-based regulation to disintermediated protocols
- Emerging approaches: regulating access points, front-ends, and fiat gateways
- Case study: a DeFi protocol raising perimeter questions
Activities:
- Case study: identify supervisory tools and approaches for a sample DeFi scenario

**Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)**
*Objective: Consolidate learning*
- Recap: DeFi structures, smart contract risk, regulatory perimeter
Activities:
- Reflection and discussion
- Q&A

## Structure and Format

Proposed as a half-day workshop (approx. 3.5 hours substantive content), comprising 4 sessions:
- Session 1: DeFi fundamentals (09:30–10:45)
- Session 2: Smart contract risk (11:00–12:30)
- Session 3: Regulatory perimeter and application (13:30–14:45)
- Session 4: Wrap-up and key takeaways (14:45–15:00)

**Key Takeaways**
- DeFi's disintermediated structure challenges the entity-based foundations of traditional financial regulation
- Smart contract vulnerabilities are a distinct, technical risk category requiring specialist assessment skills
- Regulating access points and gateways is emerging as a practical approach where direct entity regulation is difficult
