## Aim and Purpose

How to scope a financial investigation, set objectives and hypotheses, prioritise lines of enquiry and manage records, exhibits and disclosure from the outset.

## Learning Objectives

- Set investigation objectives, hypotheses and a lines-of-enquiry plan
- Prioritise enquiries in proportion to risk and resources
- Maintain decision logs, exhibits and audit trails from the start

## Syllabus

- Scoping: subjects, periods, predicate offences and assets
- Investigation plans, hypotheses and lines of enquiry
- Coordination with intelligence, asset recovery and prosecutors
- Record-keeping, decision logs and early disclosure planning
