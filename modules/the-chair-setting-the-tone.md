## Aim and Purpose

The chair's pivotal role in creating the conditions for effective challenge and decision-making, and the behaviours that separate an effective chair from an ineffective one.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the chair's responsibilities for board conduct and performance
- Assess chairing behaviours that encourage or suppress contribution
- Identify how the chair's relationship with the chief executive shapes the board

## Syllabus

- The chair's role: agenda, discussion, conclusion and board performance
- Attributes of an effective chair: authority with humility, patience with incisiveness
- Drawing out quieter voices and bringing circular debate to a close
- Chair and chief executive: partnership without capture
