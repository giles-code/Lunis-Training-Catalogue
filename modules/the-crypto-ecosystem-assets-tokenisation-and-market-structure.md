## Aim and Purpose

Maps the participants in crypto markets, from protocol developers and token issuers to trading venues, custodians and end users, and how value, risk and accountability flow between them.

## Learning Objectives

- Map ecosystem participants to accountability and control points
- Analyse the economic incentives that create concentration and integrity risk
- Recognise red flags in tokenisation and market infrastructure providers

## Syllabus

- Networks, issuers, validators, wallets, venues, brokers and market makers
- Tokenisation: tokenised assets, utility tokens and stablecoins
- Incentives, concentration and conflicts in crypto market structure
- Resilience of networks, bridges, oracles and infrastructure providers
