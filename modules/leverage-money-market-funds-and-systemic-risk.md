## Aim and Purpose

How funds use borrowing and derivatives to create leverage, how leverage is measured and limited, and why money market funds and leveraged funds matter for financial stability.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how leverage is created, measured and reported by funds
- Assess the use of leverage limits and supervisory data to identify risk
- Evaluate money market fund structures, run risk and reform options

## Syllabus

- Gross, commitment and risk-based leverage measures and the IOSCO framework
- Derivatives, securities financing and counterparty exposure in funds
- Money market funds: constant and variable net asset value models and run dynamics
- Using supervisory data to monitor leverage and systemic risk
