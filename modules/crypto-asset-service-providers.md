## Aim and Purpose

How crypto-asset exchanges, custodians and brokers operate, how they connect to the traditional financial system, and the customer and transaction records they hold.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the main crypto-asset service provider business models
- Describe how fiat on-ramps and off-ramps connect to banks and payment firms
- Identify customer, wallet and travel rule records held by providers

## Syllabus

- Exchanges, custodial wallet providers, brokers and kiosks
- Fiat on-ramps and off-ramps and banking relationships
- Customer files, wallet addresses and blockchain transaction data
- Travel rule information and requests across providers
