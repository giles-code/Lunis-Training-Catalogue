## Aim and Purpose

How funds are structured to share costs, offer choice and pool assets, and the advantages and drawbacks of each structure.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain stand-alone and umbrella structures and segregated liability
- Describe fund of funds, multi-manager and master-feeder arrangements
- Weigh the pros and cons of each structure for sponsors and investors

## Syllabus

- Stand-alone funds and umbrella funds with sub-funds
- Segregation of liability between sub-funds
- Fund of funds and multi-manager structures
- Master-feeder structures and their uses
