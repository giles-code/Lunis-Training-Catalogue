## Aim and Purpose

How ransomware and other cyber-enabled crimes generate proceeds, how payments are made and laundered, and how investigators trace and seek to recover them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the ransomware business model and payment process
- Trace ransom payments and their laundering through crypto-asset channels
- Coordinate with victims, incident responders and service providers

## Syllabus

- Ransomware-as-a-service and affiliate models
- Ransom payments, reporting and engagement with victims
- Laundering and cash-out of crypto-asset proceeds
- Seizure and recovery opportunities
