## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a board action plan covering the board's own capability, committee mandates, remuneration and incentives, and keeping governance current as standards evolve.

## Learning Objectives

- Assess the board's sustainability expertise and plan to close gaps
- Evaluate how remuneration and incentives support sustainability objectives
- Produce a board action plan for climate and sustainability oversight

## Syllabus

- Board skills, committee mandates and external advice
- Linking sustainability metrics to remuneration without perverse incentives
- Emerging themes: nature, social risk and transition finance
- Workshop: the board's twelve-month climate and sustainability oversight plan
