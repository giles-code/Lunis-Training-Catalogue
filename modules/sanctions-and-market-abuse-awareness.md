## Aim and Purpose

The basics of financial sanctions and market abuse: what they prohibit, how breaches happen and what staff must do to prevent and report them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain what financial sanctions are and how firms comply with them
- Recognise insider dealing, unlawful disclosure and market manipulation
- Identify when to escalate a potential sanctions or market abuse concern

## Syllabus

- Sanctions regimes, asset freezes and screening
- Evasion techniques and sanctions red flags
- Inside information, insider lists and personal account dealing
- Market manipulation and suspicious transaction reporting
