## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together by examining how the firm handles production orders, freezing requests and asset recovery, ending with a workshop that takes a case from alert to report and law enforcement response.

## Learning Objectives

- Handle information requests, production orders and freezing requests correctly
- Support seizure and return of crypto-assets while protecting the firm
- Apply the programme to a realistic case and agree control improvements

## Syllabus

- Verifying, prioritising and responding to law enforcement requests
- Freezing, preserving and transferring assets: keys, custody and records
- Public-private partnerships and evidencing effectiveness to supervisors
- Workshop: from alert to report, response and control remediation
