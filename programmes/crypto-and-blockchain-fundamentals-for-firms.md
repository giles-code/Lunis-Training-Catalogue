## Aim and Purpose

To give industry professionals a comprehensive, practical grounding in how blockchain technology works and how crypto-asset markets, products and service providers operate. Participants leave able to analyse business models and their economic function, identify the key prudential, conduct and financial crime risks, and build the governance and controls that clients, investors, banking partners and supervisors expect.

## What It Covers

Blockchain technology, crypto-asset typology and market structure, wallets and custody, crypto-asset service provider business models, tokenisation, stablecoins and unbacked crypto-assets, DeFi and emerging technologies, enterprise risk, financial crime and the travel rule, and the strategic and regulatory outlook for digital finance.

## Intended Audience

- Founders, senior managers and staff of crypto-asset service providers, issuers and fintech firms, and of banks, asset managers and payment firms entering digital assets
- Compliance, risk, MLRO, legal, operations and product professionals working on digital asset activities
- Consultants, lawyers, auditors, investors and other advisers who support crypto-asset businesses

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how blockchain and distributed ledger technology operate within digital finance
- Analyse crypto-asset types and business models to determine their economic function, revenue drivers and risk profile
- Evaluate the risks of custody, tokenisation and stablecoins and the controls that manage them
- Design the activities, governance and controls of a credible crypto-asset service provider
- Apply AML/CFT requirements, including the travel rule, to crypto-asset operations
- Evaluate emerging trends and regulatory developments and position the business to adapt and grow

## Structure and Format

Ten modules of about four hours each, delivered in person, virtually or in a hybrid format, either as a multi-day programme or as a series of half-day sessions. Each module combines technical explanation with case studies, red flags and practical control exercises, and modules can be selected or tailored to the business model and markets of the participants.

## Useful Prior Knowledge and Experience

Some experience of financial services, compliance, risk or advisory work is helpful. No technical background in blockchain or crypto-assets is required.

## Core Reading

- FSB, High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Crypto-Asset Activities and Markets (2023)
- IOSCO, Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets (2023)
- FATF, Updated Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (2021)
- Basel Committee, Prudential Treatment of Cryptoasset Exposures (2022)
