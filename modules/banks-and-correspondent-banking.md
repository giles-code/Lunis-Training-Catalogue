## Aim and Purpose

How retail, private and corporate banks operate, how correspondent relationships move money across borders, and what account, payment and customer records banks hold.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain retail, private and corporate banking products
- Describe correspondent banking and cross-border payment chains
- Identify the records banks hold and where they sit

## Syllabus

- Current, savings, loan and trade finance products
- Private banking and wealth management
- Correspondent banking, nested relationships and payment messages
- Bank records: onboarding files, statements, payment data and alerts
