## Aim and Purpose

How to match supervisory effort and response to the size of the risk, prioritise issues across a portfolio, and select the right tool from the supervisory toolkit.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply proportionality to the scale, nature and impact of a firm's risks
- Prioritise issues using impact, probability and supervisory objectives
- Select a supervisory tool or intervention that fits the risk

## Syllabus

- Proportionality in principle and in practice
- Impact and probability: prioritising across firms and issues
- The supervisory toolkit: from dialogue to formal powers
- Avoiding both regulatory overreach and supervisory forbearance
