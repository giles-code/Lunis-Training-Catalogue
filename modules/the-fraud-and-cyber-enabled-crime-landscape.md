## Aim and Purpose

The scale and nature of fraud and cyber-enabled financial crime, the organised groups behind it, and why speed, volume and cross-border reach challenge traditional investigation.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the main categories of fraud and cyber-enabled financial crime
- Explain the organisation and business models of fraud networks
- Identify the challenges these crimes pose for investigation and prosecution

## Syllabus

- Fraud as a high-volume, organised and transnational crime
- Social engineering and the industrialisation of scams
- Victim and loss data: reporting, under-reporting and triage
- International assessments of cyber-enabled fraud
