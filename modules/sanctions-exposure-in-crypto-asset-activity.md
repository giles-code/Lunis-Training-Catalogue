## Aim and Purpose

How sanctions risk arises through designated addresses, sanctioned services and jurisdictions, and how the firm screens, blocks, freezes and reports in a twenty-four-hour, borderless market.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify direct and indirect sanctions exposure in on-chain and customer activity
- Design address, customer and geolocation screening controls
- Respond to a sanctions hit: freezing, holding and reporting to the authority

## Syllabus

- Designated addresses, sanctioned services and high-risk jurisdictions
- Address screening, IP and device geolocation and evasion indicators
- Exposure through counterparties, nested exchanges and decentralised protocols
- Freezing, holding and reporting crypto-assets and handling tainted deposits
