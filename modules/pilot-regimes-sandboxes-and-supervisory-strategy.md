## Aim and Purpose

Brings the programme together into a strategic view of how authorities enable and oversee tokenisation, from sandboxes and pilot regimes to permanent frameworks and international coordination.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare sandbox, pilot regime and permanent framework approaches to tokenisation
- Evaluate a tokenisation initiative against supervisory and financial stability criteria
- Design the authority's approach to tokenised markets

## Syllabus

- Pilot regimes and sandboxes: exemptions, limits and exit routes
- Lessons from tokenisation pilots and central bank experiments
- International work: FSB, IOSCO, CPMI and BIS on tokenisation
- Workshop: supervisory review of a tokenised bond and settlement proposal
