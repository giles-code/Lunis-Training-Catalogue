## Aim and Purpose

How to build a board with the right mix of skills, independence and perspectives, the due diligence a director should complete before accepting an appointment, and how to manage tenure, refreshment and succession.

## Learning Objectives

- Design a board composition and skills matrix for a regulated firm or fund
- Carry out due diligence before accepting a board appointment
- Plan tenure, refreshment, succession and diversity objectives

## Syllabus

- Board size, balance of non-executive and independent directors, and skills matrices
- Due diligence before accepting an offer: good practice and red flags
- Tenure limits, board refreshment and succession planning
- Diversity of skills, background and thinking, barriers and effective initiatives
