## Aim and Purpose

How participation affects capital, liquidity, accounting and balance sheet risk, including the treatment of crypto-asset exposures and the treasury implications of holding or issuing digital money.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply the Basel Committee prudential standard for crypto-asset exposures
- Assess liquidity, concentration and counterparty risks of participation
- Identify accounting and reporting questions before launch

## Syllabus

- Basel Committee classification of tokenised assets and stablecoins
- Liquidity risk, reserve investment and redemption pressure
- Counterparty and concentration risk in issuers and platforms
- Accounting recognition, disclosure and audit considerations
