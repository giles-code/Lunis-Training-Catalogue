## Aim and Purpose

How regulation applies when funds are distributed, managed or invested across borders, and the main approaches authorities use to grant or restrict access, from national treatment to regional fund passports.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how cross-border distribution, portfolio management and investment are regulated
- Compare national treatment, unilateral and mutual recognition, equivalence and passporting
- Assess the practical effect of regional fund passports and recognition schemes

## Syllabus

- Distribution: passports, private placement and local registration
- Delegation of portfolio management and sub-advisory arrangements
- Equivalence, substitute compliance and mutual recognition in practice
- Regional fund passports in Europe and Asia Pacific
